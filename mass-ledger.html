1 · The premise, and the rule of the page

Mass is trapped rate. A bound circulation holds turning-rate it cannot spend; weigh it and you read that ledger. When $A$ circulation quanta (comparative label: nucleons) pack at dense contact, part of each quantum's rate is meshed with its neighbors and no longer counts against the scale: binding $B$ = the total discount, $b = B/A$ = discount per quantum. Of the $A$ quanta, $Z$ belong to the sense class that carries the long-range phase handle (label: protons; "charge" dissolved — a phase sense, not a substance); $N = A-Z$ is the other class.

The rule. The empirical binding curve is famous — no registration can feign ignorance of the nickel–iron peak. The discipline is therefore graded confrontation: distinguish exact identities, conditional model results, measured inputs, and failed tests before any comparison. The classical five-term formula (comparative shadow: von Weizsäcker 1935) supplies the familiar silhouette. This page asks which pieces the ledger can motivate or compute; it no longer presents the whole curve as independently derived.

Rates, not speeds (GROUNDING discipline): every quantity below is a count, a rate of angular change, or an energy of trapped rate. Nothing moves through a void; nothing pulls. The dense packing is contact of circulations, and the ledger only ever records meshing, filing, and strain.

Units — the energy currency, cashed out. The ledger's native measure is turn rate: $E = h\nu$, and one MeV of the record prices $\nu = 2.418\times10^{20}$ whole closures per second $= 241.8$ EHz $= 0.2418$ ZHz (in the calculus, $E/\hbar = 1.519\times10^{21}$ rad/s; in Pope's mechanical currency, $1.602\times10^{-13}$ J — his own practice for the coulomb). The measured record (AME2020) is kept in keV, so this page states rate first and retains MeV only as a tagged orientation to that record. The electronvolt prices energy as a charge-thing dropped through a volt of field-potential — two extant objects POAMS dissolves; the number survives the dissolution, the picture does not. Every energy on this page is a rate of turning the packed whole has given up: quoted in the record's currency for record-matching, meant — and, at the milestones, stated — as rate.
