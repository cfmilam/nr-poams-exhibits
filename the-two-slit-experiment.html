The Two-Slit Experiment: Interference Without Waves

This exhibit is an extrapolation of the foundational work of N. Vivian “Viv” Pope & Dr. Anthony D. Osborne, PhD, in the philosophy of Normal Realism (NR) and the mathematics of the Pope Osborne Angular Momentum Synthesis (POAMS).

A Commonsense Look at the Two-Slit Experiment

Philosophy first: before asking what light is, look at what the apparatus is. The two-slit experiment — Feynman's "only mystery" of quantum mechanics — stops being mysterious the moment three habits of language are surrendered: that something must travel from source to screen, that the something must be a wave or a particle, and that the pattern must be made in flight. Normal Realism surrenders all three. The apparatus supplies a static family of path-difference relations; the prepared source supplies the relevant ruling and composition conditions; the quantum is a whole turn of the one substance that either happens entire or not at all; and the connection it happens across has zero proper time — nothing is ever en route. This page rebuilds the experiment on those three feet and runs the modern record through them — single electrons, delayed choice, the quantum eraser, molecules of two thousand atoms — without making a foreign ontology the admission price.

1 · The record, plain

Young, 1801: light through two narrow slits lands on a screen in bright and dark bands. The extant books call it vivid proof that light is a wave. Then the same books produce the light one quantum at a time and count arrivals with a photocell — and the bands still form, click by indivisible click. Feynman's verdict on the pair of facts is the standard one, and it is worth quoting exactly:

"…a phenomenon which is impossible, absolutely impossible, to explain in any classical way, and which has in it the heart of quantum mechanics. In reality, it contains the only mystery."— R. P. Feynman, Lectures on Physics III·1 extant record

Any account must keep three measured facts measured:

FactWhere measured
iBoth slits open: fringes — even at intensities so low that quanta arrive one at a timeYoung 1801; Taylor 1909; Tonomura 1989 (§4)
iiEvery arrival is whole. No detector has ever caught a fraction of a quantumGrangier–Roger–Aspect 1986 (§3)
iiiMake the alternatives distinguishable and the unsorted fringes vanish; project onto erasing outputs and complementary fringes return in the corresponding conditioned sub-censuses, even when that sorting record is made after the signal countKwiat–Steinberg–Chiao 1992; Kim 2000; Jacques 2007 (§5)

A familiar popular cartoon holds all three by letting the quantum go through both slits as a wave, collapse to a point as a particle, and consult the future about which to be. That cartoon is not the operational record and does not exhaust extant formalisms. This page offers a POAMS account without those travelling-body pictures.

2 · Geometry first — the pattern is in the apparatus

Pope's opening move is to forget light entirely and measure the hardware. Pull two measuring-tapes tight from a source point, one through each slit, to the same point on the screen — bent nowhere but at the slits. The two tapes measure different lengths, and the difference $\Delta$ varies smoothly as the screen point moves. (His pub demonstration: palms together, fingertips on a wall; swing your arms sideways and watch the palms slide past each other — that slide is $\Delta$.)

Now supply a ruling $\lambda$ and mark the screen wherever $\Delta$ is an even number of half-units, leaving blank wherever it is odd. A candidate fringe-parity map appears — its sites computed from tape lengths once that ruling is supplied. Nothing was emitted, nothing travelled, nothing waved. The apparatus owns, as built-in geometry, a family of static path-difference maps; the prepared source selects the relevant $\lambda$, and the composition/counting rules of §3 select how strongly each site is represented. Block either slit — one tape — and $\Delta$ and its parity cease to exist. The remaining single-path envelope is a separate input, not a result of two-tape arithmetic.

The tape-measure map. Two tapes, one through each slit, to a screen point you move. Given the selected display ruling $\lambda$, the strip is painted from the resulting path-difference arithmetic: bright where the difference is an even count of half-units, dark where odd. The blocked-slit view uses a schematic Gaussian envelope inserted by hand; tape parity does not derive it. geometry + chosen ruling; normalized schematic

Native reading. What the extant record calls wave interference is this parity map — a property of the source–slits–screen relation, not of anything in flight. The Tantalizing paper puts the whole section in one sentence: in the apparatus “as designed and manufactured, even at the drawing-board stage there are as many whole geometrical lengths as a draughtsman may draw between the source and the screen via the two slits; and every one of these lengths is potentially a free path for a quantum transaction between directly inter-resonating atoms at the two places.” The only question the light gets to answer is which sites of the map light up, and how often. That is the next section.

3 · The whole turn — what "quantum" means

The corpus holds one substance — angular momentum — coming only in completed closures, $\oint d\varphi = 2\pi n$: whole turns of phase, action in whole units of $h$ see Aufbau as Closure · Mass as Trapped Rate. A fractional turn is not a smaller thing but no thing: it does not close and cannot persist. POAMS therefore identifies the quantum of light-interaction with one whole cycle of action — not a small bullet, but an event that either closes entire or does not occur. This is a governing POAMS premise, not a deduction from the two open-path phasors below. The operational record is consonant with event wholeness but does not by itself derive the identification: Grangier, Roger & Aspect found the two beamsplitter outputs strongly anticorrelated below the classical-wave bound (not a literally coincidence-free apparatus record) and obtained greater than 98% visibility in a separate interferometer measured · Europhys. Lett. 1, 173 (1986).

So, in the native account, the quantum is not what interferes — it is never a passenger in two places. What interferes is the paths: the two tape-lengths of §2, read as potential connections between source and screen. Their open-path phase tallies vary continuously; the whole-turn requirement belongs to the completed transaction, not to every intermediate path phase. Assign each ideal path its phase at the chosen ruling and compose the pair:

$$\text{joint potential} \;\propto\; \left|\,e^{i\varphi_1} + e^{i\varphi_2}\,\right| \;=\; 2\left|\cos\!\frac{\pi \Delta}{\lambda}\right|, \qquad \varphi_{1,2} = \frac{2\pi\, r_{1,2}}{\lambda}$$

— maximal where $\Delta$ is an even count of half-wavelengths (the two connections in synch: go), zero where odd (exactly out of synch: no-go). This composition is Pope's own, in his own units: divide the paths “into positive and negative half-units of the quantum length,” and where like half-lengths coincide “the sum of the potentials at that point is doubled (quadrupled in area terms), and where a positive half-length coincides with a negative one, the sum of the two potentials at that point is zero.” Doubled in potential, quadrupled in area — amplitude and census, stated as one geometric fact. The dark fringe is not a place where two things collided and cancelled; it is a site where the geometry offers no closable connection — a receiver standing blind to the source see POAMS & Telecommunications. An absorbing detector in one path replaces that path's connection-to-the-screen with a connection-to-itself, and the pair-composition above loses a term — the fringes go, with no spookery see POAMS & Electricity.

Stated once, honestly: what is imported here. Geometry forces $\Delta(x)$. The prepared source supplies the relevant $\lambda$; this ideal panel further adopts equal path weights, full composability, complex phasor addition, a separate one-path envelope, and the rule that arrival counts run as the square of the composed potential. Under those stated conditions the census is $4\cos^2(\pi\Delta/\lambda)$ times the envelope, peaking at four times one equal path and averaging two. The extant books call the squaring rule Born's rule. Pope identifies the composed potential as “virtually the Schrödinger wave-function,” but not as a material wave: the final edition with Osborne says there is “nothing in the vacuum that could possibly wave” and locates the substratum in the apparatus relation itself. This corpus adopts that POAMS identification and reads the squared magnitude as a census of completed transactions over potential connections. It does not claim to derive the complex composition, equal weights, envelope, squaring rule, or single-event draw from the pillars. Same honesty class as the Madelung filling rule see Periodic Table §15: native ontology, explicit imports.

4 · The census — every click whole, the pattern statistical

Run the experiment the modern way, one quantum at a time, and watch both truths at once: no arrival is ever partial, and no arrival ever shows a fringe. The fringe is not in any event; it is in the census of events — the go/no-go geometry expressing itself in arrival statistics, click by whole click.

0 arrivals

The census builder. Each release lands as one whole click — a dot, never a fraction, never a band. This is a separate normalized toy coordinate: a hand-chosen Gaussian envelope, equal path weights, perfect composability or perfect distinguishability, fixed fringe spacing, and the imported square-law sampler are hard-coded. It is not coupled to the tape controls or any tabulated apparatus. Changing the mark condition clears the old ensemble so unlike conditions are never mixed. pedagogical sampler; assumptions exposed

The flagship one-at-a-time receipt measured · Hitachi, Tonomura et al. 1989 used an electron biprism rather than a literal machined double slit: electrons at 50 kV — speed ≈ 0.4$c$, a metre-long column crossed in ~8 ns — emitted at about ten per second, so that rate × speed gives ~12,000 km mean longitudinal separation and at most one electron in the instrument. Each arrival: one whole bright spot. After twenty minutes: clean fringes made entirely of whole spots. In the native reading, the census fills the apparatus relation's map. The extant caption calls the result “far from what our common sense tells us.” The Tantalizing paper states the census principle directly: the path-potentials are independent of the number of transactions; even one quantum per month builds the same cumulative pattern. Tonomura supplies the operational one-at-a-time record; Bach et al. below supplies the literal controllable double slit.

5 · Nothing is en route — the τ = 0 connection

One mystery remains standing from §1: how does the quantum "know," before the slits, what stands after them? Strip the question of its stowaway premise — that the quantum spends time between source and screen in which knowing would have to happen. The corpus holds light as the edge of the time cone: along a light connection the proper time is zero, $\tau^2 = t^2 - (s/c)^2 = 0$ — emission and absorption are, in the connection's own books, one event see The Rate of Time · Twins Paradox. In the paper's words: “so far as the quantum is concerned it is present at both places at once, with no question of ‘velocity’ being involved.” Gilbert Lewis said it in 1926: emitter and absorber are literally touching. Pope draws the terminological conclusion: the word "photon" — a travelling particle — describes nothing in the physics, and he retires it for photum (plural phota): the quantum of interaction, not a passenger in transit. This corpus made the same retirement on other grounds see Quantum Cinematograph · GROUNDING: the photon epicycle.

Observer-time orders transactions at the finite ratio $c$; native proper-time bookkeeping assigns no elapsed interval within the completed connection. 6 · Matter keeps the same books

Electrons, neutrons, atoms, molecules — slower than light, manifestly non-instantaneous — build the same fringes. Pope's essay names the native mechanism: what connects source and screen for a matter beam is not the body's cruise but its quanta of interaction, and the rate of the connecting quantum is the beam's kinetic energy on the universal converter, $\nu = E_k/h$. The geometry unit the parity map uses is the matching length — the extant books' de Broglie wavelength. Extant bridge, stated once for this page: $\lambda = h/p$, and 1 eV ↔ 241.799 THz on the rate ladder bridge.

$$\nu = \frac{E_k}{h} \qquad\qquad \lambda = \frac{h}{p} \qquad\qquad \text{dark sites: } \Delta = \left(n+\tfrac12\right)\lambda$$

7 · The language problem — duality as a grin without a cat

What remains of wave–particle duality? Two bookkeeping columns, misread as two bodies:

The wordWhat it actually namesStatus
"the wave"the parity map — the apparatus's static geometry of closable connections (§2), unit λ = h/pbookkeeping; never observed as a body, never in any single click
"the particle"the whole-turn transaction — one completed closure, $\oint d\varphi = 2\pi n$, whole or nothing (§3)the event; never mid-flight, never fringed

The Tantalizing paper counts the absurdity of the merger twice before dissolving it: “a 'wave' whose constituents are particles travelling at the same speed as the wave is not a wave, but a moving solid object”; and particles “cannot 'interfere' with one another, far less with themselves.” (No commonsense person asks the water molecules to travel at the speed of the water wave; their motion is small circulation about fixed points — the wave is the sequence, never the passenger.) The extant model keeps the interference arithmetic while denying it a subject, then mints two subjects to carry it — a wave-thing that is never caught and a particle-thing that is never partial, alternating on observation. That is the attribute-ontology failure the corpus has named in creed: grins without cats, and cats drawn around bookkeeping — the grin is the cat. Promote the quantity to the substance and both phantoms retire at once: the closure is the "particle"; the closure-potential geometry is the "wave"; nothing is ever both, because neither is a thing in flight.

The path-integral analogy has a precise boundary. Feynman's formalism assigns histories a phase $S/\hbar$ and composes them. In the monochromatic free-path limit used here, the relative action phase reduces to a path-length phase, so Pope's tapes are a genuine geometrical picture of that special case. The general action may include time-dependent apparatus and interactions and is not merely Euclidean tape length. POAMS's further claim is ontological: the composed alternatives are potential connections, not itineraries of a travelling body. The mathematics composes histories; the native caption identifies what that composition is about. Wittgenstein's word for mysteries of caption is Scheinprobleme — and Pope's 1995 challenge to “photons,” “wave-particles,” and “the limiting speed of light in vacuo” remains the philosophical front.

The page in one paragraph. The apparatus supplies the path-difference geometry; the prepared source supplies its ruling, and the imported composition/counting law turns the pair into a census map. The quantum is a whole POAMS closure — it happens entire at a permitted site or not at all. The connection it happens across has zero native proper time, so delayed choices and erased labels belong to the joint geometry the transaction actually closes through; eraser fringes are conditioned sub-censuses, never a rewritten marginal. Matter runs the same ledger with the ruling set by $h/p$, down to 53 fm under a five-nanometre molecule. Wave and particle were the map and the event, wearing costumes. Interference without a travelling wave; arrival without bullets; geometry composing, the census keeping score.
