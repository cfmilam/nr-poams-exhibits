The Two-Slit Experiment: Interference Without Waves

This exhibit is an extrapolation of the foundational work of N. Vivian “Viv” Pope & Dr. Anthony D. Osborne, PhD, in the philosophy of Normal Realism (NR) and the mathematics of the Pope Osborne Angular Momentum Synthesis (POAMS).

A Commonsense Look at the Two-Slit Experiment

Philosophy first: before asking what light is, look at what the apparatus is. The two-slit experiment — Feynman's "only mystery" of quantum mechanics — stops being mysterious the moment three habits of language are surrendered: that something must travel from source to screen, that the something must be a wave or a particle, and that the pattern must be made in flight. Normal Realism surrenders all three. The interference geometry is a static property of the apparatus, present before any light; the quantum is a whole turn of the one substance that either happens entire or not at all; and the connection it happens across has zero proper time — nothing is ever en route. This page rebuilds the experiment on those three feet, runs the modern record through it — single electrons, delayed choice, the quantum eraser, molecules of two thousand atoms — and shows the mystery was manufactured by the vocabulary.

1 · The record, plain

Young, 1801: light through two narrow slits lands on a screen in bright and dark bands. The extant books call it vivid proof that light is a wave. Then the same books produce the light one quantum at a time and count arrivals with a photocell — and the bands still form, click by indivisible click. Feynman's verdict on the pair of facts is the standard one, and it is worth quoting exactly:

"…a phenomenon which is impossible, absolutely impossible, to explain in any classical way, and which has in it the heart of quantum mechanics. In reality, it contains the only mystery."— R. P. Feynman, Lectures on Physics III·1 extant record

Any account must keep three measured facts measured:

FactWhere measured
iBoth slits open: fringes — even at intensities so low that quanta arrive one at a timeYoung 1801; Taylor 1909; Tonomura 1989 (§4)
iiEvery arrival is whole. No detector has ever caught a fraction of a quantumGrangier–Roger–Aspect 1986 (§3)
iiiMark which slit was used and the fringes vanish; erase the mark — even after the slits — and they returnKwiat–Steinberg–Chiao 1992; Kim 2000; Jacques 2007 (§5)

The extant reading holds all three by letting the quantum go through both slits as a wave, collapse to a point as a particle, and consult the future about which to be. This page holds all three without any of that.

2 · Geometry first — the pattern is in the apparatus

Pope's opening move is to forget light entirely and measure the hardware. Pull two measuring-tapes tight from a source point, one through each slit, to the same point on the screen — bent nowhere but at the slits. The two tapes measure different lengths, and the difference $\Delta$ varies smoothly as the screen point moves. (His pub demonstration: palms together, fingertips on a wall; swing your arms sideways and watch the palms slide past each other — that slide is $\Delta$.)

Now mark the screen wherever $\Delta$ is an even number of half-units, and leave blank wherever it is odd. A fringe pattern appears — computed from tape lengths alone. Nothing was emitted, nothing travelled, nothing waved. The apparatus owns, as built-in geometry, a static map of path-difference parity, identical in form to the interference pattern, for any unit length you care to choose. Block either slit — one tape — and the parity map collapses to a smooth single-path distribution, exactly as the light experiment reports.

Native reading. What the extant record calls wave interference is this parity map — a property of the source–slits–screen relation, not of anything in flight. The Tantalizing paper puts the whole section in one sentence: in the apparatus “as designed and manufactured, even at the drawing-board stage there are as many whole geometrical lengths as a draughtsman may draw between the source and the screen via the two slits; and every one of these lengths is potentially a free path for a quantum transaction between directly inter-resonating atoms at the two places.” The only question the light gets to answer is which sites of the map light up, and how often. That is the next section.

3 · The whole turn — what "quantum" means

The corpus holds one substance — angular momentum — coming only in completed closures, $\oint d\varphi = 2\pi n$: whole turns of phase, action in whole units of $h$ see Aufbau as Closure · Mass as Trapped Rate. A fractional turn is not a smaller thing but no thing: it does not close and cannot persist. So the quantum of light-interaction is not a small bullet; it is one whole cycle of action — and wholeness is why it cannot split, duplicate, or half-arrive. In Pope's line: it either occurs or it doesn't. The measured record agrees with the wholeness clause to the letter: Grangier, Roger & Aspect put single quanta on a beamsplitter and found the two output detectors anticorrelated — one side or the other, never a divided click, at rates no classical wave can imitate — and then recombined the same source into fringes with near-unit visibility measured · Europhys. Lett. 1, 173 (1986).

So the quantum cannot be what interferes — it is never in two places to interfere with itself. What interferes is the paths: the two tape-lengths of §2, read as potential connections between source and screen. Each path admits a whole-turn connection only in proportion to how nearly the turn closes around it. Count each path's phase — its turn tally at the chosen rate — and compose the pair:

$$\text{joint potential} \;\propto\; \left|\,e^{i\varphi_1} + e^{i\varphi_2}\,\right| \;=\; 2\left|\cos\!\frac{\pi \Delta}{\lambda}\right|, \qquad \varphi_{1,2} = \frac{2\pi\, r_{1,2}}{\lambda}$$

— maximal where $\Delta$ is an even count of half-wavelengths (the two connections in synch: go), zero where odd (exactly out of synch: no-go). This composition is Pope's own, in his own units: divide the paths “into positive and negative half-units of the quantum length,” and where like half-lengths coincide “the sum of the potentials at that point is doubled (quadrupled in area terms), and where a positive half-length coincides with a negative one, the sum of the two potentials at that point is zero.” Doubled in potential, quadrupled in area — amplitude and census, stated as one geometric fact. The dark fringe is not a place where two things collided and cancelled; it is a site where the geometry offers no closable connection — a receiver standing blind to the source see POAMS & Telecommunications. A detector in one path is an absorber: it replaces that path's connection-to-the-screen with a connection-to-itself, and the pair-composition above loses a term — the fringes go, with no spookery see POAMS & Electricity.

Stated once, honestly: what is imported here. The parity of the sites — where the dark fringes sit — is forced geometry (§2). The census law between them — that arrival counts run as the square of the composed potential, $4\cos^2(\pi\Delta/\lambda)$ times the single-slit envelope, peaking at four times one slit, averaging two — is the measured record (the extant books call the squaring rule Born's rule). Pope goes one step further and names the identification outright — in the final edition with Osborne, the composed potential is “not a ‘wave’ in the real sense, for there is nothing in the vacuum that could possibly wave”; it “is virtually the Schrödinger wave-function” — with three differences: no Einstein separation between emitter and absorber, no travelling photon to conduct it, and no esoteric sub-space: “our wave-substratum… is no more nor less than the apparatus itself as a piece of ordinary hardware,” living in ordinary observer-space — nothing underlying to collapse. This corpus adopts that identification and reads the squared magnitude natively as a census of completed transactions over potential connections; it does not claim the squaring is derived from the pillars. Same honesty class as the Madelung filling rule see Periodic Table §15: the geometry is forced; one counting rule is imported intact.

4 · The census — every click whole, the pattern statistical

Run the experiment the modern way, one quantum at a time, and watch both truths at once: no arrival is ever partial, and no arrival ever shows a fringe. The fringe is not in any event; it is in the census of events — the go/no-go geometry expressing itself in arrival statistics, click by whole click.

The flagship receipt measured · Hitachi, Tonomura et al. 1989: electrons at 50 kV — speed ≈ 0.4$c$, a metre-long column crossed in ~10 ns — emitted at about ten per second, so that successive electrons stand ~12,000 km apart in flight terms and the instrument never holds more than one. Each arrival: one whole bright spot. After twenty minutes of one-at-a-time accumulation: clean fringes, made entirely of whole spots. Nothing was in the apparatus for anything to interfere with — the census simply filled in the geometry's map. The extant caption calls this "far from what our common sense tells us." It is exactly what commonsense reports once the pattern is the apparatus's and only the clicks are the light's — frames of a cinematograph, motion in the census alone see Quantum Cinematograph. The Tantalizing paper states the census in advance of watching it: the path-potentials “will be the same, regardless of how many quantum transactions actually take place… even if only one quantum is transferred per month, the cumulative effect is the same as for many transactions all at once — since, of course, it is the paths… that interfere… regardless of the actual transactions.” Tonomura's twenty minutes is that sentence performed.

5 · Nothing is en route — the τ = 0 connection

One mystery remains standing from §1: how does the quantum "know," before the slits, what stands after them? Strip the question of its stowaway premise — that the quantum spends time between source and screen in which knowing would have to happen. The corpus holds light as the edge of the time cone: along a light connection the proper time is zero, $\tau^2 = t^2 - (s/c)^2 = 0$ — emission and absorption are, in the connection's own books, one event see The Rate of Time · Twins Paradox. In the paper's words: “so far as the quantum is concerned it is present at both places at once, with no question of ‘velocity’ being involved.” Gilbert Lewis said it in 1926: emitter and absorber are literally touching. Pope draws the terminological conclusion: the word "photon" — a travelling particle — describes nothing in the physics, and he retires it for photum (plural phota): the quantum of interaction, not a passenger in transit. This corpus made the same retirement on other grounds see Quantum Cinematograph · GROUNDING: the photon epicycle.

And the standing worry — "instantaneous interaction contradicts relativity" — is backwards: the zero proper time of the light connection is supplied by relativity, first-year material read plainly. Pope's paper gives the resolution its right name: these instantaneous connections are not "superluminal" influences racing ahead of the light — they are not something additional to light at all, “but constitute an inseparable part of light”: not extraluminal, but intraluminal. What propagates at the finite $c$ is the ordering of transactions in an observer's frame — the ratio of distance-units to time-units, as Bondi put it — not a passenger's cruise. Instantaneous in its own books, finite-rate in yours: both, with no contradiction see The Rate of Time.

6 · Matter keeps the same books

Electrons, neutrons, atoms, molecules — slower than light, manifestly non-instantaneous — build the same fringes. Pope's essay names the native mechanism: what connects source and screen for a matter beam is not the body's cruise but its quanta of interaction, and the rate of the connecting quantum is the beam's kinetic energy on the universal converter, $\nu = E_k/h$. The geometry unit the parity map uses is the matching length — the extant books' de Broglie wavelength. Extant bridge, stated once for this page: $\lambda = h/p$, and 1 eV ↔ 241.799 THz on the rate ladder bridge.

$$\nu = \frac{E_k}{h} \qquad\qquad \lambda = \frac{h}{p} \qquad\qquad \text{dark sites: } \Delta = \left(n+\tfrac12\right)\lambda$$

The 53-femtometre verdict. A five-nanometre molecule of two thousand atoms fringes on a unit of 53 fm — smaller than a proton's radius, a hundred-thousandth of the body it is alleged to describe. Whatever $\lambda$ is, it is not a wobble of the molecule: nothing about a 5 nm body undulates at 53 fm. But as the geometry unit of the connection — the closure length the parity map is ruled in, set by $h/p$ — it is exactly at home: the heavier and faster the body, the finer the ruling, until the map's bands blur below resolution and the census looks classical. The classical world is not a different physics; it is the same parity map ruled too fine to see see Quantum Cinematograph — correspondence as frame rate.

7 · The language problem — duality as a grin without a cat

What remains of wave–particle duality? Two bookkeeping columns, misread as two bodies:

The wordWhat it actually namesStatus
"the wave"the parity map — the apparatus's static geometry of closable connections (§2), unit λ = h/pbookkeeping; never observed as a body, never in any single click
"the particle"the whole-turn transaction — one completed closure, $\oint d\varphi = 2\pi n$, whole or nothing (§3)the event; never mid-flight, never fringed

The Tantalizing paper counts the absurdity of the merger twice before dissolving it: “a 'wave' whose constituents are particles travelling at the same speed as the wave is not a wave, but a moving solid object”; and particles “cannot 'interfere' with one another, far less with themselves.” (No commonsense person asks the water molecules to travel at the speed of the water wave; their motion is small circulation about fixed points — the wave is the sequence, never the passenger.) The extant model keeps the interference arithmetic while denying it a subject, then mints two subjects to carry it — a wave-thing that is never caught and a particle-thing that is never partial, alternating on observation. That is the attribute-ontology failure the corpus has named in creed: grins without cats, and cats drawn around bookkeeping — the grin is the cat. Promote the quantity to the substance and both phantoms retire at once: the closure is the "particle"; the closure-potential geometry is the "wave"; nothing is ever both, because neither is a thing in flight.

Even the extant method already computes this way. Feynman's path integral assigns every path a phase — a turn tally — and composes them; the arrows are Pope's tape-measures in professional dress. The calculation sums over potential connections in a static geometry and never once propagates a body; the narration then awards the answer to a particle that took every path. The mathematics is transactional bookkeeping; only the caption travels. Wittgenstein's word for mysteries of caption is Scheinprobleme — and Pope's 1995 closing stands as written: what cannot be denied is that "photons," "wave-particles," "the limiting speed of light in vacuo" have lost touch with the logical commonness and connectedness of plain description. The experiment was never mysterious. The vocabulary was.

The page in one paragraph. The fringe map is the apparatus's static geometry — tape-measure parity, real before any light. The quantum is a whole turn — it happens entire at a site the map allows, or not at all. The connection it happens across has zero proper time — nothing is en route to know anything, so "delayed" choices and "erased" labels are just configurations of the geometry the transaction actually closes through. Matter runs the same ledger with the ruling set by $h/p$, down to 53 fm under a five-nanometre molecule. Wave and particle were the map and the event, wearing costumes. Interference without waves; arrival without bullets; geometry deciding, the census keeping score.
