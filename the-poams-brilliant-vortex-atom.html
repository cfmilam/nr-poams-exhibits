Abstract

Light-Speed, Gravitation and Quantum Instantaneity derives the entire empirically verified content of Special and General Relativity, the hydrogen spectrum, the dissolution of electrostatic “charge,” and a large part of cosmology from two premises only: (i) the constant $c$ is a dimensional conversion factor relating the units of observed distance and observed time — not a speed; and (ii) all free motion is a manifestation of angular momentum, which is holistically conserved. From these follow, in strict sequence, relativistic time-dilation as Pythagoras’ theorem, the Lorentz transformation, $E=mc^2$, the Balmer–Rydberg spectrum, the Schwarzschild metric without Einstein’s field equations, Mercury’s perihelion advance, stellar “light-bending,” and a refusal — on grounds of un-empiricism — of black holes, the Big Bang, and dark matter, the last replaced by a variable gravitational coefficient.

This paper restates that programme in full, then makes one substantive correction that the book did not carry through — the treatment of a spinning body of physical extent as a single angular-momentum accumulator — and re-expresses the entire synthesis in the single-vortex ontology of Normal Realism, in which orbital angular momentum is the winding of a closed vortex and spin is its twist, unified by the linking theorem $\mathrm{Lk}=\mathrm{Tw}+\mathrm{Wr}$. The correction identifies precisely when and where a spin-dependent weight change keeps its sign (the swamping crossover, $\rho \equiv K_s/K_0$) and shows that an extended body polarizes a fraction of its $\sim\!10^{22}$–$10^{24}$ internal accumulators into coherent alignment, so that its effective spin energy vastly exceeds the rigid-body value — vindicating the anomalous-weight measurements the rigid-body estimate had seemed to exclude. We keep to the book throughout and flag every extension.

The Touchstone

One test governs everything below: every claim must cash out as angular momentum keeping its books — a real circulation, a natural orbit, a winding or a twist, a phase. What cannot be so cashed has floated free of the physics, however elegant its mathematics. (The cautionary corpse is Wyler’s fine-structure formula — numbers spun from geometry that correspond to nothing.) This is what the [FORCED] and [EXT] tags mark throughout, and what §34 audits at the close.

Two commitments fix the frame. There is one substance — angular momentum; and everything is time, in the strict sense that this substance is its own rate of angular change, $L = dS/d\theta$. Nothing sits in a container; no force crosses a gap; there is only the one circulation, organised differently.

The single recurring trap — the authors’ own included — is to conflate the two organisations of that one substance. Orbit is not spin; writhe is not twist; speed is not weight. Where the argument turns hardest on this — the variable-$G$ weight of §14, the directional sign of §15, the linking theorem of §30 — the text names which is meant; the reader should too. PART I · THE NORMAL-REALIST FOUNDATION

1. Phenomenalism against the God’s-eye view [FORCED] (Ch. 1)

The synthesis is built on a philosophical decision made before any physics is done. Two stances are contrasted. God’s-eye-view realism (the default of textbook physics) presumes a world of things-in-themselves — particles, fields, forces, a space and a time — existing exactly as described, whether or not anyone observes them. Phenomenalism, or in the authors’ term Normal Realism, takes the opposite starting point: the data of physics are phenomena — what is actually observed — and theory is answerable to those and to logic, not to an imagined view from nowhere.

The lineage is explicit: Berkeley’s subjective idealism (esse est percipi), tempered by G. E. Moore’s common-sense realism, and above all Ernst Mach’s radical empiricism — the same Mach who rejected Einstein’s reification of space-time. The authors also trace the rise and fall of the mid-century Linguistic movement (Wittgenstein, Austin), keeping its central lesson: that a great many “problems” of physics are artifacts of language — of taking a useful manner of speaking for a description of nature. Normal Realism is therefore neo-Machian radical empiricism applied to physics, with light as the sole and sufficient fount of physical information — what we see, not something imagined to travel through a void.

This is not decoration. Every later dissolution — of the ether, the photon-in-flight, the in-vacuo force, the black hole, the Big Bang — is an application of this single discipline: refuse to reify the unobservable when a phenomenal account suffices.

2. The constant $c$ is a conversion factor, not a speed [FORCED] (Ch. 2)

Observed distance and observed duration are, at bottom, measured in the same currency: time. A metre is operationally a light-second divided by a fixed number $c$; the “number” is a units-conversion, exactly as $c^2$ converts kilograms to joules in $E=mc^2$. Writing the three spatial dimensions in seconds via $c$ renders the world four-dimensional in a single unit, and a “speed” acquires a geometric length measured in seconds. The reading is Hermann Bondi’s — in his words “all talk about the velocity of light dissolves into nothing” — and the authors’ own correspondence records his concurrence (Bondi to Pope, 20 August 1985).

Maxwell’s equations, on this reading, do not describe a wave travelling at $c$; they encode the fixed geometric ratio between the space-like and time-like measures of electromagnetic phenomena. The authors marshal the standard arguments against the speed interpretation of $c$: the null result of Michelson–Morley (no ether wind because there is no medium and nothing “moving through” it), the impossibility of catching light (a body cannot approach a conversion factor), and the observer-independence of $c$ (a units ratio is the same for everyone by construction). Light is pure information — the registration, at the observer, of a distant state of affairs.

3. Instantaneous action, and the non-paradox of delayed instantaneity [FORCED] (Ch. 3 §3.5; and the 1995 Physics Essays paper)

If light does not travel, then what we call “the transmission of light” is an immediate (non-mediated), proper-time-instantaneous quantum touching between two atoms, distant in observer-space. There is no “in-between” in which anything is en route. The seeming paradox — that we see the Sun “as it was eight minutes ago” — dissolves: the eight minutes is a fact about observer time and distance (the conversion), not about a photon’s flight. Osborne and Pope call this time-delayed instantaneity: instantaneous in proper time, delayed in observer time, and not paradoxical once $c$ is understood as a conversion factor. This underwrites the synthesis’s stance of instantaneous gravitational and inertial action-at-a-distance — correlation across distance without a mediating signal, the same holism that later makes angular momentum a global conservation law.

PART II · RELATIVITY FROM TWO IDEAS

4. Time-dilation as Pythagoras; the cone model [FORCED] (Ch. 3 §§3.2, 3.4)

With distance and time in one unit, a moving clock’s world-path is the hypotenuse of a right triangle whose legs are its proper time and its displacement (in seconds). Pythagoras gives immediately $$\tau = \left(1 - v^2/c^2\right)^{1/2}\, t, \tag{1}$$ the entire content of relativistic time-dilation, with no light postulate and no signalling argument. Experimental confirmation (atmospheric muon lifetimes, flown atomic clocks) is exactly what (1) predicts.

The cone model (Pope’s construction) makes the relation visual. Plot the accumulating measure as a cone: viewed end-on one recovers the Newtonian, unbounded-speed world; viewed from the side the same cone shows $c$ as the asymptotic slope — the Einsteinian world. One geometric object, two elevations: Newtonian and relativistic kinematics are two projections of a single Pythagorean figure.

5. The Doppler effect and the dissolution of the twins paradox [FORCED] (Ch. 3 §§3.6–3.7)

The relativistic Doppler formula follows from counting wave-periods emitted and received, $$\frac{\lambda_o}{\lambda_L} = \left(\frac{c+v}{c-v}\right)^{1/2}. \tag{2}$$ The “twins paradox” is then not a paradox but a bookkeeping of asymmetric period-counts: the travelling twin’s turn-around breaks the symmetry, and a straightforward Doppler tally of signals in each direction gives the accumulated proper-time difference with no appeal to acceleration mystique. What is symmetric (mutual time-dilation between inertial observers) and what is not (the actual elapsed-time difference for a round trip) are cleanly separated by the count.

6. Minkowski space-time and the Lorentz transformation [FORCED] (Ch. 3 §3.8; Ch. 4 §§4.1–4.4)

The four-dimensional single-unit measure is Minkowski space-time — arrived at here as the natural home of (1), not as a postulate. The special Lorentz transformation is derived from time-dilation, not from the constancy-of-light-speed axioms; length contraction and the relativistic addition of velocities follow as corollaries (worked examples 4.1–4.3), as do the four-velocity and four-acceleration. The point of method is decisive: everything Einstein obtained from two postulates about light, the synthesis obtains from one geometric fact about measure plus the meaning of $c$.

7. Relativistic dynamics; $E=mc^2$ as a conversion [FORCED] (Ch. 4 §§4.5–4.6)

Four-momentum conservation yields relativistic dynamics; mass–energy equivalence appears in its proper light — $c^2$ is the conversion factor between the mass and energy books, precisely as $c$ converts the distance and time books. $E=mc^2$ asserts no transmutation “at the speed of light”; it states an exchange rate. This is the same move as §2, one register up, and it closes Special Relativity within the two premises. (The corpus completes the thought: energy is turn rate, $E=h\nu$, so the exchange reads $m = h\nu/c^2$ — what a balance weighs is trapped rate; see Mass as Trapped Rate.)

PART III · THE DISCRETE WORLD

8. Quantisation, because information is discrete [FORCED] (Ch. 5 §§5.1–5.2)

Phenomena, qua information, must be analysable into ultimately discrete elements — a continuum, all its parts fused, carries no information (the news-print-fused-into-one-block image). Quantisation is therefore not an empirical surprise to be bolted on but a requirement of a phenomenal physics. The historical origins (Planck’s resolution of the black-body ultraviolet catastrophe; the failure of the classical continuum) are recounted as the physics catching up with a logical necessity.

9. Phota, not photons; causality and four-momentum in the quantum jump [FORCED] (Ch. 5 §§5.3–5.5)

Light-energy is discrete, $E = h\nu$, but the quantum of light is not a ballistic particle crossing space. The authors rename it the photum (plural phota) — a quantum illumination, an immediate inter-atomic transfer of a quantum of action, with no trajectory to be bent or delayed except in the observer’s bookkeeping. Causality at the quantum level is preserved as a correlation, and four-momentum is conserved across the jump. A “ray of light” is then a cinematic succession of such illuminations of intervening particles (dust, moisture) — which is why a beam is seen in a smoky room and not in clean vacuum: what is seen is matter illuminated, never light in transit.

10. The atom as a quantum accumulator; Balmer–Rydberg [FORCED] (Ch. 5 §5.6)

The hydrogen spectrum is reconstructed with the atom treated as an accumulator of quanta at integer levels. A fundamental mass unit $$m^\ast = hR/c \tag{3}$$ ($R$ the Rydberg constant) organises the spectrum. Time-dilation together with the requirement that time is discrete ($t_R/t = n/N$ for integers) fixes each accumulator level — the term energy $$\nu = cR\!\left(1 - \frac{N^2}{n^2}\right) \tag{4a}$$ (Pope, Einstein’s Lost Legacy, Appendix 3; with the ground mode as reference, $N=1$, this is the level ladder $E_n = mc^2\left(1 - 1/n^2\right)$ rising to the ionization limit $mc^2$ as $n\to\infty$; in the corpus's rate form, $\nu_n = \nu_R(1-1/n^2)$ with $cR = \nu_R = 3.290$ PHz, the Rydberg rate — see the Balmer–Rydberg exhibit). The observed spectral lines are differences of two levels — a re-closure from one standing mode to another — $$\nu = cR\!\left(\frac{1}{N^2} - \frac{1}{n^2}\right) \tag{4}$$ the Balmer–Rydberg formula (lower mode $N$, upper $n$: Lyman $N=1$, Balmer $N=2$, Paschen $N=3$…; level and line coincide at $N=1$, which is why that case reproduces observed hydrogen directly) — the electron’s parameters recovered without invoking electrostatic charge, foreshadowing Part V. Discreteness here is structural: the standing accumulation admits only whole numbers of quanta.

10a. “Light is everything” — what the phrase means and what it forces [FORCED] (Pope, Einstein’s Lost Legacy, Ch. 6–7, Figs. 1–2; Pope, “Light is Everything”, Sept. 2007)

Parts I–III are compressed in this corpus into the slogan light is everything. The phrase is not rhetoric; it fuses three claims, each already forced by what precedes it.

(i) Everything known is light. Every datum any observer possesses about any object — its position, mass, motion, its very existence — arrives as quantum transactions in integer units of $h$ (§8). There is no second channel; rulers and clocks are organized ways of receiving these transactions. The world an observer has is, without remainder, projected out of light (§1).

(ii) Everything that exists is light in one of two states. The transaction — the photum of §9: one quantum of action passing between two atoms, proper-time-instantaneous, no traveller — and the bank: the atom of §10, the quantum accumulator, in Pope’s own words “an embodiment, or static form of light-energy.” The Balmer–Rydberg reconstruction makes this literal: $m^\ast = hR/c = 2.425\times10^{-35}\ \mathrm{kg}$ (eq. 3) is the mass-equivalent of the light-quantum — the most an atom can shed or absorb in one interaction, the exchange currency between the two states. Matter is banked light; light is matter transacting. In the vortex language of Part VIII: a closed winding ($\oint d\varphi = 2\pi n$) is an atom; a re-closure between two standing modes is a photum. One substance — closed versus transferred.

(iii) Space and time are dimensions of the light-relations, not a stage light crosses. The observer’s frame is polar: azimuth, elevation, range — and time, which is range divided by $c$ (§2). $c$ converts two readings of the same radial dimension; it is not a speed along anything. Hence the inversion Pope built into the corpus: not light in space — space in the light.

The reflex to unlearn — speed → rate

Trained on Newton, a reader meets the vortex and reaches for one question: how fast is it turning at the rim? — a velocity, $v=\omega r$, metres per second. Resist it. Just as $c$ is not a speed along anything (above), the accumulator has no rim of stuff sweeping through a stage, and a velocity needs exactly such a stage to cross. What the winding has is a rate of angular change: a phase that advances, $L = dS/d\theta$, radians per unit time — never metres per second.

The proof is the accumulator itself. Drive it and it clears transactions at gigahertz; read that as a literal $v=\omega r$ over any real radius and you get a superluminal rim that would tear the winding apart. It does neither, because nothing races around a rim — the phase advances, not a body through space. So drop “how fast does it spin?” and ask the two questions that have referents:

  • the intrinsic circulation rate — a constitutive frequency, fixed by what the winding is (you cannot crank it; forcing more only re-links it into another element); and
  • the phase advance a drive imposes — the working variable, the thing actually accumulated.

Same turning, correct currency: a rate, not a speed. Catch yourself asking for a velocity and you have slipped a stage back under the physics — step back to the rate.

The time-rope carries all three at once (Lost Legacy Ch. 7 §§7.1–7.2, Figs. 1–2). Each strand is one object’s history as delivered by its transactions — the strand is not made of some further stuff; it is the cinematic sequence of quantum registrations. The weave at the pole is the observer’s interactive now: the observer’s whole world is a rope woven of light-strands. The lay of the rope is banked history — what is woven cannot be unwoven: Pope’s inexorability of time falls out of the geometry, with no thermodynamic postulate. And $c$ is not a speed along any strand — it is the rate at which the whole spherical observer-frame contracts temporally toward its pole, the metre-to-second exchange rate of the radius itself. The slogan then cashes out with nothing left over: light = the ledger’s transactions; matter = the ledger’s balances; space = the relational structure of the ledger; time = the rate of writing. 10b. The transaction, and what temperature is [FORCED] (from §§8–10; Pope, Entropy and Negentropy)

Section 10a says the atom is banked light and the photum is light transacting. This section says what a transaction is — and, because the same picture forces it, what temperature is.

An accumulator is a rate that clears, not a store that holds. By Part VIII an atom does not contain angular momentum the way a jar contains coins; it is a turning ($L = dS/d\theta$). Its books therefore run on two separate ledgers. The identity ledger is the closed winding itself — the crossing-count ($\oint d\varphi = 2\pi n$) that makes it this element; changing it means cutting and re-linking a winding, a threshold priced far above any ordinary interaction. The state ledger is everything the winding can do without touching a crossing: its flex, its modes, its re-phasings. Ordinary absorption and emission of $h$ transact on the state ledger alone. That is the entire reason a face flooded with light stays the same face — the flood lands on the state ledger; the identity ledger is never presented the bill.

Three properties of every transaction, each already forced. (i) Balanced. Because the accumulator is a rate and not a reservoir, over any equilibrium interval it pays out as emitter exactly what it takes in as absorber: as many $h$ out as in. (ii) Proper-time instantaneous (§9): the settlement is a single event with no duration — nothing is in flight to age. What looks like light-travel delay is range divided by $c$ (§2), geometry seen from outside the pair, not a duration the transaction undergoes. (iii) Intransitive. A transaction is a phase-lock between exactly two closures; it does not compose. That A settles with B and B with C builds no A-to-C through-line — no relay, no carrier, no field-stuff in the gap. This intransitivity is the content of “no photons” (§9): bulk radiative transfer only looks like a thing crossing a room; it is a chain of independent pairwise settlements, each of which had to resonance-match on its own — which is precisely why transparency exists, a link that cannot clear simply not happening.

One coin, many tempos. Every transaction hands over the same unit — one turn of action, $h$, carrying $\pm\hbar$ of phase sense. What separates a radio transaction from a $\gamma$-ray transaction is not the amount handed over but the tempo: $E = h\nu$ is action times its delivery rate (§8). Each passes one full turn; the faster one merely torques the winding harder. Ultimately everything is time — two transactions differ only in rate.

Temperature is the state ledger’s standing occupation — disordered angular re-phasing. Pope states it plainly in Entropy and Negentropy: “heat is disorder among [the] motions,” and “a concentration of heat is temperature.” Where every motion is angular (Part VIII) this becomes exact and literal: heat is the incoherent phase-churn of a winding’s flex-modes — re-phasings of random sense that sum to no net advance — and temperature is the intensity of that churn, the churn-energy per mode above the winding’s identity floor. This sharpens a distinction the everyday word blurs. The transient excess of intake over output is not temperature; it is heat flow — the books momentarily out of true. A body warms when intake outruns output and re-clears at a higher standing churn; equilibrium at a higher temperature is a busier balance, not a fuller store — a hotter body radiates faster and still balances (Stefan’s law read as bookkeeping). Two bodies reach one temperature when their churn-per-mode matches, which is why heat flows until intensities equalize, never totals.

Absolute zero is the identity floor, not stillness. Strip away all disordered churn and what remains is the bare closed winding — still turning, because by $L = dS/d\theta$ a winding that stopped turning would cease to exist. A cold atom is still banked light (§10a); the zero-point is the identity turning itself, the floor beneath which the state ledger cannot be emptied.

Why heat does not move weight — and what does. Because the churn is disordered, its re-phasings cancel: summed over the modes they advance no net winding, and a state that advances no net winding shifts no natural orbit. Heat is angular momentum churning without going anywhere. The weight lever (§§15–17; the phase-rotation experiment) is the exact complement — impose coherence, a directed phase-advance that does not cancel, and the net advance shifts the natural radius and therefore the weight. Ordering the churn is Pope’s negentropy; it is also information (an ordering of the one substance’s motions); it is also the lever. Heat, weight-change, and information are one axis of the single substance — how coherent its re-phasing is — read three ways. The element rides underneath all of it, on the ledger the transactions never touch.

PART IV · THE ANGULAR MOMENTUM SYNTHESIS

11. The redundancy of the in-vacuo force; motion is naturally orbital [FORCED] (Ch. 6 §§6.1–6.2)

No free body is ever observed to travel in an unbounded straight line; every free trajectory is curved. Newton’s First Law posits rectilinear inertial motion and then requires a second, unseen in-vacuo gravitational force to bend it back — two imaginary opposites (the “centrifugal” and “centripetal” tendencies) that, in any actual orbit, exactly cancel. The synthesis takes the empirical fact at face value: natural free motion is orbital (an Aristotelian intuition vindicated), a manifestation of angular momentum. The two cancelling fictions are dismissed together, “not only as logically redundant but — mainly — as unempirical,” exactly as the ether was dismissed.

12. The postulates and the Principle of Angular Inertia [FORCED] (Ch. 6 §6.3)

Two postulates carry the whole of Part IV:

P1. Natural (force-free) motion is orbital — each body moves under its own angular momentum. P2. Angular momentum is holistically conserved — globally, across the whole correlated system, not merely locally.

From these follows the Principle of Angular Inertia: a body persists in its state of angular momentum unless a real, measurable constraint acts. Holistic conservation is the synthesis’s form of Machian correlation-at-a-distance: everything is angular-momentum-linked to everything, “so that thou canst not stir a flower without troubling of a star.”

13. The two-body problem and Bertrand’s theorem [FORCED] (Ch. 6)

For a body $P$ of small mass orbiting a body $B$, the radial equation of motion takes the form $$\frac{d^2 r}{dt^2} = -\,h(r) + \frac{L^2}{m r^3}, \tag{5}$$ with $L$ the (conserved) orbital angular momentum. Requiring that some natural orbits close invokes Bertrand’s theorem: only $h(r)\propto 1/r^2$ (and the linear law) yields closed, precession-free orbits. The inverse-square law is thereby recovered without a force in the void — it is the shape a closed conserved-angular-momentum orbit must have. Closed solutions of (5) are ellipses: Kepler, from conservation alone.

14. Weight as a constraint force; the variable $G$ [FORCED] (Ch. 6 §6.3)

Here is the hinge of the entire book. A body resting on the ground is not in its natural state; its force-free state is an orbit at a radius $r_{\rm ff}$ set by its angular momentum. The ground holds it off that orbit, and the force required to do so is what we measure as weight. Weight is a real, outward-measurable constraint force — not the pull of a field in the void. Consequently the coefficient in (5) is not the sacrosanct constant $G$ but a variable $\mathcal{G}$ that depends on the body’s angular-momentum state.

The worked consequences are startling and literal: a mass carried eastward (adding to the Earth-frame angular momentum) weighs less; carried westward, more. Anthony names the eastward limit levitation. Example 6.1 computes the natural orbit a laboratory mass “would seek.” And the deepest statement of the chapter: it is angular momentum that keeps bodies apart; were a body’s angular momentum to vanish, it would fall together with its neighbour — “gravitational collapse” being simply the loss of angular momentum, not the action of a force.

PART V · SPIN, CHARGE, MAGNETISM — AND THE CORRECTION

15. Spin in the balance; the directional effect $\mathcal{K}=K_0\pm K_s$ [FORCED] (Ch. 7 §§7.1–7.5)

Total angular momentum — orbital plus intrinsic spin — is what P2 conserves. A spinning orbiting body therefore carries kinetic energy $$\mathcal{K} = K_0 + K_s, \tag{6}$$ and the orbit responds to the spin. The sign is dynamical and we adopt it as canonical (per the decision to follow Anthony over any rival convention):

spin parallel to orbital $L$ ⇒ $\mathcal{K}=K_0+K_s$ ⇒ orbit contracts ⇒ more strongly held ⇒ heavier; spin anti-parallel ⇒ $\mathcal{K}=K_0-K_s$ ⇒ orbit expands ⇒ lighter.

The book works the macroscopic examples: the Gravity-Probe-B rotor (a small predicted orbital shift), and the Hayasaka spinning-disc weight change. In this regime spin is a signed perturbation on a dominant orbital energy.

16. The magnitude effect — the authors’ own corrigendum [FORCED] (Pope, A Critique of Modern Physics, PIRT 2008, notes 3–4)

There is a second effect of spin, which the 2007 text under-stated and the authors themselves later corrected. Beyond the directional $\pm$ there is a sign-independent magnitude effect: spin raises $\mathcal{K}$, hence $\mathcal{G}$, whichever way it points. In their own words the omission made it “seem that the vectorial additions and subtractions were offered as the only ones concerned,” and the 2008 note “may be taken as a correction of that 2007 text.” The magnitude effect is “so minuscule … compared to the magnitude of the orbital angular momentum” for astronomical bodies, yet “extremely large” for the electron. We take the existence of this effect as [FORCED], on the authors’ own authority.

17. The swamping crossover: when and where the sign survives [FORCED regimes; EXT — formalization]

The two effects of §§15–16 do not act equally at all scales; there is a boundary between them, which we name. Define $$\rho \equiv \frac{K_s}{K_0}. \tag{7}$$

  • Direction-sensitive regime, $\rho \ll 1$ (macroscopic spinning bodies). Orbital energy dominates; spin is a signed perturbation. The force-free radius, hence the weight, shifts by $$\frac{\Delta W}{W} \sim \pm\,\rho \qquad (\rho \ll 1), \tag{8}$$ a small, reversible, direction-dependent change.
  • Swamped regime, $\rho \gg 1$ (the atom). Spin dominates; $\mathcal{K}\approx K_s$ for either sign — “the direction of spin has no effect on the parameters of the orbit” (Ch. 7, n. 24). The effect becomes magnitude-only; the sign washes out. This is why atomic binding (“charge”) is sign-symmetric in magnitude.

The crossover is the operative fact of the whole programme. A signed, reversible weight change exists only while $\rho \lesssim 1$; as $\rho\to1$ the signed response saturates, and for $\rho\gg1$ only the magnitude survives. This is “when and where to expect the swamping”: it is not a nuisance but the exact map of where a directional experiment can and cannot succeed. [OPEN] the closed form $\Delta W(\rho)$ across the crossover follows from the $r_{\rm ff}(L)$ relation of §14 with (6); the qualitative content — signed-perturbative below, saturating-then-symmetric above — is fixed.

18. Charge dissolved: binding is spin kinetic energy [FORCED] (Ch. 7 §§7.6)

At the atomic scale $\rho\gg1$, and the enormous intrinsic spin energy of the “electron” is what had been called its charge. Cashing the coulomb out in mechanical units of joules, the spin kinetic energy equals the ionisation energy, $$K_s = h c R_\infty. \tag{9}$$ Treating the atom with this $K_s$ recovers the Bohr/QM parameters with no electrostatic force: $$v_s \approx 2.1876903\times10^{6}\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}, \qquad r_s = \frac{h}{2\pi m v_s} \approx 5.2917756\times10^{-11}\ \mathrm{m}, \tag{10}$$ and Coulomb’s inverse-square law is replaced by the gravitational inverse-square law with a hugely enlarged coefficient $$\mathcal{G} = \frac{r_s v_s^2}{m} \approx 1.514\times10^{29}\ \mathrm{N\,m^2\,kg^{-2}}, \tag{11}$$ the enlargement being nothing but the relatively enormous spin kinetic energy. (Reconciliation note: two independent primary sources — the 1995 Physics Essays paper, which gives $G_C = 1.52\times10^{29}$, and the 2008 corrigendum, which gives $1.5141713\times10^{29}$ — together with a direct evaluation of (11) at $m=m_p$, all yield $10^{29}$. The $1.514\times10^{28}$ appearing in the OCR transcription of the 2007 book is therefore a transcription error, not a physical claim; we use $10^{29}$.)

19. Attraction, repulsion, and magnetism as angular momentum [FORCED] (Ch. 7 §§7.7–7.8)

“Like charges repel, unlike attract” is re-read entirely in angular-momentum terms. Assigning each of the proton- and electron-masses an intrinsic spin of magnitude $h/4\pi$, a ground-state total of $h/2\pi$ requires the two spin vectors to add (parallel) — an effective mutual “repulsion” that keeps the atom from total collapse — while the electron’s spin adds to its orbital momentum to give the net “attraction.” Anti-parallel spins would sum to zero total angular momentum and the atom would collapse. Thus electrostatic attraction/repulsion is not a contest of static charges but “the natural, automatic balance of particles in an overall conserved angular-momentum system.” Magnetism follows identically: an aligned population of electron-spins in iron “attracts” or “repels” another according to spin-vector orientation — no in-vacuo magnetic force is required. Gravity, electrostatics, and magnetism are thereby unified as one holistic angular-momentum nexus, and all in-vacuo fields are “dispensed with altogether.”

20. The polarization correction: extended bodies are not single accumulators [EXT — the one substantive departure, with reason]

That the magnitude effect exists is the authors’ own correction (§16). What the synthesis has not carried through is its size for a real, extended body, and this is our single departure from the text.

In the numerical treatment (Ch. 7 §§7.4–7.5, and the ospope-proof note, which sizes the Hayasaka 175 g / 18 000 rpm disc) the spin energy is taken as the bulk rigid-body value, $$K_s^{\rm bulk} = \tfrac12 I \omega^2, \qquad I = \tfrac25 m r^2\ (\text{sphere}),\ \tfrac12 m r^2\ (\text{disc}), \tag{12}$$ at the macroscopic $\omega$. The body is treated as one accumulator. On that basis the predicted weight change is minute — of order $0.01\ \mathrm{mg}$ for the 175 g / 18 000 rpm disc — and far below the reported anomaly; this was the ground on which the Bureau International des Poids et Mesures (Quinn & Picard) declared the Hayasaka result spurious.

But a real ball, disc, or gyroscope is $\sim\!10^{22}$–$10^{24}$ constituent accumulators, each with intrinsic spin, ordinarily randomized to a vanishing vector sum — contributing nothing to (12). Mechanical rotation does not leave them randomized. By the Barnett effect (rotation → magnetization; the mechanical dual of Einstein–de Haas), rotation coherently aligns a fraction $f$ of the internal spins along $\omega$. Each aligned internal accumulator carries spin energy on the atomic scale $K_s^{\rm atom}\sim hcR_\infty$ of (9) — enormous per atom beside the bulk rotational share. Hence $$K_s^{\rm eff} \;\approx\; \underbrace{\tfrac12 I\omega^2}_{\text{bulk}} \;+\; \underbrace{f\,N\,K_s^{\rm atom}}_{\text{polarized — dominant}}. \tag{13}$$

Three consequences: 1. The rigid-body estimate undercounts the spin content, and with it the excursion of $\mathcal{G}$, by many orders. Its “far too small” verdict is an artifact of the single-accumulator assumption, not a fact about nature. 2. Restored, the polarized internal spin recovers the larger observed effect: on this reading the anomalous-weight measurements are, in direction and rough magnitude, plausibly correct, and the Quinn–Picard dismissal loses its basis. 3. The coefficient that has long stood as an undetermined coupling is exactly the polarization fraction $f$ (times $K_s^{\rm atom}/K_0$) — a computable efficiency, not a mystery. [OPEN] compute $f$ for a given material and drive.

This closes the loop with §17. Polarizing internal spin is precisely what drives $\rho=K_s/K_0$ upward — toward and past unity. Polarization is therefore the lever that carries a body toward the swamping crossover. If the sign-blind (swamping) contribution keeps rising with polarization while the directional term saturates, there is an optimal fraction $f^\ast$ near $\rho\approx O(1)$ — the point of maximal signed, still-reversible effect, beyond which the sign is lost to swamping (§17); whether such an optimum exists, and where, is set by the empirical swamping response, not fixed by the present axioms. The interplay of (8) and (13) is, we propose, the general principle behind every spinning-mass weight anomaly — stated here without reference to any particular apparatus.

PART VI · GRAVITATION AND SPACE-TIME

21. Orbital time-dilation; the circular geodesic and the factor 3 [FORCED] (Ch. 8 §§8.1–8.4)

Extending the Pythagorean time-dilation (1) from ideal rectilinear motion to real curvilinear motion is the whole of General Relativity in this scheme. Requiring that a natural circular orbit be a geodesic of the resulting metric fixes a single exponent ($a=2$) and yields the circular-orbit time-dilation carrying the celebrated factor of 3, $$\frac{d\tau}{dt}\Big|_{\rm circ} = \left(1 - \frac{3\mathcal{M}}{r}\right)^{1/2}, \qquad \mathcal{M}\equiv \frac{GM}{c^2}. \tag{14}$$

22. The Schwarzschild metric with no field equations [FORCED] (Ch. 8 §§8.5–8.6)

Demanding that the non-circular geodesics reduce to the Newtonian ellipse equation fixes the remaining metric function, $B(r)=(1-2\mathcal{M}/r)^{-1}$, giving the full Schwarzschild line element $$c^2 d\tau^2 = c^2\!\left(1-\tfrac{2\mathcal{M}}{r}\right)dt^2 - \left(1-\tfrac{2\mathcal{M}}{r}\right)^{-1}dr^2 - r^2 d\theta^2, \tag{15}$$ obtained from time-dilation plus the geodesic postulate alone — no Einstein field equations. A structural consequence, carried forward crucially into cosmology: because (15) descends from the circular-orbit time-dilation (14), it is warranted only for $$r > 3\mathcal{M}. \tag{16}$$ Radial free-fall recovers $d^2r/d\tau^2 = -GM/r^2$; circular motion recovers Kepler’s third law, $T = 2\pi (r^3/GM)^{1/2}$.

23. The perihelion advance [FORCED] (Ch. 8 §6.3)

The geodesic orbit equation acquires a small extra term, $2\mathcal{M}u^3$ ($u=1/r$), beyond the Newtonian $$\left(\frac{du}{d\theta}\right)^2 + u^2 = k + \frac{2\mathcal{M}c^2 u}{\beta^2} + 2\mathcal{M}u^3, \tag{17}$$ and the standard (Møller) reduction gives a per-orbit perihelion advance $$\Delta\varpi = \frac{3MG\pi}{c^2}\!\left(\frac{1}{r_1}+\frac{1}{r_2}\right), \tag{18}$$ which for Mercury is 43.03″/century against the observed 43.11 ± 0.45″ — the classic test, with no field equations behind it.

24. “Light-bending” as refraction, and GPS [FORCED] (Ch. 8 §§8.7–8.8)

The same geodesic machinery, taken in the limit of a near-$c$ material ray, gives a deflection $$\delta \approx \frac{4MG}{r_0 c^2} \approx 1.75'' \tag{19}$$ for grazing the Sun. But the synthesis refuses the picture of a bent photon: there is no ballistic photon to bend. The 1.75″ is the refraction of a line of sight through the Sun’s extended, dense corona/heliopause — an oar-bent-in-water effect — slowed for the same reason clocks are slowed near mass. (Historically: the Newtonian and 1911-Einstein predictions were half this; the 1919 Eddington eclipse result is noted as genuinely disputed.) The GPS time-dilation corrections are the everyday, high-precision confirmation of (14)–(15).

PART VII · COSMOLOGY WITHOUT MYSTIFICATION

25. No black holes [FORCED] (Ch. 9 §9.1)

In GR the metric (15) is a field-equation solution with no lower bound on $r$ but $r>0$, so a collapsing star may cross $r=2\mathcal{M}$ (the event horizon) and proceed to an $r=0$ singularity; Birkhoff, Kerr, wormholes, and the Penrose–Hawking singularity theorems follow. In the synthesis (15) is a time-dilation formula for freely moving matter, valid only for $r>3\mathcal{M}$ (16). There is therefore no event horizon and no singularity — the model simply does not reach them. A “dark” companion such as Cygnus X-1 is re-read as an ordinary cold, spent stellar remnant (white dwarf / neutron star) or, more generally, as the empty barycentre of a swirling system — the “eye” of a galactic vortex, dark because it radiates nothing, not because it traps light. Matter spiralling inward as it sheds angular momentum (radiated as light) approaches this void, not a cosmic plughole.

26. The Hubble redshift as time-dilation, not recession [FORCED] (Ch. 9 §§9.2–9.4)

Hubble’s law is an observed proportionality between redshift $z$ and distance; that recession causes it is an inference, not an observation (Hubble himself preferred the neutral “law of redshifts”). The synthesis supplies a non-recession reading consistent with holistic angular-momentum conservation: distant matter has larger random speeds relative to us (being “nearer” the ubiquitous accumulation of cosmic mass), and time-dilation — which, unlike Doppler, applies to all motion and shifts spectra uniformly without dispersion — reddens their light in proportion to distance: $$z = \gamma(v) - 1 \approx \frac{v^2}{2c^2}, \qquad v \propto r. \tag{20}$$ The estimate places the cosmic mass-density squarely within textbook bounds, and accommodates the observed near-fractal (dimension ≈ 2) galaxy distribution (Roscoe).

27. The Friedmann equation from angular momentum; the Big Bang refused [FORCED] (Ch. 9 §3)

Applying conservation to $n$ mutually orbiting galaxies (an $n$-body problem approximated as $n$ two-body systems) yields the Friedmann equation $$\left(\frac{dR}{dt}\right)^2 = \frac{C}{R} + \frac{E}{K_T}, \tag{21}$$ without General Relativity. But (21) is warranted only in the present epoch; extrapolating it to an initial singularity is exactly the un-empirical over-reach the synthesis refuses — the Big Bang is “out of sight where it can never be challenged by direct appeal to observation” (Hoyle). Alternatives (steady-state; non-Big-Bang accounts of the microwave background) are noted as live. Olbers’ paradox is answered in passing: in an unbounded, matter-filled cosmos every light-quantum is absorbed by matter as much as emitted (a void absorbs no angular momentum), so the sky is not infinitely bright.

28. Dark matter is dark spin; the variable $G$ [FORCED] (Ch. 9 §5)

Flat galactic rotation curves (Zwicky, Rubin, Oort, Giovanelli), which “should” fly the galaxies apart, are exactly what the synthesis expects: densely packed stars orbit in sub-systems, rigid-body-like near the centre. What is “missing” from the Newtonian account is not exotic dark matter (WIMPs, sought fruitlessly in the deep Boulby mine) or dark energy, but neglected spin angular momentum — “dark” only because it was left out — which makes $G$ variable. This variable-$G$ result is reached here from first principles; Osborne’s 1980 doctoral thesis already held $G$ non-constant. The whole “96 % missing universe” is an artifact of omitting spin from the angular-momentum balance.

PART VIII · THE SINGLE-VORTEX FORM [EXT]

The book is two-body throughout: a mass $P$ orbiting a mass $B$, carrying orbital $L$ and intrinsic $S$. Normal Realism has since moved to a single-substance picture — the elementary object is one closed vortex of the time-substance, and there is no orbiting pair. We show the two-body results survive intact and that §20’s correction becomes native.

29. Two angular rates of one object [EXT]

A single closed vortex has two independent angular coordinates: the azimuth $\varphi$ around the loop, and the internal framing angle $\psi$ of the vortex core. Whole-turn advance of the phase around the loop is the standing-wave (de Broglie) condition $$\oint d\varphi = 2\pi n \ \Rightarrow\ L_{\rm orb} = n\hbar, \tag{22}$$ the winding — our orbital angular momentum. Rotation of the core’s framing as one traverses the loop is the twist — our spin. These are not two substances layered together — we do not reintroduce the retired “inner/outer quanta” — but two rates of the same filament, and both are literally rates of the one substance $S$ against an angle, in keeping with the Normal-Realist identity $L = dS/d\theta$: $$L_{\rm orb} = \frac{dS}{d\varphi}, \qquad L_{\rm spin} = \frac{dS}{d\psi}. \tag{23}$$ “Everything is time” is thus preserved exactly: both angular momenta are rates of change of the substance against an angle (a phase, a time).

30. Conservation is topological: $\mathrm{Lk}=\mathrm{Tw}+\mathrm{Wr}$ [EXT]

For a closed ribbon (a vortex carrying a framing) the Călugăreanu–White–Fuller theorem states $$\mathrm{Lk} = \mathrm{Tw} + \mathrm{Wr}, \tag{24}$$ with the linking number $\mathrm{Lk}$ an integer that cannot change under any smooth deformation. Identifying total angular momentum with $\mathrm{Lk}$, orbital with the writhe/winding, and spin with the twist, holistic conservation of angular momentum (P2) becomes conservation of total linking — a genuine topological invariant rather than a postulate. Anthony’s $\mathcal{K}=K_0\pm K_s$ is recovered directly: twist co-oriented with the winding adds to the linking (heavier); anti-oriented subtracts (lighter). That sign is just the linking sign — whether the twist runs with or against the winding — and needs no independent derivation; the separate sign convention we had carried is retired in favour of Anthony’s statement.

31. Spin-½, and the “2” in $\alpha$ [EXT]

A ribbon carrying a half-twist ($\mathrm{Tw}=\tfrac12$) must be traversed twice — through $720^\circ$ — before its framing returns: the spinor property, obtained with no separate assumption. This same factor “2” is the one appearing in the published fine-structure result $\alpha=\sqrt{2/N}$ (the planar $2\pi$ against the solid-angle $4\pi$). Spin-½ therefore stops being posited and becomes the minimal non-zero twist of a single vortex; and the sign of the twist (right- vs left-handed half-twist) is precisely the binary phase-sense the book already identifies with the sign of “charge” (§18). Charge, in the vortex, is not a substance but the handedness of a twist.

32. Polarization, phase advance, and swamping in vortex language [EXT]

Each atom is a micro-vortex with its own twist. Randomized, the twists point every way and the net twist is nil — precisely Anthony’s “no internal contribution” (§20), now seen as the $n{=}1$ rigid limit of a system with a whole tower of internal windings available. Alignment (Barnett) co-orients the twists so their linking numbers add, producing a macroscopic net twist — the polarization of §20 (in extant terms, dynamic nuclear polarization; Alzofon’s 1981 Dynamic Nuclear Orientation is the same mechanism). Phase advance pumps the winding ($\oint d\varphi$, i.e. adds writhe): the coherent phase advance is the accumulators’ spin correlation, as against their polarization (alignment) — the general form of driving angular momentum into a body. And swamping is spin-lock: when the aggregate twist dominates the winding, the sign of the total linking is fixed by the twist, and further winding can no longer flip the Earth-frame sense (§17). The three phenomena of §§17 and 20 are one statement about $\mathrm{Tw}$ versus $\mathrm{Wr}$.

PART IX · RÉSUMÉ AND SCOPE

33. The conceptual catharsis [FORCED] (Ch. 10)

The book closes as a clearing of language (Locke’s “under-labourer”): the mystifications of modern physics — the ether, the ballistic photon, the in-vacuo force, the black hole, galactic recession, dark matter — are relics of taking manners of speaking for descriptions of nature. Two economies do the work: $c$ as a conversion factor, and holistic angular-momentum conservation. A profound dividend is the healing of the relativity/quantum schism: in the synthesis all free motion is angular momentum in ultimate units of $\hbar$, so it is automatically quantised, and the spin Uhlenbeck and Goudsmit had to add to the electron is, from the start, an integral part of the one conserved nexus. Russell’s 1946 hope for a “radical departure … even more than relativity demanded” is offered, without immodesty, as possibly answered.

34. Scope — what is forced, what is ours, what is open

[FORCED] — Everything in Parts I–VII, and both regimes of §17, is the book (or the authors’ own 2008 corrigendum), re-expressed. We have found no reason in the text to depart from it.
