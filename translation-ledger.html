2 · Whole turn, radian, action, and rate

Identity
$$h=2\pi\hbar.$$

$h$ is action per completed whole turn; $\hbar$ is the same action read per radian.

Identity
$$\Delta S=h\,\Delta n,\qquad \nu\equiv\frac{dn}{dt},\qquad \omega\equiv\frac{d\theta}{dt}=2\pi\nu.$$

$S$ is accumulated action, $n$ is completed-turn count, $\nu$ is turns per unit time, and $\omega$ is radians per unit time. The derivatives are continuum readouts of an integer staircase.

Identity inside the declared rate account
$$E\equiv\frac{dS}{dt}=\frac{dS}{d\theta}\frac{d\theta}{dt}=L\omega, \qquad L=\hbar\ \Longrightarrow\ E=\hbar\omega=h\nu.$$

$E$ is native action-per-time pricing, $L$ is the angular-momentum entry, and the last step is for one-quantum closure. This does not by itself prove that every laboratory energy or momentum readout is already derived; §3 states the measurement bridge.

Prohibited inferenceSame phase is not same history. A record may return to the same angle modulo $2\pi$ while its integer completion count has increased. Accumulated phase, completed-turn history, and a measured rate calibration must not be collapsed into one datum.

5 · Circular atomic calibration—and the local no-go

Declare the model before reading its consequences: a two-ended circular account with reduced mass $\mu=m_1m_2/(m_1+m_2)$, orbital action $L=\mu vr=\hbar$, radius $r$, relative speed $v$, and angular rate $\omega=v/r$.

Model-conditional definitions
$$x\equiv\left(\frac vc\right)^2,\qquad \omega_0\equiv\frac{\mu c^2}{\hbar},\qquad \ell_\mu\equiv\frac{\hbar}{\mu c}.$$

$x$ is the squared speed share, $\omega_0$ the reduced-mass angular-rate scale, and $\ell_\mu$ the reduced Compton length.

Identity within the circular model
$$\boxed{x=\frac{\omega}{\omega_0}=\left(\frac{\ell_\mu}{r}\right)^2}.$$

Action closure fixes the linked ratios once one member of the scale family is supplied.

Measured calibration
$$\left.\frac vc\right|_{\rm hydrogen}=\alpha \quad\Longrightarrow\quad x=\alpha^2.$$

The empirical hydrogen calibration supplies $\alpha$. This identifies the circular state; it does not derive the numerical value of $\alpha$.

Model-conditional binding
$$|E_B|=\frac12\mu v^2 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{|E_B|}{\mu c^2}=\frac{x}{2}=\frac{\alpha^2}{2}.$$

$E_B$ is the circular-model binding energy. The dynamical rate ratio is $x$; the binding fraction is $x/2$. They are related but not identical entries.

Circular-model corollaries
$$\frac{r}{\ell_\mu}=x^{-1/2}=\alpha^{-1},\qquad \frac{T}{T_0}=x^{-1}=\alpha^{-2},$$ $$T\equiv\frac{2\pi}{\omega},\qquad T_0\equiv\frac{2\pi}{\omega_0}=\frac{h}{\mu c^2}.$$

The radius and period hierarchies follow after the measured calibration. $T$ is the circular period and $T_0$ the reduced-mass reference period.

Local scale-symmetry no-go
$$v\mapsto\lambda v,\qquad r\mapsto\frac r\lambda,$$ $$\mu vr=\hbar\qquad\text{unchanged}.$$

For nonzero scale factor $\lambda$, action closure fixes the product $vr$, not $v/c$ or $r$ separately. Therefore $L=\mu vr=\hbar$ cannot by itself select $\alpha$. An independent law or measured calibration is required.

The earned result is local and conditional: the tested circular equations calculate linked consequences once a speed share is supplied; they do not choose that share.
