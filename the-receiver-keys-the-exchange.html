1 · Half a ledger line

From Marconi to 5G the grammar has never changed: a transmitter “emits”, the emission “propagates”, a receiver “detects” what arrives. One party acts; the other party waits. Every textbook diagram points the arrow one way.

Normal Realism reads the same engineering the other way up. A quantum hand-off is a transaction: two-ended, co-signed, posted at both seats or posted at neither. No entry completes inside the transmitter alone — the “emission” that no sink takes up is not a faint signal lost in space but a transaction that never happened. The transmit-only grammar was always half a ledger line, and the engineering has quietly paid for the other half all along: the load impedance that “pulls” power the source cannot otherwise deliver, the matched termination without which the link budget is fiction, the antenna whose input impedance depends on what faces it. The receiver was a co-signer dressed as a bystander.

If the exchange is two-ended, a question follows that the one-way grammar cannot even ask: who holds the pen? Nothing in the physics elects the sender. Either end of a linkage can gate whether the transaction completes — and a gate is an alphabet.

2 · The stepping stone the art already owns

The extant record contains the seed and has spent seventy-seven years reading it as a trick. In 1948 Harry Stockman published “Communication by Means of Reflected Power” extant record: Proc. IRE 36, 1196 (1948): a station that transmits nothing of its own, signalling instead by altering how it reflects an interrogating carrier. That seed became “modulated backscatter” — the RFID tag and the contactless card extant record: ISO/IEC 14443, 15693 load modulation: the tag switches its own load impedance, and the reader reads the tag's symbols in the reader's own reflected carrier.

The art files this under clever impedance engineering at the interrogator. Read natively, it is the ontology showing through: the receiving end is keying the exchange, and the “transmitter” is functioning as the meter. What the extant reading hides is the generalization. Backscatter is broadband, promiscuous, and leaky — any reader can interrogate any tag, and the carrier sprays regardless. Make the linkage resonant and sink-exclusive, and the same inversion becomes a channel with properties the one-way grammar cannot supply at any power.

3 · The inversion, stated

Take an established resonant linkage — coupled cavities, a guided pair, a matched near-field couple (“critical coupling”, “matched load”). On such a linkage, delivery is sink-gated: no completed linkage, no transfer. The sink's readiness — presented, withdrawn, or detuned — is therefore not a passive condition of reception. It is a symbol stream.

Receiver-keyed signalling. The sink encodes by modulating its own readiness. The source transmits no message-bearing waveform at all: it reads the symbols in its own delivery telemetry — delivered power, reflected power, the lock observable. The channel's control resides at the receiving end; the sending end is the instrument.

4 · What silence means

A transmit-keyed station is loud by construction: its standby is a carrier, its message is a modulation, and both are in the air for anyone. A receiver-keyed linkage is silent by physics, not by engineering discipline. With no completed linkage the source has no transaction to post — emission-free standby is not a low-power mode but the absence of anything to intercept. The defense art has a sober name for the goal it approaches asymptotically with spread spectrum and power management: “low probability of intercept”. Here it is not approached; it is the ground state. And because completion requires the matched, resonant counterpart, an unauthorized load does not eavesdrop weakly — it completes nothing: security by linkage rather than by key exchange over an open channel.

5 · The guardrail — what this is not

Nothing travels between the seats, so nothing outruns anything. The linkage transacts as a whole; keying acts at the coupled pair's own response time — the loading dynamics of the resonators, their bandwidth — and no enable signal is transmitted from either end because none is needed and none exists. This page claims no retrograde signalling and no faster-than-light channel: the sibling exhibit already reported, at strength and with the record's own limits, why retrograde read-schemes achieve nothing (POAMS & Telecommunications, §4). The well-posed quantities are rates and boundary conditions, not a message racing a wave. The claim here is an inversion of control, not a violation of order.

Scope, stated exactly: the native domain of this page is the established resonant linkage — guided and near-field coupled systems, where sink-gating is bench physics. Whether and how the inversion extends to far-field links is an open engineering question and is stated as such.

6 · The bench that would kill it

Three gates, all cheap. (i) Fidelity: the source's delivery telemetry must reproduce the sink's readiness stream symbol-for-symbol, with no forward enable channel present — verified by severing every path but the linkage. (ii) Exclusivity: detune the sink by a stated number of linewidths — delivery and telemetry must read zero; a broadband surrogate load must read nothing at any offset. (iii) Silence: with the linkage open, the source's emission must sit at the instrument floor across the band. A failure at any gate is reported as a failure; the native reading earns its keep on the bench or not at all.
