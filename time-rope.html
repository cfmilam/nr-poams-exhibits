Space in the light — Pope’s Figs. 1–2 from Einstein’s Lost Legacy, Ch. 7, made live

The observer’s frame is polar: azimuth, elevation, range — and time, which is range divided by c. Nothing travels. Every datum arrives as a quantum registration — one unit of h — and c is the rate at which the whole spherical frame contracts temporally toward its pole, where the radial strands (the world-lines of objects) are, in Pope’s words, “so to speak, woven into a rope.” The upper region below is the observer’s frame seen in plan (elevation suppressed); the lower region is the rope: the observer’s banked history, one coloured strand per object, one bead per quantum registration.

What “light is everything” commits us to

1 · Everything known is light

Every datum any observer has about any object — position, mass, motion, existence — arrives as quantum transactions in integer units of h. There is no second channel. Rulers and clocks are organized ways of receiving these transactions. The world an observer possesses is, without remainder, projected out of light.

2 · Everything that exists is light in one of two states

The transaction: the photum — one quantum of action passing between two atoms, proper-time instantaneous, no traveller. And the bank: the atom, a quantum accumulator — in Pope’s words “an embodiment, or static form of light-energy.” The Balmer–Rydberg derivation makes it literal: m* = hR/c = 2.425×10−35 kg is the exchange currency — the most an atom can shed or absorb per interaction. Matter is banked light; light is matter transacting.

3 · Space-time is of the light, not a stage for it

The frame is polar: azimuth, elevation, range — and time, which is range divided by c. c converts two readings of the same radial dimension; it is not a speed along anything. That is the inversion in Pope’s title: not light in space — space in the light. (Toggle the range rings above between metres and seconds: nothing in the frame changes but the label.)

The inexorability of time

Press “try to unweave” above. It refuses — and that refusal is the physics. Each strand of the rope is the history of some object as viewed by the observer at the interactive now of the frame (the pole). What is woven cannot be unwoven:

“Whatever has happened cannot be caused to unhappen. The inexorable nature of time is represented by the ‘time-rope’ in Figs. 1 and 2 … where each strand of the rope is the history of some object as viewed by the observer at the interactive now of the frame.”
— Einstein’s Lost Legacy, Ch. 7 §7.2

Irreversibility here is built into the geometry of observation itself — no thermodynamic postulate, no statistical arrow bolted on afterwards. History is banked, strand by strand, registration by registration; the free ends out in the frame are the only part still in play.
