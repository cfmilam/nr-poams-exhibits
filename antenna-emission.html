The Problem

Why antennas feel like black magic

Feed alternating current into a bare metal rod and — somehow — a room away, another bare rod stirs. Nothing visible connects them. The textbook account, faithfully applied, works beautifully and explains nothing you can picture:

The conventional picture

Charge shakes a void

An oscillating current — charge accelerating up and down the rod — sets up a time-varying electric field. A changing electric field makes a magnetic field; a changing magnetic field remakes the electric one. This self-chasing pair detaches from the antenna and propagates through empty space at $c$, carrying energy to the horizon.

The mysteries left on the table: What is waving, and in what? How does a wire "let go" of a field into nothing? Why does the near field cling but the far field leave? Where does radiation resistance — a real load the transmitter must drive — come from, if the rod pushes against vacuum?

The POAMS picture

Accumulators keep a ledger

There is one substance — angular momentum — and it is conserved. The conductor is a lattice of tiny AM accumulators (its electrons, each a vortex whose intrinsic spin-sense we call "charge"). Driving the antenna does not shove charge through emptiness; it advances and reverses their collective phase, banking and unbanking angular momentum.

What "radiates" is not a thing thrown into a void. It is the ledger balancing: AM handed off from accumulators here to accumulators elsewhere. The far field is simply the portion whose books can only close by being taken up somewhere else. Nothing crosses a gap that isn't already the one substance.

The apparent outward "speed" is the exchange rate $c$ between the space-measure and the time-measure of that ledger, not a rim of anything crossing a stage.

The Reframe

Radio emission, rebuilt from the substance

Refuse the stage

No charge-thing, no field-in-void

"Charge" is not a substance the rod contains. It is the sign of intrinsic spin — the sense in which a vortex winds, $\oint d\varphi = \pm 2\pi n$. Coulombs are cashed out as the kinetic energy of that spin, in joules. So a conductor is not a pipe full of charge; it is a dense population of angular-momentum accumulators, each keeping its own books.

2
What "current" really is

A coordinated advance of phase

An electric current is not marbles rolling down a tube. It is the accumulators' phase advancing together — the same mechanism POAMS uses to drive spin coherently in the YIG experiment. Alternating current at radio frequency is that phase advanced and reversed, cyclically: the antenna's ledger is written forward, erased, written backward, millions to billions of times a second. Ask for the phase rate, not a drift speed.

3
The elementary event

Emission is a hand-off — a photum

The irreducible act is one quantum $h$ lost by one accumulator and gained by another — Pope's photum, not a "photon" particle in flight. Intrinsically, in its own proper time, that transfer is instantaneous; there is no little packet crossing the room with energy to spare for the trip. A "radio wave" is the statistical envelope of astronomically many such hand-offs, and only in that observer-time statistics does the transfer appear time-delayed.

4
What $c$ is

An exchange rate, not a speed

$c$ is a dimensional conversion factor relating the unit of observed distance to the unit of observed time — not the velocity of a thing crossing space. The "wave travelling at $c$" is the ledger's space-measure and time-measure locked by that exchange rate. This is why the far-field delay $t = r/c$ is exact and why nothing needs to physically fly the distance: the delay is the accounting, not a journey.

5
Near vs far

Bound books vs balanced-elsewhere books

The near field is AM still bound to the rod's own accumulators — banked, reactive, returned each cycle; energy that never left the local ledger. The far field is the fraction whose books cannot close locally and only balance when another accumulator, anywhere, takes the hand-off. "Detachment" is not a field ripping free of a wire; it is the point beyond which conservation can only be satisfied non-locally.

The ledger must close on the structure

A resonant antenna is $\ell \approx \lambda/2$ for the same reason a spectral line is where it is: the phase-winding has to close its books on the structure — a standing pattern of angular momentum, an integer accounting. "Impedance matching" is nothing more exotic than ledger matching: letting the drive's books and the accumulators' books balance so hand-offs proceed without reflection.

The load from nowhere

Radiation resistance is the bill

The transmitter feels a genuine load — radiation resistance — even into "empty" space. In POAMS this is no puzzle: the accumulators keep handing angular momentum outward and not returning it, so the drive must keep resupplying it. Reciprocity, because a ledger runs both ways

An antenna receives with exactly the pattern it transmits. Of course: banking and unbanking are one transaction run forward or backward. Polarization is simply the twist-sense of the transacted angular momentum — the same phase-sign that stood in for "charge" in step 1, now carried by the hand-off itself.

The Crucial Inconsistency · why this is more than a restatement

The force Maxwell's field cannot hold

It would be too easy to say POAMS merely re-narrates Maxwell. The corpus presses a sharper, older charge — and it lands with special force on an antenna, because an antenna is nothing but a driven conductor, and the driven conductor is exactly where the field theory is caught short.

Maxwell's wave has no length

In the electrodynamics of Faraday and Maxwell the radiated wave is purely transverse: the electric and magnetic vectors sit at right angles to each other and to the line of travel. There is no longitudinal vector to the wave — nothing directed along the line joining emitter and absorber. Yet that line is precisely where the real connection lives. Maxwell kept the sideways “bumpety-bump” and discarded the end-to-end link — then had it propagate through a vacuum in which, by definition, there is nothing to wave.

…but the laboratory keeps producing longitudinal forces

Ampère's original force law between current elements carried a longitudinal term — a push-and-pull along the wire. The later Grassmann–Biot–Savart–Lorentz form that Maxwell's theory inherited drops it. For the net force on a separate circuit the two agree, which is why the omission hid for a century; but within a conductor they disagree — and there the experiments side with Ampère:

  • Exploding wires rupture in tension, snapping into segments as if pulled apart lengthwise — an Ampère longitudinal stress the Lorentz force does not predict.
  • Rail and cannon recoil shows longitudinal reaction on the fixed conductor that the field-momentum account struggles to book.
  • Graneau's water-arc experiments jet water explosively along the current path — longitudinal force made visible.

“The manifest existence of longitudinal electrodynamic forces which have no representation in modern textbooks is cause enough for a thorough re-evaluation of the subject.” — Neal Graneau

Neither the Lorentz force nor the field-energy concept, the corpus argues, is fully consistent with these simple laboratory results.

The tell, and the resolution. Maxwell's equations are also not invariant under a plain Galilean change of frame — the very defect that pushed Einstein into Special Relativity. POAMS reads that non-invariance not as a summons to reshape space and time, but as the signature of a wrong ontology: a field-in-a-void that never was there. The repair is to return to the direct-action, non-field electrodynamics of Ampère, Neumann, Weber and Helmholtz — Galilean-invariant, instantaneous, free of the self-energy infinities that plague field theory, and later preferred in the direct-action work of Feynman and Wheeler.

Why this completes the antenna picture

The longitudinal connection the field theory threw away is exactly the hand-off of steps 3–7: the instantaneous, end-to-end transfer of angular momentum from emitter-accumulator to absorber-accumulator, directed along the line between them, in the proper time of the transaction. The finite-$c$ transverse wave is the local, observer-time cinematic sequence of quantum stills; the instantaneous longitudinal link is the non-local reality that sequence is of. Maxwell described the film; the longitudinal hand-off is the projector. An antenna needs both — and only one of them is in the textbook.
