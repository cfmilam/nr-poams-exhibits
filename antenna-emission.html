The Problem

Why antennas feel like black magic

Feed alternating current into a bare metal rod and — somehow — a room away, another bare rod stirs. Nothing visible connects them. The textbook account, faithfully applied, works beautifully and explains nothing you can picture:

The conventional picture

Charge shakes a void

An oscillating current — charge accelerating up and down the rod — sets up a time-varying electric field. A changing electric field makes a magnetic field; a changing magnetic field remakes the electric one. This self-chasing pair detaches from the antenna and propagates through empty space at $c$, carrying energy to the horizon.

The mysteries left on the table: What is waving, and in what? How does a wire "let go" of a field into nothing? Why does the near field cling but the far field leave? Where does radiation resistance — a real load the transmitter must drive — come from, if the rod pushes against vacuum?

The POAMS picture

Accumulators keep a ledger

There is one substance — angular momentum — and it is conserved. In the POAMS identification used here, the conductor is a lattice of AM accumulators whose intrinsic phase-sense is called “charge.” Driving the antenna does not shove a second substance through emptiness; it advances and reverses their collective phase, banking and unbanking angular momentum.

What "radiates" is not a thing thrown into a void. It is the ledger balancing: AM handed off from accumulators here to accumulators elsewhere. The far field is simply the portion whose books can only close by being taken up somewhere else. Nothing crosses a gap that isn't already the one substance.

The elementary event

Emission is a hand-off — a photum

The POAMS transaction premise is one completed full-turn action, $h=2\pi\hbar$, debited at one accumulator and credited at another. Its intrinsic transaction-time is zero in the POAMS ledger while observer records retain the distance-time $r/c$. This premise is stated here; the schematic does not derive it.

What $c$ is

An exchange rate, not a speed

POAMS reads $c$ as the dimensional conversion factor relating observed distance and observed time. Thus the observer ledger keeps the measured relation $t=r/c$ while the native transaction contains no third object in transit. This is an ontological premise of the page, not a conclusion obtained merely by writing the units of $c$.

Bound books vs balanced-elsewhere books

POAMS interprets the engineering near/far regimes as two bookkeeping limits rather than a sharp physical boundary: entries that return within the local cycle, and entries that close only with another accumulator. The page does not derive the distance at which one contribution dominates; that boundary remains antenna-size-, frequency-, and geometry-dependent.

The load from nowhere

Radiation resistance is the bill

The drive records a real power bill conventionally summarized as radiation resistance. In the page's event ledger, if closures occur at rate $\Gamma$ and each closes an energy entry $h\nu$, then $P=\Gamma h\nu$. This is dimensionally power. The full-turn action $h$ is the per-closure angular-momentum book; $dL/dt$ alone is not power.

Symmetry & polarization

Reciprocity, because a ledger runs both ways

For a linear reciprocal antenna under the same terminal, frequency, direction, and polarization conditions, transmit and receive patterns are paired. POAMS reads that reciprocity as one ledger run in opposite roles. Historical research lead · not an adjudicated result

The longitudinal question

POAMS proposes that the completed emitter→absorber relation is end-to-end and longitudinal. That claim belongs to the POAMS ontology stated above; it is not inferred from a dashed line, from the direction of a wire rupture, or from the absence of a transverse arrow in another framework.

What the historical fork actually supplies

Ampère's current-element formulation and later Grassmann-type formulations can assign internal element forces differently while agreeing on important complete-circuit results. That mathematical fork is worth preserving. It does not follow, however, that any observed axial rupture or recoil uniquely identifies the responsible ledger. Geometry, current history, material response, and uncertainty must all be specified before an operational record can select among accounts.

The Graneaus reported exploding-wire, recoil, and water-arc phenomena as evidence for longitudinal current-element action. This exhibit supplies no raw dataset, uncertainty budget, or side-by-side calculation for those reports. They therefore remain a contested research lead, not proof of the POAMS hand-off and not proof that a foreign framework is incapable in principle.

The bookkeeping, in symbols

Same equations, honest referents

POAMS keeps antenna engineering's equations and re-reads what each symbol is. A few anchors:

$$ w \equiv \frac{1}{2\pi}\oint d\varphi = \pm n \in \mathbb Z, \qquad q = Q(w) $$

POAMS identifies charge sign with winding sign. $Q$ denotes Pope's imported charge map; this page does not set coulombs equal to joules or derive $Q$.

$$ h=2\pi\hbar, \qquad \Delta E=h\nu, \qquad P=\Gamma\Delta E=\Gamma h\nu $$

$h$ is the action of one completed turn, $\nu$ is turn rate, and $\Gamma$ is completed closures per observer second. This separates per-event action, event energy, and power.

$$ s = c\,t \qquad [\,c\,] = \frac{\text{unit of observed distance}}{\text{unit of observed time}} $$

The observer record keeps $t=r/c$ exactly. POAMS interprets $c$ as a distance–time conversion; that interpretation is a premise, not a dimensional proof.

$$ \ell_{\rm dipole} \approx \tfrac{1}{2}\lambda \qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad \text{leading thin-dipole closure} $$

This is the pictured thin, centre-fed dipole's leading condition, not a universal antenna-length law.

Dimensional boundary. An imported radiated-power law cannot be converted into a phase law by renaming its variables. This exhibit supplies no derived map from conductor geometry and phase history to $P$. It therefore keeps only the dimensionally closed event ledger $P=\Gamma h\nu$ and leaves the constitutive calculation of $\Gamma$ open. “Angular momentum per unit time” alone has units of energy, not power.
