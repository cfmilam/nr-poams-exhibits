3 · ‘Black holes’: the empty barycentre

What is actually observed, at the centres of galaxies and in compact binaries, is this: matter in rapid orbit about centres that emit nothing. Stars at the core of the Milky Way whip around a dark point of enormous booked mass (the S2 orbit closes on ~4.1×106 solar masses inside a region smaller than the solar system) extant record — GRAVITY; the EHT images show a bright ring of emission ~42 μas across at M87’s core, ~52 μas at Sgr A* extant record — EHT M87* EHT Sgr A*; gravitational-wave detectors record the chirps of compact masses spiralling together extant record — LIGO. All of that is measurement, and none of it is in dispute here. The dispute is over one inference: that the dark centre must be a thing — a material object of “prodigious” density with an interior, a horizon, and a singularity.

“There is no reason to assume that centres of balance for bodies in balanced angular momentum situations are mass-objects. If the planets in our solar system were much larger than they are in comparison with our sun, the centre of rotation of the sun and planets might well lie outside the sun’s surface, somewhere in the void between the sun and all its planets. Observed from a great distance, it might seem that these bodies are rotating around an invisible centre of balance which, in itself, emits no radiation. To call this a ‘black hole’ may be picturesque but, unfortunately, lends itself to the sort of journalistic distortion that perverts the true course of science.”Pope, The Eye of the Beholder (2004), §VII.2

The Ch. 9 statement is even more direct. In a many-body circulation the centre-of-mass “has none of the characteristics normally ascribed to bodies… This has no existence as a material object but only as an empty barycentre for the matter swirling around it. It is ‘dark’ insofar as, in itself, it radiates no energy. Otherwise there is nothing singular or metaphysical about it.” The named example is the same object the EHT later photographed: “it makes no sense to explain the ‘blackness’ of an invisible centre of moment in a spiral galaxy, such as the X-ray nebula in M87, as being due to its ‘gravitational entrapment’ of the light it would otherwise emit.”

The dynamics complete the picture without any exotic object. A galaxy that shines, sheds energy; shedding energy, it sheds angular momentum; shedding angular momentum, it contracts and spins faster — the drain-eye logic of the whole corpus. Contraction has a floor (§2): matter “get[s] closer and closer to a lower radial limit,” stacking on ever-closer natural orbits around the empty centre of balance. So the prediction of the synthesis is: compact, dark-centred, extremely fast circulations of arbitrarily large booked mass — yes; holes in being — no. “POAMS finds no parallel with the idea of ‘black holes’ ‘hoovering up’ stars and other bits of matter into oblivion” (Ch. 9). What the EHT ring shows, on this reading, is emission from plasma on close natural orbits around an empty barycentre — an eyewall portrait. The darkness inside is what emptiness looks like.

The external clock — a consequence of the relational booking postulate, not an error found in GR. Grant the Schwarzschild geometry for the sake of argument, and read it under this corpus’s one booking rule: the exchange ledger privileges completed bookings. On the external observer’s books — the only books any astronomer keeps — an infalling clock’s approach to the horizon takes unboundedly long: the crossing is never booked. Each e-fold of remaining distance costs of order $r_s/c$; for M87* ($M \approx 6.5\times10^9\,M_\odot$, $r_s \approx 1.92\times10^{13}$ m) that is $r_s/c \approx 6.4\times10^4$ s ≈ 17.8 hours per e-fold, forever. The “finished” black hole — horizon crossed, interior formed, singularity reached — exists only in the proper time of the infalling ledger, which is exactly the God’s-eye completion no external observer ever audits. Be exact about the status of this argument. Extant GR resolves the horizon-crossing question in its own terms — the external divergence is a chart property, the infalling proper time is finite, and the crossing is locally unremarkable; that resolution has been on the books since the 1960s extant record. Nothing here claims an error in that mathematics. The divergence is ontological posture: whether physics is what completed exchanges book (the relational postulate, under which the crossing is never an entry in any astronomer’s ledger) or what the invariant geometry contains (the extant reading) — and separating those postures is an open observational program, not a finished argument. The pre-1960s literature’s name for the external books’ content — the frozen star — remains the honest description of what is actually audited.

And the trap for light? There is no light-projectile to trap — the corpus has no ballistic photon and no pull (the quantum hand-off; antenna emission). Light is an exchange completed between two ledgers, not a thing labouring across a stage. “Gravitational entrapment of light” is a manner of speaking from the container model, taken literally. Refuse the container and the phrase has nothing to refer to.
