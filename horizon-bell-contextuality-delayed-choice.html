Bell, Contextuality & Delayed Choice

What the completed-comparison ledger excludes—and the coherent law it still does not supply.

CLASSICAL COMPLETION NO-GO / NATIVE COHERENT RULE OPEN
Adjudication. POAMS co-signing does not evade Bell's bound when it is represented by a setting-independent shared preparation and locally factorized response. Context dependence can reject that representation, but neither contextuality nor no-signalling selects the observed quantum correlation law. The present corpus reaches a proved stopping point at the missing coherent state, composition and readout rule.

2 · Classical completed-ledger theorem

Let \(x,y\in\{0,1\}\) be freely selected settings, \(a,b\in\{-1,+1\}\) the registered outcomes, and \(\lambda\) any complete retained preparation record. Assume:

  1. Setting independence: \(\rho(\lambda\mid x,y)=\rho(\lambda)\).
  2. Local factorization: \(P(a,b\mid x,y,\lambda)=P(a\mid x,\lambda)P(b\mid y,\lambda)\).
  3. Ordinary nonnegative frequencies: the conditional probabilities normalize.

Define \(A_x(\lambda)=\sum_a aP(a\mid x,\lambda)\) and \(B_y(\lambda)=\sum_b bP(b\mid y,\lambda)\), each in \([-1,1]\). Then

\[S=E_{00}+E_{01}+E_{10}-E_{11},\qquad E_{xy}=\int d\lambda\,\rho(\lambda)A_x(\lambda)B_y(\lambda).\]

For every \(\lambda\),

\[|A_0(B_0+B_1)+A_1(B_0-B_1)|\le |B_0+B_1|+|B_0-B_1|\le2.\]

Integration therefore gives \(|S|\le2\).

The result already allows stochastic responses, arbitrary hidden census, memory inside \(\lambda\), and a preparation shared by both endpoints. Calling that preparation one co-signed transaction does not alter any premise or the bound.

3 · Exactly which exits exist

Setting dependence

Letting \(\rho(\lambda\mid x,y)\) vary can exceed 2, but requires a physical setting–preparation dependence and an intervention model. Renaming it global bookkeeping does not provide one.

Nonfactorizable completion

A joint response \(P(a,b\mid x,y,\lambda)\) can exceed 2. It must still derive no-signalling marginals, the angular law and the observed ceiling without a controllable superluminal channel.

Nonclassical composition

Signed, complex or otherwise nonclassical alternatives can evade the proof because ordinary conditional factorization no longer applies. POAMS has not yet constructed their physical state or readout rule.

4 · Why no-signalling does not finish the job

The marginal conditions

\[\sum_bP(a,b\mid x,y)=P(a\mid x),\qquad \sum_aP(a,b\mid x,y)=P(b\mid y)\]

prevent controllable signalling through setting choice. They do not select quantum correlations. A Popescu–Rohrlich box with binary bits satisfying \(a\oplus b=xy\) has uniform local marginals and reaches \(|S|=4\). Classical local completions stop at 2; quantum theory stops at \(2\sqrt2\); no-signalling alone stops at 4.

Selection debt. A native theory must explain why physical correlations occupy the quantum set rather than the whole no-signalling polytope. That requires more than conservation, co-signing, contextual readout or causal restraint.

5 · Contextuality and delayed choice

Contextuality rules out one context-independent assignment of outcomes to all incompatible measurements under the relevant compatibility assumptions. It licenses context-indexed outcome formation; it does not choose the frequencies. A separate response rule is still owed.

Delayed-choice arrangements change which completed comparison is physically available. They do not require an already registered past event to be rewritten. This is compatible with POAMS's transaction discipline, but compatibility is not a quantitative derivation.

6 · The native object now required

  1. A preparation state that is more than a classical mixture over complete instruction records.
  2. A reversible rule for alternative composition and relative phase.
  3. A product/composite rule for separated endpoint interventions.
  4. A terminal readout map producing nonnegative frequencies.
  5. No-signalling marginals derived from the same construction.
  6. The observed angular correlation and \(2\sqrt2\) ceiling, not merely a Bell violation.
  7. A prospective discriminator or a declared empirical equivalence class.

This is the same missing object exposed independently by neutrino oscillation: a phase-bearing state and coherent alternative-composition/readout rule. Bell now adds a harder constraint—ordinary local stochastic completion is not merely insufficient in practice; it is mathematically excluded.

7 · Stopping point and experimental gate

Stopping theorem: current POAMS bookkeeping can type preparation, setting, endpoint and comparison events, and it can reject travelling-particle stories. It cannot derive Bell correlations until it adds a nonclassical joint-state law or an explicit physical violation of another Bell premise. No further rearrangement of classical address counts, hidden clones, renewal kernels or transaction prose can cross the bound.

A candidate resumes only after its state and operations are frozen before data comparison. It must be tested against one event-ready CHSH record, one contextuality construction on the same declared state space, no-signalling marginals, setting/selection custody and at least one prediction not inserted from the imported quantum target.
