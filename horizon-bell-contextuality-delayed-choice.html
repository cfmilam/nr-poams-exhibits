NATIVE BOUNDARY DEDUCTIONS / FINITE CONDITIONAL SOURCE–TERMINAL CONSTRUCTION
The POAMS claim. A Bell trial is not a story about two tiny objects carrying properties through empty space. It is one source-bounded circulation offered two independently chosen terminal orientations and settled by two whole-sign registrations. The final object of study is the completed four-entry receipt: readiness, left orientation, right orientation and paired signs. Bell's inequality tells us that this receipt cannot be generated by two locally prewritten ledgers under the stated independence conditions. It does not tell us that particles, fields in a void or traveling messages exist.

1 · Begin with what occurred

Each accepted trial contains only operationally receipted events:

  1. a declared source boundary reports that a joint preparation is ready;
  2. two terminal orientations \(x\) and \(y\) are selected independently of that preparation;
  3. each terminal posts one whole registered sense, \(s,t\in\{-1,+1\}\);
  4. the two records are paired by the trial identifier and counted.

From repeated trials we form \(P(s,t\mid x,y)\) and \(E_{xy}=\sum_{s,t}stP(s,t\mid x,y)\). “Particle,” “wavefunction,” “collapse,” “electron” and “nucleus” are not observations in that receipt. They are interpretations imported by other frameworks. POAMS begins with the completed angular-momentum account and owes any deeper construction it adds.

Measured standing. Well-controlled Bell experiments report paired frequencies that exceed the applicable local-factorization bound. The settings, readiness rule, timing, losses, selections and raw sign records—not a preferred ontology—constitute the measurement custody.

2 · The locally prewritten ledger fails

Let \(\lambda\) denote every source-side fact completed before the freely selected terminal orientations. A locally prewritten account makes three commitments:

  1. Preparation independence: \(\rho(\lambda\mid x,y)=\rho(\lambda)\).
  2. Separate terminal settlement: \(P(s,t\mid x,y,\lambda)=P(s\mid x,\lambda)P(t\mid y,\lambda)\).
  3. Ordinary frequencies: all conditional weights are nonnegative and normalized.

Writing \(A_x(\lambda)=\sum_s sP(s\mid x,\lambda)\) and \(B_y(\lambda)=\sum_t tP(t\mid y,\lambda)\), each lies in \([-1,1]\). Therefore

\[S=E_{00}+E_{01}+E_{10}-E_{11}\]\[|A_0(B_0+B_1)+A_1(B_0-B_1)|\le2,\qquad |S|\le2.\]

This already permits noise, memory, arbitrarily many hidden addresses and a common source record. Calling \(\lambda\) a “co-signed transaction” changes nothing. If the two terminal signs were already locally readable facts, POAMS would fail against the Bell record.

3 · What Bell establishes

Removed

A complete, setting-independent source record followed by two conditionally separate terminal responses cannot reproduce the measured violation.

Not removed

Normal Realism, one substance, angular-momentum conservation and definite completed records survive. What fails is the extra assumption that every future comparison was separately written at preparation.

Not supplied

Bell does not provide the correct joint law, choose its ceiling, identify a carrier, or prove any microscopic picture. Those are separate debts.

4 · One completed account

POAMS treats the source and both terminal seats as one account whose settlement is defined only when the comparison closes. Nothing has to fly from one terminal to the other after an orientation is chosen. Nothing is pushed through a void. The endpoint distance belongs to the geometry of registration; it does not divide one conserved account into two ontologically independent things.

This language explains why a nonfactorizable joint receipt is admissible. It does not determine its frequencies. Co-signing alone is therefore necessary transaction grammar, not a Bell derivation.

Hard boundary. “The ledger is global” cannot be used as incense. A valid model must state the joint completion rule, recover terminal marginals, give the angular dependence, declare its ceiling and expose a way nature can reject it.

4a · The retained boundary is a derivable requirement

Two preparations may leave identical endpoint receipts and still respond differently to the same later reversal. If they do, the endpoints alone are not a sufficient description: something retained by the completed circulation has been omitted.

Predictive-boundary theorem. Histories may share one predictive state only when every permitted future intervention gives the same distribution of raw records. If a future test distinguishes them, a sufficient state must distinguish them too. With \(K\) pairwise distinguishable histories, at least \(K\) retained descriptive states are required.

This follows from the meaning of a sufficient account, without complex amplitudes or an inherited microscopic picture. It identifies what a construction must keep. A finite repertoire of tests establishes sufficiency only for that repertoire.

4b · Retention has an integer-account consequence

Take a signed whole-turn tally \(k\), its apparatus counter-entry \(-k\), a retained sense \(r=\pm1\), and its preparation counter-entry \(-r\). The complete account sums to zero. A declared write advances the tally by the retained sense:

\[(k,-k,r,-r)\longmapsto(k+r,-k-r,r,-r).\]

Two writes with the same retained sense reach \(+2\) or \(-2\), never zero. If the sense is independently replaced between writes, zero occurs in half the equally enumerated preparations. A write followed by its inverse returns every tally to zero when the same sense is retained; replacement reduces that return fraction to one half.

The distinction is constructed with balanced whole-integer receipts. The equal preparation weights and update are declared choices, not a universal frequency law or an apparatus already built. The useful consequence is a clear test of retention: preserve the present endpoint receipt, change what is retained, and compare the subsequent response.

4c · Relative phase is fixed before its response law is

Suppose the prepared comparison is covariant under a common frame rotation and unchanged by reversing both registered signs. These qualified symmetries reduce its two-sign frequencies to

\[P(s,t\mid\phi)=\frac{1+st\,f(\phi)}4,\qquad -1\le f(\phi)\le1.\]

Here \(\phi\) is the relative terminal orientation and \(f\) is the measured sign-product response. Exchange symmetry gives \(f(-\phi)=f(\phi)\); a half-turn reversal of one analyzer gives \(f(\phi+\pi)=-f(\phi)\). Exact equal-orientation opposition fixes \(f(0)=-1\).

Those conditions do not select the curve: both \(-\cos\phi\) and \(-\cos3\phi\) satisfy them. This locates the native derivation task precisely. The circulation, phase advance and terminal-posting operation must select the response before the measured curve is consulted. Conservation and the symmetry reduction alone cannot do that work.

4d · Whole signs can keep a bounded remainder

A terminal can turn an oriented input into whole signed counts without pretending that every input projection is already a whole output. Let \(x_i=\mathbf n_i\cdot\mathbf b\) be the independently calibrated axial projection of one input, and let \(r_i\) be the retained axial mismatch in the same angular-momentum unit. Choose the control

\[\begin{aligned}z_i&=r_{i-1}+x_i,\\[.35em]s_i&=\begin{cases}+1&z_i\ge0,\\-1&z_i<0,\end{cases}\\[.35em]r_i&=z_i-s_i.\end{aligned}\]

For \(|x_i|\le1\) and \(|r_0|\le1\), this keeps \(|r_i|\le1\) at every step. From a zeroed store,

\[\left|\sum_{i=1}^N(s_i-x_i)\right|\le1.\]

At a fixed input angle this gives a positive-count fraction within \(1/(2N)\) of \((1+\cos\theta)/2\). It is a finite-count theorem for a specified retained controller, not a per-trial probability assumed from the desired curve. The transverse angular momentum must go to a separately recorded port; a finite store cannot absorb the same oblique input indefinitely. Reset exports the old remainder and changes the count law.

The distinction that matters for Bell is which account is bounded. A shared store can return to zero while retaining source-sign-weighted transfers; exactly balanced examples give either \(E=+1\) or \(E=-1\). Two genuinely separate tag-routed stores give a stronger sequential result, but require physical access to the preparation tag. Moreover, if a source-conditioned vector and all its terminal transfers are locally readable, those transfers disclose the remote orientation. Balanced sign marginals do not hide that extra record. The finite scalar construction below therefore uses CCK's local-versus-joint record structure, not an unobserved vector behind the same old controller.

5 · The adopted completion calculus

POAMS presently supplies an additional mathematical rule with the Coherent Completion Kernel (CCK), an openly adopted constitutive extension. It does not replace the native construction task identified above. Its four selections are:

  • CCK-S: unresolved completion alternatives retain relative phase in a finite complex account.
  • CCK-D: an isolated reversible reorientation preserves the comparison measure.
  • CCK-C: independently addressable terminal seats compose while permitting irreducibly relational joint organization.
  • CCK-M: mutually exclusive terminal settlements receive normalized additive frequencies.

This calculus was independently developed in conventional quantum theory. POAMS adopts its operational structure without adopting particles, fields in empty space, or a second substance. The numerical constructions below are consequences of that explicit adoption and their stated source/readout premises, not deductions of the calculus from whole-turn closure.

6 · The two-seat zero-account construction

Suppose a source boundary prepares exactly two fundamental half-turn response seats and the pair exhausts one zero-total-angular-momentum account. There is then one rotation-invariant joint completion class. In a convenient imported coordinate representation it is

\[|\Omega_0\rangle=\frac{|+ -\rangle-|- +\rangle}{\sqrt2}.\]

The symbols are account coordinates, not little objects with hidden orientations. Under CCK settlement, terminal orientations \(\mathbf a,\mathbf b\) and whole signs \(s,t=\pm1\) give

\[P(s,t\mid\mathbf a,\mathbf b)=\frac{1-st\,\mathbf a\!\cdot\!\mathbf b}{4},\qquad E(\mathbf a,\mathbf b)=-\mathbf a\!\cdot\!\mathbf b.\]

Each terminal alone remains balanced at \(1/2\); the completed comparison carries the angular relation. Standard coplanar orientations yield \(|S|=2\sqrt2\). No visibility is fitted. This is a conditional POAMS theorem: it follows from the declared two-seat zero account plus adopted CCK, not from generic co-signing or whole-turn discreteness alone.

  • Joint source custody does not prove two-seat exhaustion; an unobserved source, apparatus or environmental seat may retain part of the account.

8 · A three-orientation receipt

For a declared two-seat response space, perform disjoint same-orientation calibration ensembles along three orthogonal terminal orientations. Let their registered sign correlations be \(C_{11},C_{22},C_{33}\). The zero-account fidelity is

\[F_0=\frac{1-C_{11}-C_{22}-C_{33}}{4}.\]

\(F_0=1\) certifies the unique two-seat rotation-invariant completion class within that declared response space. Values below one measure non-scalar contamination rather than inviting an ontological story. This receipt must be computed on calibration trials fixed before the separate Bell trials are opened.

The coordinate identity behind the receipt is the imported projector relation

\[\Pi_0=\frac{I-\Sigma_1\Sigma_1-\Sigma_2\Sigma_2-\Sigma_3\Sigma_3}{4}.\]

Here each \(\Sigma_k\Sigma_k\) means the paired sign comparison for orientation \(k\). The notation is mathematical scaffolding; the laboratory content is three orientation choices and paired whole-sign counts.

10 · Contextuality and delayed choice, recast

Contextuality: a terminal sign is not licensed as a freestanding property possessed before every possible comparison. It is the whole-sign settlement of the comparison actually completed. This blocks a single context-independent instruction table, but it does not by itself choose the frequencies.

Delayed choice: a later apparatus choice determines which comparison can close. It does not rewrite an already completed past event. The mistake is assigning a finished history to an unresolved account and then imagining that nature must edit it later.

12 · Read the retained completion record

The boundary theorem suggests a concrete next question: does an accessible retained record merely label the alternatives, or must the pair and record be described jointly? Endpoint-only observations can leave those descriptions indistinguishable. Read the retained record as part of the same completed account.

For this conditional construction, let \(X,Y,Z\) denote qualified binary readout settings in the adopted CCK representation. The source occupies the declared opposed-sign sector with coherence coordinate \(\kappa=u+iv\). Prepare record \(E\) in \(|0\rangle\) and specify a signed relative-phase write \(V(\theta)=\exp[-i\theta Z_A I_B Y_E/2]\). This is a separately specified write-and-joint-record construction. The scalar source and terminal comparator in §§15–18 do not implement this particular write or its three-context analyzer merely by preparing a good source; that material implementation remains to be constructed.

Choose the analyzer phase \(\phi\) on independent pilot records and freeze it. With \(X_\phi=\cos\phi\,X+\sin\phi\,Y\) and \(Y_\phi=-\sin\phi\,X+\cos\phi\,Y\), three joint sign-product readouts give

\[W=-\langle X_{\phi,A}X_B Z_E\rangle+\langle Z_A I_B X_E\rangle-\langle Y_{\phi,A}X_B Y_E\rangle.\]

The declared alternative is a mixture of separately sufficient pair and record states, \(\sum_jp_j\sigma_{AB,j}\otimes\tau_{E,j}\). Its local three-axis expectation vectors have length at most one; their scalar product and every mixture therefore obey \(W\le1\). This tests that specified alternative under qualified readouts, not every possible account of nature.

The joint-write prediction is \(W=c_\phi+(1+c_\phi)\sin\theta\), where \(c_\phi=u\cos\phi-v\sin\phi\). At a quarter write and aligned phase,

\[W=1+2|\kappa|,\qquad W_{\mathrm{ideal}}=3.\]

The reversed write reverses the last two score signs and gives the same prediction. No write, inverse, reset and record-dephasing controls remain within the alternative's bound. The phase-complete readout includes the quadrature component; it does not mistake a rotated source phase for an absent retained relation.

13 · Turn the record contrast into a usable tolerance

Analyzer misalignment changes the null ceiling. Let \(\delta_{ij}\) bound each measured cross-axis overlap, and let \(\beta\) be the largest sum of the two overlap bounds touching one axis. For the pair-side and record-side triples, use

\[B=\min\!\left(3,\sqrt{(1+\beta_A)(1+\beta_E)}\right).\]

Accept only when the lower corrected score exceeds this upper calibrated bound. Detector asymmetry, calibration uncertainty, missing signs and finite sample size all enter that lower score. A missing trial remains in the scheduled denominator with its full adverse range; independent reference records check readout stability.

If a description is written as an allowed pair/record mixture plus a residual part of weight \(w\), the score gives a quantitative minimum:

\[w\ge\max\!\left(0,\frac{W_{\mathrm{lower}}-B}{3-B}\right),\qquad B<3.\]

This is a weight in a statistical decomposition, not a fraction of angular momentum or a second substance. For a simple ideal-source noise model that retains the written record with fraction \(q\) and replaces it by an unpolarized record otherwise, the new score is \(3q\). Its threshold is \(q>1/3\); the earlier two-context score required \(q>1/2\). The error-envelope theorem separates a margin that additional trials can resolve from one that requires better calibration or record retention.

15 · A finite record can distinguish zero-total accounts

The constructive move is to put the comparison into a record whose two internal organizations both have zero total angular momentum. Four calibrated fundamental response seats admit the scalar configurations

\[\begin{aligned}q_0&=S_{12}S_{34},\\[.5em]q_1&=\frac{T^+_{12}T^-_{34}+T^-_{12}T^+_{34}-T^0_{12}T^0_{34}}{\sqrt3}.\end{aligned}\]

Here \(S\) and \(T\) name the singlet and three triplet alternatives of the adopted two-seat rotation response. Zero total does not mean an empty record: the pair organization distinguishes \(q_0\) from \(q_1\). Split this record coherently, let its alternatives acquire different phases from the total-response sector of the compared seats, compensate the reference phase, and recombine. The final record can flag the sector without borrowing an infinitely rigid directional axis.

For a source, compare two independently unpolarized seats, \(I_4/4\), retaining their purifying partners in the preparation account. Ideal scalar-sector acceptance succeeds on one quarter of attempts. More generally let \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\) be the actual acceptance probabilities for singlet and triplet input sectors. For this specified sector-preserving instrument, the rotation-invariant accepted source has

\[\begin{aligned}p_{\mathrm{ready}}&=\frac{\alpha+3\beta}{4},\\[.5em]F_s&=\frac{\alpha}{\alpha+3\beta},\\[.5em]V&=\frac{\alpha-\beta}{\alpha+3\beta}.\end{aligned}\]

The accepted source output is checked in the full occupation model to preserve its input singlet/triplet sector. Acceptance probabilities alone would not establish the stated output: an additional depolarizing channel could leave the herald counts unchanged and destroy its quality. The herald precedes terminal settings. Rejected preparations remain in the source ledger and consume stock; a failed triplet is not relabeled a fresh independent attempt.

For a terminal, compare its signal seat with a fresh finite directional reference of total response \(L\). Distinguishing sectors \(L-1/2\) and \(L+1/2\) gives ideal gain \(\eta=2L/(2L+1)\), with a finite-reference bias. Actually prepare the reference in orientation \(\chi\mathbf a\), with a fair independent selector \(\chi=\pm1\), and report \(s=\chi f\) from the raw sector sign \(f\). Retain both \(\chi\) and \(f\). Matched opposite-reference preparation and readout, together with this sign multiplication, remove the bias. It is a physical reversal plus a defined decoder, not a change of labels on the same preparation. For qualified independent terminal apparatus,

\[P(s,t\mid\mathbf a,\mathbf b)=\frac{1-st\,V\eta_A\eta_B\,\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b}{4}.\]

Finite references therefore have an explicit price. For fresh \(L=3\) references the ideal pair visibility is \(36/49\), not one. Reusing references can correlate their retained states, so a fresh-pair formula is not a reuse law.

16 · Give the comparison a material interaction

A worked candidate uses a finite set of gate-confined response modes in silicon. It adopts the one-orbital Hubbard occupation law, fermionic occupancy, CCK and a capacitive oscillator sensor. These are declared material and mathematical inputs. POAMS interprets the operation as retained circulation, transfer and settlement; the calculation does not derive the imported laws from whole-turn bookkeeping.

The four record seats have all six pair contacts. Each compared seat contacts record seats 1 and 2 equally. The source has two compared seats and ten active comparison contacts; a terminal has one signal plus six aligned reference seats and twenty contacts. A linear chain is not this graph.

\[\begin{aligned}\frac{H}{U}&=-\sum_{ij,\sigma}a_{ij}(c^\dagger_{i\sigma}c_{j\sigma}+c^\dagger_{j\sigma}c_{i\sigma})\\&\quad+\sum_i n_{i\uparrow}n_{i\downarrow}+\sum_i e_i n_i.\end{aligned}\]

\(U\) is the adopted double-occupation energy; \(a_{ij}\) and \(e_i\) are calibrated transfer and detuning ratios. The operators are the imported finite occupation representation; the sum uses each admitted unordered contact once and both response labels \(\sigma\). Set record transfer \(a=0.003\) and compared-to-record transfer \(a\sqrt r\), with \(r=0.02\). Temporary empty and double occupations are propagated, not discarded through an ideal spin-only approximation.

Write the cycle rate as \(\nu_U=U/h\) and the dimensionless duration as \(s=2\pi\nu_U t\); equivalently the angular rate is \(u=U/\hbar=2\pi\nu_U\). Seven finite isolated record pulses split, compensate and recombine the record. Every contact rises and falls over \(20/u\). Let \(R\) be the integrated rise propagator and \(K_p=(H/U)_{\mathrm{plateau}}\). For real symmetric control matrices, the complete ramped propagator is \(R^{\mathsf T}e^{-iK_ps_{\mathrm{hold}}}R\), not \(R^\dagger e^{-iK_ps_{\mathrm{hold}}}R\): the fall reverses time ordering, not the Hamiltonian's sign. The actual leaked occupations retain their phases through every pulse.

After comparison, contacts turn off. Occupation sensors first check all seats; a finite pair-to-charge conversion then reads the singlet of record pair 12. This is not a perfect projector onto \(q_0\). Its conversion failure and undetected record-spin leakage enter the probabilities below. At the source, only a charge-good singlet record heralds readiness. At a terminal, charge failure remains an outcome and receives an independent preallocated fair sign; it is never dropped from the Bell denominator.

17 · Close the readout and resource account

The charge detector is itself part of the calculation. An isolated seat's occupation \(n\) drives a finite-energy oscillator through

\[\begin{aligned}H_{\mathrm{sens}}/\hbar&=\omega b^\dagger b\\&\quad+g\sin^2(\omega t/2)n(b+b^\dagger),\\&\quad 0\le t\le2\pi/\omega.\end{aligned}\]

Here \(\omega\) and \(g\) are angular rates; \(\omega/(2\pi)\) is the oscillator's cycle frequency. The smooth pulse separates the three occupation readouts. If adjacent means are \(d\) standard deviations apart, their single-boundary Gaussian classification tail is \(e=\tfrac12\operatorname{erfc}[d/(2\sqrt2)]\). The complete retained measurement is a stronger requirement than correct classification: its error is bounded through the distance between the actual and ideal pointer writes.

At the sufficient specification \(d=16\), the seven source readings contribute at most \(14\sqrt e\approx3.49\times10^{-7}\) and the twelve terminal readings at most \(24\sqrt e\approx5.99\times10^{-7}\) to the complete-instrument error. Additional non-Gaussian or finite-range wrong-region probabilities enter the pointer error before the square root is taken; other bounded full-instrument deviations then add to the budget. A cold, noiseless example has an explicit 8,192-level pointer cutoff and a bounded omitted tail; a hot or noisy pointer needs its own finite-capacity calculation.

Fresh resources are counted. The source consumes two signal seats, two retained purifiers and four record seats per attempt. Each terminal consumes its signal, six reference seats and four record seats, with the signals already counted at source. The nominal successful-pair census averages about 52 prepared seats before failed loading, routing and other overhead. Readout energy, polarizing supports, baths and spent records remain in the account. The construction consumes qualified finite stocks; it does not claim an indefinitely cycling machine with free reset.
