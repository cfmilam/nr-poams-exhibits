2 · Classical completed-ledger theorem

Let \(x,y\in\{0,1\}\) be freely selected settings, \(a,b\in\{-1,+1\}\) the registered outcomes, and \(\lambda\) any complete retained preparation record. Assume:

  1. Setting independence: \(\rho(\lambda\mid x,y)=\rho(\lambda)\).
  2. Local factorization: \(P(a,b\mid x,y,\lambda)=P(a\mid x,\lambda)P(b\mid y,\lambda)\).
  3. Ordinary nonnegative frequencies: the conditional probabilities normalize.

Define \(A_x(\lambda)=\sum_a aP(a\mid x,\lambda)\) and \(B_y(\lambda)=\sum_b bP(b\mid y,\lambda)\), each in \([-1,1]\). Then

\[S=E_{00}+E_{01}+E_{10}-E_{11},\qquad E_{xy}=\int d\lambda\,\rho(\lambda)A_x(\lambda)B_y(\lambda).\]

For every \(\lambda\),

\[|A_0(B_0+B_1)+A_1(B_0-B_1)|\le |B_0+B_1|+|B_0-B_1|\le2.\]

Integration therefore gives \(|S|\le2\).

The result already allows stochastic responses, arbitrary hidden census, memory inside \(\lambda\), and a preparation shared by both endpoints. Calling that preparation one co-signed transaction does not alter any premise or the bound.

3 · Exactly which exits exist

Setting dependence

Letting \(\rho(\lambda\mid x,y)\) vary can exceed 2, but requires a physical setting–preparation dependence and an intervention model. Renaming it global bookkeeping does not provide one.

Nonfactorizable completion

A joint response \(P(a,b\mid x,y,\lambda)\) can exceed 2. It must still derive no-signalling marginals, the angular law and the observed ceiling without a controllable superluminal channel.

Nonclassical composition

Signed, complex or otherwise nonclassical alternatives can evade the proof because ordinary conditional factorization no longer applies. POAMS has not yet constructed their physical state or readout rule.

4 · Why no-signalling does not finish the job

The marginal conditions

\[\sum_bP(a,b\mid x,y)=P(a\mid x),\qquad \sum_aP(a,b\mid x,y)=P(b\mid y)\]

prevent controllable signalling through setting choice. They do not select quantum correlations. A Popescu–Rohrlich box with binary bits satisfying \(a\oplus b=xy\) has uniform local marginals and reaches \(|S|=4\). Classical local completions stop at 2; quantum theory stops at \(2\sqrt2\); no-signalling alone stops at 4.

Selection debt. A native theory must explain why physical correlations occupy the quantum set rather than the whole no-signalling polytope. That requires more than conservation, co-signing, contextual readout or causal restraint.

5 · Contextuality and delayed choice

Contextuality rules out one context-independent assignment of outcomes to all incompatible measurements under the relevant compatibility assumptions. It licenses context-indexed outcome formation; it does not choose the frequencies. A separate response rule is still owed.

Delayed-choice arrangements change which completed comparison is physically available. They do not require an already registered past event to be rewritten. This is compatible with POAMS's transaction discipline, but compatibility is not a quantitative derivation.

6 · Scalar-pair conditional theorem

Adopt CCK and the calibrated fundamental \(j=\tfrac12\) endpoint response. If one completed source closure prepares exactly two endpoint seats in a pure total-angular-momentum-zero sector, with no residual orbital, apparatus or environment seat carrying the account, then

\[\tfrac12\otimes\tfrac12=0\oplus1\]

contains one unique scalar ray:

\[|\Psi^-\rangle=\frac{|+ -\rangle-|- +\rangle}{\sqrt2}.\]

For analyzer axes \(\mathbf a,\mathbf b\) and outcomes \(s,t=\pm1\), CCK-M gives

\[P(s,t\mid\mathbf a,\mathbf b)=\frac{1-st\,\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b}{4},\qquad E(\mathbf a,\mathbf b)=-\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b.\]

The local marginals are exactly \(1/2\), and the standard coplanar settings give \(|S|=2\sqrt2\). No angular coefficient or visibility is fitted. The minus sign comes from the unique scalar subrepresentation—not from the words “opposite” or “co-signed.”

7 · The source receipt is the load-bearing open

Conservation selects the scalar pair only if the two endpoint seats exhaust the prepared angular-momentum account. Three weaker claims fail:

  • Zero local mean: the rotationally invariant family \(\rho_q=q|\Psi^-\rangle\langle\Psi^-|+(1-q)I/4\) has zero local vector for every \(q\), but \(E_q=-q\,\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b\).
  • Opposition on one axis: the classical mixture of \(|+-\rangle\) and \(|-+\rangle\) is perfectly opposite on that axis and uncorrelated on a transverse axis.
  • Co-signing: joint custody does not establish purity, the endpoint carrier, \(J=0\), or account exhaustion.

The remaining work is physical: construct or certify a source boundary whose zero-total-AM closure has exactly two fundamental response seats and no residual which-alternative or angular-momentum record.

8 · The scalar receipt found

For exactly two calibrated response seats, the singlet projector and total-spin account are

\[\Pi_s=\frac{I-XX-YY-ZZ}{4},\qquad J^2=2(I-\Pi_s).\]

Three same-axis calibration ensembles therefore give

\[F_s=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho\Pi_s)=1-\frac{\langle J^2\rangle}{2}=\frac{1-C_{xx}-C_{yy}-C_{zz}}{4}.\]

This is the missing operational witness. It is calculated on preregistered calibration trials, not inferred from the held-out CHSH score. \(F_s=1\) uniquely certifies the scalar pair under the declared two-seat model; imperfect preparation yields a measured triplet contamination.

A concrete implementation candidate already exists. Hensen et al.'s event-ready diamond-spin experiment separately characterized its remote two-spin source before the Bell run, reported fidelity \(0.92\pm0.03\), used randomized \(ZZ/XX\) characterization, then fixed settings and herald windows. The strengthened POAMS receipt adds a disjoint \(YY\) calibration ensemble and computes the scalar sum before opening held-out CHSH trials. Primary experiment · open preprint.

9 · No hidden which-alternative record at unit fidelity

The receipt closes more than the two-seat reduced state. Let \(E\) contain every unobserved source, orbital, apparatus and environmental degree of freedom. If

\[\operatorname{Tr}[(\Pi_s\otimes I_E)\rho_{12E}]=1,\]

then positivity forces all support into the rank-one scalar sector:

\[\rho_{12E}=|\Psi^-\rangle\!\langle\Psi^-|\otimes\sigma_E.\]

Thus no environment can retain a correlated which-alternative or angular-momentum record when the calibrated scalar fidelity is exactly one. This is a factorization theorem, not an assumption of environmental absence. For \(F_s<1\), the same projector identifies the non-scalar weight and the gentle-measurement bound places the full state within trace-norm distance \(2\sqrt{1-F_s}\) of its scalar projected branch.

The surviving loophole is prior to this theorem: the laboratory must prove that every accepted endpoint event lies in the declared two-level carrier space and that \(X,Y,Z\) are complete analyzer maps there. Leakage, loss, or a hidden third outcome cannot be renamed a qubit result.
