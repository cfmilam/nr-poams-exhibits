Bell at the Completion Boundary

What nature forbids when one angular-momentum account is mistaken for two locally prewritten instruction lists.

DEEP NATIVE RECAST / CONDITIONAL CCK RESULT / PHYSICAL SOURCE CLOSURE OPEN
The POAMS claim. A Bell trial is not a story about two tiny objects carrying properties through empty space. It is one source-bounded circulation offered two independently chosen terminal orientations and settled by two whole-sign registrations. The final object of study is the completed four-entry receipt: readiness, left orientation, right orientation and paired signs. Bell's inequality tells us that this receipt cannot be generated by two locally prewritten ledgers under the stated independence conditions. It does not tell us that particles, fields in a void or traveling messages exist.

1 · Begin with what occurred

Each accepted trial contains only operationally receipted events:

  1. a declared source boundary reports that a joint preparation is ready;
  2. two terminal orientations \(x\) and \(y\) are selected independently of that preparation;
  3. each terminal posts one whole registered sense, \(s,t\in\{-1,+1\}\);
  4. the two records are paired by the trial identifier and counted.

From repeated trials we form \(P(s,t\mid x,y)\) and \(E_{xy}=\sum_{s,t}stP(s,t\mid x,y)\). “Particle,” “wavefunction,” “collapse,” “electron” and “nucleus” are not observations in that receipt. They are interpretations imported by other frameworks. POAMS begins with the completed angular-momentum account and owes any deeper construction it adds.

Measured standing. Well-controlled Bell experiments report paired frequencies that exceed the applicable local-factorization bound. The settings, readiness rule, timing, losses, selections and raw sign records—not a preferred ontology—constitute the measurement custody.

2 · The locally prewritten ledger fails

Let \(\lambda\) denote every source-side fact completed before the freely selected terminal orientations. A locally prewritten account makes three commitments:

  1. Preparation independence: \(\rho(\lambda\mid x,y)=\rho(\lambda)\).
  2. Separate terminal settlement: \(P(s,t\mid x,y,\lambda)=P(s\mid x,\lambda)P(t\mid y,\lambda)\).
  3. Ordinary frequencies: all conditional weights are nonnegative and normalized.

Writing \(A_x(\lambda)=\sum_s sP(s\mid x,\lambda)\) and \(B_y(\lambda)=\sum_t tP(t\mid y,\lambda)\), each lies in \([-1,1]\). Therefore

\[S=E_{00}+E_{01}+E_{10}-E_{11}\]\[|A_0(B_0+B_1)+A_1(B_0-B_1)|\le2,\qquad |S|\le2.\]

This already permits noise, memory, arbitrarily many hidden addresses and a common source record. Calling \(\lambda\) a “co-signed transaction” changes nothing. If the two terminal signs were already locally readable facts, POAMS would fail against the Bell record.

3 · What Bell removes—and what it does not add

Removed

A complete, setting-independent source record followed by two conditionally separate terminal responses cannot reproduce the measured violation.

Not removed

Normal Realism, one substance, angular-momentum conservation and definite completed records survive. What fails is the extra assumption that every future comparison was separately written at preparation.

Not supplied

Bell does not provide the correct joint law, choose its ceiling, identify a carrier, or prove any microscopic picture. Those are separate debts.

4 · One account, not a message between places

POAMS treats the source and both terminal seats as one account whose settlement is defined only when the comparison closes. Nothing has to fly from one terminal to the other after an orientation is chosen. Nothing is pushed through a void. The endpoint distance belongs to the geometry of registration; it does not divide one conserved account into two ontologically independent things.

This language explains why a nonfactorizable joint receipt is admissible. It does not determine its frequencies. Co-signing alone is therefore necessary transaction grammar, not a Bell derivation.

Hard boundary. “The ledger is global” cannot be used as incense. A valid model must state the joint completion rule, recover terminal marginals, give the angular dependence, declare its ceiling and expose a way nature can reject it.

5 · The adopted completion calculus

POAMS presently closes the mathematical gap with the Coherent Completion Kernel (CCK), an openly adopted constitutive extension. In native terms:

  • CCK-S: unresolved completion alternatives retain relative phase in a finite complex account.
  • CCK-D: an isolated reversible reorientation preserves the comparison measure.
  • CCK-C: independently addressable terminal seats compose while permitting irreducibly relational joint organization.
  • CCK-M: mutually exclusive terminal settlements receive normalized additive frequencies.

This calculus was independently developed in conventional quantum theory. POAMS adopts its operational structure because it is economical and empirically successful; it does not adopt particles, fields in empty space, or a second substance. Adoption is not derivation. Nature retains the right to break any clause.

6 · The two-seat zero-account construction

Suppose a source boundary prepares exactly two fundamental half-turn response seats and the pair exhausts one zero-total-angular-momentum account. There is then one rotation-invariant joint completion class. In a convenient imported coordinate representation it is

\[|\Omega_0\rangle=\frac{|+ -\rangle-|- +\rangle}{\sqrt2}.\]

The symbols are account coordinates, not little objects with hidden orientations. Under CCK settlement, terminal orientations \(\mathbf a,\mathbf b\) and whole signs \(s,t=\pm1\) give

\[P(s,t\mid\mathbf a,\mathbf b)=\frac{1-st\,\mathbf a\!\cdot\!\mathbf b}{4},\qquad E(\mathbf a,\mathbf b)=-\mathbf a\!\cdot\!\mathbf b.\]

Each terminal alone remains balanced at \(1/2\); the completed comparison carries the angular relation. Standard coplanar orientations yield \(|S|=2\sqrt2\). No visibility is fitted. This is a conditional POAMS theorem: it follows from the declared two-seat zero account plus adopted CCK, not from generic co-signing or whole-turn discreteness alone.

7 · The source boundary is the physical debt

The formula above is earned only if the apparatus really prepares the declared account. Three shortcuts fail:

  • Balanced terminal averages do not prove that the pair is in the unique zero-account class.
  • Opposition along one orientation can be produced by a classical mixture that has no transverse relation.
  • Joint source custody does not prove two-seat exhaustion; an unobserved source, apparatus or environmental seat may retain part of the account.

The live POAMS construction debt is therefore physical, not typographical: identify a source boundary, show that exactly two calibrated response seats carry the completed circulation, count leakage and loss as outcomes, and exclude every residual angular-momentum or which-alternative record before looking at the held-out Bell score.

8 · A three-orientation receipt

For a declared two-seat response space, perform disjoint same-orientation calibration ensembles along three orthogonal terminal orientations. Let their registered sign correlations be \(C_{11},C_{22},C_{33}\). The zero-account fidelity is

\[F_0=\frac{1-C_{11}-C_{22}-C_{33}}{4}.\]

\(F_0=1\) certifies the unique two-seat rotation-invariant completion class within that declared response space. Values below one measure non-scalar contamination rather than inviting an ontological story. This receipt must be computed on calibration trials fixed before the separate Bell trials are opened.

The coordinate identity behind the receipt is the imported projector relation

\[\Pi_0=\frac{I-\Sigma_1\Sigma_1-\Sigma_2\Sigma_2-\Sigma_3\Sigma_3}{4}.\]

Here each \(\Sigma_k\Sigma_k\) means the paired sign comparison for orientation \(k\). The notation is mathematical scaffolding; the laboratory content is three orientation choices and paired whole-sign counts.

Event-ready diamond experiments provide a useful protocol comparator: source readiness was heralded, terminal choices were randomized, source quality was characterized separately and Bell trials were evaluated under frozen timing and selection rules. 10 · Contextuality and delayed choice, recast

Contextuality: a terminal sign is not licensed as a freestanding property possessed before every possible comparison. It is the whole-sign settlement of the comparison actually completed. This blocks a single context-independent instruction table, but it does not by itself choose the frequencies.

Delayed choice: a later apparatus choice determines which comparison can close. It does not rewrite an already completed past event. The mistake is assigning a finished history to an unresolved account and then imagining that nature must edit it later.

11 · Nature-first completion protocol

  1. Native intervention: vary each terminal orientation independently after the source-ready event.
  2. Raw observable: readiness flag, orientation pair, two whole signs, timestamps, leakage/loss category and trial identifier.
  3. Frozen source gate: use disjoint three-orientation trials to compute \(F_0\) before opening Bell data.

  5. Failure: unexplained leakage, source-setting dependence, unstable marginals, failure of the cosine law, or a receipted value beyond the CCK ceiling narrows or rejects the corresponding claim.
  6. Technological leverage: a controllable material source that repeatedly prepares and certifies the two-seat closure becomes a phase-linked completion resource; without such control this remains foundational metrology, not an engineered capability.
