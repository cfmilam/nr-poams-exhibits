1 · One triangle carries the whole industry

The radar papers open with an equation this corpus has met before — the same Pythagorean time relation that runs the Rate of Time and the 1986 cone. For any interaction read across a separation $s$, observational time $t_R$ and the interaction's own (proper) time $t_P$ share one triangle Pope, FTR Eq. (1):

$$\begin{aligned}t_R&=\left[\left(\frac{s}{c}\right)^2+t_P^2\right]^{1/2}\\t_P=0&\Longrightarrow t_R=\frac{s}{c}.\end{aligned}$$

Matter budgets its turning between the internal count and the motion channel — every clock in the sibling exhibit lives inside this triangle. A quantum of light-interaction is the limiting case: the record shows its proper time is zero — the whole budget in the motion channel. So one interaction carries two readings at once, with no contradiction: by its own books it is instantaneous ($s/t_P = \infty$ — a "double-ended, consummate event"), and by every observer's books it is delayed ($s/t_R = c$). The extant establishment keeps only the second reading and then puzzles over the first when experiments force it ("spooky action-at-a-distance"). The record keeps both, because they are two legs of one triangle FTR 1–2, précis.

Everything a telecommunications engineer measures is the $t_R$ leg: every latency, every ping, every echo is $s/c$ — the projection of linked transactions into observational time see Electricity §2. Nothing in this page disturbs a single measured timing. What changes is what the equipment is doing — and, at the end, what the record says might be done that the industry has never tried.

One triangle, the whole story: matter (left of the limit) splits observational time between internal count $t_P$ and the motion channel $s/c$; the light-quantum sits at the limit — $t_P = 0$, the triangle collapsed onto its motion leg. 2 · Radar, read natively

The conventional conception, in the record's summary: a pulse of energy travels to the target at $c$, reflects, and travels back — range equals delay times $c$ over two. POAMS keeps every digit of the arithmetic and dissolves the traveler: the out-leg and the return are two linked hand-offs read in sequence, each a double-ended event, each priced at $s/c$ of observational time. The radar operator's clock accumulates $2s/c$ not because something flew but because that is what reading two linked transactions across a separation $s$ is FTR §1. In the second paper the point is made as a twins case: the echo is the traveling twin whose own time is zero; the operator is the stay-at-home whose books read $2s/c$ FTR-2.

3 · The billet rule — and the half-wave benchmark

The record's central condition is prophetic: an irreducible quantum cannot set out at all unless a "billet" is assured for it — a resonant absorber whose distance and disposition fit the interaction "in terms of numbers of half-lengths $\lambda/2$" FTR §1; 2005 · source hypothesis. The source text writes $\lambda=h/E$, but $h/E$ has units of time. The dimensionally complete rate-to-length conversion is:

$$\begin{aligned}E&=h\nu,\qquad \lambda=\frac{c}{\nu}=\frac{hc}{E},\\\frac{\lambda}{2}&=\frac{c}{2\nu}.\end{aligned}$$

The two-slit record is compatible with an apparatus relation in which some source-screen pairings complete and others do not. It does not, by itself, establish that remote receiver readiness modulates source emission before an observer-time interval $s/c$; that stronger teleblocking claim remains open.

Half-wave resonance is a useful free-space benchmark, not a universal hardware dimension. Practical antennas can be shortened, loaded, folded, arrayed, or shifted by end effects and their dielectric surroundings; fibres are guided media, not half-wave dipoles. 4 · The record's own proposal — retrograde signalling, with the record's own limits

Here the papers go where no textbook goes. The proposal is prophetic and unverified; it must be reported with the limits the record itself attaches and with a gate capable of killing it.

The Gedanken FTR 1–2, précis. Let a transmitter and receiver be ideally resonant in a narrow band which — this is the idealization — they alone in the universe occupy. Since no quantum can leave without an assured billet, the far operator, by merely switching the receiver's readiness on and off, gates the transmitter's ability to emit at the source. Key the readiness in Morse patterns and information flows from receiver to transmitter — opposite to the flow of energy. The record names it retrograde (or backwards) signalling, and prices it on the triangle: a conventional radar reading costs $t_R = 2s/c$; the retrograde reading, being the interaction's own consummation condition, is priced at $t_P = 0$ — whence the title Faster than Radar. The papers close the vision with a telescope: a transmitter tuned to a spectral rate of one element, swept across the sky, would read the disposition of that element's census as it stands, not as it stood millennia of $s/c$ ago.

The hose figure précis: signal down a flowing hose by injecting dye at the tap, and the message arrives with the flow, in due time. But clamp the far end of a full, incompressible hose and the bulb at the tap swells at once — the full pipe is the retrograde channel. The reader of the electricity exhibit has met this pipe before: it is the dead circuit, run as a signalling instrument.

The record's own limits — stated by the record, kept by this page. (i) Naive interference-signalling schemes fail: any scheme where an observer O modulates a pattern that observer P must read is priced at $s/c$ like all reading — the papers' words: such an approach "achieves nothing." (ii) For teleblocking to register at the source, every alternative sink must be eliminated — the source sealed in a "cosmical vacuum flask." The record's own image: if the sun were the only object in the universe, it would not be able to shine. Energy with somewhere else to go simply goes there; only total sink-exclusivity turns far-end readiness into a source-side reading. (iii) Real conduits leak: a fibre or waveguide under end-blocking is "less like an iron pipe and more like a loose-knit fisherman's sock" — the excluded quanta squirt into the surrounding census. Pope's own fibre trials with Dr. John Hopton (1970s, Burton-on-Trent) were inconclusive on exactly this containment condition Refraction out-take, fn. 3. The apparatus anchor that survives all three limits is narrower: an absorbing detector changes the available two-slit relation and the conditioned pattern. That is compatible with endpoint-sensitive bookkeeping, but it is not direct evidence that far-end readiness suppresses source emission. Making teleblocking a channel remains an unsolved sink-engineering and measurement problem, and the record says so.

Teleblocking qualification gate

Define a calibrated source-side observable $S$ (accepted electrical input, emitted-event proxy, or both), randomize and blind far-end states READY/BLOCKED, and preregister the contrast

$$\Delta S(\tau)=\overline{S}_{\rm READY}(\tau)-\overline{S}_{\rm BLOCKED}(\tau).$$

Record trial count, confidence interval, instrument floor, clock synchronization, distance, bandwidth, temperature, and every known alternative sink. Run local-switch, detuned-receiver, matched-dummy, leakage-open, and delayed-coupling controls. A qualifying retrograde result requires a repeatable far-state contrast in a preregistered pre-$s/c$ timing bin, while local feed-through, heating, vibration, and ordinary delayed response remain below threshold. A null places a finite upper bound on teleblocking under the tested sink census; it does not become “exact zero.” ours · operational gate

So the honest status, in the corpus's terms: source-side power engineering is mature, while sink readiness and census-exclusivity remain an open POAMS design space. The record's proposal stands not as an achieved device but as a named, bounded, falsifiable hypothesis.

5 · Refraction as rate — the fibre ledger

The record's fibre paper asks why a link is read more slowly in glass without positing a traveler that itself slows. It proposes that a medium's internal activity re-prices the relay. This is a source interpretation, not a derivation of a material index from constituent data. The paper contrasts copper and glass, but operational signal velocity in either case depends on the entire guide and dielectric geometry; it is not generically “essentially $c$” in copper. What is directly defined is the time ratio Refraction in Optic Fibres · source hypothesis:

$$\mu \;=\; \frac{\sin i}{\sin r} \;=\; \frac{c}{c_1} \;=\; \frac{t_{R1}}{t_R}$$

— the refractive index is a ratio of observational times: dilation bookkeeping, not a braking force. The conduction itself is the census mechanism of the 2005 hand-off: a "relaying sequence" — a bound constituent takes the quantum up a level and reverts, handing it on through the medium — sequential resonant transactions through a census, each priced by the projection. The measured anchor the record cites is Fizeau's flowing-water experiment (1851): flow changes the observed rate only partially. For comparison, the imported velocity-composition relation gives:

$$\begin{aligned}c_1(v)&=\frac{c/n\pm v}{1\pm v/(nc)},\\c_1(v)&\approx\frac{c}{n}\pm v\left(1-\frac{1}{n^2}\right).\end{aligned}$$

The flow is honored to first order by the fraction $1-1/n^{2}$. Calling this the census share is the POAMS interpretation, not an independent derivation of the coefficient. Its limits remain informative: as $n \to 1$ (no census engaged) the flow does nothing; as $n$ grows (the reading dominated by the census) the drag approaches full freight. For water, $1 - 1/1.333^{2} = 0.437$: Fizeau's 7 m/s flow re-prices a $2.25\times10^{8}$ m/s conduction rate by 3.06 m/s — parts in $10^{8}$, read as fringe shifts. Measured, three generations deep: Fizeau 1851; Michelson–Morley 1886, $0.434 \pm 0.02$ against the predicted $0.437$; Zeeman 1914–22, down to the dispersion correction measured. The extant books name the coefficient Fresnel drag and derive it as velocity addition; the record's point is what the number says: the medium re-prices the reading exactly to the extent the medium's own books participate — rate bookkeeping, not a wind blowing photons. The working numbers an optical engineer uses daily measured: group index 1.468 at 1550 nm — 4.90 µs/km against vacuum's 3.34. Same digits, no photon crawling through glass: a census-laden projection.

The out-take ends on an honest seam: a circuit conducts only when closed, yet a fibre shines happily into an open end — "the reason for this particular difference is not clear." The corpus's later record suggests a candidate completion: the one-sink / many-sink boundary. The wire's transaction is bound to a single linked terminal — open it and all sinks are blocked (the dead circuit). Light's sinks are a distributed census — an open fibre end faces the sky's absorbers, the sprinkler that reroutes around any plugged hole 2005; see Antenna Emission · Electricity §3 — editorial synthesis of record pieces.
