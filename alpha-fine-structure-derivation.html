1. Generalize the measured coefficient before asking what selects it

$$v=\lambda c,\qquad r=\frac{\hbar}{\mu c\lambda}=\frac{\ell_\mu}{\lambda},\qquad \omega=\frac vr=\frac{\lambda c}{r}=g.$$
