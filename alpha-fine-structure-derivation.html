3. Second level: gravitation is orbital time dilation

$$v^2=\frac{GM}{r}.$$

Substituting into the same time formula yields the orbital-velocity dilation directly:

$$t_R=\frac{t}{\sqrt{1-GM/(c^2 r)}}\qquad\text{(velocity part - Osborne eq. 8.9).}$$
