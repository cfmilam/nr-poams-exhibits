The Alpha Problem

An open front in Normal Realism / POAMS
MEASURED INPUT · DERIVATION OPEN

This page replaces the retired exhibit Spectra, Gravitation, and Fine Structure in Normal Realism. The replacement was ratified after a Tier-A cold review on 7 September 2026. The failed program remains available as an auditable research record.

The short answer

POAMS does not presently derive the fine-structure ratio. The measured ratio fixes the scale of the small spectral corrections, but neither its numerical value nor the sign-sensitive mechanism that splits one level into distinct branches has been obtained from the ontology without importing the answer.

This is not a cosmetic regrade. The former exhibit made several claims that did not survive its own mathematics. It has therefore been retired from the active exhibit sequence. The current scientific position is simple: alpha is presently an imported measured input, and deriving it remains an open problem.

What failed, plainly

  1. A scalar time-dilation factor cannot split a line. It is even in the circulation rate: reversing the sense gives the same scalar shift. The old drawing inserted the two opposite branches by hand.
  2. $L=\hbar$ fixes a product, not a unique orbit. It constrains the rate–radius combination; it does not independently produce both the orbital scale and the rate ratio. The old calculation imported the measured ratio and then presented the result as geometry.
  3. The proposed winding count is not an integer. The inferred $N=2/\alpha^2$ is approximately $37{,}557.73$. Forcing the nearest whole count moves the answer far outside the measurement uncertainty. The whole-turn interpretation is falsified.
  4. The spin-half step was circular. The factor one-half emerged because the chosen spin radius already contained one-half.
  5. The collective bootstrap did not prove contingency. Tree-like connectivity was assumed, not forced, and the numerical verifier's normalization installed the marginal eigenvalue it later reported. The valid conclusion is narrower: the tested model class did not determine alpha.
  6. The proposed running law was never quantitatively confronted. It remains a research idea, not a conditional theorem.

Narrower framing added 2026-09-12. Sharpened framing added 2026-09-12 (Part 2 — ratified). The “factor of ~10” is not approximate; it is exactly $1/\sqrt{\alpha}$. Two identifications make this precise:

  1. The POAMS-native “$\sim 10^{22}$” that clean Planck/Compton combinations land at is exactly $M_{Pl}/m$ (up to $O(1)$ factors) for an elementary-charge closure of mass $m$. For the smallest observed extant elementary-charge closure ($\dot{N}_M \approx 1.24 \times 10^{20}$ Hz), $M_{Pl}/m = 2.389 \times 10^{22}$. The gravitational fine-structure ratio $\alpha_G(m) = Gm^2/(\hbar c)$ satisfies $\alpha_G(m) = (m/M_{Pl})^2$ by definition, so all clean mass/length combinations built from Planck and Compton scales for a given closure reduce to $M_{Pl}/m$ up to $O(1)$ factors.
  2. For two elementary-charge closures of mass $m$, the empirical ratio $\dot{N}_Q/\dot{N}_M$ equals exactly $\sqrt{\alpha/\alpha_G(m)} = \sqrt{F_{EM}/F_{grav}}$, following from $F_{EM}/F_{grav} = e^2/(4\pi\epsilon_0 G m^2) = \alpha/\alpha_G(m)$ and the POAMS reading $F \propto \dot N^2$ (per Coulomb and Newton). The specific value $2.04 \times 10^{21}$ corresponds to the smallest observed extant elementary-charge closure ($\dot{N}_M \approx 1.24 \times 10^{20}$ Hz).

Combining these, the empirical target admits the exact identity

$$\dot{N}_Q / \dot{N}_M \;=\; \sqrt{\alpha}\ \cdot\ M_{Pl}/m_e,$$

and the “residual factor” the exhibit named is exactly $1/\sqrt{\alpha} = \sqrt{137.036} \approx 11.706$. This is not a coincidence to be dismissed; it is the sharpened statement of what remains to be derived. Restated in the sharpened form:

The scale open, precisely stated. POAMS needs to supply a native expression for $\dot{N}_Q / \dot{N}_M$ as a function of an elementary-charge closure's geometry alone — without using $\alpha$, $1/\alpha$, or any measured EM quantity as input. Given such an expression, equating it to $\sqrt{\alpha} \cdot M_{Pl}/m$ determines $\alpha$.

Equivalently: since all POAMS-native mass/length combinations for an elementary-charge closure reduce to $M_{Pl}/m$ up to $O(1)$ factors, and the empirical $\dot{N}_Q/\dot{N}_M$ requires an additional multiplicative $\sqrt{\alpha}$, the remaining task is to derive that $\sqrt{\alpha}$ factor from POAMS-native structure. It is exactly one dimensionless number, and it is exactly $\sqrt{\alpha} \approx 0.08542$.

The three candidates named earlier (coherence-fraction between Planck and Compton scales; Călugăreanu integer count of a specific closure configuration; excess degrees of freedom at the chirality-directed sink) are the concrete research targets. The scale open, restated once more. Every elementary-charge closure carries chirality-directed sink activity at the universal rate $\dot{N}_Q = \sqrt\alpha \cdot f_{Pl}$. Sharpened framing added 2026-09-12 (Part 4 — ratified). Parallel sharpening on the gravitational side.

The same algebraic move applied to $\dot{N}_M$ itself gives a companion identity:

$$\sqrt{\alpha_G(m)} \;=\; \dot{N}_M(m)\, /\, f_{Pl}, \qquad \alpha_G(m) \equiv Gm^2/(\hbar c).$$

Combining with Part 3, both sink rates are fractions of Planck frequency, one universal and one per-closure:

  • Chirality-directed sink rate: $\dot{N}_Q = \sqrt\alpha \cdot f_{Pl}$ — universal, same for every elementary-charge closure.
  • Mass-directed sink rate: $\dot{N}_M(m) = \sqrt{\alpha_G(m)} \cdot f_{Pl}$ — per-closure, varies with $m$. For the smallest observed extant elementary-charge closure, $\sqrt{\alpha_G} \approx 4.19 \times 10^{-23}$.

Why $\alpha_G$ is harder to derive than $\alpha$. POAMS's ratified foundational dimensional constants are $\hbar$ and $c$ only. From these alone, no length or mass can be constructed. Deriving $\sqrt{\alpha_G(m)}$ as a specific value therefore requires POAMS to supply either:

  1. A specific mass as a POAMS-native quantity (derive an elementary closure mass from closure structure), or
  2. A specific length as a POAMS-native quantity (derive $l_{Pl}$ from closure structure), or
  3. The specific dimensionless ratio $\sqrt{\alpha_G(m)}$ itself for a chosen input mass $m$.

Paths (1) and (2) are the mass/hierarchy problem — genuinely hard, no path currently visible in POAMS or elsewhere. Path (3) closes Newton's $G$ derivation given one elementary closure mass as input: $G = \hbar c \cdot \alpha_G(m) / m^2$.

Sharpened OPEN for $\alpha_G$. POAMS needs a POAMS-native expression for $\dot{N}_M(m)/f_{Pl} = \sqrt{\alpha_G(m)}$. This is a dimensionless number (like $\sqrt\alpha$), but its value is per-closure (unlike $\sqrt\alpha$). Any POAMS derivation must produce that specific number for a given elementary closure without treating its mass as a free parameter — strictly harder than deriving $\sqrt\alpha$, because it also requires POAMS to derive the closure mass (or an equivalent length scale).

Honest verdict. Task 1's parallel sharpening lands cleanly: both $\alpha$ and $\alpha_G$ have the form $\sqrt{X} = \dot{N}/f_{Pl}$, one universal ($\sqrt\alpha \approx 0.0854$) and one per-closure ($\sqrt{\alpha_G} \approx 4.19 \times 10^{-23}$ for the smallest observed). But the OPEN for $\alpha_G$ is dimensionally under-constrained by POAMS's current foundational set ($\hbar$, $c$ only), whereas the OPEN for $\alpha$ is dimensionally well-posed within POAMS closure structure. The parallel sharpening is done; the underlying research problem for $\alpha_G$ is strictly harder than for $\alpha$.

Sharpened framing added 2026-09-12 (Part 5 — ratified). Candidate derivation paths for $\alpha_G$, evaluated.

Part 4 identified $\sqrt{\alpha_G(m)} = m/M_{Pl}$ as dimensionally under-constrained by POAMS's current foundational set. Closing the derivation requires adding structural content not currently ratified. Four concrete candidate paths, evaluated for what actually closes when the algebra is pushed through:

Path A — Ratify nexus mean-free-path $\lambda = l_{Pl}$ as a POAMS foundation. Newton §4 currently treats this as a modeling choice. Ratifying it would add one dimensional constant to POAMS's foundational set. Verdict: circular. $l_{Pl}$ is defined as $\sqrt{\hbar G/c^3}$. Under Path A, $G = c^3 l_{Pl}^2/\hbar$ falls out algebraically — but only because ratifying $l_{Pl}$ at its specific value $1.616 \times 10^{-35}$ m is equivalent to declaring $G$ by fiat. To break the circularity, POAMS would need an independent argument for the specific numerical value of $l_{Pl}$ (i.e., an argument that doesn't reference $G$). No such argument is currently visible in the corpus. Path A doesn't work as a first-principles derivation; it converts “G is measured” into “$l_{Pl}$ is measured” without adding physical content.

Path B — Derive closure characteristic masses from Aufbau-style POAMS closure counting. POAMS has Aufbau closure-counting machinery for atomic shells. Extending this to derive the mass hierarchy of elementary closures — including the smallest observed extant elementary-charge closure — would produce specific closure masses from POAMS closure structure. Verdict: aspirational but well-shaped as a research program. Aligns with POAMS's existing derivation program (Aufbau, valley of stability, mass ledger). But even a fully-developed Path B produces only dimensionless mass ratios among closures; converting them to Planck units requires an absolute-scale anchor, which brings back the Path A problem. Substantial multi-year research required.

Path C — Empirical calibration of one closure mass. Newton §4 Path 3. Accept the smallest observed extant elementary-charge closure's mass (equivalently, its mass-directed sink rate $\dot{N}_M \approx 1.24 \times 10^{20}$ Hz) as one POAMS-native measured input, alongside $\hbar$ and $c$. Verdict: this is the honest closable path today. Under Path C, POAMS's foundational set becomes $\{\hbar, c, \dot{N}_M^{\text{ref}}\}$ (or equivalently $\{\hbar, c, m_e\}$). $G$ falls out from $G = \hbar c \alpha_G(m^{\text{ref}})/(m^{\text{ref}})^2$ modulo one calibration. POAMS then predicts $\alpha_G$ for all other closures given their masses. What survives as CLAIMED: POAMS derives the structure of $\alpha_G$ (its scaling with $m^2$, its interpretation as $\sqrt{\alpha_G} = \dot{N}_M/f_{Pl}$) and predicts every downstream gravitational-coupling ratio; POAMS does not claim to derive $G$ or $M_{Pl}$ from ontology alone. This is more honest than the retracted “$G$ is derived” language and aligns with the standard theory-of-measurement practice.

Path D — Novel POAMS insight (currently no path visible). Derive a POAMS-intrinsic length or mass from closure geometry via reasoning not currently in the corpus. Would be genuinely first-principles. Requires substantive theoretical work; no candidate mechanism currently identifiable. Not scoped here.

The residual structural finding. Even the best-case path (Path C) does not derive $G$ from POAMS foundations — it accepts one dimensional POAMS input from measurement. The result is honest and operationally useful, but the ambition “POAMS derives $G$ from ontology alone” is not achievable without either Path A (which requires an independent argument for $l_{Pl}$'s specific value — not currently available) or Path D (novel insight — also not available). This is a genuine structural blocker, not a matter of finding a cleverer calculation.

What would count as a solution

A future derivation must earn the number before seeing it. It must begin with circulating angular momentum keeping its own books and produce a sign-sensitive splitting law plus its scale without inserting $\alpha$, $1/\alpha$, a measured orbital rate, or an adjustable normalization.

  1. Derive the operator or closure rule that distinguishes the two phase senses. (Update 2026-09-12: now available via Călugăreanu $Lk = \pm 1$ per Landé exhibit.)
  2. Compute its dimensionless scale from native closure geometry, with no target value in the derivation.
  3. Preregister held-out spectral predictions and run them against the record only after the calculation is frozen.
  4. Explain why the measured effective ratio changes with resolution, or state the domain in which the derived value applies.

Until those gates pass, the value remains an input. A rigorous null is preferable to another attractive formula that lands near 137.
