4 · The redshift: what was measured, and the candidate readings

In 1929 Hubble, on Slipher’s spectra, published a proportionality between distance and a shift of galactic spectral lines toward the red:

$$z \;=\; \frac{\lambda_{\mathrm{obs}} - \lambda_{\mathrm{prop}}}{\lambda_{\mathrm{prop}}}, \qquad z \propto r \quad \text{(the observed law)} \tag{9.1}$$

That is the entire observation: a law of redshifts. Hubble himself preferred exactly that phrase — “Hubble preferred the noncommittal term redshift and referred to his and his colleague Humason’s results as the ‘law of redshifts’ ” (Mayall, Encyclopædia Britannica, 1961) extant record. Recession is an interpretation laid on top, and the logic of the lay-on is a textbook fallacy: “The fact that all speeds of recession cause redshifts does not imply that in observing redshifts we are observing speeds of recession” (Ch. 9 §9.2). All crows are black; this black thing need not be a crow.

Two properties of the observed shift are load-bearing for everything below. It is achromatic — every band shifts by the same factor, none broaden. And it is rate-real — distant clocks genuinely run slow in our books: supernova light-curves stretch by exactly $(1+z)$ extant record, and the no-dilation reading is dead on the Tolman data at >10σ (§9). Whatever carries the redshift books rates, not just energies.

5 · The audit: a mechanism was staked, and the sky answered

The synthesis’s Ch. 9 hypothesis was concrete: deep matter has large random speeds (being cumulatively nearer “the greatest (ubiquitous) accumulation of cosmic mass”), and pairwise relativistic dilation of those random motions reddens deep light in proportion to distance. It is a beautiful mechanism — achromatic, rate-real, direction-blind — and it was published as a live alternative, not a dogma: “our figures are included only to support our logical arguments.” This page put it to the only court that matters. Three steps, all bookkeeping.

5.0 The scatter theorem — the kill that needs no thermometer

Before any microwave data, the mechanism fails on the shape of the redshift law itself. Write the booked shift for one galaxy moving randomly at speed $\beta$, angle $\theta$ to the sightline: $1+z = \gamma\,(1+\beta\cos\theta)$. Average over random directions and the first-order Doppler term cancels — the ensemble mean is the dilation shift $\gamma - 1 \approx \beta^2/2$, exactly as Ch. 9 §9.4 proposed. But the spread around that mean does not cancel: it is the first-order term itself, $\pm\beta$, both signs. The scatter-to-signal ratio is $2/\beta$ — greater than 2 at every depth, for any speed. A random-motion sky wears its velocities twice: once as a small second-order mean, once as a large first-order spread — and nearly half of all galaxies (every $\cos\theta < -\beta/2$) would arrive blueshifted. The observed sky: a redshift–distance law tight to a few percent, and essentially no blueshifts beyond the Local Group extant record, from Hubble’s own tables onward. Isotropy blocks the only exit — random orbits about a distributed barycentre still put $\langle v_r^2\rangle = v^2/3$ along every sightline; no arrangement makes everyone’s motion transverse to everyone’s view. The book’s “uniformly and without dispersion” answered band-broadening within one spectrum; the object-to-object scatter was never confronted, and it is fatal on its own. (The magnitude compounds it: the law extended to z ≈ 0.9 demands random speeds near $0.85c$, while every measured motion at those depths — line widths, kinematic SZ, the cold local flow — sits three to five orders of magnitude below.)

The strongest defence, pre-empted. “But time-dilation is direction-independent — the twins book the same slowing outbound and inbound — so shouldn’t all relative motion redden?” The direction-blindness is real and it belongs to $\gamma$ alone: the clock-rate booking, what a reunion compares. A spectrum is not a reunion. The observed shift is one booking with two factors — $1+z = \gamma(1+\beta\cos\theta)$ — and the first factor never erases the second: successive hand-offs from a closing separation book tighter arrival ordering (ledger geometry, no photon required), and that term carries a sign. The arithmetic is decisive: for head-on approach, $\gamma(1-\beta) = \sqrt{(1-\beta)/(1+\beta)} < 1$ at every speed — second order never beats first order; an approaching source is net blue at $\beta = 0.001$ and at $\beta = 0.99$ alike. The sky agrees wherever the test is available: Andromeda arrives blueshifted ($-300$ km/s); M86 in Virgo arrives blueshifted ($-244$ km/s) — blueshifts living exactly where virial speeds rival the still-small mean law, and vanishing at depth because peculiar speeds stay at a few hundred km/s while the law grows — the precise opposite of the $v \propto \sqrt{r}$ requirement; spectroscopic binaries swing their lines blue and red nightly in every observatory; and our own motion prints the floor’s dipole hotter ahead extant record throughout. So the mechanism’s mean was never the problem: averaged over random directions the first-order term cancels and the mean is the direction-blind $\gamma - 1$ — which is exactly why the class survived into its successor (§6). The sky, however, is measured per object, and each object wears its own $\pm\beta\cos\theta$ on top of the shared factor. A red mean cannot make every object red. Only shared accumulation can.

5.2 Our own floor overdetermines the bath at every intermediate seat

Here is the theorem that decides it, and it uses only this corpus’s own rules. We measure, at our seat, an isotropic Planck floor at $T_0 = 2.72548$ K extant record (FIRAS). Our own booking law (§4) says any exchange completed between a seat at shift $(1+z_c)$ and us books down by $(1+z_c)$, achromatically. A Planck shape survives such a booking with $T \to T/(1+z_c)$. Therefore: for the floor we receive to be Planck at $T_0$, the same exchange field at any intermediate seat — the bath a cloud at $z_c$ actually sits in and re-books toward us — must be Planck at $T_0(1+z_c)$.

Now hold the two claims side by side. The uniform-floor reading (an eternal static equilibrium, the same 2.725 K bath at every seat) says the Muller cloud sits in 2.725 K. Transport consistency of our own measured floor says it sits in 5.14 K. The cloud was asked. It answered 5.08 ± 0.10. The uniform static floor is not merely disfavoured by data — it is internally inconsistent with the corpus’s own booking law plus the floor we measure at home. (The Sunyaev–Zel’dovich version of the same statement: a distortion imprinted on a 2.725 K bath at the cluster would arrive with its null at $217/(1+z_c)$ GHz; the observed null stays at ~217 GHz across the cluster ladder. Same theorem, read spectrally.)

5.3 Composition — and the fall of the pairwise carrier

Generalise. Radiation reaching us from deep seat W, re-booked at intermediate seat C (a scattering gas or absorbing cloud genuinely completes hand-offs at C: absorb, re-emit — its books are real entries): consistency of one shared field across the chain requires

$$ (1+z_{\,\mathrm{us}\to W}) \;=\; (1+z_{\,\mathrm{us}\to C})\,(1+z_{\,C\to W}) \tag{T1}$$

— bookings must compose along completed hand-offs.

Pairwise velocity dilation does not compose: $\gamma(v_{AC}) \neq \gamma(v_{AB})\,\gamma(v_{BC})$ — Lorentz factors of relative random motions do not multiply along chains (they agree only to first order; the floor involves bookings far beyond first order). Therefore random-motion time dilation cannot carry the redshift of a sky that shares a thermal floor. The staked mechanism falls — not to rival dogma, but to the measured floor plus the ladder plus this corpus’s own insistence that transport is bookkeeping between real seats.

And one more turn of the screw — the static no-go. Suppose some other carrier that does compose (T1). Run §5.2 at every seat: each seat measures its own isotropic Planck bath, and composition forces the bath temperature to rise monotonically with depth along every sightline from every seat. A scalar field on a static configuration cannot decrease away from every point — nothing can be everyone’s minimum. Only two structures survive: (i) a preferred seat — we are the coldest point of the cosmos — anti-Copernican, anti-Normal-Realist, dead on arrival; or (ii) a shared monotone parameter along all worldlines, with every seat’s bath varying together — in which case $T = T_0(1+z)$ is its exact signature. Achromatic booking + isotropic Planck floors at every seat + seat-equivalence + static: pick three. The sky picked. The ladder is the measurement of the shared parameter.

6 · What the sky demands: evolution yes, singularity no

Take stock of what §5 actually forced, because it is not the Big Bang and it is not the static polyverse either.

  • The booking law that carries the cosmological redshift must be achromatic, rate-type (dilation-real — the stretch receipts stand), and composing across completed hand-offs (T1). Its observed local strength is the Hubble constant.
  • The radiation floor is shared, and its temperature varies along a monotone parameter common to all worldlines: $T = T_0(1+z)$, measured to β = 0.010 ± 0.013.
  • No seat is preferred. So the parameter is not a place — it is an accumulation: the books of every seat growing together, mutually calibrated by exchange.

In one sentence: the sky demands a shared evolution of the ambient level; the ledger forbids that evolution to have a first entry. Evolution yes, singularity no. And the corpus is not empty-handed before that demand — it already owns the evolution equation. Ch. 9 §9.3 derives the Friedmann equation from angular-momentum conservation alone, no General Relativity anywhere in it:

$$\left(\frac{dR}{dt}\right)^{2} \;=\; \frac{C}{R} \;+\; \frac{E}{K_T} \qquad \text{(Pope–Osborne, from the ledger alone)} \tag{9.9}$$

Pope & Osborne derived it, warranted it for the present epoch, and refused exactly one thing about it: the extrapolation to $R = 0$. That refusal stands in full (§2, §8). What does not survive this page’s audit is the further step the synthesis took in §9.4 — refusing the evolution itself and seeking a static carrier. The static carrier is gone (§5.3). The honest position the audit leaves is: the AM-ledger’s own Friedmann bookkeeping, running without a zero — accumulation with no first entry, a cosmos that is eternal and evolving, the no-zero floor doing at cosmic scale exactly what it does at every other scale in this corpus: forbidding the point, permitting the process.

Does a shared accumulation violate Normal Realism? It violates neither refusal of §1. No universe-object is required: “the books of every seat grow” is a statement about seats and exchanges, not about a container with a size. No God’s-eye instant is required: seats calibrate orderings pairwise, by exchange — the same way GPS clocks share a timescale with no master clock. A shared ordering built relationally is a logos-fact, exactly the kind of Whole Pope said the polyverse is. What Normal Realism refused was the master slice and the wind-back to a point — both still refused. And it can be made rigorous: composition (T1) is exactly the integrability condition for a global potential on the exchange graph — fix any reference entry, define $s$ of every entry as its booking to that reference, and path-independence does the rest. The shared ordering is constructed from pairwise reconciliation. A theorem now, not a conjecture.

And the carrier itself falls out — the last one standing. Enumerate the achromatic rate-booking classes: pairwise velocity dilation (dead, T1); a static spatial rate field (dead, §5.3); per-path accrual in a static cosmos (dead, same no-go). One class survives: booking by the ratio of the shared accumulation between the two entries of a single completed exchange. Identify the potential with the collective configuration scale the ledger already owns — the $R$ of equation (9.9) — and:

$$1+z \;=\; \frac{R(\mathrm{receipt\ entry})}{R(\mathrm{emission\ entry})} \qquad \text{(the carrier: the growth of the books between the two entries)} \tag{T2}$$

Nothing travels and nothing ages en route — the booking is a property of the relation between two ledger entries, completed at the hand-off (the photon stays outside, §4). It composes by construction (ratios), it is achromatic and rate-type, and its present strength is $H_0$ — the current fractional rate of the books. It passes the touchstone at every step: the circulation named is the collective one whose conservation generated (9.9); the booking is bookkeeping, not force; orbit and spin stay distinct; nothing was fitted.

Optics follow, and with them the isomorphism. Exchange count is conserved (nothing is lost to a void — fn. 26); receivers ride natural orbits whose separations scale with $R$ (comoving is definitional — $R$ is the collection’s configuration scale); each completed exchange books twice in rate. Those three facts give $D_L = (1+z)\,D_M$ and $D_A = D_M/(1+z)$ — Etherington duality $D_L = (1+z)^2 D_A$, which is separately measured to hold extant record. The background observables of the ledger cosmology are therefore point-for-point isomorphic to the extant expansion’s. Where the two ontologies can still differ observationally: whatever survives passage through the floor (§8) — the census fork having since closed against discrimination (§9.2) — and local dynamics (the varying-G programme). Everything else — Tolman, angular sizes, light curves — is now common ground by theorem (§9).

“Why not hot and cool patches?” — the objection, honoured and then bounded. Intuition rightly resists a cosmos-wide cooling: why not warmer and cooler regions — entropic and negentropic areas — rather than one shared law? The answer honours the intuition first: patches exist and are measured — the $10^{-5}$ anisotropies are literally warmer and cooler areas of the floor, every cluster prints an SZ hot spot on it, and local negentropy is real (every star is a negentropic engine; the whole galactic energy budget is feedback-organised structure). The question is only amplitude — and four independent locks bound the patchwork at parts in $10^5$: (i) the ladder has no cold rungs — a patchy floor would scatter absorber baths both ways, some below 2.725 K; measured, every rung in every direction sits above $T_0$, ordered by z (§5.1); (ii) the lock — each absorber’s bath excess equals its rate booking exactly, $T/T_0 = 1+z$: geography has no reason to track the booking; one parameter read twice does; (iii) FIRAS — sightlines threading order-unity patches would arrive as superposed different-temperature Plancks, a y-distorted mess; the measured floor is Planck to $\mu, y \lesssim 10^{-5}$; (iv) the seat test — patches arranged to run monotonically hotter with depth in every direction from us is a temperature field centred on our chair: the privileged seat, refused (§5.3). And the falling level itself is not a second postulate and not a broadcast: $T \propto 1/R$ is the carrier (T2) applied to the thermal census — each seat’s bath falls as its own books grow, and the seats agree by pairwise reconciliation (§6), universal-as-reconciled, never universal-as-decreed. Refusing the falling level means refusing the growth; the growth is what the ladder measures. One fact, two readings: z is the growth read spectrally, the level is the growth read thermally — which is why (ii) can never come apart. Local negentropy rides on the floor without restocking it — the galactic budget is feedback-coupled; the floor is the one uncoupled term (§7 notes). Stars decorate the reservoir; they cannot move it.
Plain words: what is falling, why, where it stops, and why it is not expansion. First, what isn’t: no atom is cooling. No star, no bound system — every closed book keeps its own internal turning at full rate forever; the thermal account holds only the loose change between the closed books, the ambient bath of hand-offs criss-crossing the unbound deep, and its “temperature” is nothing but how often those engagements arrive. Why the level falls: picture an unlimited crowd — no edge, no outside — where the spacings between unbound neighbours grow. Nobody leaves, nothing is lost; bumps per minute fall for everyone, everywhere. Not heat escaping — rate diluting: the floor era wrote the densest entry the ledger ever took, and every seat since reads that one entry across ever more accumulated books. Why growth at all: the wall is not a restful place — the floor is maximum constraint, the skater’s arms at their tightest — and what we inhabit is the unwinding; you do not ask a pendulum mid-swing why it is moving. Where it stops: nowhere, and at no event — no final entry, for the same reason there is no first one. The ambient level thins without limit; persistence is untouched, because persistence was never carried by the loose change. Why this is not expansion: unlimited extent stays unlimited — infinity does not get bigger, no edge advances, no “size of everything” exists to increase; what grows is each pair’s mutual booking, relations reconciled seat to seat. And so the extant narrative arc — hot birth → cooling universe → heat death — dissolves at both ends: no hotter-denser beginning (the floor is a wall state, not a birth — §8), no cold ending (a thinning level is not a dying fire; the bound books never join the decline). The falling level is not something happening to the polyverse — it is what persistence looks like from inside. Everything is time; a ledger that persists accumulates; a fixed densest entry read across growing books reads lower from every seat, forever, with no God’s-eye anywhere in the telling. The polyverse is not cooling like a coal. It is aging like a ledger.

12 · Beyond the books: what Viv proposed, what the sky answered, what survives

This page is the first in the corpus to move past the foundation texts on a point of substance, and that deserves its own plain statement. Pope proposed — explicitly as a live alternative, “our figures are included only to support our logical arguments” — that the cosmological redshift is relativistic time-dilation of random deep motion: the deeper the field, the greater the enclosed mass about a distributed barycentre, the greater the speeds, the redder the books. The suggestion was vetted here with the full discipline of this corpus, and it fails — four independent ways, any one fatal:

  1. The scatter kill (kinematics; no instrument newer than Hubble’s plates required). Random motion books its speeds twice — a second-order mean ($\beta^2/2$) and a first-order spread ($\pm\beta$, both signs). Spread exceeds signal by $2/\beta > 2$ at every depth; near half the sky would arrive blueshifted. The observed law is tight to percent with no deep blueshifts (§5.0).
  2. The magnitude kill (dynamics). $v \propto \sqrt{r}$ run to z ≈ 0.9 demands $\beta \approx 0.85$; every measured motion at those depths — line widths, kinematic SZ (~300 km/s), the cold local flow — sits 10³–10⁵ below (§5.0).
  3. The thermometric kill (measurement). Absorber baths read $T_0(1+z)$ — 5.08 ± 0.10 K at z = 0.886 against a required uniform 2.725 — with the excitation-free SZ channel concurring; and no boost of a 2.725 K bath reproduces either the measured values or the unsmeared 217 GHz null (§5.1–5.2).
  4. The composition kill (structure). A sky sharing one thermal floor requires bookings that multiply along chains of completed hand-offs; pairwise Lorentz factors do not compose. This executes not one mechanism but the whole class of pairwise-velocity carriers (§5.3).

And the squaring — what of Viv’s suggestion survives, because its shape was right: “deeper field → more accumulated mass in view → greater booked shift” is structurally exactly the surviving carrier (T2). Depth of view is depth into the accumulation; the booking does grow with how much ledger the exchange spans; the distributed barycentre survives as seat-equivalence — no privileged centre, every observer reading the same deepening books in every direction. Every class property the mechanism was chosen for — achromatic, rate-real, direction-blind — carries into its successor. What died is one word: the accumulation is not kinetic. Not speeds through space — growth of the collective configuration between the two entries of the hand-off. The suggestion was the right shape drawn in the wrong currency, and the books that taught this corpus how to audit are the books whose proposal the audit retired. Pope & Osborne’s own method — stake the mechanism, refuse the unauditable, let observation answer — is the instrument that both killed the proposal and found its successor. That is not the foundation failing. That is the foundation working beyond its authors.
