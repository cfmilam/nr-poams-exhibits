How to read an atom that is not made of things

  • No electrons. A "mode-unit" (convention: an electron) is one ħ of organized turning. Each is drawn as its closed streamlines — the rings along which its circulation actually flows about its own axis.
  • Streamlines, not lobes. Textbook lobes picture amplitude; these rings carry the turning itself. Every ring in this atom — planar core included — is one and the same object: a closed loop of phase.
  • Count the crests. The bright wave circling a ring is its phase crest — the winding number made visible: p-rings carry 1 crest, d-rings 2; s-rings breathe uniformly (winding 0 — near-pure internal twist, no transport). The crest is a wave of turning, not a thing going around.
  • Orientations are derived, not assumed: each angular harmonic l admits exactly 2l+1 orientations × 2 phase senses — s = 2, p = 6, d = 10. Pure eigen-counting on a sphere. (Each orientation is drawn in its maximal-circulation form about its own axis; the counting is untouched.)
  • Paired senses counter-flow and cancel — a closed pair is two adjacent rings whose crests chase in opposite directions, drawn calm and dim. Unpaired senses co-flow bright: carbon's two open p-rings are its chemistry; iron's four co-turning d-rings are a net twist — its magnetism, with no "spin of a particle" anywhere. Slide "closed pairs" down to watch the cancellations fall silent: what survives is the atom's net circulation — for iron, four green rings and the core. The pairs are still there (the counting never changes); zero is just what their sum looks like.
  • Shells are radial harmonics (n = 1, 2, 3…). True spacing goes as n² — compressed here for display (slider below), the same compromise every textbook makes.
  • The planar core is the standalone closed loop of twisted momentum, embedded at the atom's heart doing structural duty: its integer twist count IS the extra ħ that deepens the vortex without changing its pattern (convention: neutrons). C-12 carries Tw = +6 — count the colour cycles; Fe-56 carries +30, blurring toward apparent smoothness (the discreteum lesson again).
  • Dim grey ghost rings mark derived-but-unoccupied orientations (carbon shows 2 of 6 p-positions lit). Chemistry is the reach toward closing them.
