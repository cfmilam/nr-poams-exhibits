Every Element — 118 Organizations of One Circulation

Normal Realism → POAMS. Philosophy first: there is one substance — angular momentum — and everything is time. Then the physics has no choice: an atom cannot be a collection of things; it must be one organized circulation of phase. This exhibit draws that organization, for every element.

No electrons orbit here, because there are no electrons — there is one circulation, and what chemistry counts are its angular harmonic modes. Every glowing ring is the same object: a closed streamline of phase. The bright crest moving along each ring is its winding made visible — count crests and you have counted the integers. Counter-flowing senses cancel (closed shells sit calm); uncancelled senses add (iron's magnetism is four of them, turning together). The barycenter is empty. Nothing is solid. This is phase, not matter — any of the 118 named organizations of it.

How to read an atom that is not made of things

  • No electrons. A "mode-unit" (convention: an electron) is one ħ of organized turning. Each is drawn as its closed streamlines — the rings along which its circulation actually flows about its own axis.
  • Streamlines, not lobes. Textbook lobes picture amplitude; these rings carry the turning itself. Every ring in this atom — planar core included — is one and the same object: a closed loop of phase.
  • Count the crests. The bright wave circling a ring is its phase crest — the winding number made visible: p-rings carry 1 crest, d-rings 2, f-rings 3; s-rings breathe uniformly (winding 0 — near-pure internal twist, no transport). The crest is a wave of turning, not a thing going around.
  • Orientations are derived, not assumed: each angular harmonic l admits exactly 2l+1 orientations × 2 phase senses — s = 2, p = 6, d = 10, f = 14. Pure eigen-counting on a sphere. (Each orientation is drawn in its maximal-circulation form about its own axis; the counting is untouched.)
  • Paired senses counter-flow and cancel — a closed pair is two adjacent rings whose crests chase in opposite directions, drawn calm and dim. Unpaired senses co-flow bright: carbon's two open p-rings are its chemistry; iron's four co-turning d-rings are a net twist — its magnetism, with no "spin of a particle" anywhere. Slide "closed pairs" down to watch the cancellations fall silent: what survives is the atom's net circulation. The pairs are still there (the counting never changes); zero is just what their sum looks like.
  • Shells are radial harmonics (n = 1, 2, 3…). True spacing goes as n² — compressed here for display (slider below), the same compromise every textbook makes.
  • "Why do two gold bands ride way out there, alone?" — the first question this drawing gets (first asked of manganese; iron answers identically). In manganese, iron, the alkaline earths — any atom whose outermost occupied shell holds only its s-pair — two gold rings ride the outermost sphere by themselves, tilted perpendicular to everything beneath: the valence s-pair, and nothing has left its shell. s has winding 0 and owns no transport axis, so a lone s-sense is drawn on the equator and a pair straddles ±40° about the vertical axis — a drawing convention for a mode whose orientation is a basis choice; the counting is untouched. Every ring is a circle of its sphere: center a distance c up the axis, radius ρ, and c² + ρ² = R² identically. Iron's 4s pair at the default compression: √(4.558² + 5.432²) = 7.091 = R₄ to the digit. The faint cages — two orthogonal great circles per shell, a third closing the outermost — are drawn so your eye can verify it. (A single equatorial trace, foreshortened under camera tilt, once read as a disk the s-pair seemed to overhang: the guide was under-drawn; the geometry never moved.)
  • The planar core is the standalone closed loop of twisted momentum, embedded at the atom's heart doing structural duty: its integer twist count IS the extra ħ that deepens the vortex without changing its pattern (convention: neutrons). C-12 carries Tw = +6 — count the colour cycles; Fe-56 carries +30, blurring toward apparent smoothness (the discreteum lesson again). Hydrogen-1 carries none — the bare fundamental.
  • Why a sphere and a disk at all? Two channels, exhaustively: a quantum either opens a new slot in the orientation census (the pattern — and a completed census is exactly spherical: the sum of any full harmonic set of orientations is angle-free, Unsöld's theorem) or deepens the winding of the one central loop (the core — one loop, one plane; Laplace's disk). Winding a pattern ring instead would change its crest count — its chemical identity; isotopes prove the pure-depth channel exists. Full demonstration in the Elements exhibit's Vortex panel.
  • Dim grey ghost rings mark derived-but-unoccupied orientations (carbon shows 2 of 6 p-positions lit). Chemistry is the reach toward closing them.
  • Why this filling order? The sequence these atoms build by — the Madelung (n+l) rule — is no longer an imported postulate: it is computed as closure geometry at the atom's growth frontier (zero adjustable parameters) in the companion exhibit Aufbau as Closure — The Order of the Census.
