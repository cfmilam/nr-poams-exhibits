The possibility

Information has to survive
the whole apparatus.

A useful link is more than a transfer equation. The signal has to be prepared, guided, interrogated and returned—and its readable outcome must belong to the signal that actually entered.

POAMS begins with one physical substance: organized angular momentum. A source, its guide, its controls and its records are different arrangements of that substance, exchanging and retaining circulation. The engineering question is concrete: can a finite organization carry coherent angular information along a guided route and reveal a controllable relative-phase response at its destination?

This candidate answers that question on paper with one joined construction. It keeps the same original source and arbitrary retained reference throughout. A favorable endpoint cannot be substituted for a failed transfer, and a requested operation cannot be counted as a completed one.

The word optical names this integrated guided, angular and relative-phase information operation. It does not identify an intrinsic optical oscillation, a wavelength, passive bandwidth, silica, or a fabricated fiber. Those require additional material and SI performance evidence.

Each traversed edge changes two physical work pairs. On a tree, every arrival at the same vertex has the same retained edge-work word; reverse travel undoes the corresponding change.
