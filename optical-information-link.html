01 · The physical idea

The exchange comes first.

The adopted working picture places a completed exchange—not a travelling passenger—at the center of the calculation.

Within the POAMS account under test, light is whole exchanges of action h between accumulators. Sender, receiver and apparatus belong inside the same optical projection. Relative phase, persistent clock memory and retained records supply the distinctions the calculation must preserve. The rule for forgetting is exact: forget nothing that a future admitted operation could still distinguish. The accepted representation gives that picture mathematical form through persistent-clock dynamics, clock kernels of rank at most three, and a six-term source decomposition. Its acceptance is conditional on the declared laws, not a measurement of the ontology. [A] [E]

Action—not cargo

In this account, the accumulator and the light are not two substances. One is retained organization; the other is its completed exchange. Transfer from one accumulator to another means a change between participating accounts, not an extra object transporting something across an independent stage.

For the declared unit winding: L = ħ;   ∮L dθ = 2πħ = h.

The exchange is whole; its possible connections can nevertheless have different relative phases. Action h is neither an energy in joules nor an elapsed time in seconds. A source rate and an exchange law are still needed. [F]

Two-slit puts the whole apparatus in the picture

The foundational question is not which opening a little passenger used. It is: what completed source–receiver connections does this arrangement permit, and with what relative weights? The source, both openings, receiver and any distinguishing records participate in the answer.

A registration is whole; a fringe pattern belongs to accumulated registrations. A dark fringe is not action destroyed halfway across the apparatus. Under the adopted coherent calculus, a common phase cancels from a count, while relative phase can change it. Distinguishable retained records cannot be combined as if they were the same coherent alternative.

In Pope’s account, sender and receiver themselves belong to the optical projection. They are not independently existing machinery outside the picture that somehow manufactures it. That interpretation does not make apparatus geometry optional: the arrangement still constrains which exchanges and records are possible. [F]

“All time” does not mean one clock

Begin with relative turning, phase, clock counts and the ordering of completed exchanges. The spatial drawing represents those relations; it must not quietly become an additional physical stage. There is no inference here to a universal clock. Nor does the completed connection’s null proper-time interpretation erase the timing registered by the participating clocks or make a multi-interaction link instantaneous.

What controllable change at the sender produces a distinguishable change in the receiver’s completed record, relative to its clock and references?

What changes in the calculation

Answering that question need not require an extraordinarily accurate reconstruction of the entire joint state. It requires a description preserving everything capable of changing the requested receiver record. The existing model already contains apparatus and memory; neither can disappear by a change of vocabulary. The proof we need is which distinctions matter to the observable, and which do not.

Two-slit makes the obligation vivid: preserve relative phase and retained distinguishability. Derive the exchange and its observable timing relations first; let the computational representation follow. [B]

The organizing claim under test

If POAMS is correct, the glass is light too: retained organization and propagating light, ultimately information, share one root. The “carry” and what is “carried” are the same; neither is cargo carried across a separate void. Their spatial appearance is optically projected information—turnings, “non-things in a non-void,” in the owner’s formulation.

This is why the program matters beyond a better engineered fiber. It brings the observer into the observed and asks the whole account to face a concrete test. The thesis is the POAMS account under test, not an established empirical fact. RCM-1 below tests one specified memory ingredient; it cannot by itself establish the whole ontology. [C]

02 · The construction

One source, through the whole link.

The integrated paper model joins preparation, guided transport, readout, paid phase comparison, return and recorded acquisition. It never repairs a failed transfer by substituting a freshly prepared endpoint signal.

  1. Prepared source. Supply the original source and its arbitrary retained reference within the declared joint initial-state boundary.
  2. Guided transport. Follow a finite 1,024-edge route with nonzero transverse cladding. Coherent angular information remains in the qualified joint model; the third angular component is retained.
  3. Angular readout and full cold SWAP. Record the angular result and retain the actual source for the endpoint sequence, with its reference and complement states.
  4. Paid relative-phase comparison. Act on the same signal. Include terminal energy and both work accounts: at the specified witness, 7k₀/8 + k₀/8 = k₀.
  5. Source return. Close the source, apparatus and return accounts, including two complete receipt couriers for the return acknowledgments.
  6. Recorded acquisition. Use actual source/code diagnostics and completed-event receipts. The independent clock seals failed attempts as well as successful ones; a requested command or history Boolean is not a completion receipt.

This is an actively guided construction. It does not derive an intrinsic optical carrier or a passive fiber passband. Its finite material types, preparation, controls, thermal accounts, clocks and acquisition laws are supplied conditional operations. Imported finite-spin, operator and concentration mathematics is the declared representation—not evidence that those material organizations have been realized. [L]

03 · The reassessment

A smaller description must remember enough.

The full-state calculation met a computational wall. The important question was what the attempted method required—not what nature was obliged to calculate.

The stopped rank-1024 global-state method and its accumulated full-state norm criterion remain stopped in their own scope. Their failure is not a lower bound on receiver error. Writing the receiver readout as an observable was not a new solution: the model already had a full-trace population readout and joint apparatus accounts. [F]

The exchange-record representation was adopted

The reassessment produced three exact reductions within the supplied CCK/CT1 laws. CCK is the adopted Coherent Completion Kernel; CT1 names the candidate’s specified clock/contact dynamics.

  1. Persistent clocks. Clock-dependent interactions are diagonal in three labels; each cycle advances them by one. The exact bulk form is U^t = Σ_q |q+t⟩⟨q| ⊗ V_q(t), with the original ordered interactions. A site’s repeated history has a three-component record interface and a Gram kernel of rank at most three. Its initial label persists across revisits; mathematical contraction does not refresh the clock.
  2. Restricted-sector edge factorization. In the nominal magnetic-deficit-at-most-two sector, two separated order singlets cannot coexist. The edge gate factors into a clock-independent factor and two commuting single-clock factors. Arbitrary old/fault stock still requires the original full gate.
  3. Six source populations. The actual mixed source needs six positive population contractions instead of eight. Both rails and longitudinal reaction weight remain. Two branches have known nonreacting dynamics; four still require the coupled calculation. No pure bright source is substituted.

Coherence averages amplitudes; population averages full populations. Squaring the former generally does not give the latter. Actual TOTAL and actual DARK, references, retained records and their future interactions remain in the account. [E]

The obstruction is memory coupled to recombining histories

Three labels per clock do not make the whole guide a three-state system. Site-labelled clock records persist while reacting order and spatial histories separate and recombine. The receiver’s interference-sensitive functional can distinguish histories that a present population summary cannot.

An exact reduced-family counterexample makes the loss concrete: identical diagonal populations can lead to later receiver counts of 1/10 versus 9/10. Diagonal observables, parity and local flux alone do not close the future readout. The six-branch source is a fixed multiplicity, not the asymptotic driver; local contact amplitudes are not uniformly small. This diagnoses the specified candidate and stopped representations. It is not mathematical undecidability or a universal algorithmic or physical no-go. [R]

A record may be summed out after its last relevant contact, or after an equivalent future-effect sufficiency proof. Equality of today’s receiver marginal is not enough. That rule preserves the physical account while allowing a different mathematical representation.

From diagnosis to a specific discriminator

The full-size CT1 solver remains parked. At the frozen t=10 candidate, both opposite-helicity transfer tests still require |g−| ≥ 4e−6, and the actual useful-count test still requires p ≥ 1e−8. Those thresholds remain undecided; positive subthreshold lower bounds are inconclusive, not failures.

The diagnosis nevertheless supplied a narrower question with a complete nominal full-size paper proof: does a second encounter retain the same clock’s phase record, or act as though the clock had been independently renewed? That is RCM-1. It is a separately posed diagnostic, not a shortcut declaring the whole transport problem solved.

04 · The acid test

RCM-1: meet the same clock twice.

The decisive contrast is persistent same-contact memory versus accounted independent renewal. The test asks for a receiver record—not an inferred hidden clock trajectory.

The separate diagnostic word

The baseline applies the existing contact bank twice, retaining the same clocks between encounters, and addresses the receiver to the same bank and matched mode with distinct receiver stock. The full 65³ inventory, 125-site bank, both rails, source and receiver laws, contact angles, inverses and complements remain.

AP-RCM is the conditional availability hypothesis that this selective contact itinerary and receiver addressing can be supplied. Unselected operations are identity letters in a separate RCM program word, not deletions from a claimed CT1 cycle. There is no intervening transport or clock advance. Unavoidable extra contacts, clock advance, altered tariffs, nonseparable routing, reference reuse or a changed source law invalidate qualification. [Z]

The M and H control rows add a separate premise, AP-M: controllable clock SWAP/export and independent fresh clock stock, without renewing order, changing phase or introducing a hidden return interaction. AP-M is not implied by AP-RCM. Neither availability premise is physically established.

Controls decide whether a bin means anything

Common controls V, I, S, O, M and Z must lie wholly within their own target ± 1/960. A disjoint common-control interval gives INCONCLUSIVE / CONTROL-DISCREPANCY for the full comparison, even if the primary N bin has an arithmetic label. Partial overlap is inconclusive.

Q, R and H are evaluated separately against each hypothesis’s target ± 1/960. Do not require the persistent model’s controls as a precondition for testing renewal. With common controls and independent certificates satisfied, a disjoint model-specific control rejects that model’s full signature; if N already excludes the other, both frozen signatures are rejected, or an unrecognized posing/error failure is present.

Inconclusive—never passage. Missing or corrupt records, unavailable controls, failed AP-RCM/AP-M conditions, invalid certificates, excessive error, incomplete schedules, failed custody or unjustified statistics do not count as a successful test. Retain every adverse record. Controls may not be used to rescale N or tune angles, clock preparation, phase, decoder, DARK, error allowance or bins.
