Josephson Effects & Superconducting Flux Quantization

A phase clock, a closed-loop flux receipt, and a hard benchmark for POAMS completion language.

Macroscopic phase can be wired, counted and converted into voltage. The record is exact enough to expose every hand-wave.

Current research program

Josephson and flux-quantization measurements supply the quantitative baseline. POAMS proposes to construct the collective phase state, the \(2e\) species scale, current–phase and voltage–phase dynamics, loop periodicity, environmental exchange, and phase-slip law from one completed closure account.

2 · The experimental record

Josephson predicted a supercurrent through a weak link and a phase evolution proportional to voltage. Microwave irradiation locks junction oscillation to the drive, producing Shapiro voltage steps. Independently, superconducting rings quantize fluxoid in units associated with coherent charge \(2e\); SQUIDs turn that phase closure into exquisitely sensitive flux readout.

Primary and authoritative anchors: Josephson (1962); Deaver & Fairbank (1961); Doll & Näbauer (1961); NIST Josephson voltage standards.

3 · Gauge-invariant phase and the phase clock

\[I_s=I_c\sin\delta,\qquad \frac{d\delta}{dt}=\frac{2e}{\hbar}V.\]

The sinusoidal current–phase relation is the tunnel-junction ideal, not a universal law for every weak link. The voltage relation integrates to a direct posting rule:

\[\Delta\delta=\frac{2e}{\hbar}\int Vdt=2\pi\frac{\int Vdt}{\Phi_0},\qquad \Phi_0=\frac{h}{2e}.\]

One \(2\pi\) phase advance carries voltage-time area \(\Phi_0\). Under microwave locking at frequency \(f\):

\[V_n=n\Phi_0f=n\frac{h}{2e}f.\]

This is an unusually clean operational correspondence between counted phase turns and an electrical receipt.

4 · Closed loops: fluxoid, not slogan

\[\oint\left(\nabla\theta-\frac{2e}{\hbar}\mathbf A\right)\cdot d\boldsymbol\ell=2\pi n.\]

In a loop with screening current, the quantized object is fluxoid: magnetic flux plus a kinetic/supercurrent term. Bare \(\Phi=n\Phi_0\) is an approximation for an appropriate thick loop or negligible current contribution.

An ideal symmetric low-inductance DC SQUID has \(I_c(\Phi)=2I_0|\cos(\pi\Phi/\Phi_0)|\). Junction asymmetry, self-inductance, flux focusing and trapped vortices alter the response but not the need for a complete loop receipt.

Why \(h/2e\neq h/e\). The superconducting phase closes in a coherent \(2e\) sector; a single-electron AB ring has \(h/e\) periodicity. Topology alone does not choose the flux unit.

5 · Quantitative receipts

  • \(\Phi_0=2.067833848\times10^{-15}\,\mathrm{V\,s}\).
  • A 1 µV DC voltage corresponds to \(483.598\,\mathrm{MHz}\).
  • At 10 GHz, the first Shapiro step is \(20.678\,\mu\mathrm V\).
  • For \(A_{eff}=1.00\,\mu\mathrm m^2\), the ideal SQUID field period is 2.0678 mT.
  • A 1.00 mV, 1.00 ps pulse advances 0.4836 of a full phase turn.

Effective area, electron temperature, parity, junction dynamics and circuit environment belong in the uncertainty budget.

6 · What POAMS must earn

  1. Derive a \(2\pi\)-periodic gauge-invariant phase state.
  2. Derive or explicitly import the coherent \(2e\) sector.
  3. Produce the junction current–phase and voltage–phase relations.
  4. Distinguish \(h/2e\) loop periodicity from \(h/e\) single-electron periodicity.
  5. Recover integer Shapiro locking.
  6. Predict finite-temperature coherence loss, poisoning and phase-slip statistics.
  7. Keep energy, quasiparticle, phonon and electromagnetic receipts for each slip.

Calling \(\delta\) a “closure count” changes no mathematics. A native result begins only when the state, coefficient, dynamics and failure boundary are independently generated.

7 · Discriminators and engineering

Pairing versus topology

Compare ordinary junctions, normal AB rings, parity-controlled hybrids and poisoned islands under one frozen analysis. Any \(h/2e\leftrightarrow h/e\) change must track an independently measured change in coherent species/parity.

Phase-slip receipt

Correlate individual \(2\pi\) slips with voltage-pulse area, microwave/phonon emission, temperature and parity. A POAMS residual must be predicted before the events are opened.

Engineering value is immediate: Josephson voltage standards, SQUID magnetometry, rapid single-flux-quantum logic, superconducting quantum circuits and exact phase/flux calibrators for completion detectors.
