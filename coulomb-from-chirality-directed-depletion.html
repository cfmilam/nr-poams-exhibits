§1 — What is supplied, and what is calculated

Ontology. In the POAMS reading, the participating organizations are angular momentum, not separate bearers placed in a void. The two ends and the observer belong to the same account. A completed exchange is one event with two entries; recording both ends does not automatically double its energy. See The Receiver Keys the Exchange.

Mathematical model. The calculation uses a stationary, linear, isotropic exterior response; reciprocal signed pair booking; and, for the circular atomic scale, an effective two-ended kinetic energy with fixed orbital action $J=n\hbar$. These are the stated conditions of the calculation. A smooth exterior chart represents averaged relations; it is not a claim that the underlying discreteum is a continuous medium.

Calibration. Measured $\alpha$ fixes the elementary pair coefficient. Measured masses fix the absolute atomic scale. Measured $G$ remains a separate input wherever the gravitational comparison is used. No equality between the gravitational and chirality-response normalizations is assumed.

§2 — Spherical response and the signed pair law

Why an inverse distance appears

In a spherically symmetric stationary exterior chart, let $\psi$ denote the signed response and let $D$ be its specified response coefficient. If the integrated radial response $\mathcal J$ is constant outside the source, then

$$-4\pi D r^2\frac{d\psi}{dr}=\mathcal J,\qquad \psi(\infty)=0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \psi(r)=\frac{\mathcal J}{4\pi D r}.$$

This is the spherical area law plus the stated linear response and boundary condition. Geometry fixes the radial dependence under those conditions, not the magnitude or a microscopic exchange law. Nothing in this chart requires a carrier crossing a gap.

One calibrated coefficient, four sense combinations

For the elementary signs $s_1,s_2\in\{-1,+1\}$, the phenomenological identification of the Coulomb channel is

$$U_{12}(r)=s_1s_2\frac{K_Q}{r} =s_1s_2\frac{\alpha\hbar c}{r},\qquad F_r=-\frac{dU_{12}}{dr}=s_1s_2\frac{\alpha\hbar c}{r^2}.$$

Here positive $F_r$ means increasing separation. Equal signs give the outward branch; opposite signs give the inward branch. This sign identification is part of the calibrated pair account; spherical shells alone do not force it. “Force” here is the derivative used to book a constrained displacement, not an additional substance.

The spherical resonant shells are not an extra electromagnetic force. They describe allowed organization of a bound state. The exterior spherical response supplies an inverse-distance form under its assumptions; the resonant/circular conditions select the available states once the pair strength is supplied. These are related parts of the calculation, not interchangeable objects.

What the recent joint-network calculation added

A specified quadratic circulation cost in a regular three-dimensional chart produces $K_Q=(\kappa/4\pi)\hbar c$. Supplying measured $\alpha$ therefore fixes that particular cost weight to $\kappa=4\pi\alpha=0.0917012368266\ldots$. The chosen cost, chart and energy interpretation remain model assumptions. This is useful calibration, not a proof that the source commitments uniquely select that network.

§3 — Derive the gross atomic scales from measured $\alpha$

Both ends participate. Their effective relative inertia is $\mu=m_1m_2/(m_1+m_2)$; neither is installed as a stationary nucleus. In the quadratic circular scale calculation,

$$E_n(r)=\frac{n^2\hbar^2}{2\mu r^2}-\frac{\alpha\hbar c}{r}.$$

Setting $dE_n/dr=0$ and substituting the resulting radius back into the energy gives

$$\boxed{r_n=\frac{n^2\hbar}{\mu c\alpha}},\qquad \boxed{E_n=-\frac{\mu c^2\alpha^2}{2n^2}}.$$

The same energy differences determine the gross line frequency and its conventional vacuum-wavelength readout:

$$f_{n_2\to n_1}=\frac{\mu c^2\alpha^2}{2h} \left(\frac1{n_1^2}-\frac1{n_2^2}\right),\qquad n_2>n_1,\qquad \lambda_{\rm vac}=\frac cf.$$

This is a scale calculation. It is not a literal drawing of the lowest angular state as a small particle orbit. Full angular states, transition selection and resolved line corrections need their own treatment. The $n^2$ radius relation uses the energy law as well as $J=n\hbar$; action quantization alone does not determine it.

This is the useful cascade: one calibrated elementary strength, together with the stated energy and action relations, sets linked sizes, energies and rates. These are not six unrelated knobs. Nor does this imply that magnetic anomalies, hyperfine structure, $G$, or the mass hierarchy follow automatically.

§5 — What can we now use?

A common scale for calculations

The calibrated $K_Q$ supplies one long-range elementary pair normalization. Gross atomic lengths and energies provide reference units for configuration calculations, spectral comparisons and drive-energy accounts. Molecular work can use that anchor without reopening the origin of $\alpha$; overlap, many-centre terms and short-range behavior must still be specified rather than inferred from a pair coefficient alone.

A way to isolate genuinely joint response

$$D(r)=\frac{E_{++}+E_{--}-E_{+-}-E_{-+}}4=\frac{K_Q}{r},\qquad \Omega_{\rm con}=\frac{D}{\hbar}=\frac{\alpha c}{r}.$$

The four-sense contrast cancels additive contributions belonging to either end alone. It is useful for analysis of measured interaction energies or phases when equivalent preparations and a common reference are available. It does not guarantee that material bias, source or mounting contributions cancel. Over the comparison interval $r/c$, the connected phase coordinate is $\alpha$ radians; this is a reference interval, not a derived transit or completion time.

A practical coupling lead, separate from propulsion

The final high-voltage pass found that a specified electrical geometry can give appreciable local forces and controlled mode coupling. For a voltage-held terminal geometry, the source-inclusive potential is $\mathcal U=-C(d)V^2/2$, so $F_d=V^2 C'(d)/2$. For the illustrative assumptions $C=10$ pF, $d=50$ mm, $C'=-C/d$, and $V=50$ kV, the attraction is 0.25 N and stored energy is 0.0125 J. This is not a measured device or a force arising from counter-rotation. The other terminal receives the counterpart.

Two established material results provide concrete engineering leads: Zhou et al. (2019) demonstrated electrically tunable coupling between modes of a silicon ring resonator; Yu et al. (2019) demonstrated voltage/strain control of YIG orientation and resonance. These support investigation of specified directional material response and electrical state-setting. They do not demonstrate weight control. A voltage-controlled YIG state is not automatically a suitable low-loss Prov-A medium; its loaded response and damping must be measured.

§6 — The original objective: useful propulsion

Current result: calibrated elementary interaction scales and useful local-coupling calculations. Not yet a result: a demonstrated propulsion advantage, whole-apparatus weight change, or predictive drive-to-support-force relation.

Accepting $\alpha$ removes the need to derive its numerical origin before proceeding. It does not identify the actual driven material state or determine how that state participates in an Earth-relative support response. Those are apparatus questions, not another demand to explain why nature chose $1/137$.

§8 — What is settled for use, and what remains research?

Accepted for this working account: measured $\alpha$ is an explicit anchor; the displayed equations calculate the consequences in their stated regimes. Its unexplained numerical origin does not block applications. We have neither derived that value from ontology alone nor proved it fundamentally underivable.

The old “elementary completion rate” and six-result claim

The observable fixes the product $K_Q=k_Q\nu_Q^2$, not $\nu_Q$ separately. Writing $\zeta=k_Q/k_M$ gives $\alpha=\zeta(\nu_Q/f_{\rm Pl})^2$. Only after setting $\zeta=1$ does $\nu_Q=\sqrt\alpha f_{\rm Pl}\approx2.522\times10^{41}$ Hz follow. A shared nexus does not prove that normalization. It is not an independently observed clock or a literal count inferred from $\alpha$.

The earlier statement that deriving this rate automatically supplies magnetic anomalies and hyperfine spectra is withdrawn. Those require additional spin, state and interaction relations. The measured-anchor cascade above concerns leading pair strength and gross atomic scales with masses supplied.

Source-energy and high-precision back-substitution

Pope and Osborne's atomic example supplies intrinsic turning energy from the spectral scale. Inverting our explicitly assumed reciprocal circular extension to match measured $K_Q$ requires $K_s(n)\simeq\mu c^2\alpha^2/(2n^2)$. It reconstructs that necessary energy input; it does not independently choose it or establish a microscopic sense-dependent pair law.

The structured reverse calculation has 74 numerical outputs and 50 mathematical/input checks, with 80-digit output agreement at 150 and 230 working digits. These verify the algebra and supplied-input consistency, not physical truth of all the tested models. The rejected finite-spectrum sense assignment still has the wrong sign; entering $\alpha$ does not repair it.
