§1 — Grounding: What POAMS Already Holds

Every claim in this exhibit rests on commitments already ratified in the POAMS foundation. Nothing here re-derives them; the reader is pointed at the exhibits that carry the load.

One substance: angular momentum. The one stuff is circulation, and everything perceived as world is that circulation reading itself — the angular-momentum nexus (per GROUNDING §1). There is no aether, no background container in which the nexus sits; the nexus is the reality.

Discreteum, integer turns. Angular momentum comes in whole $\hbar$ closures ($Lk = \pm 1$ for elementary closures, integer for composites).

Newton's law from substance-depletion. The Counter-Rotating exhibit's preamble (per its §1 grounding) and the ratified n-centre closure derivation in The Navigation Engine establish that Newton's inverse-square law emerges from a trapped-rate sink depleting the local availability of completion routing in the surrounding nexus. For a mass $M$ with trapped-rate sink $\dot{N}_M = Mc^2/h$:

$$\rho_N(r) = \rho_N(\infty) - \frac{\dot{N}_M}{4\pi D r}, \qquad g(r) = \frac{GM}{r^2}$$

where $D$ is the nexus's completion-propagation coefficient and $\rho_N(\infty)$ is its far-field completion count density.

Charge is the sign of intrinsic spin. Per GROUNDING §3: charge is not a substance, it is the phase-sense of a closure ($\oint d\phi = \pm 2\pi n$). What is new in this exhibit. The Navigation Engine's derivation handles tangled trapped-rate sinks (mass). It does not yet handle chirality-directed sinks (charge). §2 — The Derivation

An elementary closure carrying spin-sign $s$ has a chirality-directed contribution to the nexus that is distinct from its tangled trapped-rate contribution. Where the tangled contribution depletes ambient completions isotropically (no preferred direction, since the underlying $Lk = \pm 1$ orientations of its constituent loops average to zero), the chirality-directed contribution depletes with a sign carried by $s$.

Chirality-directed sink

For an elementary closure with chirality $s$, define a chirality-directed sink rate $\dot{N}_Q$ — the rate at which chirality-directed completions leave the closure, carrying its phase-sense signature into the surrounding nexus. Unlike $\dot{N}_M$ (magnitude, sign-agnostic), $\dot{N}_Q$ has a sign attached (the closure's chirality).

By the same spherical-shell geometry that yields Newton's $1/r$ — the completion-count depletion at radius $r$ from a spherically-symmetric sink is set by the sink rate divided by the flux passage area $4\pi r^2$, integrated over the propagation coefficient — the depletion pattern from a chirality-directed sink is:

$$\Delta\rho_N^{(Q)}(r) \;=\; -\frac{s \cdot \dot{N}_Q}{4\pi D r}$$

The sign of the depletion is carried by $s$. A positive-chirality closure depletes chirality-directed availability radially outward with one sign; a negative-chirality closure depletes with the opposite sign.

Two-closure coupling

A second elementary closure with chirality $s_2$ sitting at radius $r$ from the first source experiences the depletion pattern above. Its own completions preferentially route where the depletion-availability matches its chirality — that is, its route-preference direction depends on the product $s_1 \cdot s_2$:

  • $s_1 \cdot s_2 = +1$ (like chiralities): the depletion pattern from source 1 is unfavourable to source 2's completions in the shared direction; source 2's natural-orbit shifts outward — the two closures' natural configuration has them further apart (extant label: “repulsion”).
  • $s_1 \cdot s_2 = -1$ (opposite chiralities): the depletion pattern from source 1 is favourable to source 2's completions in the shared direction; source 2's natural-orbit shifts inward — the two closures' natural configuration has them closer together (extant label: “attraction”).

Coulomb's potential form

Working the coupling to the same order as the Newton derivation, the potential energy of the two-closure pair is:

$$\boxed{\;V_{Coulomb}(r) \;=\; \frac{k_Q \cdot s_1 s_2 \cdot \dot{N}_{Q,1} \dot{N}_{Q,2}}{r}\;}$$

where $k_Q$ is a coefficient with dimensions such that $V$ has units of energy. Substituting $s_1 s_2 = \pm 1$ and the empirical identification $\dot{N}_{Q,i} \propto |q_i|$ (charge magnitude), this reproduces the empirical Coulomb form:

$$V_{Coulomb}(r) \;=\; \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0} \cdot \frac{q_1 q_2}{r}$$

with sign carried by the product of the individual charge signs.

Coulomb's law form emerges from POAMS substance-depletion by the same mechanism that produces Newton's law — with chirality-directed depletion in place of tangled depletion. The $1/r$ potential is the geometry of spherically-symmetric depletion in the nexus, and it is what mass and charge share. The sign-sensitivity is what distinguishes charge from mass: chirality-directed depletion carries the closure's phase-sense outward, so the pair-coupling reads their chirality product. Like charges route away from each other; unlike charges route toward each other. Newton's law is the sign-agnostic case; Coulomb's law is the sign-sensitive one; both are the same substrate-depletion physics.

§3 — Cross-checks Against Known Behaviour

Reproduces the sign of Coulomb's phenomenology across every pair

Two positive elementary charges: $s_1 s_2 = +1$ → natural configuration further apart (classical: “repel”). Two negative: $s_1 s_2 = +1$ → further apart. One positive, one negative: $s_1 s_2 = -1$ → natural configuration closer (classical: “attract”). Matches classical Coulomb behaviour across every pair without exception.

Reproduces the $1/r^2$ force law from the derived $1/r$ potential

$F = -\nabla V = k_Q \cdot s_1 s_2 \cdot \dot{N}_{Q,1} \dot{N}_{Q,2} / r^2$. Inverse-square force falls out of the derived potential, as expected. Same $1/r^2$ shape as Newton; same origin (spherical-shell depletion geometry).

Two-endedness, not signal propagation

The pair coupling is a proper-time exchange between two closures reading each other's contribution to the nexus (per The Receiver Keys the Exchange). There is no photon, no field carrier, no signal, no container across which anything propagates — each closure keeps its own book, and the observer's apparent $r/c$ delay under specific measurement configurations is optical projection of the two-endedness onto their space-projection, not something travelling. The depletion pattern is a bookkeeping fact readable from either endpoint's own local ledger.

Superposition

For an assembly of many charged closures, each contributes its own chirality-directed depletion to the shared nexus. The pattern experienced by a test closure at some point is the sum of individual contributions (since depletion is additive for small perturbations of $\rho_N$). This reproduces the superposition principle of electrostatics without additional POAMS commitment.

§4 — The Scale Gap: What Would Give $\alpha$

The structural derivation above delivers Coulomb's $1/r$ potential form. The coupling coefficient $k_Q \cdot \dot{N}_Q^2 / (4\pi D \rho_N(\infty))$ — extant $1/(4\pi\epsilon_0)$ — is not yet fixed in POAMS-native units. Fixing it requires one specific derivation.

What must be derived

The nexus-propagation coefficient $D$ and background completion density $\rho_N(\infty)$ appear identically in both the Newton and Coulomb derivations (they are properties of the shared nexus). Therefore the ratio of Coulomb coupling to Newton coupling for a pair of elementary closures reduces to the ratio of chirality-directed sink rate to tangled sink rate:

$$\frac{V_{Coulomb}/V_{Newton}}{\text{(same pair, same }r\text{)}} \;=\; \left(\frac{\dot{N}_Q}{\dot{N}_M}\right)^2 \cdot \text{(units and geometry factors, both derivable)}$$

The empirical ratio for two elementary charges (electron-mass, elementary charge magnitude): $|V_{Coulomb}|/|V_{Newton}| \approx 4 \times 10^{42}$. So the sink-rate ratio $\dot{N}_Q/\dot{N}_M$ per elementary closure is of order $10^{21}$.

The mass sink rate is POAMS-native: for the electron closure, $\dot{N}_M = m_e c^2/h \approx 1.24 \times 10^{20}$ Hz (from Mass as Trapped Rate).

The chirality-directed sink rate must be $\dot{N}_Q \approx 10^{21} \times 1.24 \times 10^{20}$ Hz $\approx 10^{41}$ Hz per elementary closure.
