Depicting the Non-Thing

There is no thing here — no rim, no strip, no stuff in a void. Only phase: an angle that closes on itself (∮dφ = 2πn) and keeps turning (that turning is time). The one real, countable quantity is the integer linking number. Where "twist" ends and "writhe" begins is a smooth representational choice — only their sum is invariant.

How to read a no-thing

  • There is no object here — no rim, no strip, no stuff in a void. The glowing strokes are not matter; each is only where the phase points now — the local sense of rotation.
  • The faint ellipse is not a wire. It is the closed locus the phase winds around — a relation, not a thing.
  • The strokes circulate because the substance is its own turning. That turning is time (L = dS/dθ) — there is nothing else underneath.
  • The one real, countable quantity is the integer linking number Lk — how many times the phase closes on itself (∮dφ = 2πn).
  • Twist ↔ Writhe: drag it and spin trades for coil, yet Lk never moves. Which part we call spin vs orbit is a viewpoint; only the invariant sum is real (Călugăreanu: Lk = Tw + Wr).
  • Discreteum, not continuum: at low Lk you can count the turns. Crank it high and they blur into an apparent smooth glow — continuity is just many turns seen at once, never a substance beneath them.
