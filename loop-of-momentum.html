How to read a no-thing

  • There is no object here — no rim, no strip, no stuff in a void. The glowing strokes are not matter; each is only where the phase points now — the local sense of rotation.
  • The faint closed locus is not a wire. It is the centerline the phase winds around — a relation, not a thing.
  • The strokes circulate because the substance is its own turning. That turning is time (L = dS/dθ) — there is nothing else underneath.
  • The phase-map winding is n, with ∮dφ = 2πn. It supplies the framing winding in this construction; for sufficiently small ε, the implied disjoint curves C± = r ± εU define the ribbon linking number Lk = n.
  • Twist ↔ Writhe: move the deformation slider and the centerline coils continuously. The displayed Tw and Wr are separately evaluated from the framing and Gauss integrals; their residual against Lk is printed in the HUD (Călugăreanu–White: Lk = Tw + Wr).
  • Discreteum, not continuum: at low Lk you can count the turns. Crank it high and they blur into an apparent smooth glow — continuity is just many turns seen at once, never a substance beneath them. The display phase may advance through intermediate angles, but only the completed global closure enters the integer ledger.
