The Smooth Single-Beta Floor as Ledger Arithmetic

Philosophy first: there is one substance — angular momentum — and at fixed census \(A\) the quanta divide between two channels: the pattern channel (count \(Z\), the long-range phase handle; extant label: protons) and the core twist ledger (count \(A-Z\); extant: neutrons). A single beta transition changes \(Z\) by one at fixed \(A\); in the native reading, that is a proposed re-filing between the two ledgers. This page establishes a narrower result than its first edition claimed: it computes the smooth energetic floor for such one-step moves. It does not compute decay rates, observational longevity, or stability against alpha decay, fission, or double beta decay. The page evaluates coefficient packages inherited from the Mass Ledger: an imbalance magnitude and a far-field strain price. The 2026-09-08 Mass Ledger audit quarantined the cluster calibration behind the former, so the numerical curves here are now explicitly conditional model audits, not an independent coefficient derivation. Given the displayed packages, the integer floors lie within two charge units of six selected reference charges and the familiar bend away from \(Z=A/2\) follows from the ratio \(a_c/4a_{\text{sym}}\), with no coefficient adjusted on this page. The confined-ladder and frontier-density calculations remain candidate seat constructions. The sparse integer gate does not select among the four package combinations; that earlier discriminator is retired (§4).

"The valley was already on the books. Nobody had opened it to the record."

1 · The Question

Across the measured isobar chains, the charge \(Z\) favored against a one-step beta move lies near \(A/2\) for light nuclei and bends to \(Z/A = 92/238 = 0.387\) for beta-stable U-238 (which is nevertheless alpha radioactive). Extant physics calls the broader locus the valley of stability and computes it by minimizing the semi-empirical mass formula — with coefficients fitted to the same masses. The POAMS interpretation calls a beta transition a re-filing — one quantum moves from one channel's ladder to the other at fixed total. The calculation on this page asks only the static energetic question: at which integer \(Z\) does neither adjacent one-step move lower the displayed ledger cost? That criterion is \(E(A,Z)<E(A,Z{-}1)\) and \(E(A,Z)<E(A,Z{+}1)\); it says whether a move is energetically allowed, not how fast it occurs.

The record's valley was known before this page. Its discipline is therefore limited and explicit: evaluate inherited coefficient packages without retuning them here, preserve their source grades, and call the result a conditional audit rather than a blind prediction. Units are native: every cost on this page is a turn rate, printed on the Hz ladder (EHz per step, ZHz for deep totals); the record's MeV appears only as tagged parentheticals for orientation (1 record MeV \(= 241.8\) EHz \(= 0.2418\) ZHz of whole closures; Mass Ledger §1).

Here \(C = a_v+\tau_b = 8.668\) ZHz (record orientation: 35.85 MeV) and \(\gamma=0.85(5)\) are inherited calibration inputs; the contact column \(C/(1+2\gamma)\) is not presently a derived coefficient. Likewise, \(E_F\) is evaluated at the model-selected capacity density \(\rho_0=3/(4\pi r_0^3)\), whose physical identification remains a source-page assumption rather than a theorem. Extant cross-reference, tagged: the fitted symmetry coefficient is 5.61–5.73 ZHz (23.2–23.7 record MeV). The old assembly's proximity is now a retrospective comparison, not validation of the quarantined contact normalization.

2b · The circulation strain

The pattern channel's \(Z\) co-organized same-sense circulations are modeled with a far-field \(1/r\) strain cost (the n-body charter's standing import, shared with the atom front). Mass Ledger P5 supplies the candidate:

\[ E_{\text{strain}} = a_c\,\frac{Z(Z-1)}{A^{1/3}}, \qquad a_c = \frac{3}{5}\cdot\frac{k}{r_0} = \frac{3}{5}\cdot\frac{348.2\ \text{EHz·fm}}{1.2\ \text{fm}} = 174.1\ \text{EHz} \quad (\text{record: } 0.72\ \text{MeV}), \]

The \(3/5\) follows within a uniform-sphere model and \(Z(Z-1)\) is the no-self-pair count; \(k = 348.2\) EHz·fm (record 1.44 MeV·fm) the strain quantum measured outside the curve (\(= \alpha\hbar c\): \(\alpha\) enters only as a measured input), \(r_0 = 1.2\) fm the named size import. The 174.1 EHz value is exact only inside this uniform-sphere approximation with the displayed rounded inputs; it is not an exact physical coefficient. The ledger also books an untuned exchange filing layer, \(-x_{\text{ex}}\,Z^{4/3}/A^{1/3}\) with \(x_{\text{ex}} = 128.2\) EHz (record 0.53 MeV; T-P3′), which enters the fork table below. Extant labels, tagged: Coulomb term, Coulomb exchange.

The result that survives: the minimization makes the bend depend chiefly on \(a_c/(4a_{\text{sym}})\). The displayed candidate values predate this audit and are not adjusted here, so their conditional curve is a legitimate consistency check. Because the imbalance magnitude is now quarantined, however, numerical agreement does not derive that coefficient or retire the empirical valley problem.

3 · A Candidate Seat from the Confined Ladder

The §6 assembly prices the filling tax at capacity density (a local read of the pinned interior). But the corpus now owns a second, global picture of the same books: the Inner Census ladder \(N = 2n_r + l\), capacities \((N+1)(N+2)\) per class — and beta re-filing is literally a move between the two class copies of that ladder. Filling each class to its top rung (continuum: \(n = \Lambda^3/3\) quanta, \(E = \tfrac{1}{4}h\Delta\nu\,(3n)^{4/3}\)) and taxing an imbalance gives a quadratic with coefficient

\[ a_{\text{kin}}^{\text{ladder}} \;=\; \frac{h\Delta\nu\;A^{1/3}}{\sqrt[3]{144}} \;=\; \frac{10.08\ \text{ZHz}}{5.24} \;=\; 1.93\ \text{ZHz} \quad (\text{record: } 7.96\ \text{MeV}), \]

using the ladder's inherited spacing \(h\Delta\nu \approx 10.08\,A^{-1/3}\) ZHz (record orientation: \(41.7\,A^{-1/3}\) MeV; booked with the Inner Census pricing run and retaining that source grade). The \(A^{1/3}\) cancels inside this model, so the ladder seat is census-independent — the same cancellation that makes the extant coefficient a constant, here falling out of the spacing law rather than being assumed. Two structural notes, booked: a pure rung is degenerate, so re-filing within a partially filled rung is locally free — the quadratic lives in the seam staircase (the discrete check in scripts/valley.py reads 1.79–2.22 ZHz around the continuum value, seams and all); and the seat disagrees with the capacity-density read:

The seat fork, named. Total \(a_{\text{sym}}\): 5.79–6.03 ZHz (frozen-capacity seat) versus 5.02–5.26 ZHz (ladder seat; record 24.0–24.9 vs 20.8–21.7 MeV). Both are ledger-native candidate seats. 4 · The Valley — Integer Nuclides on a Smooth Envelope

Total re-filing cost at fixed \(A\) (smooth part; the tagged parity shadow is treated separately in §5):

\[ E(Z;A) = a_{\text{sym}}\frac{(A-2Z)^2}{A} + a_c\frac{Z(Z-1)}{A^{1/3}} + \Delta_{\text{dress}}(Z;A), \qquad \Delta_{\text{dress}} = \begin{cases} -x_{\text{ex}}Z^{4/3}/A^{1/3}-\delta_c Z,&\text{dressed},\\ 0,&\text{bare}, \end{cases} \]
\[ Z_{\text{env}}^{*}(A)=\arg\min_{Z\in\mathbb{R}}E, \qquad Z_{\text{int}}^{*}(A)=\arg\min_{Z\in\mathbb{Z}}E . \]

The bracket holds the two refinement entries: the booked exchange layer \(x_{\text{ex}} = 128.2\) EHz (§2b), and the class rest step \(\delta_c = 189.1\) EHz (record 0.782 MeV) — re-filing core→pattern returns that much trapped rate because the core-class quantum weighs more than the pattern pair it becomes (extant: \(m_n - m_p - m_e\)). The step is a measured import, tagged: the corpus has not derived the class rest split, and this page will not pretend it has. Four historical combinations of the two seat readings and bare/dressed books are retained as an audit cross. The first edition gated the fractional envelope \(Z_{\text{env}}^{*}\) against integer nuclides; the 2026-09-08 referee repair correctly gates \(Z_{\text{int}}^{*}\) instead (scripts/valley.py). 5 · The Parity Staircase — Model Consequences, Not Lifetime Predictions

The pairing entry (Mass Ledger P4: within one class, reversed-orientation quanta mesh pairwise; order ee < eo < oo forced) adds a parity term to the parabola: even-even discounted by \(\Delta\), odd-odd surcharged by \(\Delta\), odd-\(A\) untouched (one class is odd either way — the term is class-blind). Three model consequences fall out of the staircase run on the smooth valley (magnitude class \(\Delta \approx 2.90/\sqrt{A}\) ZHz — record \(12/\sqrt{A}\) MeV — used as the tagged comparative shadow: the ledger's own plateau count is its named open item):

  • Odd \(A\): normally one integer energetic floor. The parity term is class-blind at odd \(A\), so sampling one smooth convex parabola normally gives one minimum (two only at an exact half-integer tie). This is the limited energetic analogue of the Mattauch isobar rule. The completion-rate book is separate, so an energetically downhill re-filing may still be extraordinarily slow.
  • The model odd-odd local-minimum window closes near \(A \approx 19\). The condition is internal to the tagged \(2.90/\sqrt A\) ZHz pairing shadow. N-14 and F-18 may orient the scale, but the page does not claim that F-18 is marginal in lifetime or \(Q\)-value, and it does not validate the threshold against a complete evaluated census.
  • Even-\(A\) pairs of local one-step minima. Where the pairing shadow lifts the odd-odd bridge, two even-even neighbors can each sit below that bridge. The script's first pair near \(A\approx32\) and its rising 20%→93% fraction are outputs of this smooth-plus-shadow model, not measured demography. Crucially, the bridge is lifted, not trapped: K-40 decays downhill toward both Ca-40 and Ar-40. Its ≈1.25 Gyr lifetime shows that a downhill ledger direction does not by itself set the completion rate; this page does not calculate that rate.

The POAMS result is re-filing: a change in the two-class census at fixed total count. Neighboring atomic masses state which direction lowers the booked total. The page does not yet derive the completion-rate rule, branching among available re-filings, or half-life; those are native open books, not missing pieces of an extant mechanism that POAMS must import.

6 · Where the Smooth Valley Should Miss — and Does

The smooth ledger deliberately carries no shell closures, deformation, or detailed pairing structure. Some residuals coincide with known closure charges: Ca-40 carries the 20/20 double closure; Sn-120 sits at \(Z=50\); Pb-208 at 82/126. That coincidence makes shell corrections a plausible next layer, not an explanation already earned. The six-anchor comparison neither specifies a quantitative shell correction in advance nor shows that every residual has the predicted sign. The honest booking is therefore simple: the smooth bend survives; its local residuals remain model error until the missing layers are computed independently.

6b · The Conditional Seat Model — Formula and Target-Known Contact Identification

The keystone. Beta re-filing moves quanta between the two class copies of one ladder filled in the same well. Moving \(x\) quanta from a symmetric split costs \(E(n_0{+}x) + E(n_0{-}x) - 2E(n_0) = x^2/g(\nu_F)\), where \(g = dn/d\nu\) is the frontier level density per class. With \((N-Z)^2 = 4x^2\):

\[ a_{\text{kin}} \;=\; \frac{A}{4\,g(\nu_F)} \]

— for a specified one-body well, the seat is its frontier density, and both candidate seats are special cases of this formula: a sharp box at capacity density gives \(n \propto \nu^{3/2}\) hence \(a_{\text{kin}} = \tfrac13 E_F = 2.69\) ZHz, and the oscillator ladder gives \(n \propto \nu^3\) hence \(a_{\text{kin}} = h\Delta\nu\,A^{1/3}/\sqrt[3]{144} = 1.92\) ZHz (both reproduced as instrument controls). The remaining model question is: which well and contact prescription price the physical frontier? The candidate used here was booked for another front (Inner Census §6, the eyewall pricing run): \(V(r)=-C\,u(r)\), with \(C=8.668\) ZHz (record orientation: 35.85 MeV) and \(u(r)\) a capacity-pinned interior plus an E-L skin using \(\lambda(u)=1/(1+8u)\). The Mass Ledger audit now classifies \(C\), the capacity identification, and the measured-selected \(\lambda\) profile as quarantined/model inputs. The calculation below is therefore a conditional evaluation of that specified well, not a derivation of the physical well.

Within the specified well, the calculated seat is a mixture and moves with the census. The staircase-integrated frontier density of the specified well (scripts/seat_fork.py) prices the kinetic seat at 2.07 ZHz at \(A=63\) rising to 2.43 ZHz at \(A=238\) (record 8.55 → 10.05 MeV) — strictly between the pure seats, because the skin census fraction falls as \(A^{-1/3}\). The sharp box is this well with the skin truncated (2.69 ZHz, the no-skin limit); the oscillator is this well misprofiled (1.92 ZHz, the all-skin limit — harmonic books everywhere instead of only in the selected skin model). Bulk sea plus frontier skin correction, with weights fixed by that profile, places the result between the two limits. The fact that a composite inferred from this same valley also lies between them is in-sample consistency, not held-out validation.

The atomic-mass dressing. When the target is atomic-mass energetics, atomic masses carry the class step by identity — \(M(A,Z) = A\,m_n - Z\,\delta_c - B(A,Z)\) with \(\delta_c = 189.1\) EHz (record 0.782 MeV) — while the exchange layer retains its separately booked source-page grade (T-P3′, Mass Ledger). Omitting the class step is a truncation of the atomic-mass currency; including the exchange layer is a separate model choice. At \(A=238\), omitting the two dressing entries and inflating \(a_{\text{sym}}\) can produce similar shifts in the continuous envelope. This explains a numerical compensation, but the repaired integer gate does not empirically choose either package pairing: all four combinations pass.

Target-known identification, not a blind derivation. The candidate contact column \(C/(1+2\gamma)\) is evaluated at model-selected capacity; both its normalization and what the sub-capacity skin census pays remain unforced. Three ledger-consistent readings were compared: frozen-on-the-whole-census (CR-A), contact-strength \(\propto u\) (CR-B — the ledger's own \(\Delta(u)\) contact column is linear in \(u\)), and free-limit-skin (CR-C, the floor). Within the selected profile, CR-A applies a capacity value to a sub-capacity skin and lands +0.32 ZHz outside the target band; CR-B is closest (+0.06 ZHz), while CR-C also survives at −0.22 ZHz. But CR-B was expected before the confrontation and the 5.32 ZHz target was inferred from the same valley. Therefore CR-B is a target-known phenomenological identification, not an independent prediction, and the comparison does not uniquely select it. The script now says so before it prints the record.

The local channels failed their own controls. Two discriminators were registered to test the seat off the valley: odd-\(A\) isobar-parabola curvatures (\(\kappa = 8a_{\text{sym}}/A + 2a_c/A^{1/3} - \) exchange curvature; the class step is linear and drops out; the parity stagger is carried as an explicit fit term) and parabola apex offsets (which read the dressing directly). Scored as registered against five mid-shell chains (tagged AME2020 masses), every smooth package failed — including the control row, the extant fitted shadow itself (5.61 ZHz / 23.2 MeV fails 5/5). Band medians read 4.09 / 4.21 / 5.73 ZHz (record orientation: 16.9 / 17.4 / 23.7 MeV; light/mid/heavy) against the smooth books' 4.84–5.90 ZHz. The local mass surface contains structure the smooth ledger does not hold. The dressed-apex error correlates with the curvature residual at \(r=+0.92\) across the five selected chains. That coherence is consistent with a shared omitted layer—shell, deformation, collective, or a combination—but five chains do not identify a unique cause. Self-consistent extant mass models can generate deformation rather than adding it as an external correction, so no universal “added by hand” claim survives. Both local channels are disqualified as seat discriminators by the failure of the extant-shadow control.

7 · What This Buys

  • A smooth single-beta energetic floor. Given the displayed symmetry and far-field strain prices, minimizing the ledger cost produces the observed qualitative bend away from \(Z=A/2\). The six-chain audit places each package's integer minimum within two charge units of a selected evaluated one-step-beta reference. That is a useful orientation result, not a derivation of the full nuclide chart.
  • Direction, not dynamics. The sign of \(E(A,Z\!\pm\!1)-E(A,Z)\) says whether a one-step beta move is energetically downhill in this model. It does not say how fast that re-filing completes or which of several available re-filings wins. The ontological identification stands; its timing and competition rules remain open.
  • A conditional seat identity. The relation \(a_{\text{kin}}=A/[4g(\nu_F)]\) turns the seat question into a frontier-density question for a specified well. The skin-profile calculation supplies an \(A\)-dependent candidate between the sharp-box and oscillator limits. Its contact prescription is still an identification against a target already known from this valley, not a held-out prediction.
  • A parity program, not a parity result. Integer sampling plus the tagged pairing shadow generates possible odd-even staircases and paired local minima. Those are model outputs to test against a complete mass surface; they are not yet claims about empirical abundance, longevity, or decay rates.
