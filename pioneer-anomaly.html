The Pioneer Anomaly Mystery

In the 1980s, NASA discovered something impossible: Pioneer 10 and 11 spacecraft were slowing down more than predicted by Newtonian gravity. The probes experienced an unexpected 8.74 × 10⁻¹⁰ m/s² sunward acceleration.

"In the unlikely event that there is new physics, one does not want to miss it because one had the wrong mind-set."
— John Anderson, JPL

Dozens of explanations were proposed: thermal radiation, gas leaks, relativistic effects. None fully explained the anomaly. POAMS theory provides a simple solution: Newton's gravity ignores spin angular momentum.
