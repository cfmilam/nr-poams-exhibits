A source-grounded study of loop phase, excluded flux, coherence, and what a POAMS completion account must actually derive.

A phase is observed only when two histories close into a comparison. The experiment is decisive; its ontology is not uniquely fixed by the fringe shift.

2 · Experimental record

Aharonov and Bohm predicted that coherent charged paths enclosing flux acquire a relative phase even where the local magnetic field on the paths vanishes. Tonomura's electron-holography lineage used shielded magnetic flux to isolate the effect. Mesoscopic rings show flux-periodic conductance, while a dedicated time-of-flight experiment found no classical delay of the magnitude needed by lag accounts.

The raw receipt is not “the vector potential is a substance.” It is detection coordinates and fringe phase versus calibrated enclosed flux, with leakage fields, beam geometry, energy, visibility and apparatus state accounted for.

Sources: Aharonov & Bohm (1959); Tonomura et al. (1986); Caprez, Barwick & Batelaan (2007); Vaidman (2012).

3 · The imported invariant

\[\Delta\varphi=\frac{q}{\hbar}\oint\mathbf A\cdot d\boldsymbol\ell=\frac{q\Phi_B}{\hbar}.\]

For winding number \(n\), the phase multiplies by \(n\). Interference is periodic under \(\Phi_B\to\Phi_B+h/|q|\). An open-arm phase is gauge dependent; the completed two-arm comparison is observable.

\[\Delta\varphi_E=-\frac{q}{\hbar}\int\Delta V(t)\,dt.\]

The electric form is equally clear mathematically but harder experimentally because switching a potential can create ordinary fields and impulses. Common-mode voltage must be null; equal voltage-time area should give equal phase within bandwidth and coherence limits.

Ontology guardrail. The receipt establishes gauge-invariant loop holonomy. It does not make a particular gauge-dependent value of \(\mathbf A(\mathbf x)\) directly observable, nor does it exclude all source-inclusive local accounts.

4 · Quantitative scale

For an electron, one magnetic phase period is \(h/e=4.135667696\times10^{-15}\,\mathrm{Wb}\). For a ring of area \(1.00\,\mu\mathrm m^2\), the ideal field period is about 4.136 mT. A superconducting \(h/2e\) flux quantum produces a \(\pi\) electron AB phase—not a full electron period.

The distinction matters: topology alone does not select the coefficient. The coherent species and its coupling must be named.

5 · POAMS constraint ledger

  1. Name the closed relational observable; neither arm owns the measured phase alone.
  2. Recover signed species coupling: \(q\to-q\) reverses phase orientation.
  3. Derive flux proportionality and \(h/|q|\) periodicity rather than inserting them.
  4. Compose winding number and survive smooth path deformation at fixed linkage.
  5. Predict coherence loss from path information, noise and inelastic exchange.
  6. Do not require a classical delay excluded by experiment.
  7. Include source, shield and apparatus in any local exchange narrative.
  8. Treat switching causally, with induced fields and propagation.

The promising POAMS analogy is a completed relational circulation receipt: two histories rejoin, and the result depends on their winding relative to excluded circulation. The analogy stops where the quantitative state, \(q/\hbar\), dynamics and failure law begin.

6 · Experiments and engineering relevance

Source-inclusive discriminator

Measure fringe phase, arrival-time distribution and source mechanical/electrical witnesses in the same shots while stepping trapped flux. Phase without delay supports dispersionless holonomy; correlated delay supports a lag; retained source records should alter visibility or condition the phase.

Electric AB program

Use shielded coherent charge states, symmetric feeds, measured switching waveforms, field/impulse witnesses and reversal. Test oddness in \(\Delta V\), linearity in \(\int\Delta Vdt\), waveform-area equivalence and common-mode null.

Engineering value includes flux metrology, mesoscopic sensors, coherence diagnostics, shield verification and phase-register discipline. It carries no superluminal signalling implication: the result is read after ordinary causal recombination and apparatus operation.

7 · Useful nulls, opens and promotion criteria

  • Null delay with nonzero phase: closes the classical-lag route in the tested geometry.
  • No source-correlated residual: bounds extra completion terms while leaving the standard AB effect intact.
  • No electric phase after strict field/impulse closure: constrains the ideal electric implementation, not magnetic AB.
  • Visibility loss that tracks a source record: maps the coherence boundary rather than refuting the phase law.

Open work: full electric-AB custody, source-inclusive conservation, a native holonomy construction, and one technically feasible nonstandard discriminator. Promotion requires all four, or a comparably decisive native derivation.
