An instructional bridge between measured magnetomechanical reciprocity, POAMS angular-momentum bookkeeping, and the YIG engineering program.

Magnetic order and mechanical angular momentum exchange measurable receipts. That strong fact is not yet a POAMS support-force law.

Queue status and promotion gate

This page is a public holding pen for mature facts and unfinished POAMS work. Extant equations remain explicitly imported; the POAMS contribution is presently a constraint ledger and experimental architecture.

Promotion requires: a specimen-calibrated magnetic angular-momentum receipt; closure of ordinary torque, strain, thermal, field-gradient and vibration channels; a preregistered residual rule; and independent blinded replication. A beautiful analogy does not graduate itself.

2 · What was measured

Einstein–de Haas

Changing magnetization produces mechanical torque or rotation. Modern RF work shows EdH torque can rival the ordinary \(\boldsymbol\mu\times\mathbf B\) torque and can occupy a distinct quadrature near quasistatic equilibrium.

Barnett

Rotating a body changes its magnetization. Reversal estimators such as \([B(+\Omega)-B(-\Omega)]/2\) suppress even backgrounds and expose the odd-in-rotation signal.

Ultrafast demagnetization experiments further show that lattice strain and phonon channels can accept angular momentum promptly. Ferrimagnetic compensation measurements show that net magnetization and net angular momentum are not interchangeable observables.

Primary record: Barnett (1915); Barnett (1917); Mori et al. (2020); Dornes et al. (2019); Imai et al. (2019).

3 · Imported mathematics and the complete boundary

\[\boldsymbol\mu=\gamma\mathbf J,\qquad \mathbf J_{mag}=\frac{V\mathbf M}{\gamma}.\]

The dimensions close: \([VM]=\mathrm{J/T}\), so \([VM/\gamma]=\mathrm{J\,s}\), angular momentum.

\[\Delta\mathbf L_{mech}=-\Delta\mathbf J_{mag},\qquad \boldsymbol\tau_{EdH}=-\frac{V}{\gamma}\frac{d\mathbf M}{dt}.\]

For harmonic modulation, \(|\widetilde\tau|=\omega V|\widetilde M|/|\gamma|\). Frequency and phase therefore belong in the raw receipt. This torque equation does not predict vertical support force.

\[\mathcal H_{rot}=-\boldsymbol\Omega\cdot\mathbf J,\qquad \mathbf B_\Omega=\frac{\boldsymbol\Omega}{\gamma}.\]

For a linear specimen, \(M=\frac{\chi}{1+N\chi}\frac{\Omega}{\mu_0\gamma}\). Hysteresis, anisotropy, domains, saturation and sublattice compensation can invalidate this small-signal comparator.

4 · Scale receipts

For a 10.00 mm YIG sphere with \(\rho=5.17\times10^3\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}\) and \(M_s=142\,\mathrm{kA/m}\): \(V=5.236\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m^3}\), \(m=2.707\,\mathrm g\), \(\mu=7.435\times10^{-2}\,\mathrm{A\,m^2}\), and, using the free-electron magnitude only as a scale reference, \(|J_{mag}|=4.223\times10^{-13}\,\mathrm{J\,s}\).

A 1% modulation at 100 Hz corresponds to \(\tau_{pk}\approx2.65\times10^{-12}\,\mathrm{N\,m}\). At \(\Omega=10^3\,\mathrm{rad/s}\), the Barnett field scale is only about 5.68 nT. These are audit targets, not specimen predictions.

5 · POAMS bridge—and its hard stop

The productive POAMS reading is direct: magnetic-state changes post angular momentum into the specimen, lattice, suspension, cavity, source and environment. The apparatus must be treated as one ledger.

Not earned: EdH does not prove weight change; Barnett does not prove a persistent topological register; neither maps FMR to Călugăreanu twist/writhe; neither fixes a support-response sign; neither selects POAMS over extant conservation theory.

The open bridge is a separately specified law from characterized magnetic state to a support observable after all ordinary exchange paths have been closed.

5b · Operational tariff, reciprocity gate, and the compensation trap [added 2026-09-27]

Operational, Coulomb-free tariff. Over a declared closed window with reservoir postings bounded, the magnetic angular-momentum change is defined from receipts, not from an imported electron story:

\[\Delta\mathbf J_{mag}\equiv-\Delta\mathbf L_{mech}\ \text{(closed window)},\qquad \gamma_{eff}\equiv\frac{V\,\Delta M}{\Delta J_{mag}}.\]

Co-registered magnetometry and mechanical-impulse records fix \(\gamma_{eff}\) per specimen and state; no \(e/2m\) value is imported.

Reciprocity as a conditional gate. On the same specimen in the same preparation state, one-substance bookkeeping structurally expects \(\gamma_{EdH}=\gamma_{Barnett}\). Empirically that equality is an acceptance test, not a given: it is adopted as a preregistered gate in the Alignment, Phase Drive & Support Response program (protocol §14); failure blocks downstream sign interpretation for that specimen state.

M-null ≠ J-null. The compensation record in §2 already shows net magnetization and net angular momentum are not interchangeable observables: a magnetometry null never certifies an angular-momentum null, and conversely. The two are booked as distinct typed fields (data schema §9).

6 · Experimental and engineering program

  • Classical torsion: reverse axial magnetization; record angular displacement, current, local field, magnetization, temperature and phase.
  • RF EdH: jointly fit in-phase and quadrature motion against \(M\), \(dM/dt\), RF power and transfer functions.
  • Barnett rotor: measure \(\pm\Omega\), zero-speed points, vibration, temperature and residual field.
  • Compensation: vary temperature/composition while separately measuring magnetization and rotation-induced response.

YIG work should add torsion/yaw readout, three-axis magnetometry, common-clock RF phase, thermometry, accelerometers and a strain witness. Controls must include \(\pm M\), resonant/detuned/off drive, phase quadratures, amplitude and frequency scans, material dummies and geometry reversal.
