The World Is Language

What this is, and why — a Normal Realist reading of two old questions, and the open questions it preserves.

facts, not things · bookings, not objects · grammar, not stage · a sentence laughed at in Swansea, 1960s · a logos, not a physis

This exhibit is an extrapolation of the foundational work of N. Vivian “Viv” Pope & Dr. Anthony D. Osborne, PhD, in the philosophy of Normal Realism (NR) and the mathematics of the Pope Osborne Angular Momentum Synthesis (POAMS).
No equations are required of the reader. §2 gives excerpts from Pope’s retrospective account, with omissions marked and typography normalized; it is not a transcript of Rhees. Physics receipts and primary texts are linked below. Distilled from a workshop dialogue of 2026-08-27 — see the Notes.
How to read this essay. native premise One substance, closure and two-ended completion are the adopted POAMS starting points. defined reading “Language” names the relational organization of that substance, not a second material or a human description imposed on it. source / test Historical testimony and measured results have the limits given at their links. open argument The philosophical identification and the ultimate why-question are not theorems established by a linguistic resemblance.

1 · The two questions everyone asks

A night sky invites two old questions. What is this? And why is there this, rather than nothing?

A description of how the world behaves does not, by itself, answer why there is a world. One familiar picture starts with a great container scattered with things; another adds an author standing behind the book. Normal Realism challenges that picture: an explanation that merely adds another bearer invites the same question about the bearer. This is a criticism of that explanatory move, not a claim that every scientist or theologian makes it.

This page develops the corpus’s proposed answer by examining its grammar. One formulation of the why-question is excluded by the adopted premises; the broader question remains open.

THE ANSWER ON ONE CARD.

What is this? In this reading, not a void with independently borne things in it. There are completed exchanges — whole-turn postings of one substance, each entry co-signed at its endpoints — and “the world” is the ledger they compose. Facts, not things; bookings, not objects; grammar, not stage. The world is language in the sense defined here: discrete completed tokens, closure and conservation rules, and relations through which entries can be reconciled. “Language” names this organization itself; a full equivalence with human language has not been proved.

Why is there this rather than nothing? The adopted no-zero, no-outside ontology excludes a chooser selecting a world at an earlier external instant. It also refuses to turn “Nothing” into another object. That does not prove that all contrastive why-questions are meaningless. The remaining questions include why these rules, and how fecund they are — what additional premises admit atoms, stars and readers of the ledger. Those questions remain on the board; no theorem here says they can never be answered.

2 · The sentence they laughed at — Swansea, 1960s

The title comes to us through Viv Pope’s account of Swansea in the 1960s. Pope, then a GPO lineman developing his ideas, reports meeting Rush Rhees and later telling his Maths and Physics associates about that contact. They rejected what Pope described as Rhees’ claim. This is Pope’s retrospective testimony, not a surviving verbatim speech by Rhees or evidence that Rhees addressed those scientists himself. Rhees was Wittgenstein’s student, friend and literary executor; his papers are held by Swansea University. Pope writes in the third person:

“The driving force of the local University College Philosophy Department was, at that time, an influential philosopher by the name of Rush Rhees. Our lineman was informed… that this Rhees had sat on the right hand of the ‘Great Wittgenstein’… In a meeting the student had arranged for him with Rhees, at Rhees’ home, he was roundly informed that Berkeley was now ‘old hat’, and had been ‘overtaken’ by Wittgenstein. Philosophy, he was told, was now all about language…”Pope, The Odyssey, Chapter III, printed p.19 (excerpt)
“It was when he informed his Maths and Physics associates of this new ‘Wittgenstein’ contact of his that he learned how deep was the antipathy between these scientists and the philosophers. So far as the scientists were concerned, Rhees’ claim that ‘the world is language’ was completely ridiculous, and they were fulsome in expressing their rejection of that way of thinking.”Pope, The Odyssey, Chapter III, printed p.19; emphasis added

The two factions were, in Pope’s telling, “at daggers drawn”: for Rhees, “Ordinary Language” was sacrosanct and scientism a curse-word; for the scientists, the philosophers were innumerate mystics. Pope was caught in the middle — “with ‘Einstein’ as irrelevant to the Wittgensteinians as ‘Wittgenstein’ was to the Einsteinians” — and finally walked out of Rhees’ seminar, “as obtrusively as possible, on one of Rush Rhees’ most pontifical pronouncements on the evils of ‘Scientism’.” His theory, as he began to call it, “was forced to continue its logical course… in a veritable no-man’s land between two completely hostile Educational factions.”

And then, years later, the flash:

“Then, in a flash, it occurred to him that what Rush Rhees and his associates… had been rejecting in the name of ‘Scientism’ must have been this mechanistically projected world of ‘theory’ and all the jargon that had become associated with it. The world these ‘Wittgensteinians’ wanted to describe was, ostensibly, the same world that he did, the world of undivided, ordinary commonsense experience…”Pope, The Odyssey, Chapter IV, printed p.25 (excerpt)

Our reading is that the dispute separated two tasks worth bringing together: scrutiny of the concepts and scrutiny of the calculations. Pope pursued a synthesis in that no-man’s land. This essay proposes that “world as language” helps articulate it. The historical episode motivates the proposal; it does not prove that all philosophers refused arithmetic, that the two factions held exhaustive halves of an answer, or that every physical test verifies this philosophical identity.

3 · What this is not: the void with grains

The picture Normal Realism refuses is a great emptiness scattered with grains — galaxies, stars, atoms — like sand on a floor that was there first. Its alternative is relational: the apparent floor belongs to the description, not to a separate substance.

Dark to the eye does not mean absent from the record. The measured microwave sky has a spectrum close to a 2.725 K blackbody; NASA’s FIRAS spectrum and residuals state the observational result and its calibration. That is a finite-band, instrument-mediated record, not an observation of every possible depth. It neither proves nor requires the container picture. Reading it as a shared ledger is the native interpretation, distinct from the measurement.

And it is not a void. Between two seats there is no stretch of stage waiting to be crossed: there is a booking, or there is not yet one. “Empty space” is a name for relations nobody is currently occupying — grammar for unwritten entries — not a substance that could be scattered with anything. Distance is a relation in the books, and where there is no relation there is no “where” at all.

The native reading of the grains is closure architecture: one substance organized at different scales. The census test must be stated separately. The Polyverse — Audit, §9.2 found tracer-dependent counts in limited windows, but its wider-window controls showed data and comparison catalogues climbing together toward dimension 2.9. The earlier no-turnover claim was withdrawn; the final LRG residual closed under the survey’s fiber-assignment treatment. Through that pipeline, the test did not distinguish the ontologies. It remains a reproducible census result, not a measurement proving “there is no container.”

“The world is the totality of facts, not of things.”Wittgenstein, Tractatus Logico-Philosophicus 1.1 — the ledger, in one line, 1921

4 · What this is: a ledger of whole turns

One substance: angular momentum — circulation rather than a bearer of circulation. POAMS adopts whole completed closures as its persistent ledger tokens. A partially advanced phase is not a fractional completed posting; that does not forbid phase coordinates within a circulation. Closure and balanced accounting are the native conditions of persistence, not by themselves a derivation of every allowed species. The momentum is the thing, not an attribute attached to it — the grin, closed into a whole turn, is the cat.

In the two-ended account, emission and absorption constitute one completed entry, with no independently travelling content between them. This is not a claim that the observer’s dated records have zero separation. The receipt-time relation can remain finite, as explained in Time-Rope and The Logos. The world is read as a ledger writing itself: “objects” are stable organizations and “time” is the accumulation of exchanges. That last identification is an adopted interpretation, not a consequence of notation alone.

The one equation, with its scope. In $L=dS/d\theta$, $S$ is action and $\theta$ is phase angle; $L$ is angular momentum, action per angle. A temporal angular rate is instead $\Omega=d\theta/dt$. For constant $L$, a full cycle has action $2\pi L$; with $L=\hbar$ this is $h=2\pi\hbar$. The equation does not identify angular momentum with a frequency or prove that everything is time.

Read that structure as a language: tokens are completed postings; grammar is the rule-set for their composition; sentences are completed relations. Here “language” means this objective organization, not a second substrate carrying symbols about a hidden first one. Ordinary words can have parts, and people can speak to themselves: those linguistic facts are not exceptions to a physical closure rule because the two descriptions are not already the same formal theory. The proposed connection is about rules and intelligible relations, not a proof that every utterance requires a literal two-person transaction.

5 · Why — the half that dissolves

One way of asking “why this rather than nothing?” imagines a shelf of alternatives, a choosing instant before all events, and someone outside the whole selecting an entry. That formulation conflicts with the adopted premises: the books have no first entry, and there is no seat outside all exchange. Normal Realism also refuses to make “Nothing” an object beside the world, as though absence were another substance waiting on a shelf.

The restricted conclusion matters. Excluding an outside choosing-event does not exclude every contrastive question. An internal reader can ask why one rule holds rather than another without positing a physical shelf, an earlier instant or a chooser. Saying that “Nothing” names no object is not, by itself, a proof that such a question has an empty logical comparison class. This essay adopts the refusal of reified nothingness and dissolves the external-selection picture; it does not claim to have demonstrated the necessary existence of this particular world.

6 · Why — the residue that will not dissolve

But only half dissolves. Here is the part that survives every grammatical treatment we know, stated as plainly as we can state it.

The grammar is proposed as fecund; closure alone does not demonstrate its full fecundity. The corpus describes a hierarchy of persistent organizations and readers who can record other records. But “only closures persist” is a constraint, not a sufficient construction of atoms, stars or auditors. Each claimed level needs its additional premises and derivation. The existence of readers motivates the question; it does not make a one-line answer sufficient.

Anthony Osborne described the foundation to C.F. Milam as “an information-based worldview” personal communication, as reported by Milam. Here information means the distinguishable organization and relations of the one circulation substrate, not another substance beside angular momentum. Describing a record as about another record additionally requires a specified correspondence or use. That semantic role is not supplied merely by calling a configuration information; this page does not derive consciousness or aboutness from closure alone.

The split. Adopted: the native grammar — closure, conservation, two-ended completion, no external seat and no first entry. Conditional results and continuing work: which organizations and ratios follow when further premises are supplied, with their receipts and open inputs. Open: why these premises, and what makes a ledger-reader possible. There is no solution here to the ultimate why-question, but there is also no proof that explanation would require an outside observer. An internal derivation can explain a structural consequence without explaining why its starting rules exist.

7 · The world is language — the proposed identity

The title is a philosophical identification developed here, not a result obtained by joining two quotations.

Two Wittgensteinian touchpoints motivate it. The Tractatus, 1.1, speaks of facts rather than things. Philosophical Investigations, §43, explains meaning through use for a large class of cases, explicitly not every case. Reading facts as completed relations and use as their role in a ledger gives this essay its phrase facts that happen; grammar in time. That is our synthesis, not a theorem attributed to Wittgenstein.

The private-language argument and co-signature have a proposed structural connection, not an established theorem identity. In §243 Wittgenstein allows intelligible speech to oneself; §258 asks what would distinguish correct use of a private sensation sign from merely seeming correct. POAMS’s two-endpoint rule concerns a different predicate: completion of a physical exchange. Neither claim entails the other without bridge definitions and a proof in both directions. Reconciliation is the connection this essay emphasizes; it is not a derivation of physical endpoint counting from linguistic criteria.

The language/world limit is read here in observer-centred terms. A seat’s accessible records are limited; the native polyverse is not identified with that seat’s view. This is our application of Tractatus 5.6, not a deduction of a cosmic timescale from it. The Polyverse carries the separate cosmological premises and observations. The proposed realism is shared and relational: the world is not dependent on our human vocabulary.

Within the defined reading, the identity is constitutive, not representational: an entry is a completed exchange, not a report concealing a separate bearer. A later record about it is a further relation. Calling the first-level organization “language” preserves this distinction and the one-substance commitment. It proposes what language can mean at this level; it does not claim that every feature of human semantics has thereby been derived.

8 · Extant physics, diagnosed: grammatical error

Under this reading, a number of familiar physical nouns conceal choices about what exists. Philosophical Investigations §109 connects philosophical confusion with language. The table states Normal Realism’s proposed grammar repair; renaming a concept is not, on its own, a calculation or a resolution of its measured phenomena.

Pope’s Naked Emperor was a grammar critic before he was anything else: the chalk circle is a syntax error — a boundary drawn by a hand the boundary claims to contain. And the corpus’s working discipline down to this week’s edits — extant nouns quoted and tagged, native verbs forced where the extant art has no word — is the same repair, carried out entry by entry.

9 · Where he went silent, the audit stays open

The Tractatus, 6.522 and 7, places what shows itself beyond what can be expressed and ends with an injunction to silence. This essay takes that as a philosophical touchpoint, not as a theorem forbidding investigation of complex organization.

Our response is to keep an audit open. No ultimate explanation of why the rules exist is supplied here. More bounded questions can still be made testable: how many configurations a specified rule permits, which structures a stated model produces, and which predictions survive their controls. “Fecundity” is not a single measured quantity until a rule-set, counting procedure and scope are defined. Internal reasoning may explain such structural results; the lack of an outside observer does not prohibit it.

Pope’s own account supplies the language of logos:

“Logos, in the original Greek, simply meant something like what we would nowadays describe as ‘language,’ ‘reason,’ ‘ratio’. It was the ‘something’ that for any normal thinking person obviously has to be there and acts as the ordering principle of the universe.”Pope, The Odyssey, XIV.5, printed p.113 (excerpt beginning mid-sentence; initial capital normalized)

The distinct phrase “a logos rather than a physis” appears in Wondering in the Wilderness, “Dimensions of existence”. These passages support the intellectual lineage. This essay extends it by identifying the world's relational organization as language; the extension should stand on its own stated premises and arguments. The grammar is what we audit.

In this reading, the world is written in whole turns, co-signed at every exchange —
a language that contains its own readers.
Why these rules remains a question.
What they entail remains an audit.
