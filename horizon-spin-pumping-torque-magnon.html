2. Three processes—do not conflate them

Spin pumping

A precessing magnetic order parameter exports angular momentum at an interface. A common imported convention is

$$\mathbf J_L^{pump}={\hbar\over4\pi}g_{\uparrow\downarrow}^{eff}\,\mathbf m\times\dot{\mathbf m}.$$

The formula is a constitutive model. Mixing conductance is measured or fitted; it is not a universal POAMS constant.

Spin torque

An injected interfacial angular-momentum flux changes the magnetic dynamics. Damping-like and field-like components need separate phase and polarity tests. Heating and nonlinear mode selection can mimic a threshold.

Magnon transport

Inside YIG, coherent waves, diffusive magnon populations, and thermally driven transport use different state variables. A diffusive signal can follow

$$\nabla^2\mu_m={\mu_m\over\lambda_m^2},$$

but $\lambda_m$ is not a universal coherence length.

Electrical transduction

The detector reads voltage after charge redistribution in the metal—not angular momentum directly. Odd/even harmonics, field reversal, contact geometry and thermal lag are part of the receipt.

3. Measured receipts and quantitative scale

  • Kajiwara et al. demonstrated electrical injection, YIG transmission and electrical recovery.
  • Cornelissen et al. reported room-temperature nonlocal transport to 40 µm and a fitted diffusion length near 9.4 µm in their geometry.
  • Collet et al. reported spin–orbit-torque auto-oscillation in YIG/Pt discs, with polarity, threshold and nonlinear-mode caveats.
  • Chen et al. found no evidence for coherent spin pumping in their tested geometry—a useful scoped negative, not a universal null.
Units warning. “Spin current” may mean angular-momentum flux, particle-number flux, or charge-equivalent $(2e/\hbar)J_L$. A number without convention, area, bandwidth and calibration path is not portable.

4. POAMS constraint and engineering relevance

The POAMS touchstone asks for the actual circulation and its complete apparatus account. Here the named circulation is the driven magnetic angular-momentum organization; the interface, YIG, metal contacts, supports, drive source and return path belong to one ledger. That framing is useful. It does not derive $g_{\uparrow\downarrow}^{eff}$, the spin Hall angle, $\sigma_m$, $\lambda_m$, or the voltage.

Engineering use now

Mode spectroscopy, damping control, nonlocal transport, thermal/phase separation and polarity reversals directly inform the YIG support-response apparatus and magnetic interconnect design.

Prospective discriminator

At fixed static alignment, toggle coherent microwave drive and compare support response while recording absorbed power, mode population, detector voltage and mechanical artifacts. The preregistered sign must track a named twist/writhe deposition channel, not an after-the-fact handedness story.
