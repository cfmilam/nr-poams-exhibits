The Navigation Engine

This exhibit is an extrapolation of the foundational work of N. Vivian (Viv) Pope and Dr. Anthony D. Osborne, PhD, in the philosophy of Normal Realism (NR) and the mathematics of the Pope Osborne Angular Momentum Synthesis (POAMS).

Operational POAMS orbital mechanics — the useful core distilled from Max Franks's Mathematical Extrapolation of the Synthesis

In POAMS there is no gravitational force. A free body rides its natural (force‑free) orbit; "weight" is only the bill for holding it off that orbit, and every orbital quantity is angular‑momentum keeping its books. Max Franks — an aeronautical engineer — pushed that idea toward something an engineer can actually fly: equations of motion built from observed angular momentum instead of Newton's $G$.

This exhibit pulls out the parts of that work that are genuinely operational and POAMS‑clean, and turns them into a working tool. The parts that lean on constructs POAMS sets out to dissolve — or that contradict the Liturgy — are named and left aside at the bottom, not carried forward.

A · Orbit From Observables

Franks' first practical result: an orbit is fully fixed by locally measured speeds and radii — without knowing the central mass or $G$. Conservation of angular momentum gives the far speed, and the natural velocity is simply the mean of the apoapsis and periapsis speeds:

$$r_a v_a = r_p v_p \qquad v_0=\tfrac12\,(v_a+v_p)\qquad r_0=\frac{2\,r_a r_p}{r_a+r_p}\qquad \beta=\frac{a}{b^2}=\frac1{r_0}$$

Everything on the right is derived from the three sliders alone — no $M$, no $G$. $\mu=h\,v_0$ is the measured orbital invariant (numerically equal to $GM$, but obtained from observation, not postulated as a force).

B · Spin and the Weight Sign

Add a second rotation — spin — and its kinetic energy enters the force bookkeeping as a ratio $K_s/K_n$ tilted by the angle $\theta$ between the spin and orbital axes. Aligned spin stiffens the natural orbit (heavier); opposed spin loosens it (lighter), reaching a force‑free, weightless state when the two match:

$$F=\frac{L\,v_n}{r^2}\sqrt{1+\frac{K_s}{K_n}\cos\theta}\qquad\Rightarrow\qquad F\to 0 \ \text{ when spin opposes orbit, } \ \tfrac{K_s}{K_n}\cos\theta\to-1$$

This is the POAMS variable‑$G$ / weight‑sign channel made operational: co‑sense spin → heavier, anti‑sense → lighter, equal‑and‑opposed → weightless. It is exactly the effect the YIG weight‑change experiment is built to read off by the sign of its result.

C · The G‑free Equations of Motion

The pay‑off: a Lagrangian in which the central body's mass and $G$ never appear — only the measured geometric invariant $\beta=1/r_0$ and the coordinates. This is the propagator a navigation system actually integrates.

$$\mathcal L=\tfrac{m}{2}\!\left(\dot r^2+r^2\dot\theta^2\sin^2\!\phi+r^2\dot\phi^2\right)+m\beta\!\left(r\dot r^2+r^3\dot\theta^2\sin^2\!\phi+r^3\dot\phi^2\right)$$

Take the planar case ($\phi=\tfrac\pi2$) and propagate a body from apoapsis using only the invariant $\mu=h\,v_0$ read off in Panel A. The trajectory it draws is the ellipse — the observable‑built engine flies the orbit:

What was kept — and what was left aside

Preserved (operational, POAMS‑clean):

  • KEPT Orbit from observables — $v_0=\tfrac12(v_a+v_p)$, natural radius $r_0$, invariant $\mu=h v_0$ — a real navigation hook.
  • KEPT Spin as an energy ratio $K_s/K_n$ setting the weight sign — the variable‑$G$ mechanism, testable by the YIG experiment.
  • KEPT The $G$‑free Lagrangian and equations of motion as the navigation propagator.

Set aside (would dilute or contradict the synthesis):

  • ASIDE The rest‑energy section — it inserts $E=mc^2$ and treats $c$ as a velocity, directly contradicting Exhibit I's central point that $c$ is a pure constant, not a speed. (The POAMS definition needs no velocity anywhere: energy is turn rate, $E=h\nu$, and mass is trapped rate, $m=h\nu/c^2$, with $c^2$ the ledger exchange rate — see Mass as Trapped Rate.)
  • ASIDE The Coulomb re‑derivation — it is the Bohr model re‑expressed, inserting $\alpha$ and $c$ to land a number it already assumed. No new prediction.
  • ASIDE "Relative angular momentum" left undefined — it quietly mixes spin with relative orbital angular momentum (the orbit‑vs‑spin conflation POAMS specifically warns against).
  • ASIDE The strong/weak "nuclear force" reading of a sign change — asserted, not derived.

$\mu=h v_0$ is honest bookkeeping, not a derivation of gravity: it equals $GM$ and is obtained by measurement. The value of Franks' work is that it makes POAMS compute — the philosophy becomes a propagator you can fly.

Scope — one orbit, not the n‑body solar system

A limitation worth stating plainly: Max's derivation resolves and propagates a single, localized orbit. The whole engine is built from one set of local observables — the speeds and radii at apoapsis and periapsis — which fix one geometric invariant, $\beta=1/r_0$, and one orbital invariant, $\mu=h v_0$. That $\mu$ is numerically the Newtonian $GM$ of that one body, but obtained strictly from observation, with no invisible in vacuo fields or forces. That is exactly its strength and its boundary.

Because the equations of motion ride on invariants read off a specific orbit's extremes, the material does not address:

  • OPEN Multi‑body / n‑body dynamics — how a computer running these equations would compute simultaneous interactions among several attracting bodies at once.
  • OPEN Transitions between spheres of influence — how $\beta$ and $\mu$ would be re‑measured and re‑established when a craft hands off from one body's neighbourhood to another's.
  • OPEN Complex environments with multiple barycentres — navigating the real solar system, where no single two‑body invariant holds across the whole trajectory.

None of this refutes the POAMS core — a single force‑free orbit really is fixed by its own observables. But the leap from one clean invariant to a full n‑body propagator is the same age‑old, analytically intractable problem that Newtonian mechanics also cannot close in closed form; POAMS re‑books the accounting in angular momentum, it does not dissolve the many‑body difficulty. Recasting the single‑orbit invariants into a piecewise, patched‑conic or fully numerical n‑body scheme is left as open work. Section D below takes up exactly this boundary — separating what dissolves (the framing), what restates (the ledger), and what stands (the arithmetic of divergence).

D · The Two-Body "Problem," Dissolved — and What That Buys for n

Newton himself refused the picture his mathematics was read as endorsing: that one body may act upon another at a distance through a vacuum, without the mediation of anything else, “is to me so great an absurdity that I believe no man who has in philosophical matters a competent faculty of thinking can ever fall into it” (letter to Bentley, 1693). The standard escape was to add machinery — a field to mediate the pull, then a particle (the graviton) to carry the field — each patch inheriting the void it was invented to fill. POAMS takes the other exit: there is no pull to mediate.

The two-body problem is a scheinproblem — a problem of the picture, not of nature. There are no two bodies (there are two organized circulations of the one substance) and no attracting (nothing crosses a gap). What remains is real, and it is one thing: a single coupled angular-momentum ledger with two centres, each riding the natural radius the joint books assign — Panel A's $r_0$, written for both partners about the common barycentre. Kepler's ellipses survive untouched, as conservation identities of the one ledger, not the choreography of mutual pulls.

And the instantaneity that scandalized Newton is kept, not cured — relocated from force to bookkeeping. Nothing travels instantly, because nothing travels at all: in Normal Realism the elementary transfer is “an immediate (non-mediated), instantaneous and distance-less” quantum of action passed “by direct inter-resonance” between atoms, with “no sensibly describable 'in between'” (Osborne & Pope, Light-Speed, Gravitation and Quantum Instantaneity, 2007). The coupling of two circulations is an instantaneous harmonic inter-resonance — the standing consistency of one system's books, with no gap to cross except on the observer's map. What is not schein: the exchange dynamics. Tides, resonance capture, precession are genuine transfers of winding between the two centres — the perihelion member of the $\pi:3\pi:6\pi$ anholonomy ladder lives exactly there.

Then the n-body scandal, read honestly. For $n\ge 3$ there is no closed-form general solution, and the reason was proved, not conjectured: Bruns (1887) and Poincaré (1890s) showed the system's only algebraic/analytic integrals are the ten classical ones — energy, the three components of total momentum, the three of total angular momentum, and the centre-of-mass pair. For 140 years that theorem was read as defeat. Restate it in momenta and it inverts into the founding claim of this exhibit series: the exactly conserved content of an $n$-body system is its momenta — nothing else. The theorem does not say nature's books fail; it says the continuum map's demand — analytic trajectories, in closed form, for all time — was never going to be honoured, while the momentum ledger never once slips. Bruns' theorem is not the obituary of celestial mechanics; it is the inventory of what is real.

Restating all such problems in momenta — what it delivers, and what it honestly cannot:

  • DISSOLVED The mediation problem. No force crosses a void at any $n$; there is one ledger with $n$ centres. Gravitons, fields in vacuo and action-at-a-distance paradoxes go with the pull they were invented to save.
  • RESTATED Solvability itself. The two-body case is “solvable” precisely because variables exist in which the momenta sit still — the classical action–angle variables, where the momenta are constants and the angles advance at fixed rates $\omega$. Solving was always finding the momentum description; POAMS says those variables are the ontology, not a computational trick.
  • RESTATED Structure over trajectories. For $n\ge3$ the working description is the resonance network: circulations couple where their rates lock in low-order harmonic ratios, and stay effectively separate where the ratios are incommensurate. The 4:2:1 lock of Io–Europa–Ganymede, Pluto protected inside Neptune's 3:2, the Kirkwood gaps swept clean at 3:1 and 5:2 — these are instantaneous harmonic inter-resonances at planetary scale, the same frequency-matched exchange that runs spectral lines at atomic scale. Modern dynamics already computes this way — KAM persistence for sufficiently irrational ratios, chaos where resonance channels overlap — without owning the ontology. POAMS' addition: the frequency-ratio description is not a technique but the territory — rates of angular change exchanging quanta of winding through matched channels.
  • KEPT The operational engine scales. Max's Lagrangian dissolved $G$ and $M$ into one measured invariant for one orbit; the same move generalizes — one measured circulation invariant $\mu_i$ per centre, no $G$, no masses anywhere, and the summed ledger propagated by exactly the discrete stepping Panel C already runs. A symplectic integrator is momentum bookkeeping: it conserves total momentum and angular momentum by construction, turn by turn — integrate, don't differentiate. What the continuum map dismisses as “merely numerical” is, on the discreteum reading, the honest form of the dynamics.
  • OPEN The general POAMS n-body Lagrangian — pairwise measured invariants plus explicit resonance-channel accounting, replacing patched two-body conics — is real, unfinished work: Max's line continued, not completed.
  • STANDS Sensitive dependence. In any variables, neighbouring ledgers diverge exponentially (solar-system Lyapunov time ≈ 5 Myr — Laskar), and the discreteum denies the fantasy of infinitely precise initial books. Dissolution removes the scandal, not the arithmetic: long-horizon trajectories degrade into statistics, while the momenta — the only things the theorems ever certified — stay exact forever.
