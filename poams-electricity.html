1 · There is no charge-stuff in the wire

Ask the extant books what electricity is and the answer often arrives in a costume: a "flow of electrons" down the wire, like water in a hose. The bulk drift arithmetic below is enough to defeat kilometre-scale electron freight without importing a microscopic mean-free-path story: the stipulated carrier census drifts at a small fraction of a millimetre per second while the linked circuit is read on microsecond scales. Nothing in that arithmetic says that a particular constituent crosses the cable. The record then asks what the workshop's "charge" — $1.602\times10^{-19}$ coulomb per elementary count — names ontologically. POAMS answers: not a second substance or disembodied energy, but the signed circulation census developed below.

The books' own numbers — the hose audited. Take the extant model at its word. A one-ampere current in standard one-millimetre-square copper cable — carrier census $n = 8.49\times10^{28}\,/\mathrm{m^3}$ measured — drifts at

$$\begin{aligned}n&=8.49\times10^{28}\ \mathrm{m^{-3}},\\A&=10^{-6}\ \mathrm{m^2},\qquad e=1.602\times10^{-19}\ \mathrm C,\\[2pt]v_d&=\frac{I}{nAe}=7.35\times10^{-5}\ \mathrm{m/s}\\&\approx0.074\ \mathrm{mm/s}.\end{aligned}$$

— 157 days to traverse one kilometre of a cable that answers the switch in microseconds. And the stipulated drift inventory carries no useful freight: the drift kinetic energy of every counted carrier in the whole kilometre sums to $\tfrac{1}{2}m_e v_d^2 \cdot nAL \approx 2.1\times10^{-13}$ J — a 100-watt lamp spends that in 2.1 femtoseconds arithmetic on stated inputs. Standard circuit accounts therefore do not assign load delivery to that drift kinetic-energy inventory; their field-based bookkeeping commonly places the dominant delivery outside the conducting metal. That is an extant interpretation, not this page's ontology. POAMS declines both the electron-hose and field-in-a-void pictures in favor of the linked hand-off ledger in §3.

POAMS declines the costume. There is no charge-thing at any scale see Periodic Table §charge: what the books call a particle's "charge sign" is the sense of its intrinsic circulation — angular momentum, the one substance — and what they call electrostatic binding is spin kinetic energy. Substance, not attribute — the grin is the cat: a "charge" with no body, no location, and no bearer is Carroll's grin hanging in mid-air, and the circulation whose sense it names is the cat. The word charge survives only in the electrician's honest macro sense — a charged capacitor, a cannon charged with powder: a counted collection, not a property. The workshop language — volts, amps, coulombs, farads — survives untouched, because it was always a set of measures of phenomena, not an inventory of things. Pope, Synopsis

2 · The dead circuit — the record's decisive operational claim

Viv's decisive demonstration is an experiment any electrician can run. A source at A feeds a long circuit with a switch at the far end B and a sensitive detector at A watching what enters the wire. If energy truly traveled — if it were a stream of anything with a speed — it would set out from A regardless of what awaits it at B, like vehicles leaving a factory gate toward a distant bridge: they depart at full rate whether or not the bridge is open, and pile up if it isn't.

The record's claim (i): with the switch at B open, the detector at A registers nothing entering the circuit at all. Not a reduced current — none; the cited paper says the result persists with a large capacitor inserted in the open circuit. Close the switch at B, and the linked circuit energizes. Pope & Osborne, Proof 4 / definitive paper

Evidence boundary. The cited record states the apparatus and result but does not publish an oscilloscope trace, cable parameters, switching sequence, detector bandwidth, or numerical floor. This exhibit therefore preserves claim (i) as the foundational POAMS operational claim and states its replication gate without replacing it by another ontology: predeclare the circuit geometry, source state, detector location/bandwidth, switch chronology, capacitor placement, and current floor $\delta I$; then report the complete A-trace. In a finite instrument, “none” means $|I_A|<\delta I$ over the declared open-state window. replication required

The traveling-hose picture requires a start-up inventory: energy leaving A, discovering the blockage at B, and backing up. The cited record reports no detected A-entry of that kind under its open-state setup. POAMS reads the result as a completion rule: the source does not send and find out; the circuit conducts only as a completed link. The replication gate above is what gives that claim empirical content without importing a rival mechanism.

The constant that never moved

The projection reading is not a reinterpretation bolted on after the fact — it is motivated by how $c$ entered electrical metrology. In 1856, Weber and Kohlrausch measured the ratio between electrostatic and electromagnetic charge units by charging and discharging a Leyden jar and reading a galvanometer. The experiment did involve a transient current and needle motion; what it did not do was track an object crossing the implied $3.1\times10^{8}$ m/s. The measured unit ratio had velocity dimensions and was later recognized as numerically close to the optical constant. In modern notation:

$$c \;=\; \frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_0\,\varepsilon_0}} \;=\; 2.998\times10^{8}\ \mathrm{m/s}$$

The constant later associated with optical timing was first recovered electrically as a ratio between two unit ledgers, not by clocking an identified object across the implied distance. POAMS reads that dimensional result literally: $c$ converts between the distance ledger and the time ledger. The same leg stands in the corpus's oldest geometry — the $s/c$ term of the Pythagorean rate ledger, observed separation priced in observational time, is exactly what the Morse key reads as $L/c$. 3 · The hand-off — why the pipe is always full

What, then, is conducted? Quanta of action — whole, irreducible, in units of $h$. And the conduction obeys a rule with no hose analogue Pope 2005:

A quantum of electrical energy cannot be fed into one end of a conductor until that exact quantum amount is removed from the other.

The circuit is source-and-return specifically linked: nothing enters at A except as its equal is filed through the completed return relation. Viv's water model is a closed plumbing loop on a pump — with the pipe full, no circulation occurs until the far valve opens, and then the loop moves as one body. The 2005 paper proposes the same completion rule for light, with possible absorber relations distributed rather than bound to one return circuit. “One sink” on this page therefore means one completed return relation, not one physical load: a branched network may contain many loads while still requiring a closed source-return ledger. The hose keeps an in-flight inventory that must fill before delivery begins ($t_B - t_A = L/v_{\text{carrier}}$); the ledger keeps none — entry and delivery are a single linked event, and the $L/c$ separation between the two readings is §2's projection, not a transit time. formalizing Pope 2005

4 · What a battery does; what a dynamo does

If nothing flows down the wire like water, what does it mean that a battery powers a machine?

DC — the battery. A battery is a chemical ledger holding two terminals at a sustained difference of ionic organization — a standing bias in the one substance's bookkeeping (the electrician measures it as e.m.f., in volts, and POAMS keeps the unit). Close a link between the terminals and the biased ledger discharges through it as a train of linked hand-offs: each filing leaves the chemical book in the same completed transaction that enters a load book. For one specified filing rate $\nu$, $P=\dot N h\nu$; for several channels, the closed bookkeeping is $P=\sum_k\dot N_k h\nu_k$. The equation is dimensional and conservative, but it does not by itself derive the channel census $\{\dot N_k,\nu_k\}$ for an arbitrary DC or broadband load. identity + open constitutive map

AC — the dynamo. A dynamo is enforced re-organization: mechanical work drives conductor circulations through a magnet's ordered region, alternately biasing the ledger one way and the other, fifty or sixty times a second. Nothing sloshes to the customer and back; the bias alternates, and every linked load hands off its quanta in step with the alternation. The grid, read natively, is a continent-wide standing linkage in which billions of loads take quanta from generators they have never met — each hand-off whole, each instantaneous in itself, the ensemble read through $L/c$ projection as the engineer's familiar propagation timings.

The motor, completing the circle, is the dynamo run in reverse — delivered quanta filed as coherent circulation, angular momentum made to lean on a shaft. Battery to wire to motor is one unbroken story in the single currency: organized rate, re-filed. Nothing else is present to flow.

5 · Telecommunications — signaling on the link

Every telecommunication is a modulated hand-off pattern on a standing linkage — the switch at B worked as a Morse key is already the entire principle. What the engineer calls propagation delay is the projection reading of the link's extent ($L/c$); what he calls the channel is the linkage itself; what he calls the signal is the modulation of hand-off timing and rate. Tuning is engineered sink-readiness. The record's emission rule — a quantum cannot leave the source until a resonant place stands ready for it Pope 2005 — is the fact every radio exploits in reverse. A tuned circuit's own turn rate is $\nu = 1/(2\pi\sqrt{LC})$; set it to the transmitter's rate and your apparatus becomes a ready sink for exactly those hand-offs, deaf to the rest of the sky's rates (to the sharpness the census achieves — the engineer's $Q$). The dial does not strain a soup of arriving waves; it elects which linked transactions your census can be party to. Selectivity is census matching. And the fibre row above carries the same bookkeeping into glass: the medium's census adds its own hand-off ledger to the reading — the engineer measures group index 1.468 at 1550 nm, 4.90 µs/km against vacuum's 3.34 measured — the projection priced through a census instead of past one.

Nothing here licenses signaling faster than the observed timings — the projection is what observation is, and every measurement returns it. What changes is the engineering imagination: a channel is not a pipe to be filled but a linkage to be established and modulated. Design attention shifts from pushing harder down the hose to the two quantities the hand-off picture makes primary: link completion (the dead-circuit fact — no partial channels, no energy lost seeking closed gates) and sink readiness (teleblocking — emission governed by the receiving census, the sprinkler run in reverse).

Spin as carrier — the industry that arrived without the map

Spintronics stores and reads information in organized spin populations, while magnonics uses ordered precession in materials that may be electrical insulators. Those operational facts make angular organization a relevant engineering variable; they do not by themselves prove the POAMS ontology. Iron garnets are therefore presented here as experimentally established coherence media and POAMS engineering candidates, not as a first-principles selection established on this page.

The testable engineering hypothesis is narrower and stronger: treating organized turning as a controllable state may expose winding and phase degrees of freedom that a two-valued label does not use. Whether that yields new capability is an open device question, not a conclusion from the existence of spintronics or magnonics. The public bridge is POAMS & Memory.

6 · More here than meets the eye

Every section of this page performs the same proposed operation: a traveling thing is replaced by bookkeeping of the one substance while the quoted circuit timing, ranging, and fibre-delay arithmetic is retained. That numerical compatibility is necessary, but it is not independent evidence for the ontology because the page adopts the measured timing coefficients as inputs. The empirical work is the predeclared endpoint, selectivity, silence, and device tests; the engineering wager is that the linkage reading exposes useful controls that a traveling-thing picture does not foreground.
