Particle Decay & Radioactive Lifetimes

The missing completion-rate law confronted with competing channels, branching fractions and changing environments.

MULTI-CHANNEL ADDRESS THEOREM · INTEGER PARTITION OPEN · NO NATIVE CENSUS YET
Transfer verdict. The storage-ring program conditionally closes one-state geometric survival but proves that symmetry, renewal, sidebands and finite-precision commensurability cannot construct the address census. This page carries that earned kernel into multiple completion channels. It derives a joint lifetime–branching relation under explicit premises; it does not claim that any isotope or species census has been constructed.

2 · Native multi-channel construction

Declare one stationary retained preparation, one finite address set of size NA, and mutually exclusive primitive completing subsets Gj with integer sizes gj. Let G=∑jgj. Under the Finite Completion-Address Principle and a renewal kernel that restores the same conditional retained state after every survived turn,

pj=gj/NA,   ptot=G/NA,   q=1−G/NA.

For completion on turn n through primitive channel j,

Pr(T=n,J=j)=qn−1gj/NA,
S(n)=qn,   Pr(J=j | completion)=Bj=gj/G.

Joint theorem. Lifetime and branching fractions are two readings of one integer partition. NA/G fixes the total mean-turn scale; the ratios gj/G fix primitive branching. They may not be fitted independently and then described as one construction.

3 · Kernel form

Let Q be the substochastic transition matrix among surviving addresses and Rj the transition weights into completion class j. With prepared distribution π, the one-state law requires

πQ=qπ,   πRj1=gj/NA,   q+∑jπRj1=1.

This is an exact representation theorem. It does not derive Q, π, NA, the partition {Gj}, or the physical selector. The candidate Co-signed Address Renewal Principle remains unratified.

Detector-category trap. Several detector signatures may be downstream reductions of one primitive completion posting. Counting them as separate Gj multiplies ontology by the readout. Conversely, two experimentally merged primitive postings cannot be assigned one g without a demonstrated equivalence. Primitive exclusivity must be established before integer counting.

4 · What exponential survival means

  • One sufficient retained state and one stationary kernel give geometric survival in internal turns.
  • Several primitive channels still give one geometric law when their eligible sets share that state and kernel.
  • Opening or closing a channel changes G and therefore the total hazard as well as the branch partition.
  • A channel label that changes only detector efficiency belongs in the reduction, not the address census.

5 · What non-exponential survival means

  • A mixture of retained classes gives S(n)=∑rwrqrn.
  • Age- or history-dependent address visitation gives a changing conditional hazard.
  • A changing environment can alter preparation weights, the kernel, the address set, or eligible subsets.
  • None of these licenses a smooth continuum underneath the whole-turn record.

6 · Integer-partition discriminator

For one independently identified address construction, all primitive channel fractions must belong to one integer partition:

B1:B2:…:Bm = g1:g2:…:gm,   ∑gj=G.

This is stronger than fitting one real coefficient per channel, but finite precision cannot prove exact rationality or discover an unbounded denominator. The integers must be frozen from an independent address construction. Branching data then test them.

Prospective cross-environment test

  1. Freeze one preparation identity, primitive channel map, address census and integer partition before examining the held-out condition.
  2. Change one declared boundary condition—ionization, pressure, temperature, host or imposed phase relation—while recording all detector-response changes.
  3. Predict which address subsets are unchanged, removed, added or split. This fixes both the total-hazard change and branching change.
  4. Reject the construction if lifetime and channel shifts cannot be produced by the same registered partition update.

8 · Construction debt

  • Identity: construct the retained organization independently of its eventual completion time.
  • Census: construct NA without lifetime inversion, rational fitting or imported matrix elements.
  • Partition: identify primitive mutually exclusive eligible subsets and their integer counts.
  • Renewal: disclose the co-signed physical operation implementing Q and its quasi-stationary π.
  • Readout: map primitive postings to detector categories with an efficiency/confusion matrix.
  • Leverage: demonstrate address-selective control; otherwise the model redescribes timing but cannot engineer it.

9 · Promotion decision

Remain in the Research Queue. The multi-channel integer-partition theorem is conditionally closed. No species-specific address set, eligible partition or renewal operation has been constructed, and no event-level dataset has been replayed. Promotion requires one forward-calculated joint lifetime/branching change that survives detector controls and a held-out environment.
