The task is native: determine how a completed exchange changes two retained circulations, their clock tariffs and their next permitted returns. Given the declared endpoint addresses, fixed rest-tariff norms and complete event-moment conditions, the nonzero momentum posting is determined. Different admitted completion graphs give different next circulation states:

The result is a finite accounting construction. No experiment was performed.
