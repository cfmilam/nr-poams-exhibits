# C08 — Inertia, mass and Mach

## Holdings

**C08-H0001 — Mass is a measure within relations, not a self-sufficient substance.** The philosophical order matters: the observed whole is analyzed into mass, length and time; isolated masses are not made the source of that whole.

> "Ultimately, then, in Normal Realism there is no such thing as mass, existing in and of itself. Mass is just one of the triad of inseparable fundamental measures, mass, length and time, in all forms of angular momentum." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0443

Pope also states the relational restriction in terms of ordinary objects:

> "There is no mass, motion, position, duration or whatever of any object relative to nothing but itself – that is, in complete isolation." — eye-of-beholder:P0334

**C08-H0002 — Inertia is explained through the body's relation to other bodies.** In the braking-bus example, the experienced force is the restraint changing the passenger's motion. The book denies a separate force that throws the passenger forward.

> "There is no 'force' throwing us forward. The real force is what is holding us back." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0558

> "This is where some surface or other stops our bodies from orbiting freely with respect to the Earth, the Sun, the stars and so on in the way they did before the thrust was applied." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0559

This is an author explanation of an ordinary reported experience, not an instrument record demonstrating the proposed distant connection.

**C08-H0003 — The authors connect inertial orientation to a Machian reference frame.** The joint book identifies the fixed-star frame with a rotational reference and a proposed reference-zero of time dilation.

> "It is the reference-frame with respect to which, as Mach pointed out, all gyroscopes orientate themselves, and, of course, all free orbits and spins of bodies are ‘gyroscopes’ in their way." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0564

> "That is to say, its intrinsic time-standard is what, in POAMS, we have called Deep-Space Time (DST)." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0564

The attribution to Mach is the book's historical attribution. No independent historical or experimental verification is claimed by quoting it.

**C08-H0004 — Mass and moment of inertia are distinct quantities in the authors' calculations.** Moment of inertia depends on geometry as well as mass. Calling both relational does not make them identical.

> "Here, the quantity mr², which gives information about both the matter content and the shape of the orbit of P, is the moment of inertia, I, of P." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P1054

> "The moment of inertia, $I$, of $P$ depends on the mass of $P$ and its shape." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P1058

The surrounding construction, P1055–P1061, restricts its spin examples to a rigid, axially symmetric body about a fixed axis. These are defined measures in a mathematical representation, not a derivation of a body's mass from a cosmic census.

**C08-H0005 — The later book describes the fundamental inertial connections as discrete.** Its macroscopic discussion of an overall nexus is accompanied by quantum-sequence language.

> "As it turns out, then, inertia, the action-at-a-distance connection between bodies envisaged by Mach, is basically quantised in the same way as light" — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0565

The direct-event and observer-time distinction belongs in C05; its application to joint statistics belongs in C10. Neither a human observer nor a continuously occupied intermediate medium is supplied as the cause of inertia.

## What the texts reject

They reject mass possessed relative to nothing, and inertia as an additional force pushing the unconstrained body. They also reject the inference that removing a hidden-force interpretation removes the restraint experienced in a vehicle.

Pope's rejection of isolation is not a denial of objects when no person perceives them:

> "Objects exist relatively to one another even when there is nothing around that would fit the description of a ‘percipient’." — eye-of-beholder:P0335

This is the relevant philosophical boundary. It does not establish the magnitude of an inertial response.

## Differences and open rules

**A relation is not yet a response law.** H0001–H0003 identify the authors' interpretation and proposed reference frame. They do not provide, in these passages, an operational rule assigning weights to all remote bodies or deriving a local mass calibration from those weights. The limited finding is about the passages cited, not a declaration that every mathematical treatment in the corpus has been exhausted here.

**Overall versus discrete connection.** Einstein's Lost Legacy:P0560 says a jolt instantly affects the balance with other bodies; P0563 uses a hypothetical seismograph comparison, not an actual measurement receipt. P0565 then describes discrete connections. These passages must remain alongside the intransitivity restrictions in C06/C10. They do not license an editorially invented all-pairs update or universal clock.

**Reference-time tension.** Deep-Space Time in P0564 is affirmative source content. It cannot simply be deleted because P0476 denies a single overall now. The Odyssey:P0783/P0787 gives stronger universal-time wording. C11 is the primary home of that unresolved comparison.

**Other contributors are not Pope or Osborne.** B1A-R10 routes Graneau and Rowlands inertia constructions as alternatives. B2b P14-27/P14-29/P14-30/P14-32 retains Neal and Peter Graneau's model assumptions, simulation status, normalization issues and qualifications. Their equations do not become Pope–Osborne holdings because they appear in the same source collection. The accepted B3b index supplies destinations, not definitions.

**Dialogue extension.** In wondering-wilderness:P1122, Turner says observation makes the object minutely less massive relative to its observer, with a reciprocal loss on being observed in turn. B3a S03 authenticates the attribution, not a quantitative apparatus balance. This is dialogue testimony, not an independently measured mass-transfer law.

**Later premises remain outside authorship.** Later program rules about anisotropic response, custody or renewal are not used to fill the missing operational link. Their adoption belongs to later research, not this record of the authors' texts.

## Test for new work

*Editorial synthesis.*

- **C08-T0001:** Identify the force or motion record, the restraint, and the bodies relative to which the motion is described.
- **C08-T0002:** Keep mass, moment of inertia, angular momentum and energy distinct, with units and geometrical assumptions.
- **C08-T0003:** Separate the assertion of relational dependence from a quantitative response law and from a measurement of that law.
- **C08-T0004:** Specify whose clock and reference frame are used; retain the source's universal-time tensions.
- **C08-T0005:** Name the contributor and version of any proposed inertial law. Label later additions as additions, not recovered author doctrine.
