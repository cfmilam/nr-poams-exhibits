I strongly support the approach. It is the missing front-end control that would have stopped an OIL-style fishing trip before the mathematics acquired its own passport and left the country.

The decisive feature is that every new exhibit must begin with:
1. the observed phenomenon and observer;
2. the authenticated source position;
3. the Normal Realist constraints;
4. the physical and mathematical premises being added;
5. competing approaches and why one is chosen;
6. the exact record that could defeat it;
7. independent review before construction.

That changes the workflow from invent → calculate → retrofit meaning to ground → choose → derive → test.

There are three safeguards we must preserve:
- The canon cannot judge the sources during reconciliation. Contrary passages must correct the canon, not be filtered out by it.
- The authors' holdings must remain separate from later program premises. Pope/Osborne source claims, our editorial synthesis, your ratified constitutive extensions, imported mathematics, and measurements each need their own status.
- Do not inject all 23 full pages into every session. The final system should generate a compact core plus the relevant subject pages, source passages, known conflicts, and project-start questions. Otherwise the cure becomes a context-window landslide.

"Definitive" should mean approved and controlled for a declared authenticated corpus and scope—not unrevisable. New source evidence or an explicit amendment can produce a new version.

The correct final deliverable is therefore a matched set:
1. the full, quotation-backed Source-Grounded Canon;
2. a compact generated Canon Core;
3. a subject-specific Opening Context for each new exhibit;
4. the approved Project-Start Protocol;
5. hashes, source receipts, unresolved questions, and change control.
