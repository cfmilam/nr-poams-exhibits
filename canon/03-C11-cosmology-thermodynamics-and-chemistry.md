# C11 — Cosmology, thermodynamics and chemistry

## Holdings

### Cosmology

**C11-H0001 — The authors separate redshift observations from a recessional interpretation.** Their alternative is a proposed cosmology, not a measurement established by the criticism of another interpretation.

> "What is observed is the redshift." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0621

> "For instance, one such alternative explanation of the Redshift is that it is a relativistic time dilational effect of increasing omnidirectional motion with distance, which has no bizarre ‘expanding universe’ and associated ‘Big Bang’ implications." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0621

The same passage assumes increasing random motion with distance and discusses a distributed barycenter. Neither the alternative's mathematical consistency nor its claimed plausibility establishes the distribution observationally.

**C11-H0002 — The later joint book advocates a steady state of indefinite extent.** This is affirmative author content, not merely an objection to expansion.

> "It is the Normal Realist view then, as opposed to a current cosmological view of a closed universe, that the universe as such is in a constant, steady state of indefinite extent." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0622

### Thermodynamics

**C11-H0003 — Statistical laws govern wholes rather than arising from autonomous microscopic causes.** This is the authors' interpretation of entropy and conservation. C06 owns the general account of statistical causality; here its subject is thermal regularity.

> "Now the point to be stressed, here, is that all these laws, of entropy, energy conservation, the conservation of angular momentum and so on are, essentially, holistic laws. They all state, in effect, that the whole determines the parts and not the parts the whole." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0659

**C11-H0004 — The book proposes a larger entropy–negentropy balance.** It joins local decay and local organization within an overall steady-state picture.

> "By default, then, the ‘universe’, however we may choose to conceive it, has to remain in an overall-conserved, non-local steady state, with entropy, or decay in one locality compensated by negentropy, or growth, in another" — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0680

This is an author inference. The passage does not provide a measured global entropy budget.

### Chemistry

**C11-H0005 — Chemical combination remains legitimate ordinary description.** The atomic discussion retains conventional ionization and valency language as a comparison, rather than supplying a separate finished chemistry.

> "Those separated masses, customarily called ‘ions’, are traditionally conceived as being captured by other ions of opposite sign to form other atoms in what is known as chemical valency – analogous to changing partners in Country-and-Western dancing." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0525

The analogy describes reorganization. It is not a derivation of a table of valencies, bonding energies or reaction rates. C09 owns the atomic and charge interpretation; C12 owns the account of life and purpose.

## What the texts reject

The joint book rejects making a recessional interpretation logically obligatory merely because recession can produce redshift. It rejects the imagined outside standpoint from which the universe could be measured against an external ruler and clock, and opposes a global terminal decay picture.

These are the authors' criticisms, not independently verified summaries of every competing cosmology. Their thermodynamic argument likewise rejects microscopic autonomy without denying the regularity of pressure, heat or chemical work.

## Differences and open rules

**Universal time is a source disagreement.** The Odyssey's third-person account of its investigator asserts:

> "The proper-time of that statistical zero of motion and acceleration, he concluded, is the standard time —the universal 'GMT', as it were— of the whole relational system, or universe, the time relative to which all other times are either equivalent or dilated." — the-odyssey:P0783

> "there is a universal now of physical existence in which it makes sense to talk about the present positions in relation to one another of distant bodies and a universal time in which all those universal nows, or present-moments evolve." — the-odyssey:P0787

The later joint book says:

> "In Normal Realism, then, there is no need whatsoever to think of everything having to be connected in the single, overall now that classical physicists and astronomers talk about." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0476

These statements are not made equivalent by calling the first merely a reunion convention. B1b authenticated the Odyssey passages locally; its final ledger assigns this conflict here. Deep-Space Time in the later book, P0564, adds a further affirmative reference-frame claim. C05 and C08 should cross-reference this disagreement rather than assert unqualified corpus agreement.

**Alternatives change.** OD-C11-02 records the possibility of cumulative temporal-geodesic nonlinearity in the-odyssey:P0938–P0941. B2b P14-12 records a tentative bent-path account in the Heresy versions, distinct from the later random-motion account. These are not one established cosmological law. B2b also preserves differing claims about the role of background radiation and other evidence.

**Dimensional and observational inputs remain distinct.** The accepted source packages retain D2's observed/required/derived dimensionality issue and contributor-specific cosmologies. Graneau, Roscoe and Rowlands are not interchangeable with Pope and Osborne. No parameter fit or source-reported galaxy result is promoted to a measurement receipt here.

**Entropy and purpose.** The joint book extends statistical end-state language toward prospective activity in P0660–P0668. That extension belongs partly to C12, but it cannot be silently treated as a quantitatively demonstrated final-cause law. Local organization and an asserted global compensation are different claims.

**Chemistry is sparse.** The passages used here provide comparisons and interpretive vocabulary, not an independently specified chemical dynamics. This dossier remains short rather than importing a chemical theory to fill the gap.

## Test for new work

*Editorial synthesis.*

- **C11-T0001:** Separate the spectral or thermal record from its reduction and cosmological interpretation.
- **C11-T0002:** State the assumed distribution of matter and motion, dimensionality, distance calibration and clock convention.
- **C11-T0003:** Preserve the universal-time disagreement and the distinct historical redshift proposals.
- **C11-T0004:** Define the system boundary before asserting entropy increase, decrease or compensation; do not infer a measured global balance from a local example.
- **C11-T0005:** Identify chemical analogies as analogies. Do not present absent reaction or bonding rules as author-derived results.
