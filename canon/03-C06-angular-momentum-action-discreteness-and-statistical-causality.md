# C06 — Angular momentum, action, discreteness and statistical causality

## Holdings

**C06-H0001 — POAMS identifies angular momentum, rather than mass alone, as its underlying physical substance.** This is the authors' physical proposal within Normal Realism. It follows the philosophical starting point, not the reverse.

> "According to POAMS, the universal underlying 'substance' (building block) is not mass but angular momentum." — poams-and-the-atom-synopsis:P0009

Osborne explicitly presents the physical starting points as postulates:

> "This chapter gives an account of an angular momentum synthesis, based on Pope's Normal Realist philosophy. It is postulated that all motion is naturally orbital, and that angular momentum is holistically conserved." — immediate-distant-action:P1375

These statements do not independently establish an equation of motion. C07 owns the orbital construction; C08 owns inertia and mass; C09 owns the atomic and charge account.

**C06-H0002 — Action h and angular momentum h/2π are distinct quantities in the authors' account.** The same physical interpretation does not make the units interchangeable.

> "Planck's quantum h, which is the product of energy and time" — eye-of-beholder:P0266
> "the related quantum of angular momentum, h/2π, which is the product of the measures mass, radius and velocity." — eye-of-beholder:P0266
> "energy is transacted in instantaneous amounts of inter-resonant action, $nh$ (note: action in units of $nh$, not angular momentum in units of $\hbar$)." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0498

The spectral energies vary even though the action unit is fixed:

> "This example also demonstrates that different phota may have different energies." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0762

The joint book retains Planck's energy-frequency relation, here printed with v for frequency; this is not the relative-speed variable used in C05:

> "$$E = h v \tag{5.1}$$" — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0706

Pope connects the exchange energies with spectral series:

> "whose energies of interaction form the spectral, bar-code-like series described in section 2 of chapter IV." — eye-of-beholder:P0266

The elementary exchange and its receiving end belong to C05. No new winding, closure or numerical quantization law is added here.

**C06-H0003 — The authors treat physical information and recordable measures as ultimately discrete.** This is a source thesis about the phenomena, not a theorem supplied by modern information theory merely by using its vocabulary.

> "for information to be information it has to be analysable into ultimately discrete elements." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P1781
> "All recordable physical measures are therefore, de facto, ultimately discrete." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0699

Their express permission to use continuous variables in mathematical models belongs to C02. It prevents treating discreteness as a ban on every continuous calculation.

**C06-H0004 — Elementary events are not a deterministic causal chain.** The authors locate ordinary cause and effect in statistical populations of events.

> "One quantum does not cause another, any more than one letter in this sentence causes the next in sequence." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0740
> "cause and effect are a function of statistical numbers of quantum events occurring in vast and fine-grained profusion." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0740

This does not by itself specify a probability distribution, independence condition or transition kernel. “Statistical” is not a quantitative law until those are supplied.

**C06-H0005 — Instantaneous relations are nontransitive and do not form a regularly clocked universal sequence.** A completed relation between two ends does not make every other relation simultaneous with it.

> "the instantaneous to-and-fro linkages between phota into the sequences of which macrophenomenal time ultimately analyses, cannot be transitive." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0737
> "the quantum stills do not follow one another in a regular sequence, one after another, like the photographic frames in a reel of film." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0475
> "These quantum angular momentum systems are, by definition, connected only sporadically (intransitively)." — action-at-a-distance---oct-1996:P0027

**C06-H0006 — A cycle's nonzero period is distinct from zero transition-time.** The cinematic analogy has limits. It cannot justify either an elementary carrier or a universal projector.

> "the period of each angular momentum cycle in itself was not instantaneous but irreducible" — the-odyssey:P0529
> "Like the angular momentum period it, too, had a definite period, one which was, again, non-zero but irreducible." — the-odyssey:P0529
> "the transfer-time of that same quantum from the atom in the light-source to the one in the spectrograph was zero" — the-odyssey:P0529
> "The duration, or temporal ‘thickness’, of that present moment is the time of one quantum cycle, which is the inverse of its spectral frequency." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0477

The second quotation refers to the interval separating angular-momentum stills in the autobiographical explanation. It is not a claim that the transition itself has that duration. A full operational rule for the relation between cycle, interval, readiness and observed succession remains open.

**C06-H0007 — Holistic balance is an explicit premise with both every-body and one-partner formulations.** The all-body wording must remain in the Canon, together with its qualifications.

> "POAMS begins with the premise that the observable universe is in an holistic balance, so that the natural or unrestricted motion of any body is (albeit in statistical numbers of sporadic quantum contacts) instantly correlated with that of each and every other body in the System." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P1046
> "so, according to my thesis, every object is connected with every other at every distinguishable instant." — the-odyssey:P0588
> "This is by hosts of different and overlapping angular momentum balances and transactions, with nothing ever falling out of account." — the-odyssey:P0588

In his reply to Clive Kilmister, Pope explicitly affirms both formulations:

> "My answer is to affirm both, that every body is paired with every other and that one is paired with one other." — new-discussion-file-challenges-to-poams:P0067
> "But nowhere in POAMS does it say – or even suggest – that those different transactions have to occur together, all at the same time." — new-discussion-file-challenges-to-poams:P0068
> "when atom $A$ swaps a quantum with atom $B$, it cannot, in that same instance, swap that same quantum with atom $C$" — new-discussion-file-challenges-to-poams:P0069

The same quantum is not shared with a third partner in that transaction. This local explanation narrows the every/one conflict; it does not supply a complete many-body update law.

**C06-H0008 — Orbital and spin angular momenta are distinct vector contributions.** The source includes both in a total; naming one does not identify the other. The ensuing orbital-change claims and their assumptions belong to C07, not to a universal coupling law introduced here.

> "Angular momentum is such a vector quantity. Orbital and spin angular momenta therefore add and subtract in the same way as velocities." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0787
> "The total angular momentum of an orbiting particle is the sum of its orbital and spin angular momentum vectors." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0787

## What the texts reject

They reject treating the statistical whole as one monolithic elementary system:

> "A fallacy, then, is to imagine all these quantum stills (quantum angular momentum systems) forming one great, monolithic angular momentum system, or universe." — action-at-a-distance---oct-1996:P0027

They reject deterministic elementary selection as the required missing machinery. Pope's answer to Kilmister continues:

> "there is no mechanism in POAMS to determine whether your A will transact with B or with C or, as you say, with any other particle in the universe." — new-discussion-file-challenges-to-poams:P0071

That is a rejection of micromechanical determinacy, not a quantitative derivation of the statistical weights. The apparatus-specific geometrical argument immediately following it belongs to C10.

They also reject conservation as sufficient by itself to produce dynamics:

> "no conservation principle automatically provides an equation of motion." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0914

This does not reject conservation. It states the limit of what conservation alone establishes.

## Differences and open rules

**Every body and one partner.** Kilmister's objection in new-discussion-file-challenges-to-poams:P0055–P0059 is his criticism, not Pope's conclusion. Pope's reply is P0063 onward. Accepted B2b original-page work confirms the distinction and the local microlevel qualification. B2c retains the strong automatic-pairing wording in *Theological Discussion*, P0885; B3c retains it in Osborne's Lectures 14, 18 and the staged Lecture 21. It would therefore be false to say that no source has every-body language. Conversely, these statements do not establish that every pair completes an exchange at one universal instant. Preserve both claims and their stated domains. The general all-body/discrete-event reconciliation remains open.

**Duration vocabulary.** *Information*, P0005, defines events broadly as finite-duration occurrences; P0007 denies determinate causal connection at the elementary level. B3a's S01 and S05 comparisons retain short-duration and chain/knock-on wording beside later zero-time and nonconnection language. Some differences concern scale; a universal equivalence has not been demonstrated. C06-H0006 records an explicit distinction from *Lines from Einstein - An Intellectual Odyssey by Viv Pope*, not an editorial replacement for every other use of “event.”

**Information and randomness.** The source argues from informational discreteness to indeterminacy and statistical law. No general theorem establishing that all information requires unconnected random elementary events is independently proved here. The precise distribution, correlations, allowable histories and relation of macroscopic causality to those histories remain unspecified at this level. C10 owns probability claims tied to two-slit and polarization apparatus.

**Symbols and transmission.** Accepted source packages distinguish missing bars and damaged equations from actual author differences. B2b confirms that the original state/action diagram in the-dual-local-and-non-local-nature-of-the-quantum---2001 distinguishes h and ħ; the same source has a dimensional wording defect concerning distance times frequency. Other h/ħ ambiguities cannot be repaired by indiscriminate substitution. C06-H0002 uses the explicit book distinction instead of deriving a new unit rule from damaged notation.

**Substance and earlier ether analogies.** action-at-a-distance---oct-1996:P0066 uses an angular-momentum substratum analogy; P0069 uses waves to describe probabilities. Later direct-exchange passages reject a medium. The levels may differ, but that does not erase the earlier language or settle the projection/underpinning conflict recorded in C01. No continuous plenum is introduced to reconcile them.

**Conservation and law.** Holistic conservation alone supplies neither an equation of motion nor event weights, a coupling magnitude, a partner-selection rule or a clock. Those are separate requirements. This dossier adds no later program closure, twist/writhe, renewal, action-posting or measurement premise to fill them.

## Test for new work

**Editorial synthesis.**

- **C06-T0001:** Identify whether a quantity is angular momentum, action, energy, cycle period or an interval between records. Keep h and ħ distinct.
- **C06-T0002:** State whether a claim concerns one exchange, an atomic cycle, a statistical collection or a macroscopic model.
- **C06-T0003:** Do not infer an elementary deterministic chain or a universal clock from a cinematic analogy.
- **C06-T0004:** Preserve literal every-body assertions and their sporadic/one-partner qualifications. Do not replace either with an unqualified absence claim.
- **C06-T0005:** Give the actual statistical law if claiming a quantitative result. Mark every added selection, weighting or succession rule as an added premise.
- **C06-T0006:** Separate conservation from the dynamics used with it. Neither conserved totals nor agreement with a target establish the missing operation.
- **C06-T0007:** Distinguish author thesis, conditional calculation and measurement. Check damaged units against the relevant original witness before consequential use.
