# C03 — Observer, objectivity, information and projection

## Holdings

**C03-H0001 — Observation is not confined to a conscious human spectator.** Several texts call any interagent an observer. This is a broad physical usage, not a claim that every interagent speaks human language or has human consciousness.

> "this 'observer' may be any object, animal, vegetable or mineral, conscious or otherwise" — phenomenalism:P0015
> "by 'observers', we mean interagents of all kinds, animal, vegetable and mineral." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P1288

**C03-H0002 — Interaction is not dependent on a person's presence.** The world exceeds what any human community currently observes. C01 distinguishes this unknown remainder from an inaccessible second world.

> "things are communicating/interacting with one another continually, even in what is, to us, complete darkness, or when we are asleep, unconscious, unborn or even dead." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0275
> "Their unseen and unknown character is a logically necessary extension of all that lies right there in front of us." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0741

**C03-H0003 — Objectivity is communal and relational.** It is not the possession of one privileged viewpoint. Individual perspectives and the shared spatial description sustain one another.

> "In Normal Realism, this is what provides the objectivity of the world, as projected and described by an interactive community of observers, not by a single observer such as oneself." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0219
> "the communal, or Cartesian, grid-space of objective physics and commonsense is a secondary or a posteriori extrapolation out of interactions between these polar centres." — phenomenalism:P0018
> "Without these polar centres, there can be no objective Cartesian space (grid-space) and without the objective grid-space there can be no objective polar space." — phenomenalism:P0019

**C03-H0004 — Dimensions are informational projections from sensory and instrumental patterns.** This is an analysis of phenomena, not the manufacture of experience from dimensionless numbers.

> "dimensions of space, time, mass, etc. – including those of so-called ‘light-velocity’ – are then no more nor less than informational extrapolations made by the decoder (the observer) from directly observed patterns and sequences of the informational bits." — eye-of-beholder:P0122
> "the aim of the other is to explain things in terms of systems of actual sensory/instrumental information (what Mach termed 'sense-data')." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0702
> "Our Normal Realist philosophy, however, dictates that we follow the second digital approach, in which geometry is essentially a natural a priori feature of phenomena" — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0702

**C03-H0005 — The observed whole precedes its analytical parts.** Terminal elements acquire their characteristics within the observational whole. The source does not warrant constructing that whole from independently specified miniature objects behind observation.

> "these ultimate parts of observational elements into which those things may be sensibly and/or instrumentally analysed, have no qualities or characteristics other than in the contexts of those observational wholes of which they are the analysed parts" — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0698
> "these 'atoms' have no independent existence or permanence as 'things-in-themselves'." — eye-of-beholder:P0267

**C03-H0006 — A projected observational distance depends on a pattern of exchanges, not an isolated quantum alone.** The information associated with a single exchange must not be confused with an entire spatial reconstruction. C05 owns the exchange's two ends and time relation.

> "Rather, the distance, $s$, of a source relative to an observer, $O$, is the manifestation of distributions of large numbers of phota, of which that particular atomic source is just one among many, all observed more or less together at $O$." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0733

**C03-H0007 — Ordinary temporal experience remains a starting datum.** Analytical priority of exchanges does not make the experienced dimensions arbitrary inventions with no empirical content.

> "In fact, the concept of time can come from nowhere but ordinary temporal experience, where it is one of the indissociable dimensional components of ordinary existence, duration and motion." — anpa--poams---two-systems-of-combinatorics-2002:P0033

## What the texts reject

They reject a detached, all-seeing standpoint as the foundation of objective physics:

> "So, as it transpires, this splitting-off of a presumed 'God's-eye-view' of physical objects from our perceptions of them created a false dichotomy." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0202

They also reject the inference that a world described through observers disappears when one observer does:

> "the demise of any one of these 'observers', even of oneself, does not entail the demise of the whole intercommunicational network." — phenomenalism:P0020

The opposition is therefore to both a world wholly divorced from possible observation and a world confined to one mind. It is not opposition to instruments, inference, unobserved ordinary objects or communication among nonhuman things.

The rejection of a hidden mechanical producer of information belongs constitutionally to C01-H0005 and physically to C05. It must be read with C01's retained substratum disagreement, not converted into an assertion that every author passage denies every kind of substantiality.

## Differences and open rules

**Broad and narrow observer.** The broad usage in C03-H0001 is not uniform across the corpus. A later paper distinguishes interagents from observers who communicate in language:

> "By 'interagent', here, we mean something that is party to a physical interaction, as distinct from something that observes and communicates in language." — the-universe-of-normal-realism:P0018
> "An interagent may nevertheless be conceived, in the context of NR, as a 'rudimentary observer'." — the-universe-of-normal-realism:P0018

The 1978 synopsis describes its terms as delineations of human experience:

> "all such things are delineations of the language of human experience." — a-synopsis-of-normal-realism-revised:P0087

The autobiography tentatively extends communicational language even to mineral things (the-odyssey:P0719). These are distinctions in vocabulary and scope, not evidence for a measured boundary of consciousness. State which sense is used in each argument.

**Polar and public space.** Phenomenalism:P0018 describes the common grid as subsequent to polar interactions, while P0019–P0020 require the grid for an objective polar perspective. Both directions remain. “Subsequent” here cannot silently mean a chronological production of a public world by isolated private minds. The texts give a conceptual dependence and mutual qualification, not an algorithm establishing a globally unique coordinate system from arbitrary records.

**Bare sensation and interpreted description.** One paper treats bare sensation as indubitable:

> "So far as bare unclassified sensation is concerned, no question of its existence can arise." — normal-realism-1:P0012
> "Doubt and contradiction can arise only when language is employed to classify and divide these sensations, to put them into a logical order." — normal-realism-1:P0012

Another says:

> "no data of sense or instrumentation are objectively specifiable but are always contextual, depending upon the ways in which language is used." — phenomenalism-1-the-philosophical-root-source-of-relativity:P0035

Indubitable occurrence is not infallible description. That distinction is a useful editorial reading, but no prelinguistic calibrated measurement protocol follows from these passages. C04 retains the obligation to distinguish an occurrence from its interpretation.

**Information and its scale.** The accepted B2a/B3a comparisons retain broader claims that individual quanta convey no information alongside claims that quanta are information. C03-H0006 supports the narrower claim that a single event does not specify observational distance; it does not erase the broader wording. *Wondering in the Wilderness* must retain its narrative speakers and the S02 supplemental comparison rather than be quoted as one undifferentiated author assertion. No Shannon formula, probability weighting or uniquely invertible reconstruction is derived here.

**Analytical and temporal priority.** C03-H0004 and C03-H0007 coexist. The first concerns analysis of dimensional experience; the second refuses to obtain time from pure number alone. The texts do not supply a general operational reconstruction of geometry, clock comparison and calibration from one isolated record. Hypothetical stellar clocks in the paired Intellectual Challenge/Problem papers are explicitly hypothetical in the accepted B2b P14-33 disposition, not observed clock readings.

## Test for new work

**Editorial synthesis.**

- **C03-T0001:** Name the observer or interagent and state which meaning of observer applies. Do not infer consciousness from participation alone.
- **C03-T0002:** Separate the record, its interpretation and its comparison with other records. A common description is not a view from nowhere.
- **C03-T0003:** Begin analysis with the observed whole. Identify the context in which each proposed part acquires its properties.
- **C03-T0004:** For every reconstructed distance or time, identify the wider record, calibration and assumptions. One exchange cannot silently supply an entire geometry.
- **C03-T0005:** Preserve ordinary temporal experience while distinguishing analytical priority from chronological causation. Do not insert an unobserved universal clock.
- **C03-T0006:** Keep differences of observer vocabulary, information scale and polar/public dependence visible where they affect a claim.
