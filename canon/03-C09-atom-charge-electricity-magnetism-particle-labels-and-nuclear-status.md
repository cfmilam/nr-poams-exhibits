# C09 — Atom, charge, electricity, magnetism, particle labels and nuclear status

## Holdings

**C09-H0001 — Electrical language describes phenomena before it describes analytical parts.** Pope starts with such occurrences as rubbed amber, lightning and currents. Their conventional names do not arrive attached to independently known microscopic objects.

> "We must not, however, lose sight of the fact that, in the last analysis, all this 'electrical' language is about phenomena and that those measures described in the above paragraph are measures derived from the analysis of phenomena on the ordinary macro-scale." — poams-and-electricity-synopsis:P0008

This philosophical priority governs the atomic comparison. It does not deny the occurrences being described.

**C09-H0002 — The atomic representation is an indivisible angular-momentum organization, not a miniature planetary machine.** The joint book expressly limits its familiar orbital picture.

> "In POAMS, an ‘atom’ is therefore a configuration of pure indivisible angular momentum in which these component masses may be conceived, simplistically, as orbiting a common centre of balance (barycentre)." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0450

> "It needs to be stressed here however, that there is no implication in POAMS that the component masses actually orbit each other as the planets orbit the Sun on the macro-scale." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0450

Conventional particle names in the authors' comparison identify measured quantities and roles in the model. They do not establish hidden, continuously orbiting constituents.

**C09-H0003 — The authors replace microscopic charge-as-property with spin quantities, while retaining ordinary electrical measures.** The replacement is an author proposal about interpretation, not the disappearance of voltage, current or stored charge.

> "The traditional ‘electrostatic charge’ on those masses, in units of coulombs, is replaced by an equivalent spin kinetic energy in ordinary mechanical units of joules." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0450

> "The meaning of that word 'charge' is retained, however, as appropriate to macro-level collections of either positive or negative ions in the usual electrician's sense, or the sense in which a cannon may be 'charged' with gunpowder." — poams-and-electricity-synopsis:P0006

The synopsis also says:

> "In Normal Realism this language is virtually the same as in standard textbook physics." — poams-and-electricity-synopsis:P0007

**C09-H0004 — The worked atomic example is a reconstruction with an empirical energy input.** Osborne explicitly defines an energy through the conventional ionization potential and charge unit. The assignment of that energy to spin is a further interpretive step.

> "Here, $I_{0} = 13.6$ volts and $e \approx 1.60217733 \times 10^{-19}$ coulomb." — immediate-distant-action:P1608

> "And again it must be emphasized that what we are proposing here is not some new concept of the atom to compete with the current physical models that superseded Bohr's." — immediate-distant-action:P1619

> "It is essentially a logical demonstration of how, ahistorically, our concepts of motion and distant interaction might have developed in direct observational terms, without postulating invisible intermediaries such as 'gravitational' and 'electrostatic' forces, linking one atom or part of an atom to another." — immediate-distant-action:P1619

Agreement with the supplied spectral or ionization scale is therefore not an independent prediction of that scale.

**C09-H0005 — The magnetic account is proposed as a spin relation, not supplied as a complete material law.** Osborne attributes this interpretation to himself and Pope. His own comparison with conventional electrodynamics qualifies a clean division between electrical and magnetic explanations.

> "The implication here is that the POAMS model of the hydrogen atom contains an element of both electrostatics and magnetostatics." — immediate-distant-action:P1627

> "Pope and I believe that whereas the POAMS pure-angular-momentum paraphrase of ‘electrostatic’ orbits is that of a spin-orbital relation between electron-masses and proton-masses, magnetic effects can be explained in terms of a spin angular momentum relation between electrons only." — immediate-distant-action:P1628

This quotation retains the source's conventional comparison labels. It does not supply an independently checked magnetic constitutive equation.

**C09-H0006 — Nuclear unification is prospective in the cited paper.** The degree of completion must not exceed the author's wording.

> "Further studies (so far unpublished [19]) indicate that in vacuo nuclear forces may be dispensed with in the same way." — pirt-2004-proc-paper:P0076

> "Indeed, it appears that the whole of physics could, in principle, be reconstructed on the basis of a systematic quantum-informational phenomenology" — pirt-2004-proc-paper:P0076

These are indications and a proposed reach, not a completed nuclear theory or a nuclear measurement receipt.

## What the texts reject

They reject a continuously operating microscopic mechanism underneath phenomena and an unexplained intrinsic power of charge to pull or push across a void. They do not reject the ordinary laboratory or electrical vocabulary merely because its historical interpretation differs.

The atomic chapter explicitly says its model is not a literal picture:

> "The fact that our model is not intended to provide a true physical description of the hydrogen atom does not prevent us from associating vectors with the quantities involved" — immediate-distant-action:P1595

Pope and Osborne retain real work required to separate macroscopic charged bodies in einsteins-lost-legacy:P0461. The denial concerns an invisible intermediary, not the work record.

## Differences and open rules

**Atomic identity and practical usage.** B2b P13-18 distinguishes the-atom-in-normal-realism:P0017–P0020, which denies an atom continuously ticking behind appearances, from P0022–P0024, which retains chemical and experimental object usage. Neither passage cancels the other. The same accepted review records reciprocal time/energy forms on the original page; this dossier does not silently select a corrected formula.

**Inputs and factors.** B2a's ANPA comparison records a twice-Bohr-radius result and empirical spectral input; B2b P14-08/P14-11 records conflicting energy expressions and coefficient choices in the Heresy versions. B3c Q10–Q11 retains the absent atomic-speed exponent, adopted excited-state scaling and formal spin pairing in Lecture 21. These prevent combining selected factors from different versions into a single supposed author derivation.

**Electrical wording is not dimensional certification.** The synopsis calls electromotive force a force while giving volts. H0003 records its retained practical vocabulary; it does not identify volts with mechanical force units or supply a quantitative conversion between every electrical and mechanical quantity.

**Nuclear hypothesis and damaged witness.** The paper titled *The Electron within the Neutron* is a conventional-model comparison, not a completed native nuclear account. B2b P14-23 reports uncertain dating, missing printed page 2, an explicitly labeled hypothesis and a target-informed mass calculation. Missing text remains unknown. No numerical nuclear result from that witness is promoted here.

**General theory remains open.** The cited texts do not supply a complete account of all atoms, material magnetism or nuclear transformations. This is a bounded reading of the examples and qualifications cited, not permission to replace them with later research premises. Chemistry is treated separately in C11.

## Test for new work

*Editorial synthesis.*

- **C09-T0001:** Begin with spectra, current, voltage, work or another specified observable; separate its record from the microscopic interpretation.
- **C09-T0002:** Mark conventional model comparisons and particle labels explicitly. Do not turn the authors' analytical representation into a continuous hidden mechanism.
- **C09-T0003:** List empirical mass, energy and spectral inputs before claiming a prediction. A target used to construct a parameter is not independent confirmation.
- **C09-T0004:** Keep action, angular momentum, charge, energy and voltage dimensionally distinct; identify every proposed equivalence.
- **C09-T0005:** Preserve version-specific factors and source defects. Label magnetic and nuclear extrapolations as proposals until the missing law is supplied and separately evaluated.
