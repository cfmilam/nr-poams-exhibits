# Canon Notes

## Scope and order

This edition contains thirteen source-grounded dossiers, C01–C13. Normal Realism comes first. POAMS is the physics that follows from it.

The source authority is the completed in-scope authored corpus: the books, papers and lectures of Pope and Osborne. Later Exhibits, provisional patents and Engineering documents are downstream work. They do not supply or complete the authors' holdings.

## Status

Each dossier separates source attribution from editorial synthesis, later program premises, imported tools, measurements, fits, reconstructions and conjectures. A source statement is not a measurement receipt. Differences between texts and rules the texts do not supply remain visible in the owning dossier.

Legacy page 01 and legacy page 07 remain preserved as historical compatibility snapshots. Their content has been routed into the thirteen-dossier edition; they are not additional current dossiers.

The bundled opening-context packet is a legacy compatibility snapshot; activation/migration against this edition is unreviewed.

## How to use the set

Use the Core for orientation. Read the full relevant dossier before relying on a holding. Use Grounding and the Foundation Wall to identify later program commitments and evidence standards. Use the Project-Start Protocol before a new construction. The full Exhibit is the reference library, not the default payload for every working session.

## Revision rule

Preserve source locations, prior versions, the change and its reason. New authored-source evidence can revise the Canon. A later program premise must be labeled as an addition and cannot be attributed backward to Pope or Osborne.
