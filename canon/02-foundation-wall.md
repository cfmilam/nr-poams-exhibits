# POAMS Foundational Wall

**Status:** amended 2026-10-18; governing provenance, measurement and paper-first research discipline for POAMS work  
**Control rule:** if another working document conflicts with this wall, this wall controls until Christopher F. Milam explicitly amends it.

## 1. POAMS is not confined to reconstruction, but reconstruction is allowed

POAMS is its own developing account of the measured world. It does not owe permanent allegiance to inherited ontology, but at this early stage its exhibits may use inherited measurements, mathematics, classifications, and successful physical or chemical results as declared inputs, scaffolds, comparators, and targets.

The following distinctions govern such work:

- A reconstruction toward a known answer may be useful, but it is not an independent prediction.
- Imported mathematics may be used, but its source and role must be stated.
- Agreement with an inherited result may show compatibility, economy, reach, correction, or organization; by itself it does not validate POAMS ontology.
- A genuinely native derivation or prospective prediction must be identified separately and held to the stronger standard appropriate to that claim.
- Mathematical and empirical contributions are retained on their merits; they are not rejected merely because their lineage is inherited.

No exhibit fails merely because it performs a bridge, reconstruction, recovery, or comparison. The audit question is whether the work states what was imported, whether the mathematics and logic are sound, and whether downstream claims remain within what the work actually establishes.

## 2. Provenance rule for ideas and mathematics

Every live claim must identify its inputs and status. Native POAMS ingredients include whole circulation, angular-momentum accounting, closure, phase sense or advance, twist, writhe, co-signed exchange, constraint, natural orbit, and directly defined compositions of those objects. Imported ingredients are permitted when labeled.

Every consequential symbol and operation must have a **provenance receipt**:

1. whether it is native, imported, measured, fitted, definitional, or conjectural;
2. where it comes from and what role it plays;
3. what conclusion it supports and what it does not support;
4. where applicable, what observation or logical test would make the proposed relation fail.

A mathematical operation is admissible when it is:

- transparently reconstructed from native objects;
- openly adopted from extant mathematics or physics as a conditional tool or model; or
- a disclosed arithmetic/data-reduction operation whose effect on records is independently checkable.

A familiar mathematical name carries no automatic physical authority and imports no ontology by stealth. Starting from an inherited equation or known target is allowed when declared. Fitting or reconstruction must not be called a first-principles derivation or independent confirmation. If a native chain cannot be written, label the result conditional, imported, fitted, or reconstructive and continue only within that boundary.

## 3. The measurement wall

The measured world is the only court of appeal, but a statement in a paper, a plotted curve, a fitted parameter, or a theoretical reconstruction is not thereby a measurement.

A measurement claim is admissible only with a **measurement receipt** that identifies:

- the raw sensor event, count, voltage, displacement, image, timing record, or other instrument output;
- the instrument, specimen, geometry, wiring, orientation, environment, and acquisition timing;
- calibration records before and after the run, including range, drift, saturation, and cross-sensitivity;
- control, sham, reversal, blank, and blinded/randomized states where applicable;
- every exclusion, correction, transform, average, normalization, and uncertainty calculation between raw record and reported quantity;
- missing data, failed runs, stopping rules, and analyst degrees of freedom;
- immutable raw-data location and hash;
- independent repetition or a concrete replication plan.

Keep three layers separate:

1. **raw occurrence** — what the apparatus actually registered;
2. **operational reduction** — the disclosed calculation applied to that record;
3. **story** — an inferred object, path, cause, or ontology.

Only the first two can be measured. The third must be derived natively or left unclaimed. In a two-slit or any other canonical experiment, screen marks, detector events, settings, timings, and geometry are measurements; claims about an unseen traveler, path, wave, collapse, or state are not measurements merely because they accompany the plot.

### Evidence grades

- **M0 — POAMS direct:** our raw records, complete receipt, adversarial controls, and successful repeat.
- **M1 — externally reproducible:** accessible third-party raw records and a complete receipt that we can independently replay.
- **M2 — reported result:** a paper, table, image, or processed number without adequate raw provenance. It is a lead only, never a foundation or target.
- **M3 — theory-laden inference:** an entity, latent parameter, corrected value, or reconstruction whose existence depends on the model used to extract it. It is inadmissible as a measurement.

When provenance is doubtful, the answer is not automatic acceptance or convenient dismissal. The answer is **replication**. If we cannot yet replicate, the phenomenon remains open and cannot carry a derivation.

## 4. Data can still defeat us

The hard wall is not permission to protect POAMS from an inconvenient result. A repeatable raw observation with a complete receipt outranks a favored derivation. A preregistered null is a result. A reproducible contradiction narrows or kills the POAMS claim that made the prediction.

We do not reject data because its consequence is unwelcome; we reject unsupported stories masquerading as data. Replicate, do not rationalize. Record all runs, preserve negative results, and never alter a gate after seeing the outcome.

## 5. Direct calibration outranks inherited explanation

External material may be used for component identification, vendor limits, safety practice, fabrication tolerances, legal requirements, and access to raw records. Those uses do not authorize the material's physical ontology or equations as POAMS premises.

For an instrument or confound, characterize the response directly whenever possible: inject heat, vibration, force, orientation, timing offsets, electrical pickup, gradients, and loading in controlled amounts and measure the transfer into the output. A calculated conventional expectation may not substitute for the control measurement. Safety limits remain mandatory.

## 6. Technology-directed, paper-first gate

**Amended by Christopher F. Milam on 2026-10-18.** `GROUNDING.md` §0.2 supplies the full paper-completion standard. Exhaust the proposed path's available logical, mathematical and ontological derivations before experimental design; adopt explicit parsimonious constitutive extensions where a necessary law or operation is otherwise underdetermined. A candidate that proves wrong is closed and preserved on paper rather than sent to the laboratory with an unspecified mechanism.

A theoretical campaign identifies these five targets and derives their content before clearing paper review:

1. **Native intervention:** exactly what circulation, closure, channel, partition, or constraint is proposed to change, with a defined state, preparation, update and complete account.
2. **Raw observable:** the specified instrument-level record predicted to change—before any ontological interpretation. Sensor selection and apparatus layout follow paper closure.
3. **Prospective discriminator:** derived sign, magnitude or justified parameter-dependent range, ordering, threshold, scaling, memory, reversal, or null; freeze the decision rule before data.
4. **Artifact-control obligations:** identify the competing accounts and credible artifact paths that must eventually be independently characterized. Derive what their records would distinguish; do not confuse a paper control requirement with a completed apparatus design or a measured control.
5. **Technological leverage:** what reproducible control would enable in sensing, actuation, navigation, communication, materials, computation, or energy handling.

Missing theory is an obligation to continue or reject the paper path, not a direction to design an experiment around the gap. The paper receipt must close source, terminal, retained, outlet, reset and apparatus accounts, carry through the relevant consequences and countermodels, and distinguish irreducible empirical calibrations from undetermined mechanisms. Explicit extensions are conditional research choices with independent failure conditions, not retroactive deductions or empirical confirmation.

Only a completed, reviewed paper target proceeds to **experimental design and resourcing**, followed by a **separate green-light decision**, then construction, operation and receipted comparison. Completing theory alone grants no spending or build authority. Mathematical completeness does not establish that nature follows the candidate. Choose projects by technological leverage multiplied by experimental discriminability and divided by unearned assumptions—not by resemblance to an inherited theory. This replaces the former instruction to design a missing experiment before completing the theoretical path.

## 7. Audit and enforcement

A valid POAMS audit asks only:

- Is the claim native from declared POAMS primitives?
- Is the arithmetic, logic, geometry, code, or bookkeeping internally valid?
- Does every claimed observation have the required measurement receipt?
- Does the apparatus discriminate the claimed effect from measured artifacts?
- Was the prediction frozen before the result?
- What useful control follows if the result repeats?

When inherited ontology, target mathematics, or an external measurement enters a live argument, label its provenance and limit the downstream claim accordingly. Correct only mathematical mistakes, clear logical mistakes and their downstream consequences, empirical misstatements, or materially missing provenance. Do not quarantine, rewrite, or reject an exhibit merely because it relies on imported work or a known target.

Historical files are not silently destroyed; they are evidence of what was tried. The native-wall audit launched 2026-09-24 is superseded as a governing standard and is warehoused for later reference. Its findings remain leads, but only independently verified mathematical errors, clear logical errors and downstream consequences, empirical misstatements, and missing import labels may be carried forward.

**Public/private custody amendment (ratified 2026-10-09).** Preservation does not require public display. Superseded derivations, failed mechanisms, abandoned devices, repair diaries, adverse branches and dated working records belong in a private corrective archive with their original bytes, context, date, cryptographic hash and supersession reason. Remove them from Hub navigation and public-facing delivery once a current successor exists. Public exposition carries the present construction and only those limitations that materially affect its meaning, safety, evidential grade or next experiment. Git history is redundant custody, not the sole research memory. No archive item is erased merely because it leaves the public corpus.

**Authorial posture amendment (ratified 2026-10-09).** The authorial burden is a coherent, intelligible and committed presentation. The critic's burden is criticism. Public POAMS does not apologize for its ambition, pre-write hostile review, or repeat caveats to demonstrate seriousness. It states the proposal at full responsible strength and invites understanding, testing, acceptance, rejection and debate. The polished tonal lineage is *Einstein's Lost Legacy*, *Eye of the Beholder*, and *Light-Speed, Gravitation and Quantum Instantaneity*. Source papers remain evidence and inspiration; the books govern the public narrative voice.
