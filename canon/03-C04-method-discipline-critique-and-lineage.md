# C04 — Method, discipline, critique and lineage

## Holdings

**C04-H0001 — Inquiry begins with phenomena.** The philosophical starting point precedes the physical model. C03 owns the associated whole-before-parts analysis.

> "The Normal Realist approach to POAMS is fundamentally observational, so that we begin with the phenomena at the empirical level of reality, that is, with things we actually observe." — poams-and-the-atom-synopsis:P0008

**C04-H0002 — Observation requires interpretation.** The existence of an occurrence and the adequacy of its description are different questions.

> "observations have to be interpreted, and it is these interpretations of physical phenomena that create the mysteries." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P1774
> "no data of sense or instrumentation are objectively specifiable but are always contextual, depending upon the ways in which language is used." — phenomenalism-1-the-philosophical-root-source-of-relativity:P0035

**C04-H0003 — Conflicting conventions are to be revised for logical and practical adequacy.** Logical tidiness alone does not make a vocabulary sufficient for experience.

> "Where conventions do not hang together we simply make adjustments until they do." — a-synopsis-of-normal-realism-revised:P0093
> "The only criterion to be observed is that the result of any adjustment represents a maximum of both logical and practical efficiency – and sufficiency." — a-synopsis-of-normal-realism-revised:P0079

**C04-H0004 — Conceptual economy matters alongside predictive success.** The authors apply Ockham's principle to the number of explanatory ideas, not merely to the compactness of a formula.

> "This rules that one should not increase, beyond what is strictly necessary, the number of ideas required to explain a phenomenon." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0164
> "Some of these other criteria are every bit as important as that of purely pragmatical, predictive power." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0164

**C04-H0005 — Philosophical rejection does not require abandonment of useful theories.** Working hypotheses and retained mathematical elements have a legitimate practical role. Their success does not establish their ontology.

Attribution: Osborne.

> "However, on a purely practical level, this does not imply that all existing physical theories should be abandoned." — immediate-distant-action:P1636
> "Many true advances in practical physics had undoubtedly been secured by means of such theories and their purely practical spin-offs had obviously been of great benefit to mankind." — the-odyssey:P0351
> "whilst retaining some of the mathematical elements of existing theories, it dispenses with other elements regarded as unnecessary" — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0913
> "Now from a purely pragmatical point of view, there are, obviously, advantages to be gained from both kinds of curiosity concerning the occurrences of numbers in nature" — anpa--poams---two-systems-of-combinatorics-2002:P0034

**C04-H0006 — The discipline concerns explicit premises and incompatible precepts, not dogmatic closure.** A contradiction must be identified, rather than inferred from a proposal's unfamiliarity.

> "Normal Realism is therefore not a dogma; but to be a Normal Realist is to submit to a discipline, which involves familiarising oneself with the full range of basic presuppositions and with their method of logical evolution." — a-synopsis-of-normal-realism-revised:P0057
> "preserving it from dilution in terms of foreign (incompatible) precepts and prejudices" — a-synopsis-of-normal-realism-revised:P0057
> "Where these may be discovered in the course of argument they are signified by being declared unconstitutional." — a-synopsis-of-normal-realism-revised:P0057

**C04-H0007 — Physical laws require more than a philosophical preference or a conservation statement.** Osborne declares additional assumptions in the orbital account. The detailed physical construction belongs to C07.

> "no conservation principle automatically provides an equation of motion." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0914
Attribution: Osborne.

> "However, as made explicit in this chapter, there are other assumptions made and so POAMS does not attempt to be a new axiomatic physical theory in any accepted sense but rather represents a synthesis of ideas." — immediate-distant-action:P1631
Attribution: Osborne.

> "Hence, it should be noted that the POAMS account of orbital motion does not make fewer assumptions than the Newtonian approach." — immediate-distant-action:P1631

**C04-H0008 — The stated lineage is selective, not wholesale adoption.** The books place the philosophy in an empiricist and linguistic tradition, extend ordinary commonsense beyond its customary domain, and distinguish their purpose from Bondi's pedagogical use of the conversion factor.

> "This is a continuation of that historical skein of commonsense philosophy which was woven by those empiricists, Mach, Moore, Austin, Ryle, Wittgenstein, et al., in the tradition of George Berkeley." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0302
> "Normal Realism is an articulation of the normal, logical way of thinking and use of language which extends the Wittgensteinian method of 'purified commonsense' beyond ordinary everyday experience" — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0226
> "In contrast to Bondi, our different reason was, on that same basis, to develop a whole new philosophical approach to physics" — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0289

## What the texts reject

They reject unnecessary conceptual additions, insulated physical pictures and the claim that useful prediction alone settles philosophical interpretation (C04-H0004–C04-H0005). They reject treating the existing ordinary language as finished; its revision belongs to C02.

Their criticism of Berkeley concerns the move to mind alone, not every aspect of his philosophical challenge (C01). Their criticism of Einstein's interpretation is not rejection of all relativistic results or permission to erase their own changed positions. The accepted B2a Apologia and B2b NPA/aberration comparisons retain those qualifications.

The texts' own methodological criticism also applies to claims made in POAMS's name. Conservation is not an equation of motion, and minimal interpretation is not proof that no assumptions were added. C04-H0007 expressly prevents that inference.

## Differences and open rules

**Pure deduction and additional postulates.** The accepted B1A ledger records no-theory and purely syllogistic descriptions in immediate-distant-action:P0878, P1841 and P1902 alongside the explicit postulate at P1894 and Osborne's assumptions in P1631. These claims of method are not automatically equivalent. This dossier preserves the distinction and does not supply an assumption-free derivation to reconcile them. C05 separately identifies the clock-triangle assumptions; C07 must retain the premises of its orbital equations.

**Useful theory and physical ground.** The theory permission in C04-H0005 is affirmative, not merely a grudging exception. *Lines from Einstein - An Intellectual Odyssey by Viv Pope*, P0364, also describes educated guesses as necessary theories of empirical science. Its P0351 criticism of one theoretical picture is explicitly retrospective; P0828–P0829 reconsiders wave-functional speculation. An undated absolute ban would erase that development. Practical retention does not itself establish any proposed microscopic machinery.

**Criticism and evidence.** The accepted B2b P14-03 comparison records the claim in the Heresy papers that enough experiments already exist and logical analysis should take priority. That is an author methodological position, not a measurement receipt or current permission to bypass evidence requirements. Likewise, reported institutional reactions, claimed experiments and autobiographical conversations remain testimony unless separately documented. This boundary is editorial evidence classification, not a new claim about what the authors observed.

**Names and dates.** The sources disagree about the inception of the name:

> "Normal Realism began under that title in Pope's association, in the late 1960s, with another mature student, G. A. Evans" — normal-realism-the-machian-legacy:P0018
> "the word 'normal' in Normal Realism was carefully chosen at its inception in the 1970s." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0225

The different emphases in “normal” remain in C01-H0004. A later website date, scan date or corpus processing date cannot resolve a composition-date question. The edited volume's copyright year is 2005; its scan metadata is not an author revision date.

**Speaker and genre.** *Immediate Distant Action* contains other contributors, summarized correspondence and editorial conclusions; B1A records an explicit absence of consensus. *Wondering in the Wilderness* declares fictional human participants and uses thesis/antithesis narration. *Theological Discussion* preserves interlocutors and Pope's corrections, including concessions to Gower and Alison. Those forms matter to attribution. B3b's index can locate topics but cannot establish endorsement. A quotation of Bondi, Lewis, Mach or another predecessor in these sources is a mediated historical attribution unless checked in that predecessor's own work.

## Test for new work

**Editorial synthesis.**

- **C04-T0001:** State the phenomenon and separate the record from its interpretation. Identify whether an empirical assertion is a report or a reproducible measurement.
- **C04-T0002:** List physical and mathematical premises. State what follows conditionally and what remains assumed.
- **C04-T0003:** Permit useful imported calculations as declared tools or comparisons. Do not promote their ontology by successful calculation alone.
- **C04-T0004:** Test economy and sufficiency together. Removing a necessary assumption is not conceptual economy.
- **C04-T0005:** Identify the exact incompatible precept before invoking constitutional rejection. Preserve the rejected route and the source disagreement without rewriting history.
- **C04-T0006:** Preserve author, contributor, dialogue speaker, genre, work version and date. Do not turn reported agreement or an edited debate into consensus.
- **C04-T0007:** Apply the same evidential distinction to favored and adverse results. A source statement is not a measurement receipt.
