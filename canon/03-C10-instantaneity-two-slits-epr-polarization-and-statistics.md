# C10 — Instantaneity, two slits, EPR, polarization and statistics

## Holdings

**C10-H0001 — The two-slit argument begins with the apparatus and its geometry.** The observed pattern is not first explained by giving an unseen traveler knowledge of the screen. Pope asks what the experimental arrangement already determines.

> "Let us, for the moment, forget about light and the technical questions as to whether it consists of waves and/or particles and look at the two-slit apparatus itself. Like any other object, this has built-in geometrical features." — a-commonsense-look-at-the-two-slit-experiment:P0020

The tape construction compares two source-to-screen routes. It is a geometrical model of alternatives, not a claim that tapes or an intervening substance are physically present in an exchange.

**C10-H0002 — A single exchange and a statistical interference pattern are different subjects.** Pope locates interference-type structure in the probability description of many events.

> "There is, then, no interference, diffraction, diminution, entropy or whatever, in a single quantum jump." — action-at-a-distance---oct-1996:P0070

> "All these 'wave'-type phenomena are features of the probabilistic wave-function which, in the two-slit apparatus, extends, in its statistical, fuzzy-geometrical way, through the two slits at once." — action-at-a-distance---oct-1996:P0070

This is source statistical language. It does not establish a material wave in empty space.

**C10-H0003 — Instantaneous links are not transitive links.** The later joint book explicitly limits the cinematic analogy. Connecting one pair and another pair does not establish one universal instantaneous event.

> "Quantum interactions (quantum stills) are discrete and intransitive, which simply means that when atom $A$ is interacting with atom $B$, and atom $B$ is interacting with atom $C$, it doesn’t necessarily mean that atom $A$ is interacting with atom $C$" — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0474

C05 owns the distinction between an exchange's own time and observer time. The present holding concerns the composition of exchanges.

**C10-H0004 — Pope resolves the stated EPR paradox by changing the interpretation of c.** The argument identifies the two ends of a direct exchange as one event; it retains the observer's time relation.

> "In the Bondian interpretation of $c$ as a plain constant, or distance-time conversion factor, the ‘paradox’ simply disappears." — eye-of-beholder:P0278

> "As can be seen in that formula, when the intrinsic transition-time of the quantum is zero, the observer-time $t_R$ is the delayed time $s/c$." — eye-of-beholder:P0278

This is the author's proposed resolution of a conceptual contradiction. It is not itself a derivation of a Bell correlation function. The historical experimental account in P0276 is a report about other experiments, not their raw measurement record.

**C10-H0005 — Direction and polarization are described through atomic orientation and patterns of observations.** The source gives a directional condition, not a complete analyzer-angle law.

> "Every quantum of energy emitted by an atom is particular and directional, according to the vector orientation of the atom's internal angular momenta (orbit, spin, etc.)." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0823

> "Recall, also, that all physical phenomena such as polarisation, reflection, diffraction, the dispersion and refraction of light in some medium or other are, ultimately, optical projections from directly perceived cinematic sequences of informational patterns of quantum pixels (phota)." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0538

**C10-H0006 — The probability mathematics is proposed for use, not wholly derived in the philosophical account.** The Odyssey describes a possible accommodation of existing probability functions and expressly leaves detailed work to others.

> "Now approaching his seventies, he decided to leave the detailed working-out of these suggestions to others, younger and more practically and mathematically disposed who, now that the implications for physics were plain, might wish to pursue them." — the-odyssey:P0630

The surrounding P0628–P0630 invokes de Broglie and Schrödinger mathematics. It is an adopted or proposed mathematical tool within Pope's interpretation, not a newly supplied frequency law.

## What the texts reject

They reject the single quantum dividing into independent pieces or interfering with itself as a material wave. They reject the necessity of an unseen traveler selecting its course before the exchange can be complete. They also reject the inference from elementary instantaneity to a universal present.

The rejection of a traveler is not the rejection of the experimental pattern. H0001 and H0002 expressly retain the arrangement and statistical observations. Nor does changing the interpretation of c make an operator's elapsed time vanish.

## Differences and open rules

**Parity conflict.** The 1996 paper assigns the same parity to suppression and enhancement:

> "differ by some even number of units λ/2 the probability of a quantum appearing at that point on the screen approaches zero." — action-at-a-distance---oct-1996:P0070

> "where the lengths either agree, or differ by some even number of units λ/2 the probability of a quantum of that wavelength λ appearing at that point is enhanced" — action-at-a-distance---oct-1996:P0070

The Commonsense paper P0021/P0026 distinguishes odd from even. The conflicting supplied wording is retained, not corrected by silently substituting one version for another. Original-use authentication of these exact conflicting spans remains necessary before describing the defect as a verified first-print error.

**Event occurrence versus probability.** The Commonsense paper states:

> "It either occurs or it doesn't. In probabilistic terms, this means that the probability of its occurring is between nought and one, with no possibilities in between." — a-commonsense-look-at-the-two-slit-experiment:P0025

Editorial analysis: an event's occurrence or nonoccurrence does not restrict its probability to those two endpoints. The quoted sentence does not supply an intermediate-frequency rule.

**Statistical mathematics is present.** It would be too strong to say that the texts contain no probability rule at all. The 1996 paper invokes amplitude squared, and B2b P13-14 identifies phase and squared-amplitude use in the-absolute-instantaneity-of-relativistic-light-interaction:P0091. These are mathematical selections within the accounts. What remains unestablished here is an independently derived rule selecting actual pairs and normalized terminal frequencies for a specified apparatus.

**Polarization and joint outcomes.** H0005 supplies orientation language; the cited passages do not determine the full setting-dependent joint distribution, loss treatment or analyzer response. Conservation of an opposed pair is not, without further mathematical assumptions, a derivation of all analyzer-angle statistics.

**Witness and attribution limits.** The published, draft and later two-slit versions must remain separate; later owner prose is not Pope's. The source-map warning about Lecture 11:P0014's reversed Bell wording remains a specific original-page question, not a sentence to adopt. No damaged EPR equation or later coherent-completion premise is used to repair the author account.

**Vocabulary changes.** The 1996 paper P0066/P0069 uses a statistical ether analogy. Later direct-exchange descriptions deny a medium. The analogy must be read at its stated statistical level, while the difference in wording remains recorded.
