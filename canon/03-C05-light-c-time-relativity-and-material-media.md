# C05 — Light, c, time, relativity and material media

## Holdings

**C05-H0001 — Light is information, not an unseen carrier of information.** The philosophical reversal is established in C01 and the observer/whole account in C03. Its application here denies that light travels in a preexisting void.

> "In our information-based approach, light is not a ‘carrier’ of information, it is the information itself." — eye-of-beholder:P0271
> "In NR, the philosophy on which our lateral-thinking alternative approach to modern physics is based, light is not something that travels in space." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0293
> "far from thinking of light being ‘in space’, we think of space – and all it contains – as being in the light." — eye-of-beholder:P0271

The scope includes radio, not visible illumination alone:

> "In fact, light and radio are, basically, one and the same phenomenon, except that the frequencies of visible light are vastly greater than those of radio." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0466

**C05-H0002 — An elementary exchange is one event with two ends.** The authors describe loss at one atom and gain at another as a reciprocal relation, not two independent events joined by an intervening carrier. This is their physical account, not an independently established measurement.

> "Conservation of energy demands that every quantum of interaction has to be an instantly consummated, double-ended affair, a one-ended quantum jump being as impossible to conceive as a one-ended coil of wire." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0730
> "At the quantum level, therefore, the two atoms are in a direct and instantly reciprocal inter-resonance relation in accordance with Newton’s law of equal and opposite action-and-reaction." — faster-than-radar-precis---sept-2010:P0012

**C05-H0003 — The exchange's own time is zero; its observer interval need not be.** Intrinsic transition-time, a body's proper time and observer-time must remain distinct. The atomic cycle period is a further distinction, developed in C06.

> "Since their intrinsic transition-time ('proper-time') t is zero, their intrinsic transition-distance is also zero (i.e., s = ct = 0)." — eye-of-beholder:P0293
> "the emitter and absorber of a photum, which are non-separated in quantum space and time, are separated in observer-time by the observational interval $s/c$." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0733
> "To the question, then, of whether action-at-distance is instantaneous or time-delayed, the answer, as far as light is concerned, is plainly that it is both." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0383

Here s is the separation assigned through the observer's wider record, not a distance determined by the isolated exchange (C03-H0006). Proper-time instantaneity is not a demonstrated zero-delay message on an operator's clock.

**C05-H0004 — c is the constant ratio of distance and time units for inertial observers using the same conventions.** The authors retain the first relativity postulate and replace the interpretation of the second.

> "Postulate 1: The laws of physics are the same in all inertial frames." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0249
> "Postulate 2: Observational distance and time have a constant ratio of units, c, for all inertial observers sharing that same conventional choice of units." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0285
> "although the constant c is the least upper bound of all relative speeds, it is not a speed as such." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0383

The bound concerns the body's relative speed s/t_R, measured with the observer's clock. The different quotient s/t uses the body's proper time and is unbounded in the authors' account. Neither quotient is a speed assigned to light.

> "The first is what we can describe as the 'Newtonian' speed and is s/t, where s is the distance travelled by X in proper time t. The second is what can be described as the 'Einsteinian' speed and is s/t_R, where t_R is the time taken for X to travel the distance s relative to O's clock." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0383
> "a theoretically unbounded Newtonian speed" — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0383

**C05-H0005 — The uniform-motion time relation uses a right-triangle construction.** The source identifies the proper time t and displacement measure s/c as perpendicular components and the hypotenuse as observer-time t_R.

> "let us now think of $t$ as the passage of time recorded by a 'clock' moving with the body $X$." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0350
> "Then $t_R$ will be simply the length of the hypotenuse of the triangle in Fig. 3.1(b), given by Pythagoras's theorem as" — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0352
> "t_R^2 = t^2 + (s/c)^2." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0353
> "Now the speed, $v$, of $X$ relative to $O$ (that is, relative to $O$'s clock) is given by $v = s/t_R$" — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0357
> "t = (1 - v^2/c^2)^{1/2} t_R." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0364

**Editorial premise analysis:** conversion of units alone does not establish orthogonality or identify the hypotenuse with a clock reading. The authors present a derivation; this dossier records the additional geometric and clock-identification premises. No later program premise supplies them retrospectively. For nonuniform motion, proper time must be accumulated along the path, not computed from final displacement alone:

> "It follows by (3.2b) and the definition of the Riemann integral that in this case, the relatively moving clock $X$ records proper time" — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0425

**C05-H0006 — Resonance and a receiving end are conditions of the exchange.** They do not constitute a complete pair-selection or probability law.

> "It is only when these two measures are resonant that the atoms interact" — from-light-in-space-to-space-in-light-the-complete-relativis:P0062
> "quantum energy cannot 'start out' from a source if the conditions for its 'reception' at a distance are not instantly assured." — why-the-constant-c-cannot-be-a-speed-definitive:P0050

The preceding period statement is:

> "The period is the inverse of the quantum energy divided by Planck's constant $h$." — from-light-in-space-to-space-in-light-the-complete-relativis:P0061

C06 distinguishes action h, angular momentum h/2π and the nonzero cycle from zero transition-time. A ready receiver is not by itself an independently derived control mechanism.

**C05-H0007 — Reflection involves two exchanges.** In the source/mirror example, each contributes an observer interval s/c; the stated round-trip interval is 2s/c.

> "Obviously, a single atom cannot both gain and lose a quantum in a single instance." — eye-of-beholder:P0292
> "This involves another lapse of observational time s/c. Hence, the overall observational time recorded at A, for the signal transaction will be 2s/c (see Fig. 5.1)." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0736

**C05-H0008 — Doppler intervals must be distinguished from dilated clock intervals.** For a receding source, the source displacement between records adds to the observer's pulse interval. This is not a change in the conversion constant.

> "Let the very small period of time between successive 'pulses' be δt, as recorded by a clock moving with the source X. This time will be recorded by O's clock as δt_R" — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0393
> "\delta t_R^* = \delta t_R + v\delta t_R/c." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0396

The starred pulse interval is not δt_R. Pope also reads aberration as evidence of the observer's motion among surrounding bodies:

> "So aberration tells us that it is the earth that is moving in the heavens, not the heavens that are moving with respect to the earth." — eye-of-beholder:P0221

This records the author's interpretation, not a new observational result. The alternative cosmological reading of redshift belongs to C11.

**C05-H0009 — A material sequence is not one elementary exchange.** The books distinguish direct reciprocal events from mediated statistical processes; the fiber paper proposes time dilation of the sequence recorded by an observer. For apparent deflection near a star, the Light-Speed book proposes material refraction and a formal limiting Schwarzschild-geodesic comparison; Legacy emphasizes informationally projected lines of sight and orbital geometry. C05, Differences and open rules, preserves these formulations without identifying them or deriving an index law from the angular comparison.

> "Such processes are subject to entropy, in which the mediated quanta lose their directness and therefore the certainty of connection which there is in each single quantum (photum)." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0316
> "the process of light conduction, like all other processes in the medium, was time-dilated relatively to the observer." — refraction-in-optic-fibres:P0012

The paper proposes an effective material-motion comparison:

> "the fibre can be likened to a system moving as a whole relatively to the observer at an effective (rms) speed v" — refraction-in-optic-fibres:P0010

That is a proposed bridge, not a derived quantitative law of material response. Storage or residence in an atom, discussed in the accepted B2a angular-momentum-iaaad---june-2001:P0006–P0011 comparison, must not be assigned to the elementary transition itself.

**C05-H0010 — The wire/fiber analogy has an explicitly unresolved limit.** Pope compares them but does not identify electricity with light.

> "they are both conduits for disembodied energy." — refraction-in-optic-fibres:P0014
> "But, of course, this does not mean that 'electricity’ can be identified as a form of light." — refraction-in-optic-fibres:P0017
> "An electric current cannot flow without a completed circuit, whereas light flows in a fibre towards an open end. The reason for this particular difference is not clear" — refraction-in-optic-fibres:P0018

## What the texts reject

They reject light as a substance or carrier traversing empty space and reject an ether for the direct exchange:

> "What we do not accept is the customary view of light being composed of space-travelling ‘photons’." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0218
> "And these resonances are not those of ‘waves’ travelling in an ‘ether’ or in any other kind of medium." — eye-of-beholder:P0270
> "any assumption as to the existence of an 'ether' is not only un-empirical but is also logically redundant." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0278

Pope's visibility argument says that seeing light in transit would require it to produce further light (why-the-constant-c-cannot-be-a-speed-definitive:P0057). The joint book describes a visible beam in dust as successive atomic illuminations, not observation of a traveler (light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0294), and rejects recoil as sufficient evidence of a ballistic quantum (P0762). These are interpretive arguments, not fresh measurements.

The open-switch argument reports no near-end transient with the far switch open:

> "There is no detectable transient flow. The circuit remains completely empty of charge until the switch at B is closed." — why-the-constant-c-cannot-be-a-speed-definitive:P0019

The accepted B2c review classifies this as an empirical assertion without the apparatus, calibration and raw records needed for a measurement receipt. The conclusion about conservation and readiness is the author's argument, not independent experimental certification. The book itself records differing reported wire rates and says its argument does not depend on the chosen value (light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0329).

## Differences and open rules

**Time, global frames and vocabulary.** The rejection of a universal present in eye-of-beholder:P0295 coexists with preferred-frame and system-wide time language in the accepted B2b comparisons: new-discussion-file-challenges-to-poams:P0118–P0122/P0174–P0180, the-angular-momentum-ether:P0056, and should-i-remain-a-member-of-the-npa:P0049–P0055. Some passages use an ideal average, others stronger fixed-frame language. This dossier does not harmonize them into a universal scheduler. C11 owns the direct universal-time disagreement, including the Odyssey's universal-now wording and the later joint book's denial of one overall now. General nontransitivity and the every-body conflict belong to C06. Authors' occasional affirmative travel vocabulary is a recorded textual variant, not adopted terminology here.

**Time-relation status.** The right-triangle identification is not obtained from a conversion constant alone. Accepted B1B/B2b/B3a routes retain orthogonality, clock identification and formula-transmission questions. The quoted book equations above state the source result; no new figure or purported independent proof is supplied. The B1A original-page check places t outside the radical at immediate-distant-action:P1893/P1895; the corpus defect is not an author contradiction. Other damaged formulas remain individually unverified rather than repaired by analogy.

**Apparent deflection near a star.** *Light-Speed, Gravitation and Quantum Instantaneity*, P1499–P1501, distinguishes an elementary exchange without an intervening carrier from a material sequence. It proposes time dilation associated with the mediating material's energy and a spectral-frequency-dependent ratio, with refractive index η = c/c1. Its positive interpretation is:

> "In Normal Realism, it is the refraction of a light ray that causes it to appear bent." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P1501

P1502 applies a formal Schwarzschild-geodesic comparison to the proposed medium around the Sun: the conventional calculation concerns material bodies with speeds below but close to c and then takes the limit of proper time approaching zero. This is imported mathematical comparison, not an adopted light-carrier picture:

> "Hence, formally, we begin with the geodesic equations and let the proper time, $\tau$ approach zero." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P1502

P1503–P1505 attributes apparent deflection to the medium around a star and reports the angular result δ ≈ 4MG/(r0 c²), or 1.75 seconds of arc for the stated solar substitution. That formal result does not derive from a completed material-index law; the material proposal and the mathematical limit are distinct steps.

The later *Einstein's Lost Legacy*, P0538, instead emphasizes informationally projected lines of sight:

> "light-geodesics as secondary (a posteriori) backwards-in-time projections from directly perceived informational patterns and sequences of these directly perceived light-pixels." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0538

P0542 locates the relevant distortion in orbital geometry, not empty space itself:

> "the only such bendings or twistings that can be sensibly contemplated have to be those of the actual orbital trajectories of the bodies of which that space-time consists." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0542
> "these geodesics determined by angular momentum, extrapolated in a manner similar to that of General Relativity, are distorted like the grain around the knots in a plank of wood" — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0542

The material-refraction proposal, formal angular comparison and later informational/orbital-geometrical formulation are source positions. These passages do not establish their equivalence or supply empirical validation. C07 owns the orbital and perihelion account; no quantitative material law is supplied by that cross-reference.

**Fiber reciprocal and flow conflicts.** The fiber paper gives:

> "c1/c = tR/tR1" — refraction-in-optic-fibres:P0006
> "It is scarcely coincidental that this ratio is the same as the refractive index, μ" — refraction-in-optic-fibres:P0010

It also identifies the index as c/c1. Those ratios are reciprocals. P0012 says both that the index increased with flow and that the effective conduction rate increased in that direction. Under one fixed index definition those statements conflict. P0014's proper-time velocity multiplies where the stated time relation requires division. These are corpus-witness issues retained from legacy page 07; this draft has not newly authenticated the printed fiber original. B3a separately records a dimensional problem in the related refraction appendix of *Wondering in the Wilderness*. Neither witness silently repairs the other.

**Period, readiness and material laws.** The source distinguishes the nonzero cycle from zero transition-time (C06), but supplies no complete operational relation between that cycle, selection of a partner and the statistical succession of exchanges. No quantitative law for index, its frequency dependence, dispersion or material residence times follows merely from the effective-rms analogy. The wire/open-end difference remains the author's stated open question.

**Other retained source defects.** B2b confirms that *Shedding Light on Light* contains a missing factor in its time formula and the dimensional error of dividing mass by c² to obtain energy. B2c retains the 0.707c footnote in *You Don’t Have to Be Einstein* against its Doppler-factor-two example; the usual longitudinal Doppler relation gives 0.6c for that factor. This last comparison is imported mathematical checking, not an author correction or physical result. None is silently emended here.

**Proposals and historical reports.** Teleblocking and source-glitch proposals in *Lines from Einstein - An Intellectual Odyssey by Viv Pope*, P0558–P0573, remain proposed tests and reported limited trials, not demonstrated control. The Bondi quotation and claim of agreement in the c papers are mediated historical evidence. Römer's result is interpreted by the authors as a ratio of units (light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0261); this dossier has not replayed historical records. Lewis and Wheeler–Feynman are source-reported precedents/comparisons, not imported proof of the entire account.

## Test for new work

**Editorial synthesis.**

- **C05-T0001:** Name both ends of each elementary exchange. Do not add a carrier or an intervening state without declaring a different premise.
- **C05-T0002:** Distinguish transition-time, cycle period, body proper time, observer-time and pulse interval. Show the clock and geometry assumptions of every conversion.
- **C05-T0003:** Use c as the ratio of units and the bound in the stated relative-motion relation, not as a speed of light.
- **C05-T0004:** Identify the wider record used for s. Do not infer a complete distance geometry from one exchange.
- **C05-T0005:** Separate resonance/readiness conditions from a partner-selection law. Label an added probability, timing or control rule as an added premise.
- **C05-T0006:** In a material, name separate exchanges, storage and retained apparatus states. Derive or declare their laws; do not identify an arbitrary clocked relay chain with a derivation of direct interaction.
- **C05-T0007:** Keep reported observations, proposed tests, mathematical checks and complete measurement receipts distinct. Instantaneity alone establishes no operational communication performance.
- **C05-T0008:** Preserve source formulas and conflicts. Authenticate consequential damaged equations before using them; do not repair them by inserting a familiar result.
