# C01 — Normal Realism: constitution and reversal

## Holdings

**C01-H0001 — Normal Realism is objective commonsense empiricism.** The observed world is not a mental copy of a second physical world. This is the authors' philosophical starting point, not a measurement result.

> "this global norm of objective commonsense empiricism has been called Normal Realism." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0302
> "in Normal Realism it is never even suggested that the world we observe in everyday life may not be the same world as that occupied by objects themselves and their various parts." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0302

**C01-H0002 — Phenomena are physical reality, not diminished substitutes for it.** Sensory and instrumental categories both belong to that reality. Removing the division between matter and perception does not leave a world made of one person's mind.

> "They are the one and only physical reality which is manifest in all the observational (i.e., sensory and instrumental) categories articulated by the barest, most economic use of communal language." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0223
> "Normal Realism is the paradigm of Natural Philosophy which is the logical result of removing from contemporary physics all vestiges of both the Cartesian and the Berkeleyan fallacies." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0221
> "They virtually cancelled the ‘matter’ side of the duality to leave only ‘mind’, instead of cancelling the dichotomy altogether." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0303

**C01-H0003 — The real includes what nobody presently knows.** Unknown things in this world remain legitimate subjects of empirical conjecture. The rejection concerns a reality made inaccessible in principle by its definition, not every unobserved thing or every scientific theory.

Attribution: Pope, chapter 5.

> "This huge remnant is all part of what is normally called reality." — immediate-distant-action:P0776
> "These things which are not directly known may nevertheless be reasonable subjects of commonsense conjecture, or theory. Indeed, without such theories no true science could ever survive, let alone exist." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0691
Attribution: Pope's autobiographical account.

> "Nor does it offend commonsense in the least to say that they may have in themselves, as we speak, these objective features, even though neither we nor anyone else may ever know it." — the-odyssey:P0364

**C01-H0004 — “Normal” sets a standard for description.** In the later joint book, the standard is economical, adequate logical language. The autobiographical account also emphasizes everyday critical judgment; it must not be reduced to an exclusively prescriptive definition.

> "This was so as to be taken, not as synonymous with 'usual', or 'conventional' but as normative, or prescriptive, as opposed to purely descriptive." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0225
> "The norm or standard of scientific language thus prescribed is that of the best possible use of rational or logical language, the most conceptually efficient, most wholesome and least theoretical usage in describing ongoing experience." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0225
> "Mainly, however, it was used to signify the norms and standards of the everyday thinking person whose mind had not been funnelled by our Educational history." — the-odyssey:P0292

**C01-H0005 — The reversal makes information prior to the space projected from it.** The detailed account of information, observers and dimensions belongs to C03. The constitutional constraint here is that a projected gap cannot also be the prior machinery producing its information. This states one source position; the underpinning passages remain in C01, Differences and open rules.

> "All such gaps or voids are observational projections out of the information. They cannot also be preconditions for the information out of which they are projected." — eye-of-beholder:P0102
> "This reverses the traditional priority of conception which puts light in space by putting space – and time, and everything – in the light." — eye-of-beholder:P0103

**C01-H0006 — Nature is primarily kinematic; mechanics belongs to its representation.** The reversal is conceptual, not a newly supplied physical update law.

> "adopting instead an alternative view of nature as being primarily kinematical and mechanical only in our observational representations of it." — eye-of-beholder:P0119
> "to make kinematics a priori and mechanics a posteriori – a complete conceptual turnaround." — eye-of-beholder:P0119

**C01-H0007 — Normal Realism is the philosophy on which POAMS is based.** Its empirical starting point does not remove the need for physical postulates. C04 owns their declaration and limits; C06 owns angular momentum and conservation.

> "POAMS is an approach to problems of Modern Physics, based on the Normal Realist philosophy." — phenomenalism:P0009
Attribution: Pope.

> "Our programme of Normal Realism for physics is not that of hard-line Rationalism or Axiomatics. It is that of empiricism" — immediate-distant-action:P0785
Attribution: Osborne.

> "We believe that although one cannot build a scientific theory (or synthesis) on empirical facts alone, any postulates for such a theory must have their bases in empirical (observational) fact." — immediate-distant-action:P1630

## What the texts reject

The joint books reject both a physical world separated from its appearances and the deletion of the physical side in favor of mind alone (C01-H0001–C01-H0002). Osborne explicitly rejects confinement of reality to the instant of perception:

> "Thus, Normal Realism firmly rejects the extreme positivistic view that only what is directly perceived at an instant can be real." — immediate-distant-action:P1378

Pope rejects an explanatory substratum outside all possible appearances. His criticism is not a prohibition on ordinary inference about presently hidden things (C01-H0003). He also rejects a carrier as the prerequisite of direct observation; the physical light account is in C05, not an additional constitutional mechanism here.

> "there is a profound fallacy in supposing that we can ever have information about anything underlying or conveying that information which is not, in itself, information of the same kind." — eye-of-beholder:P0102

## Differences and open rules

**Projection and underpinning remain a substantive source tension.** The projection-first passages above cannot erase Osborne's explicit residual-substratum language:

Attribution: Osborne.

> "Also, since perception is not self-supporting, we may sensibly refer to a residual class, or substratum, of unperceived and perhaps unknown things." — immediate-distant-action:P1379

Pope's autobiography calls the earlier all-light account insufficient and seeks an underlying substantial ingredient:

> "It was not that his commonsense synthesis, following Moore, had been wrong; it was just that it had not been sufficient." — the-odyssey:P0350
> "To avoid making a new kind of 'idealism' of commonsense, the relative appearances obviously had to be of something other than just themselves." — the-odyssey:P0350

That same account limits substantiality to this world:

> "In a commonsense science, as he conceived it, substantiality had to be that of things in the world, not of things metaphysically behind and beyond it." — the-odyssey:P0374

By contrast, the earlier paper calls for dropping the substratum analogy altogether:

> "To make that revolution complete we have to drop altogether the notion of a 'substance' or quasi-material substratum underlying the phenomena, like a reel of film or tape, and express all physical laws as positivistic functions of the phenomena themselves." — a-question-of-paradigms:P0055

These are not interchangeable sentences. The same-world qualification narrows the conflict but does not establish that every use of “underlying” means the same thing. No new mechanism or retrospective reconciliation is supplied here. The accepted B2b ledger adds the self-sufficient-but-not-isolated wording of *Nature, Machine or Hologram (1)*, P0205–P0212, to this comparison; it does not license an unqualified denial of all object independence.

**Normal and naming.** C01-H0004 preserves differing emphases. The late-1960s naming in normal-realism-the-machian-legacy:P0018 differs from the 1970s inception in light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0225. C04 retains the historical question; neither date is silently corrected.

**Axioms and empiricism.** The 1978 synopsis calls the premises and deductions a system; Pope's later chapter rejects hard-line Axiomatics. C02 keeps that difference open. “Not a closed system” is an editorial reading, not permission to delete the earlier formulation.

## Test for new work

**Editorial synthesis; these are drafting tests, not additional author axioms.**

- **C01-T0001:** State the phenomenon before its proposed representation. Identify which source position the representation follows.
- **C01-T0002:** Distinguish presently unknown things from a second world defined as inaccessible. Do not treat empirical conjecture as automatically unconstitutional.
- **C01-T0003:** If a projected dimension or object becomes a prerequisite for its own information, disclose that reversal and confront C01-H0005. Do not hide it in a change of terminology.
- **C01-T0004:** Keep philosophical starting points, physical postulates and demonstrated consequences separate. Compatibility with a starting point is not derivation from it.
- **C01-T0005:** Preserve contrary author passages. Reject a stated incompatibility with reasons; do not claim that absence from this dossier alone refutes a premise.
