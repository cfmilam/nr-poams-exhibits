# NR-POAMS Project-Start Protocol

Draft 3 for Star Lord's approval — 14 November 2026

Before a construction is chosen, set out its Normal Realist premises and show how the physics follows from them. No new mathematical construction starts until the approach has passed review and Star Lord has said go.

This applies the discipline Pope describes in section 25 of A Synopsis of Normal Realism (Revised). A Normal Realist submits "to a discipline, which involves familiarising oneself with the full range of basic presuppositions and with their method of logical evolution." The system is to be preserved "from dilution in terms of foreign (incompatible) precepts and prejudices. Where these may be discovered in the course of argument they are signified by being declared unconstitutional."

## Before construction

1. **The question.** Star Lord's question, in his words. It heads every brief and every report.

2. **Source reading.** Search the books, the papers and the record of earlier failures for the subject. Record the collections, versions, search terms and relevant works found. Read those works in full, in stages, and follow relevant references. A needed source that cannot be read blocks the approach decision. Write down what Pope and Osborne hold, what they left open, what tests they proposed and how those came out, with quotations and locations. Where two texts disagree, or a text looks wrong, say so. Do not blend them. Say what the search did not find.

3. **Grounding statement.** Start from Normal Realism: words get meaning from experience; physical and psychological descriptions do not name separate worlds; dimensions are conventions, not a hidden stage. Cite the passages and show how they constrain this project. Name who observes, what they record and what they count. Then the physics: the two atoms in each exchange, the angular momentum conserved, the counts, and c as the conversion between units of distance and time. Mark what follows from the premises and what has to be added. Name the result sought, the record that would test it, and what it would be good for.

4. **Approach test.** Compare two approaches, including the corpus's treatment when it has one. Say why one is chosen. For that approach:
   - Trace the proposed operation from preparation to final record: each exchange, its two ends, what each keeps and what every clock step counts.
   - Reject any unseen traveler, carrier, mediating substance, background stage or unexplained transit clock. Account for the apparatus and any intervening atoms as well. Renaming does not pass.
   - List borrowed mathematics apart from physical premises. Name each tool and its use. Reject a physical premise that conflicts with the grounding, wherever it came from.
   - An added premise is named as an addition, with the test that could defeat it.

5. **Independent review.** A reviewer from a different vendor and model family gets the question, the Grounding Injection, the reading record, the source passages and both approaches. The review identifies the exact input versions and checks the proposed operation and every clock step, not just its summary. An unexplained essential operation or a contradiction means reject. Otherwise the reviewer gives a reasoned pass. A reject blocks the start until the reviewer clears the correction.

6. **Go from Star Lord.** Only a passed approach reaches him. He sees the question, the chosen approach in plain words, the approach rejected, the review, the result sought and the budget. His go covers that paper work and nothing else.

## During the work

- **Budget.** Limits for the whole project in work packages, days and dollars, set before the start and counted across sessions and successor versions. Reading and review count. Stop at the first limit reached. Going on needs his approval of a named next result and a new limit.
- **Stop signals.** Two reports in a row that cannot show the central object is the physical thing he asked about, or that still lean on a conventional model in place of the derivation: stop for a direction review. A new name or a new version does not reset the count. A contradiction with the grounding stops the affected work at once. After a stop or a change to an essential premise, restart requires a passed approach review and Star Lord's go.
- **Corrections.** When a premise is corrected, withdraw every claim that depended on it. Do not put the correction on top of unchanged work.
- **Reports.** First line: does the work still answer his question from the grounding, and why. Then the result and the next decision. A count of checks is not a result.
- **Briefs.** Every brief to an AI worker, and every resumed session, carries his Grounding Injection word for word, the question, the grounding statement, the chosen approach and its test, the review decision, the active stop signals and the remaining budget. Check that the brief is not truncated and that the worker can search and open the full sources. Maintain one linked topic index of source passages, rejected approaches and prior failures.
- **Review.** A different-vendor reviewer for the approach, for any change to a premise, and for the final claim. Arithmetic and software checks are local and are not called independent review.
- **Writing.** American English outside quotations. Plain sentences. State assumptions, results and missing work once, directly. c is the measured conversion between distance and time, never a speed. Action is never a traveler or a carrier across empty space. Particle names only for comparison.

## Before anything is published

- The paper argument is complete and independently reviewed, as GROUNDING.md §0.2 and the Foundation Wall require. The page states what was derived, what was borrowed, what was measured and what it is good for. Publish only claims supported by that argument and evidence.
- A different-vendor reader checks the page itself against the reviewed result, the sources and the wording rules. Preserve that version; substantive edits require another check.
- Star Lord reads the page and approves it. A summary is not enough.

## When an effort is stopped

Keep the original files and reviews in the private failure archive, with dates, hashes and the reason. Link them from the topic index. Take the claims out of public view. The next effort on that subject reads that record first.
