Quantum Hall Effects

Integer counting, fractional collective order, exact resistance and the boundary/interior bookkeeping problem.

The plateaus are robust enough to realize the ohm. Any POAMS account must explain not only the count, but why it persists—and exactly how it breaks.

Horizon status and promotion gate

Integer and fractional Hall physics are established but physically distinct regimes. Their mathematical machinery is imported here. POAMS presently contributes constraints, candidate bookkeeping analogies and discriminator design.

Promotion requires: a native state count, conductance coefficient, interaction-dependent fractional construction, bulk/edge identity, breakdown law and at least one prospective prediction beyond inserting a measured filling factor.

2 · Instrument record

Integer effect

Von Klitzing, Dorda and Pepper observed sharply quantized Hall resistance in 1980. A plateau is a finite field or density interval with stable transverse resistance and longitudinal dissipation below a declared limit—not one attractive data point.

Fractional effect

Tsui, Störmer and Gossard observed the \(1/3\) plateau in 1982. Laughlin supplied a correlated many-body construction; later shot-noise work found an effective charge consistent with \(e/3\) in the low-energy excitation sector.

Raw records include source current, longitudinal and transverse voltages, field, density/gate setting, temperature, contact configuration, noise and sweep history.

Sources: von Klitzing et al. (1980); Tsui et al. (1982); Laughlin (1983); TKNN (1982); Saminadayar et al. (1997); BIPM metrology.

3 · Integer regime: imported machinery

\[n_\Phi=\frac{eB}{h},\qquad \nu=\frac{n_sh}{eB},\qquad \ell_B=\sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{eB}}.\]

Spin, valley, layer and subband degeneracies alter the observed sequence. In the conventional tensor, vanishing \(\sigma_{xx}\) gives \(|R_{xy}|=R_K/|\nu|\), where \(R_K=h/e^2\).

\[\sigma_{xy}=C\frac{e^2}{h},\qquad C=\frac1{2\pi}\sum_{n\in occ}\int_{BZ}\Omega_n(\mathbf k)d^2k\in\mathbb Z.\]

The Chern account explains stability under smooth change while the relevant gap/localization structure persists. Edge channels give the same terminal response in a bounded device. Bulk and edge are complementary books for one current, not two currents to add.

4 · Fractional regime: correlation is constitutive

A partially occupied Landau level is macroscopically degenerate before interactions. Coulomb correlations can reorganize it into an incompressible collective state. For odd \(m\), the imported Laughlin trial state is

\[\Psi_m=\prod_{i<j}(z_i-z_j)^m\exp\!\left(-\sum_i\frac{|z_i|^2}{4\ell_B^2}\right).\]

The construction supports fractional low-energy excitations. It does not mean a vacuum electron was cut into persistent thirds. Plateau conductance \(\sigma_{xy}=\nu e^2/h\) and quasiparticle charge are distinct observables; the latter needs a controlled noise measurement and regime-specific model.

Guardrail. Replacing an integer \(\nu\) by a fraction in an integer-state story is not a fractional Hall theory. The many-body gap, excitation algebra, edge structure and terminal measurement map must be built.

5 · Quantitative receipts

  • \(R_K=25\,812.8074593045\,\Omega\) exactly in the revised SI.
  • \(R_{xy}(\nu=2)=12\,906.4037296523\,\Omega\).
  • \(R_{xy}(\nu=1/3)=77\,438.4223779135\,\Omega\).
  • For \(n_s=3.00\times10^{15}\,\mathrm{m^{-2}}\), \(B=6.204\,\mathrm T\) at \(\nu=2\), and 37.22 T at \(\nu=1/3\).
  • At 10 T, \(\ell_B=8.11\,\mathrm{nm}\) and \(n_\Phi=2.418\times10^{15}\,\mathrm{m^{-2}}\).

Exact \(h\) and \(e\) make the SI numerical value exact; universality, low dissipation and validated device operation remain empirical achievements.

6 · POAMS constraints

  1. Counting must name what is counted. The \(h/e\) orbital count differs from superconducting \(h/2e\) closure.
  2. Robustness needs a failure law. Quantization fails near transitions, excessive current, temperature, bad contacts or breakdown.
  3. Boundary and interior must agree. Do not double-count bulk organization and edge transport.
  4. Fractional response is collective. “Incomplete closure” is not a theory of \(e/3\).
  5. Circulation words are not identity proofs. Cyclotron orbit, Berry curvature, Chern number and chirality are not automatically POAMS twist, writhe or natural orbit.

No support-force anomaly, Receiver coupling, teleblocking or superluminal conclusion follows from Hall transport.

7 · Prospective tests and engineering

Multi-terminal closure audit

Reroute or equilibrate an edge channel while holding bulk filling fixed. Record every terminal current and voltage, leakage, gate waveform, field, temperature, noise and timing. Freeze any proposed residual beyond Landauer–Büttiker transport before data.

Matched integer/fractional device

Measure Hall/longitudinal response, activation, noise, thermal transport, equilibration and breakdown in the same device. Predeclare what one claimed closure operation keeps invariant and what collective order changes.

Engineering value includes resistance metrology, the quantum electrical triangle, graphene standards, low-noise impedance work, edge-channel electronics, quasiparticle interferometry and a hard calibration platform for POAMS electrical ledgers.

8 · Useful nulls, opens and promotion criteria

  • No extra multi-terminal residual bounds a proposed POAMS correction while validating ordinary transport.
  • No fractional-charge anomaly leaves POAMS with a constraint, not a new effect.
  • Breakdown fully correlated with heating/contact error closes a false ontology lead and improves device specification.
  • No common invariant beyond imported Hall relations rejects an over-broad integer/fractional analogy.

Open work: plateau-width prediction from declared inputs, a native conductance coefficient, interaction-dependent \(1/3\) construction, fractional charge prediction independent of noise fitting, and quantitative breakdown scales. Promotion requires prospective success across that ledger.
