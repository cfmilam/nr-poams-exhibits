2. The measured state

More than zero resistance

The Meissner–Ochsenfeld receipt is magnetic: a specimen expels flux on entering the state. A perfect ordinary conductor could freeze pre-existing flux; Meissner screening identifies a distinct thermodynamic organization.

London screening is imported phenomenology:

$$\nabla^2\mathbf B={\mathbf B\over\lambda_L^2}.$$

Three theoretical layers

  • London: current and field screening; phenomenological.
  • Ginzburg–Landau: complex order parameter, coherence length $\xi$, penetration depth $\lambda$, vortex energetics.
  • BCS: conventional microscopic pairing and coherent state; not universal for every superconductor.

The GL parameter $\kappa=\lambda/\xi$ separates ideal type-I and type-II behavior near the applicable regime. In the mixed state, each vortex carries one phase winding and approximately one flux quantum.

3. Vortex density, motion, and dissipation

For mean induction $B$, an ideal single-quantum vortex density is

$$n_v={B\over\Phi_0},\qquad a_\triangle=\sqrt{{2\Phi_0\over\sqrt3 B}}.$$

At $B=1$ T, the triangular-lattice spacing is about 49 nm. This is arithmetic from imported relations, not a new measurement.

Pinning

Defects can lower vortex energy and immobilize vortices. Strong pinning can support large practical currents, but “critical current” depends on electric-field criterion, ramp, temperature, magnetic history, strain and geometry.

Flux flow and creep

When vortices move across a current path, phase slips and electric fields appear. Thermal or quantum creep can produce slow relaxation below the nominal depinning threshold. A retained winding is therefore a lifetime distribution, not a magical forever-state.

4. POAMS constraint: where the analogy stops

Phase winding, closed paths, discrete flux and retained current are unusually close to POAMS vocabulary. Yet the extant equations use an imported order parameter, electromagnetic response, charge $2e$, material free energy and constitutive lengths. Relabeling $\psi$ as a POAMS circulation derives nothing.

  • Name orbit, twist and writhe separately; a flux line’s spatial path is not automatically intrinsic twist.
  • No gravity, weight, or nonlocal-transfer inference follows from flux quantization.
  • A native account must derive the closure variable, material response, flux unit and failure modes from one-substance bookkeeping.
