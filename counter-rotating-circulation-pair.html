§1 — Grounding: What POAMS Already Holds

§2 — The Coherent-AM Column in the Ledger

The invariant available to the ledger

Expanding the invariant $Lk_1 \cdot Lk_2 = (Tw_1 + Wr_1)(Tw_2 + Wr_2)$ gives four bilinear channels:

§3 — The Configuration: Counter-Rotating Self-Compressing Pair

Why this geometry is special

Four properties combine only in this configuration:

  1. Maximal compressed coherent-AM density. Two opposed circulations arranged coaxially collide at their shared plane. In fluid media the collision is between induced axial flows through each ring’s central hole; in field-topology media the collision is between opposed magnetic dipoles. In both cases the central region compresses: mass migrates radially outward while circulation concentrates axially. The coherent-AM density in the compressed volume can exceed what either ring alone can sustain.
  2. Non-trivial Călugăreanu linking. Two centerlines stacked with opposite poloidal senses produce a specific total $Lk$ that is not accessible with a single ring, a co-oriented pair, or a separated pair. The topology is engineered: driving the compression drives $Lk$ across integer boundaries.
  3. Combined twist and writhe. Each ring's poloidal rotation contributes $Tw$; the coaxial stacking with opposite sense contributes mutual $Wr$. The pair coupling in §2 reads both simultaneously, unlike geometries that carry only one component.
  4. Driven, not merely observed. The circulation is continuously energized against relaxation. In POAMS terms the ledger reads a structure whose alignment is externally maintained. The coherent-AM density can be held above what the medium's own trapped-rate would sustain in isolation.

§4 — The Non-Perturbative Regime — Open Question

The pair coupling derived in §2 is perturbative. Applied to any laboratory-realistic configuration, its magnitude at meter scales is small compared with the mass-mass pair term — consistent with the fact that Kerr / Lense-Thirring frame-dragging is a subtle effect at everyday distances.

The fluid-dynamics analogy (instructive scaffolding)

Counter-rotating vortex rings in a fluid undergo reconnection under compression: two vortex lines that come sufficiently close can locally exchange partners and rejoin, changing the total mutual linking number by an integer. This is a well-studied phenomenon in classical hydrodynamics and plasma physics.

Applied by analogy to a driven counter-rotating pair held stacked-not-merged: full merger is forbidden by the drive (would collapse $Lk_1 + Lk_2 \to 0$), but edge reconnection at the shear plane can change the mutual linking by $\pm 1$ without merging the rings. Each such reconnection is a discrete topology change: the system's Călugăreanu $Lk$ jumps by an integer at a specific drive threshold.

The ledger-native description

Beyond the analogy, POAMS gives the mechanism directly. The ledger reads only integer $Lk$: a fractional turn is not a smaller turn but no turn (per GROUNDING §1, discreteum). Between integer values, no valid closed-loop configuration exists. When a drive parameter carries the system across an integer boundary, the ledger must reorganize discretely — one whole additional $\hbar$-turn is either completed or un-completed in a single event.

By $E = L\omega$ (from Mass as Trapped Rate), the energy signature of one such event is:

$$\Delta E_{Lk\text{-jump}} \;=\; \hbar \,\omega_{drive}$$

where $\omega_{drive}$ is the characteristic turn-rate of the driven configuration at the transition. This is the POAMS-native prediction: a single Călugăreanu integer-crossing event releases (or absorbs) one $\hbar\omega_{drive}$ quantum, discretely, at a well-defined drive threshold. Not a smooth response, not a perturbative shift — a discrete jump in the ledger's book.

Numerical scale, and where the observability bar sits

For a laboratory drive at $\omega_{drive} \sim$ GHz ($10^{10}$ Hz), a single event releases $\hbar\omega_{drive} \sim 10^{-24}$ J. Torsion balances are sensitive at approximately $10^{-12}$ J-scale accumulated over an integration window. A single event is undetectable by torsion balance; cumulative signatures require of order $10^{12}$ events per measurement window.

Two paths to observability:

  • High event rate. If each drive-parameter sweep across a threshold triggers many events (e.g. because the compression is dynamic and re-crosses the threshold at $\omega_{drive}$ rate), cumulative signature over a run duration could be observable.
  • Multi-quantum reorganization. If a compressed coherent ensemble undergoes a cascade at threshold (one initial event triggering many sympathetic reorganizations across the ensemble), the effective jump per drive sweep is $N \cdot \hbar\omega_{drive}$ rather than a single quantum.

Coherent-domain amplification — the $N_{local}$ sharpening

For a truly phase-coherent ensemble the reorganization is not per-closure but per-coherent-domain. When the drive crosses an integer-$Lk$ threshold, the whole coherent domain reorganizes simultaneously, and the single-event energy scales with the domain's closure count $N_{local}$:

$$\Delta E_{event} \;=\; N_{local} \cdot \hbar\,\omega_{drive}$$

For laboratory-realistic apparatus the range of $N_{local}$ spans many orders:

  • Fluxon-scale local reorganization in a bulk superconductor: $N_{local} \sim 10^6$ → $\Delta E \sim 10^{-18}$ J per event. Below torsion-balance sensitivity ($10^{-12}$ J) but within reach of sensitive calorimetry.
  • Persistent-current-ring collective mode: $N_{local} \sim 10^{14}$ → $\Delta E \sim 10^{-10}$ J per event. Observable by torsion balance and by direct electrical detection.
  • Full-condensate reorganization in a coupled superconducting-coil pair: $N_{local} \sim 10^{20}$ → $\Delta E \sim 10^{-4}$ J per event. Macroscopically observable.

Sharpened experimental signature. As the drive parameter is swept continuously, the observable is not a smooth response but a stair-step: energy released in discrete quantized jumps at specific drive values corresponding to Călugăreanu integer-$Lk$ boundaries. Between crossings, response is smooth and Kerr-perturbative. At each crossing, a discrete $N_{local}\hbar\omega_{drive}$ event fires. The stair-step signature is distinctive from any perturbative-Kerr expectation; if observed, it confirms the non-perturbative mechanism and its $N_{local}$ pins the coherent-domain size at the transition.

OPEN The single-event mechanism is derived and predicts jumps at Călugăreanu integer crossings with magnitude $\hbar\omega_{drive}$ per event. Whether laboratory-realistic apparatus accumulates enough events for torsion-balance detection, or whether cascade dynamics amplify the effect at threshold, remains open. Both are POAMS-native questions with predictable answers; neither is answered here.

A modern experimental test

Two counter-wound superconducting coils, coaxially mounted stacked-not-merged, with controllable mutual inductance driving the pair's effective $Lk$ across integer values under a GHz-scale AC drive. Two complementary instrumentation channels:

  • Microwave-frequency calorimetry at the shear plane, looking for $\hbar\omega_{drive}$-scale energy exchange coincident with drive-parameter passage through integer-$Lk$ thresholds. Tests the single-event mechanism directly.
  • Torsion-balance instrumentation above the assembly, integrating over long runs, looking for cumulative or cascade-amplified signatures. Tests the multi-event / cascade hypothesis.

A positive signal at either channel with the predicted threshold structure confirms the non-perturbative mechanism. A null at both, given adequate instrumentation floors, bounds the amplification factor and forces POAMS to reduce to perturbative $\alpha = -2$ gravitomagnetism at all accessible scales.

§5 — The Self-Coupling Case: Intrinsic-Plus-Orbital Mixed Pair

The pair coupling derived in §2 was for two bodies each carrying coherent angular momentum, both at some separation $r$. The same Călugăreanu + two-endedness machinery applies to a distinct configuration of physical importance: a single body carrying an intrinsic (pumped) coherent-AM $\vec{L}_{coh}$ while also possessing an orbital-AM $\vec{L}_{orb}^{nat}$ of its own natural orbit around a nearby mass sink. This is the self-coupling case — the body's intrinsic circulation reading its own orbital circulation through the shared ledger.

The derivation carries through

Both $\vec{L}_{coh}$ (intrinsic, twist-account per GROUNDING §2) and $\vec{L}_{orb}^{nat}$ (orbital, writhe-account) are Călugăreanu quantities of the same body. The pair-invariant argument of §2 — only $Lk_1 \cdot Lk_2$ survives the Călugăreanu constraint — applies unchanged. Both endpoints of the “exchange” are on the same body (twist and writhe of one closure structure), so two-endedness (per Receiver Keys §7) applies exactly as in the two-body case — the same factor of 2, from the same self-referential-geometry argument that gives $g_S = 2$ in Landé §4.

The self-coupling potential:

$$V_{mix} \;=\; -\frac{2G}{c^2} \cdot \frac{\vec{L}_{coh} \cdot \vec{L}_{orb}^{nat}}{r^3}$$

evaluated at the body's location $r$ from the mass sink (e.g., Earth's centre). Same coefficient as the two-body case; same sign convention; same Bertrand-preservation at long range. What differs: the two vectors $\vec{L}_{coh}$ and $\vec{L}_{orb}^{nat}$ live on the same body, and their relative orientation is an engineering degree of freedom of the device rather than a fact about two distant sources.

Scaling and observability

§5B — Framework proposal: discrete center-of-mass reorganization events

The continuous scaling above puts $\Delta g/g$ ~30 orders below torsion-balance sensitivity for laboratory-achievable pumped-AM. This subsection sketches a POAMS-native alternative observability path via discrete center-of-mass reorganization events — analogous to §4's discrete Călugăreanu integer-crossing regime, applied at the whole-body scale rather than to internal-spin dynamics. This is a research framework, not a CLAIMED result; the three derivation gaps at the end name exactly what has to close before the framework can be tested empirically.

Framework. As pumped $L_{coh}$ is driven at frequency $\omega_{\text{drive}}$, the body's whole coherent-AM state passes through discrete topological thresholds — fluxon reorganizations in a superconductor, vortex-line reconnections in a superfluid, or the analogous discrete topological transition in the pumping medium. At each threshold crossing, an $N_{local}$-amplified discrete event occurs (per §4's mechanism, now transferred from internal-spin to whole-body scope), releasing energy

$$E_{\text{event}} \;\sim\; N_{local} \cdot \hbar\omega_{\text{drive}}.$$

The observable is a time-varying force at the event rate, not a steady weight offset. Under directional recoil (event energy released as coherent momentum in a preferred direction rather than isotropic heat), the time-averaged force is

$$F_{\text{recoil}} \;\sim\; R_{\text{event}} \cdot f \cdot \frac{E_{\text{event}}}{c}, \qquad R_{\text{event}} \;=\; \omega_{\text{drive}} \cdot n_{\text{threshold}},$$

where $R_{\text{event}}$ is the event rate (pumping frequency times per-cycle threshold-crossing density $n_{\text{threshold}}$) and $f \in [0,1]$ is the fraction of event energy released as directional momentum.
