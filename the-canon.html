Recital

The approved reason this work comes first.

Owner-approved 15 November 2026Read Markdown

I strongly support the approach. It is the missing front-end control that would have stopped an OIL-style fishing trip before the mathematics acquired its own passport and left the country.

The decisive feature is that every new exhibit must begin with:

  1. the observed phenomenon and observer;
  2. the authenticated source position;
  3. the Normal Realist constraints;
  4. the physical and mathematical premises being added;
  5. competing approaches and why one is chosen;
  6. the exact record that could defeat it;
  7. independent review before construction.

That changes the workflow from invent → calculate → retrofit meaning to ground → choose → derive → test.

There are three safeguards we must preserve:

  • The canon cannot judge the sources during reconciliation. Contrary passages must correct the canon, not be filtered out by it.
  • The authors' holdings must remain separate from later program premises. Pope/Osborne source claims, our editorial synthesis, your ratified constitutive extensions, imported mathematics, and measurements each need their own status.
  • Do not inject all 23 full pages into every session. The final system should generate a compact core plus the relevant subject pages, source passages, known conflicts, and project-start questions. Otherwise the cure becomes a context-window landslide.

"Definitive" should mean approved and controlled for a declared authenticated corpus and scope—not unrevisable. New source evidence or an explicit amendment can produce a new version.

The correct final deliverable is therefore a matched set:

  1. the full, quotation-backed Source-Grounded Canon;
  2. a compact generated Canon Core;
  3. a subject-specific Opening Context for each new exhibit;
  4. the approved Project-Start Protocol;
  5. hashes, source receipts, unresolved questions, and change control.

C01 — Normal Realism: constitution and reversal

Holdings
  • C01-H0001 — Normal Realism is objective commonsense empiricism. The observed world is not a mental copy of a second physical world. This is the authors' philosophical starting point, not a measurement result.
  • C01-H0002 — Phenomena are physical reality, not diminished substitutes for it. Sensory and instrumental categories both belong to that reality. Removing the division between matter and perception does not leave a world made of one person's mind.
  • C01-H0003 — The real includes what nobody presently knows. Unknown things in this world remain legitimate subjects of empirical conjecture. The rejection concerns a reality made inaccessible in principle by its definition, not every unobserved thing or every scientific theory.
  • C01-H0004 — “Normal” sets a standard for description. In the later joint book, the standard is economical, adequate logical language. The autobiographical account also emphasizes everyday critical judgment; it must not be reduced to an exclusively prescriptive definition.
  • C01-H0005 — The reversal makes information prior to the space projected from it. The detailed account of information, observers and dimensions belongs to C03. The constitutional constraint here is that a projected gap cannot also be the prior machinery producing its information. This states one source position; the underpinning passages remain in C01, Differences and open rules.
  • C01-H0006 — Nature is primarily kinematic; mechanics belongs to its representation. The reversal is conceptual, not a newly supplied physical update law.
  • C01-H0007 — Normal Realism is the philosophy on which POAMS is based. Its empirical starting point does not remove the need for physical postulates. C04 owns their declaration and limits; C06 owns angular momentum and conservation.
Test for new work
  • C01-T0001: State the phenomenon before its proposed representation. Identify which source position the representation follows.
  • C01-T0002: Distinguish presently unknown things from a second world defined as inaccessible. Do not treat empirical conjecture as automatically unconstitutional.
  • C01-T0003: If a projected dimension or object becomes a prerequisite for its own information, disclose that reversal and confront C01-H0005. Do not hide it in a change of terminology.
  • C01-T0004: Keep philosophical starting points, physical postulates and demonstrated consequences separate. Compatibility with a starting point is not derivation from it.
  • C01-T0005: Preserve contrary author passages. Reject a stated incompatibility with reasons; do not claim that absence from this dossier alone refutes a premise.

C02 — Language, logos, logic and mathematics

Holdings
  • C02-H0001 — Physical terms obtain their meaning through experience. Naming, classifying and measuring are activities within language, not guarantees of a separate bearer answering to each noun.
  • C02-H0002 — Nature does not arrive already classified. Classifications remain answerable to expanding experience. The same word can belong to different categories of existence.
  • C02-H0003 — A name and its measures do not establish a physical object. The source's explicit conventional comparison illustrates the distinction, without making its named entity a Canon primitive.
  • C02-H0004 — Meaning can be direct or progressively abstract. Physical description, history, psychology and fiction are not thereby the same category. A higher-order delineation must retain its connection to what it delineates.
  • C02-H0005 — Language is developmental and cannot certify its own adequacy. Neither communal agreement nor the current vocabulary completes knowledge.
  • C02-H0006 — Logic examines consistency; it is not an automatic oracle of physical truth. This does not exhaust the authors' claims about logic: their positive-certainty passages remain in C02, Differences and open rules.
  • C02-H0007 — Logos is not merely a stock of arbitrary verbal symbols. Pope uses it for interpreted observational information and extends communicational language beyond humans. He also uses stronger natural and super-lingual formulations; they must retain their different scopes.
  • C02-H0008 — Mathematics represents measures; its continuous variables do not establish a continuous physical reality. The book expressly permits continuous mathematical modeling. A blanket prohibition on calculus would misstate this position and Osborne's mathematical practice.
Test for new work
  • C02-T0001: Classify each consequential term as record, inference, definition, mathematical representation or proposal. Show how it connects to experience.
  • C02-T0002: Do not infer a physical object from a name, a fitted measure or a mathematical solution alone.
  • C02-T0003: State the premises and domain of a deduction. Logical validity does not by itself establish its physical premises.
  • C02-T0004: Identify which use of logos a claim requires. Preserve the within-language/beyond-human-language difference rather than selecting an unqualified slogan.
  • C02-T0005: Separate continuous calculation from a claim of continuous nature. Identify imported tools without attributing them retrospectively to the authors.
  • C02-T0006: Treat a revision of language as revisable too. Neither a checklist nor this dossier is an oracle of truth.

C03 — Observer, objectivity, information and projection

Holdings
  • C03-H0001 — Observation is not confined to a conscious human spectator. Several texts call any interagent an observer. This is a broad physical usage, not a claim that every interagent speaks human language or has human consciousness.
  • C03-H0002 — Interaction is not dependent on a person's presence. The world exceeds what any human community currently observes. C01 distinguishes this unknown remainder from an inaccessible second world.
  • C03-H0003 — Objectivity is communal and relational. It is not the possession of one privileged viewpoint. Individual perspectives and the shared spatial description sustain one another.
  • C03-H0004 — Dimensions are informational projections from sensory and instrumental patterns. This is an analysis of phenomena, not the manufacture of experience from dimensionless numbers.
  • C03-H0005 — The observed whole precedes its analytical parts. Terminal elements acquire their characteristics within the observational whole. The source does not warrant constructing that whole from independently specified miniature objects behind observation.
  • C03-H0006 — A projected observational distance depends on a pattern of exchanges, not an isolated quantum alone. The information associated with a single exchange must not be confused with an entire spatial reconstruction. C05 owns the exchange's two ends and time relation.
  • C03-H0007 — Ordinary temporal experience remains a starting datum. Analytical priority of exchanges does not make the experienced dimensions arbitrary inventions with no empirical content.
Test for new work
  • C03-T0001: Name the observer or interagent and state which meaning of observer applies. Do not infer consciousness from participation alone.
  • C03-T0002: Separate the record, its interpretation and its comparison with other records. A common description is not a view from nowhere.
  • C03-T0003: Begin analysis with the observed whole. Identify the context in which each proposed part acquires its properties.
  • C03-T0004: For every reconstructed distance or time, identify the wider record, calibration and assumptions. One exchange cannot silently supply an entire geometry.
  • C03-T0005: Preserve ordinary temporal experience while distinguishing analytical priority from chronological causation. Do not insert an unobserved universal clock.
  • C03-T0006: Keep differences of observer vocabulary, information scale and polar/public dependence visible where they affect a claim.

C04 — Method, discipline, critique and lineage

Holdings
  • C04-H0001 — Inquiry begins with phenomena. The philosophical starting point precedes the physical model. C03 owns the associated whole-before-parts analysis.
  • C04-H0002 — Observation requires interpretation. The existence of an occurrence and the adequacy of its description are different questions.
  • C04-H0003 — Conflicting conventions are to be revised for logical and practical adequacy. Logical tidiness alone does not make a vocabulary sufficient for experience.
  • C04-H0004 — Conceptual economy matters alongside predictive success. The authors apply Ockham's principle to the number of explanatory ideas, not merely to the compactness of a formula.
  • C04-H0005 — Philosophical rejection does not require abandonment of useful theories. Working hypotheses and retained mathematical elements have a legitimate practical role. Their success does not establish their ontology.
  • C04-H0006 — The discipline concerns explicit premises and incompatible precepts, not dogmatic closure. A contradiction must be identified, rather than inferred from a proposal's unfamiliarity.
  • C04-H0007 — Physical laws require more than a philosophical preference or a conservation statement. Osborne declares additional assumptions in the orbital account. The detailed physical construction belongs to C07.
  • C04-H0008 — The stated lineage is selective, not wholesale adoption. The books place the philosophy in an empiricist and linguistic tradition, extend ordinary commonsense beyond its customary domain, and distinguish their purpose from Bondi's pedagogical use of the conversion factor.
Test for new work
  • C04-T0001: State the phenomenon and separate the record from its interpretation. Identify whether an empirical assertion is a report or a reproducible measurement.
  • C04-T0002: List physical and mathematical premises. State what follows conditionally and what remains assumed.
  • C04-T0003: Permit useful imported calculations as declared tools or comparisons. Do not promote their ontology by successful calculation alone.
  • C04-T0004: Test economy and sufficiency together. Removing a necessary assumption is not conceptual economy.
  • C04-T0005: Identify the exact incompatible precept before invoking constitutional rejection. Preserve the rejected route and the source disagreement without rewriting history.
  • C04-T0006: Preserve author, contributor, dialogue speaker, genre, work version and date. Do not turn reported agreement or an edited debate into consensus.
  • C04-T0007: Apply the same evidential distinction to favored and adverse results. A source statement is not a measurement receipt.

C05 — Light, c, time, relativity and material media

Holdings
  • C05-H0001 — Light is information, not an unseen carrier of information. The philosophical reversal is established in C01 and the observer/whole account in C03. Its application here denies that light travels in a preexisting void.
  • C05-H0002 — An elementary exchange is one event with two ends. The authors describe loss at one atom and gain at another as a reciprocal relation, not two independent events joined by an intervening carrier. This is their physical account, not an independently established measurement.
  • C05-H0003 — The exchange's own time is zero; its observer interval need not be. Intrinsic transition-time, a body's proper time and observer-time must remain distinct. The atomic cycle period is a further distinction, developed in C06.
  • C05-H0004 — c is the constant ratio of distance and time units for inertial observers using the same conventions. The authors retain the first relativity postulate and replace the interpretation of the second.
  • C05-H0005 — The uniform-motion time relation uses a right-triangle construction. The source identifies the proper time t and displacement measure s/c as perpendicular components and the hypotenuse as observer-time t_R.
  • C05-H0006 — Resonance and a receiving end are conditions of the exchange. They do not constitute a complete pair-selection or probability law.
  • C05-H0007 — Reflection involves two exchanges. In the source/mirror example, each contributes an observer interval s/c; the stated round-trip interval is 2s/c.
  • C05-H0008 — Doppler intervals must be distinguished from dilated clock intervals. For a receding source, the source displacement between records adds to the observer's pulse interval. This is not a change in the conversion constant.
  • C05-H0009 — A material sequence is not one elementary exchange. The books distinguish direct reciprocal events from mediated statistical processes; the fiber paper proposes time dilation of the sequence recorded by an observer. For apparent deflection near a star, the Light-Speed book proposes material refraction and a formal limiting Schwarzschild-geodesic comparison; Legacy emphasizes informationally projected lines of sight and orbital geometry. C05, Differences and open rules, preserves these formulations without identifying them or deriving an index law from the angular comparison.
  • C05-H0010 — The wire/fiber analogy has an explicitly unresolved limit. Pope compares them but does not identify electricity with light.
Test for new work
  • C05-T0001: Name both ends of each elementary exchange. Do not add a carrier or an intervening state without declaring a different premise.
  • C05-T0002: Distinguish transition-time, cycle period, body proper time, observer-time and pulse interval. Show the clock and geometry assumptions of every conversion.
  • C05-T0003: Use c as the ratio of units and the bound in the stated relative-motion relation, not as a speed of light.
  • C05-T0004: Identify the wider record used for s. Do not infer a complete distance geometry from one exchange.
  • C05-T0005: Separate resonance/readiness conditions from a partner-selection law. Label an added probability, timing or control rule as an added premise.
  • C05-T0006: In a material, name separate exchanges, storage and retained apparatus states. Derive or declare their laws; do not identify an arbitrary clocked relay chain with a derivation of direct interaction.
  • C05-T0007: Keep reported observations, proposed tests, mathematical checks and complete measurement receipts distinct. Instantaneity alone establishes no operational communication performance.
  • C05-T0008: Preserve source formulas and conflicts. Authenticate consequential damaged equations before using them; do not repair them by inserting a familiar result.

C06 — Angular momentum, action, discreteness and statistical causality

Holdings
  • C06-H0001 — POAMS identifies angular momentum, rather than mass alone, as its underlying physical substance. This is the authors' physical proposal within Normal Realism. It follows the philosophical starting point, not the reverse.
  • C06-H0002 — Action h and angular momentum h/2π are distinct quantities in the authors' account. The same physical interpretation does not make the units interchangeable.
  • C06-H0003 — The authors treat physical information and recordable measures as ultimately discrete. This is a source thesis about the phenomena, not a theorem supplied by modern information theory merely by using its vocabulary.
  • C06-H0004 — Elementary events are not a deterministic causal chain. The authors locate ordinary cause and effect in statistical populations of events.
  • C06-H0005 — Instantaneous relations are nontransitive and do not form a regularly clocked universal sequence. A completed relation between two ends does not make every other relation simultaneous with it.
  • C06-H0006 — A cycle's nonzero period is distinct from zero transition-time. The cinematic analogy has limits. It cannot justify either an elementary carrier or a universal projector.
  • C06-H0007 — Holistic balance is an explicit premise with both every-body and one-partner formulations. The all-body wording must remain in the Canon, together with its qualifications.
  • C06-H0008 — Orbital and spin angular momenta are distinct vector contributions. The source includes both in a total; naming one does not identify the other. The ensuing orbital-change claims and their assumptions belong to C07, not to a universal coupling law introduced here.
Test for new work
  • C06-T0001: Identify whether a quantity is angular momentum, action, energy, cycle period or an interval between records. Keep h and ħ distinct.
  • C06-T0002: State whether a claim concerns one exchange, an atomic cycle, a statistical collection or a macroscopic model.
  • C06-T0003: Do not infer an elementary deterministic chain or a universal clock from a cinematic analogy.
  • C06-T0004: Preserve literal every-body assertions and their sporadic/one-partner qualifications. Do not replace either with an unqualified absence claim.
  • C06-T0005: Give the actual statistical law if claiming a quantitative result. Mark every added selection, weighting or succession rule as an added premise.
  • C06-T0006: Separate conservation from the dynamics used with it. Neither conserved totals nor agreement with a target establish the missing operation.
  • C06-T0007: Distinguish author thesis, conditional calculation and measurement. Check damaged units against the relevant original witness before consequential use.

C07 — Orbits and gravity

Holdings
  • C07-H0001 — The account begins with observed orbital relations, not an unseen pull. Normal Realism supplies the philosophical starting point. Osborne presents naturally orbital free motion as a physical postulate, not as a definition forced by the philosophy alone.
  • C07-H0002 — A natural orbit is the motion a body would have without imposed restrictions. The distinction is between a free orbit and a constrained one. Weight is treated through the latter, rather than as an invisible force already acting in the former. This is Osborne's account of the physical meaning of force.
  • C07-H0003 — Conservation alone does not select the equation of motion. Osborne explicitly retains mathematical elements of existing dynamics. An orbital calculation therefore needs its physical postulates, mathematical assumptions, initial conditions and empirical inputs stated separately. The joint Light-Speed book extends its time-dilation account to nonclosed, precessing orbits; its perihelion calculation expressly imports Møller’s standard derivation. The assumptions and reported comparison are retained in C07, Differences and open rules.
  • C07-H0004 — The worked two-body construction is an idealization. It is not the full many-body universe. The chapter starts with a much smaller orbiting mass and negligible spin; its time parameter is distinguished from discrete observational duration. In Einstein's Lost Legacy, the authors claim slower clocks nearer a central mass and an ideal remote zero of orbital time dilation called Deep Space Time (DST). Its satellite confirmation claim coexists with a numerical comparison reaching only approximately 60% of the cited difference. These are source reports under the stated idealization, not fresh measurements; C08 and C11 retain the DST reference-frame issues.
  • C07-H0005 — The later joint book proposes spin-dependent orbital changes. In its stated example, co-sense spin reduces the natural orbital radius and opposite spin increases it. These are source predictions. They are not measurements or a general transition law.
  • C07-H0006 — The authors distinguish their prediction from disputed experimental reports. The book questions both the magnitude and replication of the cited gyroscope results. Its hope for a decisive test remains prospective.
Test for new work
  • C07-T0001: Identify the observed trajectory or constraint record before specifying its interpretation.
  • C07-T0002: List the orbit law, symmetry, closure, mass hierarchy, initial conditions and empirical constants separately from conservation.
  • C07-T0003: Distinguish orbital from spin angular momentum, total from component conservation, and a family of circular solutions from an actual transition between them.
  • C07-T0004: State the constraint that makes a force measurable. Do not erase measured work by rejecting a vacuum-force picture.
  • C07-T0005: Attach source version, orientation, fixed quantities and evidential status to every spin-effect claim. Do not resolve a source disagreement with a later program premise.

C08 — Inertia, mass and Mach

Holdings
  • C08-H0001 — Mass is a measure within relations, not a self-sufficient substance. The philosophical order matters: the observed whole is analyzed into mass, length and time; isolated masses are not made the source of that whole.
  • C08-H0002 — Inertia is explained through the body's relation to other bodies. In the braking-bus example, the experienced force is the restraint changing the passenger's motion. The book denies a separate force that throws the passenger forward.
  • C08-H0003 — The authors connect inertial orientation to a Machian reference frame. The joint book identifies the fixed-star frame with a rotational reference and a proposed reference-zero of time dilation.
  • C08-H0004 — Mass and moment of inertia are distinct quantities in the authors' calculations. Moment of inertia depends on geometry as well as mass. Calling both relational does not make them identical.
  • C08-H0005 — The later book describes the fundamental inertial connections as discrete. Its macroscopic discussion of an overall nexus is accompanied by quantum-sequence language.
Test for new work
  • C08-T0001: Identify the force or motion record, the restraint, and the bodies relative to which the motion is described.
  • C08-T0002: Keep mass, moment of inertia, angular momentum and energy distinct, with units and geometrical assumptions.
  • C08-T0003: Separate the assertion of relational dependence from a quantitative response law and from a measurement of that law.
  • C08-T0004: Specify whose clock and reference frame are used; retain the source's universal-time tensions.
  • C08-T0005: Name the contributor and version of any proposed inertial law. Label later additions as additions, not recovered author doctrine.

C09 — Atom, charge, electricity, magnetism, particle labels and nuclear status

Holdings
  • C09-H0001 — Electrical language describes phenomena before it describes analytical parts. Pope starts with such occurrences as rubbed amber, lightning and currents. Their conventional names do not arrive attached to independently known microscopic objects.
  • C09-H0002 — The atomic representation is an indivisible angular-momentum organization, not a miniature planetary machine. The joint book expressly limits its familiar orbital picture.
  • C09-H0003 — The authors replace microscopic charge-as-property with spin quantities, while retaining ordinary electrical measures. The replacement is an author proposal about interpretation, not the disappearance of voltage, current or stored charge.
  • C09-H0004 — The worked atomic example is a reconstruction with an empirical energy input. Osborne explicitly defines an energy through the conventional ionization potential and charge unit. The assignment of that energy to spin is a further interpretive step.
  • C09-H0005 — The magnetic account is proposed as a spin relation, not supplied as a complete material law. Osborne attributes this interpretation to himself and Pope. His own comparison with conventional electrodynamics qualifies a clean division between electrical and magnetic explanations.
  • C09-H0006 — Nuclear unification is prospective in the cited paper. The degree of completion must not exceed the author's wording.
Test for new work
  • C09-T0001: Begin with spectra, current, voltage, work or another specified observable; separate its record from the microscopic interpretation.
  • C09-T0002: Mark conventional model comparisons and particle labels explicitly. Do not turn the authors' analytical representation into a continuous hidden mechanism.
  • C09-T0003: List empirical mass, energy and spectral inputs before claiming a prediction. A target used to construct a parameter is not independent confirmation.
  • C09-T0004: Keep action, angular momentum, charge, energy and voltage dimensionally distinct; identify every proposed equivalence.
  • C09-T0005: Preserve version-specific factors and source defects. Label magnetic and nuclear extrapolations as proposals until the missing law is supplied and separately evaluated.

C10 — Instantaneity, two slits, EPR, polarization and statistics

Holdings
  • C10-H0001 — The two-slit argument begins with the apparatus and its geometry. The observed pattern is not first explained by giving an unseen traveler knowledge of the screen. Pope asks what the experimental arrangement already determines.
  • C10-H0002 — A single exchange and a statistical interference pattern are different subjects. Pope locates interference-type structure in the probability description of many events.
  • C10-H0003 — Instantaneous links are not transitive links. The later joint book explicitly limits the cinematic analogy. Connecting one pair and another pair does not establish one universal instantaneous event.
  • C10-H0004 — Pope resolves the stated EPR paradox by changing the interpretation of c. The argument identifies the two ends of a direct exchange as one event; it retains the observer's time relation.
  • C10-H0005 — Direction and polarization are described through atomic orientation and patterns of observations. The source gives a directional condition, not a complete analyzer-angle law.
  • C10-H0006 — The probability mathematics is proposed for use, not wholly derived in the philosophical account. The Odyssey describes a possible accommodation of existing probability functions and expressly leaves detailed work to others.
Test for new work
  • C10-T0001: State the apparatus, settings, recorded events and sampling boundary before the interpretation.
  • C10-T0002: Separate elementary occurrence, possible alternatives and measured frequencies.
  • C10-T0003: Identify every phase, amplitude, composition and normalization rule as author-derived, imported or newly assumed.
  • C10-T0004: Do not infer a universal clock from instantaneous pair relations or a Bell law from conservation alone.
  • C10-T0005: Preserve edition and speaker distinctions. Keep source-reported experiments separate from raw measurement receipts.

C11 — Cosmology, thermodynamics and chemistry

Holdings
  • C11-H0001 — The authors separate redshift observations from a recessional interpretation. Their alternative is a proposed cosmology, not a measurement established by the criticism of another interpretation.
  • C11-H0002 — The later joint book advocates a steady state of indefinite extent. This is affirmative author content, not merely an objection to expansion.
  • C11-H0003 — Statistical laws govern wholes rather than arising from autonomous microscopic causes. This is the authors' interpretation of entropy and conservation. C06 owns the general account of statistical causality; here its subject is thermal regularity.
  • C11-H0004 — The book proposes a larger entropy–negentropy balance. It joins local decay and local organization within an overall steady-state picture.
  • C11-H0005 — Chemical combination remains legitimate ordinary description. The atomic discussion retains conventional ionization and valency language as a comparison, rather than supplying a separate finished chemistry.
Test for new work
  • C11-T0001: Separate the spectral or thermal record from its reduction and cosmological interpretation.
  • C11-T0002: State the assumed distribution of matter and motion, dimensionality, distance calibration and clock convention.
  • C11-T0003: Preserve the universal-time disagreement and the distinct historical redshift proposals.
  • C11-T0004: Define the system boundary before asserting entropy increase, decrease or compensation; do not infer a measured global balance from a local example.
  • C11-T0005: Identify chemical analogies as analogies. Do not present absent reaction or bonding rules as author-derived results.

C12 — Life and mind

Holdings
  • C12-H0001 — Mind and matter belong to one reality. Normal Realism's philosophical claim comes before its physical applications: removing the dualism does not remove mental life.
  • C12-H0002 — Deliberation, volition and responsibility are taken as real. Pope does not offer them as illusions produced by a complete microscopic mechanism.
  • C12-H0003 — Form and substance can be distinguished but not separated. Pope uses reproduction, planning and animal behavior to explain how an organization can persist or be represented without being identical to one parcel of material.
  • C12-H0004 — Life and intelligence are described as prospective and information-guided. The joint book groups them with the creation of order and illustrates prediction through ordinary animal behavior.
  • C12-H0005 — The authors seek continuity between physical and life sciences. The proposal is broader than using a machine analogy for organisms.
Test for new work
  • C12-T0001: State the experience, behavior or communication being explained before proposing its mechanism.
  • C12-T0002: Keep philosophical recognition of mind distinct from a biological or computational model and from measurement.
  • C12-T0003: Specify whether a claim concerns form, memory, individual identity, consciousness or moral agency; do not treat them as synonyms.

Holdings

C01-H0001 — Normal Realism is objective commonsense empiricism. The observed world is not a mental copy of a second physical world. This is the authors' philosophical starting point, not a measurement result.

"this global norm of objective commonsense empiricism has been called Normal Realism." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0302 "in Normal Realism it is never even suggested that the world we observe in everyday life may not be the same world as that occupied by objects themselves and their various parts." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0302

"They are the one and only physical reality which is manifest in all the observational (i.e., sensory and instrumental) categories articulated by the barest, most economic use of communal language." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0223 "Normal Realism is the paradigm of Natural Philosophy which is the logical result of removing from contemporary physics all vestiges of both the Cartesian and the Berkeleyan fallacies." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0221 "They virtually cancelled the ‘matter’ side of the duality to leave only ‘mind’, instead of cancelling the dichotomy altogether." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0303

Attribution: Pope, chapter 5.

"This huge remnant is all part of what is normally called reality." — immediate-distant-action:P0776 "These things which are not directly known may nevertheless be reasonable subjects of commonsense conjecture, or theory. What the texts reject

The joint books reject both a physical world separated from its appearances and the deletion of the physical side in favor of mind alone (C01-H0001–C01-H0002). Osborne explicitly rejects confinement of reality to the instant of perception:

"Thus, Normal Realism firmly rejects the extreme positivistic view that only what is directly perceived at an instant can be real." — immediate-distant-action:P1378

Holdings

C02-H0001 — Physical terms obtain their meaning through experience. Naming, classifying and measuring are activities within language, not guarantees of a separate bearer answering to each noun.

"Plainly, the objects and processes with which physics deals did not come ready classified, categorised and labelled by nature." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P1771 "All those things we think of as existing are always, in the last analysis, linguistic interpretations of phenomena, which prove to be good or bad, as expanding experience reveals." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0289 "This is a failure to realise that in common language there is more than just one category of existence." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0189

"the fact that something can be named, such as an ‘electron’, and that it can be described in terms of the observational/experimental measures allocated to it does not provide an absolute guarantee that anything answering to that name and those predications really exists." — existence-science-and-language---dec-2004:P0018

C02-H0004 — Meaning can be direct or progressively abstract. Physical description, history, psychology and fiction are not thereby the same category. A higher-order delineation must retain its connection to what it delineates.

"words have practical meaning only as delineations of phenomena (in any of the conventional categories like physics, psychology, history, language, fiction, or whatever)" — a-synopsis-of-normal-realism-revised:P0075 "or if not, then as delineations of delineations of phenomena (that is, second, third, fourth etc. orders of generality)" — a-synopsis-of-normal-realism-revised:P0075

C02-H0005 — Language is developmental and cannot certify its own adequacy. Neither communal agreement nor the current vocabulary completes knowledge.

"Language, in Normal Realism, is essentially developmental, evolving as our communal experience evolves, hence always, in principle, essentially revisable in the face of natural uncertainty, as befits our finite position in the scheme of things." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0223 "no language, in and of itself, can guarantee, at any stage in its development, that whatever it suggests to us is 'real' is in fact real." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0214

"what makes these conventions hang together is logic, which is the science of relations internal to language" — a-synopsis-of-normal-realism-revised:P0089 "Logic is what systematically tests our perceptions and conceptions for consistency and integrity." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P1775 Attribution: Pope.

What the texts reject

They reject a detached, all-seeing standpoint as the foundation of objective physics:

"So, as it transpires, this splitting-off of a presumed 'God's-eye-view' of physical objects from our perceptions of them created a false dichotomy." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0202

They also reject the inference that a world described through observers disappears when one observer does:

"the demise of any one of these 'observers', even of oneself, does not entail the demise of the whole intercommunicational network." — phenomenalism:P0020

The opposition is therefore to both a world wholly divorced from possible observation and a world confined to one mind. It is not opposition to instruments, inference, unobserved ordinary objects or communication among nonhuman things.

The rejection of a hidden mechanical producer of information belongs constitutionally to C01-H0005 and physically to C05. It must be read with C01's retained substratum disagreement, not converted into an assertion that every author passage denies every kind of substantiality.

Differences and open rules

Broad and narrow observer. The broad usage in C03-H0001 is not uniform across the corpus. A later paper distinguishes interagents from observers who communicate in language:

"By 'interagent', here, we mean something that is party to a physical interaction, as distinct from something that observes and communicates in language." — the-universe-of-normal-realism:P0018 "An interagent may nevertheless be conceived, in the context of NR, as a 'rudimentary observer'." — the-universe-of-normal-realism:P0018

The 1978 synopsis describes its terms as delineations of human experience:

"all such things are delineations of the language of human experience." — a-synopsis-of-normal-realism-revised:P0087

The autobiography tentatively extends communicational language even to mineral things (the-odyssey:P0719). These are distinctions in vocabulary and scope, not evidence for a measured boundary of consciousness. State which sense is used in each argument.

Polar and public space. Phenomenalism:P0018 describes the common grid as subsequent to polar interactions, while P0019–P0020 require the grid for an objective polar perspective. Both directions remain. “Subsequent” here cannot silently mean a chronological production of a public world by isolated private minds. The texts give a conceptual dependence and mutual qualification, not an algorithm establishing a globally unique coordinate system from arbitrary records.

Bare sensation and interpreted description. One paper treats bare sensation as indubitable:

"So far as bare unclassified sensation is concerned, no question of its existence can arise." — normal-realism-1:P0012 "Doubt and contradiction can arise only when language is employed to classify and divide these sensations, to put them into a logical order." — normal-realism-1:P0012

Another says:

"no data of sense or instrumentation are objectively specifiable but are always contextual, depending upon the ways in which language is used." — phenomenalism-1-the-philosophical-root-source-of-relativity:P0035

Indubitable occurrence is not infallible description. That distinction is a useful editorial reading, but no prelinguistic calibrated measurement protocol follows from these passages. C04 retains the obligation to distinguish an occurrence from its interpretation.

Information and its scale. The accepted B2a/B3a comparisons retain broader claims that individual quanta convey no information alongside claims that quanta are information. C03-H0006 supports the narrower claim that a single event does not specify observational distance; it does not erase the broader wording. Wondering in the Wilderness must retain its narrative speakers and the S02 supplemental comparison rather than be quoted as one undifferentiated author assertion. No Shannon formula, probability weighting or uniquely invertible reconstruction is derived here.

Analytical and temporal priority. C03-H0004 and C03-H0007 coexist. The first concerns analysis of dimensional experience; the second refuses to obtain time from pure number alone. The texts do not supply a general operational reconstruction of geometry, clock comparison and calibration from one isolated record. Hypothetical stellar clocks in the paired Intellectual Challenge/Problem papers are explicitly hypothetical in the accepted B2b P14-33 disposition, not observed clock readings.

C04 — Method, discipline, critique and lineage

Holdings

C04-H0001 — Inquiry begins with phenomena. The philosophical starting point precedes the physical model. C03 owns the associated whole-before-parts analysis.

"The Normal Realist approach to POAMS is fundamentally observational, so that we begin with the phenomena at the empirical level of reality, that is, with things we actually observe." — poams-and-the-atom-synopsis:P0008

C04-H0002 — Observation requires interpretation. The existence of an occurrence and the adequacy of its description are different questions.

"observations have to be interpreted, and it is these interpretations of physical phenomena that create the mysteries." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P1774 "no data of sense or instrumentation are objectively specifiable but are always contextual, depending upon the ways in which language is used." — phenomenalism-1-the-philosophical-root-source-of-relativity:P0035

C04-H0003 — Conflicting conventions are to be revised for logical and practical adequacy. Logical tidiness alone does not make a vocabulary sufficient for experience.

"Where conventions do not hang together we simply make adjustments until they do." — a-synopsis-of-normal-realism-revised:P0093 "The only criterion to be observed is that the result of any adjustment represents a maximum of both logical and practical efficiency – and sufficiency." — a-synopsis-of-normal-realism-revised:P0079

C04-H0004 — Conceptual economy matters alongside predictive success. The authors apply Ockham's principle to the number of explanatory ideas, not merely to the compactness of a formula.

"This rules that one should not increase, beyond what is strictly necessary, the number of ideas required to explain a phenomenon." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0164 "Some of these other criteria are every bit as important as that of purely pragmatical, predictive power." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0164

C04-H0005 — Philosophical rejection does not require abandonment of useful theories. Working hypotheses and retained mathematical elements have a legitimate practical role. Their success does not establish their ontology.

Attribution: Osborne.

"However, on a purely practical level, this does not imply that all existing physical theories should be abandoned." — immediate-distant-action:P1636 "Many true advances in practical physics had undoubtedly been secured by means of such theories and their purely practical spin-offs had obviously been of great benefit to mankind." — the-odyssey:P0351 "whilst retaining some of the mathematical elements of existing theories, it dispenses with other elements regarded as unnecessary" — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0913 "Now from a purely pragmatical point of view, there are, obviously, advantages to be gained from both kinds of curiosity concerning the occurrences of numbers in nature" — anpa--poams---two-systems-of-combinatorics-2002:P0034

C04-H0006 — The discipline concerns explicit premises and incompatible precepts, not dogmatic closure. A contradiction must be identified, rather than inferred from a proposal's unfamiliarity.

"Normal Realism is therefore not a dogma; but to be a Normal Realist is to submit to a discipline, which involves familiarising oneself with the full range of basic presuppositions and with their method of logical evolution." — a-synopsis-of-normal-realism-revised:P0057 "preserving it from dilution in terms of foreign (incompatible) precepts and prejudices" — a-synopsis-of-normal-realism-revised:P0057 "Where these may be discovered in the course of argument they are signified by being declared unconstitutional." — a-synopsis-of-normal-realism-revised:P0057

C04-H0007 — Physical laws require more than a philosophical preference or a conservation statement. Osborne declares additional assumptions in the orbital account. The detailed physical construction belongs to C07.

"no conservation principle automatically provides an equation of motion." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0914 Attribution: Osborne.

"However, as made explicit in this chapter, there are other assumptions made and so POAMS does not attempt to be a new axiomatic physical theory in any accepted sense but rather represents a synthesis of ideas." — immediate-distant-action:P1631 Attribution: Osborne.

"Hence, it should be noted that the POAMS account of orbital motion does not make fewer assumptions than the Newtonian approach." — immediate-distant-action:P1631

C04-H0008 — The stated lineage is selective, not wholesale adoption. The books place the philosophy in an empiricist and linguistic tradition, extend ordinary commonsense beyond its customary domain, and distinguish their purpose from Bondi's pedagogical use of the conversion factor.

"This is a continuation of that historical skein of commonsense philosophy which was woven by those empiricists, Mach, Moore, Austin, Ryle, Wittgenstein, et al., in the tradition of George Berkeley." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0302 "Normal Realism is an articulation of the normal, logical way of thinking and use of language which extends the Wittgensteinian method of 'purified commonsense' beyond ordinary everyday experience" — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0226 "In contrast to Bondi, our different reason was, on that same basis, to develop a whole new philosophical approach to physics" — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0289

What the texts reject

They reject unnecessary conceptual additions, insulated physical pictures and the claim that useful prediction alone settles philosophical interpretation (C04-H0004–C04-H0005). They reject treating the existing ordinary language as finished; its revision belongs to C02.

Their criticism of Berkeley concerns the move to mind alone, not every aspect of his philosophical challenge (C01). Their criticism of Einstein's interpretation is not rejection of all relativistic results or permission to erase their own changed positions. The accepted B2a Apologia and B2b NPA/aberration comparisons retain those qualifications.

The texts' own methodological criticism also applies to claims made in POAMS's name. Conservation is not an equation of motion, and minimal interpretation is not proof that no assumptions were added. C04-H0007 expressly prevents that inference.

C05 — Light, c, time, relativity and material media

Holdings

C05-H0001 — Light is information, not an unseen carrier of information. The philosophical reversal is established in C01 and the observer/whole account in C03. Its application here denies that light travels in a preexisting void.

"In our information-based approach, light is not a ‘carrier’ of information, it is the information itself." — eye-of-beholder:P0271 "In NR, the philosophy on which our lateral-thinking alternative approach to modern physics is based, light is not something that travels in space." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0293 "far from thinking of light being ‘in space’, we think of space – and all it contains – as being in the light." — eye-of-beholder:P0271

The scope includes radio, not visible illumination alone:

"In fact, light and radio are, basically, one and the same phenomenon, except that the frequencies of visible light are vastly greater than those of radio." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0466

C05-H0002 — An elementary exchange is one event with two ends. The authors describe loss at one atom and gain at another as a reciprocal relation, not two independent events joined by an intervening carrier. This is their physical account, not an independently established measurement.

"Conservation of energy demands that every quantum of interaction has to be an instantly consummated, double-ended affair, a one-ended quantum jump being as impossible to conceive as a one-ended coil of wire." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0730 "At the quantum level, therefore, the two atoms are in a direct and instantly reciprocal inter-resonance relation in accordance with Newton’s law of equal and opposite action-and-reaction." — faster-than-radar-precis---sept-2010:P0012

C05-H0003 — The exchange's own time is zero; its observer interval need not be. Intrinsic transition-time, a body's proper time and observer-time must remain distinct. The atomic cycle period is a further distinction, developed in C06.

"Since their intrinsic transition-time ('proper-time') t is zero, their intrinsic transition-distance is also zero (i.e., s = ct = 0)." — eye-of-beholder:P0293 "the emitter and absorber of a photum, which are non-separated in quantum space and time, are separated in observer-time by the observational interval \(s/c\)." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0733 "To the question, then, of whether action-at-distance is instantaneous or time-delayed, the answer, as far as light is concerned, is plainly that it is both." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0383

Here s is the separation assigned through the observer's wider record, not a distance determined by the isolated exchange (C03-H0006). Proper-time instantaneity is not a demonstrated zero-delay message on an operator's clock.

C05-H0004 — c is the constant ratio of distance and time units for inertial observers using the same conventions. The authors retain the first relativity postulate and replace the interpretation of the second.

"Postulate 1: The laws of physics are the same in all inertial frames." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0249 "Postulate 2: Observational distance and time have a constant ratio of units, c, for all inertial observers sharing that same conventional choice of units." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0285 "although the constant c is the least upper bound of all relative speeds, it is not a speed as such." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0383

The bound concerns the body's relative speed s/t_R, measured with the observer's clock. The different quotient s/t uses the body's proper time and is unbounded in the authors' account. Neither quotient is a speed assigned to light.

"The first is what we can describe as the 'Newtonian' speed and is s/t, where s is the distance travelled by X in proper time t. The second is what can be described as the 'Einsteinian' speed and is s/t_R, where t_R is the time taken for X to travel the distance s relative to O's clock." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0383 "a theoretically unbounded Newtonian speed" — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0383

C05-H0005 — The uniform-motion time relation uses a right-triangle construction. The source identifies the proper time t and displacement measure s/c as perpendicular components and the hypotenuse as observer-time t_R.

"let us now think of \(t\) as the passage of time recorded by a 'clock' moving with the body \(X\)." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0350 "Then \(t_R\) will be simply the length of the hypotenuse of the triangle in Fig. 3.1(b), given by Pythagoras's theorem as" — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0352 "t_R^2 = t^2 + (s/c)^2." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0353 "Now the speed, \(v\), of \(X\) relative to \(O\) (that is, relative to \(O\)'s clock) is given by \(v = s/t_R\)" — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0357 "t = (1 - v^2/c^2)^{1/2} t_R." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0364

Editorial premise analysis: conversion of units alone does not establish orthogonality or identify the hypotenuse with a clock reading. The authors present a derivation; this dossier records the additional geometric and clock-identification premises. No later program premise supplies them retrospectively. For nonuniform motion, proper time must be accumulated along the path, not computed from final displacement alone:

"It follows by (3.2b) and the definition of the Riemann integral that in this case, the relatively moving clock \(X\) records proper time" — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0425

C05-H0006 — Resonance and a receiving end are conditions of the exchange. They do not constitute a complete pair-selection or probability law.

"It is only when these two measures are resonant that the atoms interact" — from-light-in-space-to-space-in-light-the-complete-relativis:P0062 "quantum energy cannot 'start out' from a source if the conditions for its 'reception' at a distance are not instantly assured." — why-the-constant-c-cannot-be-a-speed-definitive:P0050

The preceding period statement is:

"The period is the inverse of the quantum energy divided by Planck's constant \(h\)." — from-light-in-space-to-space-in-light-the-complete-relativis:P0061

C06 distinguishes action h, angular momentum h/2π and the nonzero cycle from zero transition-time. A ready receiver is not by itself an independently derived control mechanism.

C05-H0007 — Reflection involves two exchanges. In the source/mirror example, each contributes an observer interval s/c; the stated round-trip interval is 2s/c.

"Obviously, a single atom cannot both gain and lose a quantum in a single instance." — eye-of-beholder:P0292 "This involves another lapse of observational time s/c. Hence, the overall observational time recorded at A, for the signal transaction will be 2s/c (see Fig. 5.1)." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0736

C05-H0008 — Doppler intervals must be distinguished from dilated clock intervals. For a receding source, the source displacement between records adds to the observer's pulse interval. This is not a change in the conversion constant.

"Let the very small period of time between successive 'pulses' be δt, as recorded by a clock moving with the source X. This time will be recorded by O's clock as δt_R" — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0393 "\delta t_R^* = \delta t_R + v\delta t_R/c." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0396

The starred pulse interval is not δt_R. Pope also reads aberration as evidence of the observer's motion among surrounding bodies:

"So aberration tells us that it is the earth that is moving in the heavens, not the heavens that are moving with respect to the earth." — eye-of-beholder:P0221

This records the author's interpretation, not a new observational result. The alternative cosmological reading of redshift belongs to C11.

C05-H0009 — A material sequence is not one elementary exchange. The books distinguish direct reciprocal events from mediated statistical processes; the fiber paper proposes time dilation of the sequence recorded by an observer. For apparent deflection near a star, the Light-Speed book proposes material refraction and a formal limiting Schwarzschild-geodesic comparison; Legacy emphasizes informationally projected lines of sight and orbital geometry. C05, Differences and open rules, preserves these formulations without identifying them or deriving an index law from the angular comparison.

"Such processes are subject to entropy, in which the mediated quanta lose their directness and therefore the certainty of connection which there is in each single quantum (photum)." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0316 "the process of light conduction, like all other processes in the medium, was time-dilated relatively to the observer." — refraction-in-optic-fibres:P0012

The paper proposes an effective material-motion comparison:

"the fibre can be likened to a system moving as a whole relatively to the observer at an effective (rms) speed v" — refraction-in-optic-fibres:P0010

That is a proposed bridge, not a derived quantitative law of material response. Storage or residence in an atom, discussed in the accepted B2a angular-momentum-iaaad---june-2001:P0006–P0011 comparison, must not be assigned to the elementary transition itself.

C05-H0010 — The wire/fiber analogy has an explicitly unresolved limit. Pope compares them but does not identify electricity with light.

"they are both conduits for disembodied energy." — refraction-in-optic-fibres:P0014 "But, of course, this does not mean that 'electricity’ can be identified as a form of light." — refraction-in-optic-fibres:P0017 "An electric current cannot flow without a completed circuit, whereas light flows in a fibre towards an open end. The reason for this particular difference is not clear" — refraction-in-optic-fibres:P0018

What the texts reject

They reject light as a substance or carrier traversing empty space and reject an ether for the direct exchange:

"What we do not accept is the customary view of light being composed of space-travelling ‘photons’." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0218 "And these resonances are not those of ‘waves’ travelling in an ‘ether’ or in any other kind of medium." — eye-of-beholder:P0270 "any assumption as to the existence of an 'ether' is not only un-empirical but is also logically redundant." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0278

Pope's visibility argument says that seeing light in transit would require it to produce further light (why-the-constant-c-cannot-be-a-speed-definitive:P0057). The joint book describes a visible beam in dust as successive atomic illuminations, not observation of a traveler (light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0294), and rejects recoil as sufficient evidence of a ballistic quantum (P0762). These are interpretive arguments, not fresh measurements.

The open-switch argument reports no near-end transient with the far switch open:

"There is no detectable transient flow. The circuit remains completely empty of charge until the switch at B is closed." — why-the-constant-c-cannot-be-a-speed-definitive:P0019

The accepted B2c review classifies this as an empirical assertion without the apparatus, calibration and raw records needed for a measurement receipt. C06 — Angular momentum, action, discreteness and statistical causality

Holdings

C06-H0001 — POAMS identifies angular momentum, rather than mass alone, as its underlying physical substance. This is the authors' physical proposal within Normal Realism. It follows the philosophical starting point, not the reverse.

"According to POAMS, the universal underlying 'substance' (building block) is not mass but angular momentum." — poams-and-the-atom-synopsis:P0009

Osborne explicitly presents the physical starting points as postulates:

"This chapter gives an account of an angular momentum synthesis, based on Pope's Normal Realist philosophy. It is postulated that all motion is naturally orbital, and that angular momentum is holistically conserved." — immediate-distant-action:P1375

These statements do not independently establish an equation of motion. C07 owns the orbital construction; C08 owns inertia and mass; C09 owns the atomic and charge account.

C06-H0002 — Action h and angular momentum h/2π are distinct quantities in the authors' account. The same physical interpretation does not make the units interchangeable.

"Planck's quantum h, which is the product of energy and time" — eye-of-beholder:P0266 "the related quantum of angular momentum, h/2π, which is the product of the measures mass, radius and velocity." — eye-of-beholder:P0266 "energy is transacted in instantaneous amounts of inter-resonant action, \(nh\) (note: action in units of \(nh\), not angular momentum in units of \(\hbar\))." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0498

The spectral energies vary even though the action unit is fixed:

"This example also demonstrates that different phota may have different energies." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0762

The joint book retains Planck's energy-frequency relation, here printed with v for frequency; this is not the relative-speed variable used in C05:

"\[E = h v \tag{5.1}\]" — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0706

Pope connects the exchange energies with spectral series:

"whose energies of interaction form the spectral, bar-code-like series described in section 2 of chapter IV." — eye-of-beholder:P0266

The elementary exchange and its receiving end belong to C05. No new winding, closure or numerical quantization law is added here.

C06-H0003 — The authors treat physical information and recordable measures as ultimately discrete. This is a source thesis about the phenomena, not a theorem supplied by modern information theory merely by using its vocabulary.

"for information to be information it has to be analysable into ultimately discrete elements." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P1781 "All recordable physical measures are therefore, de facto, ultimately discrete." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0699

Their express permission to use continuous variables in mathematical models belongs to C02. It prevents treating discreteness as a ban on every continuous calculation.

C06-H0004 — Elementary events are not a deterministic causal chain. The authors locate ordinary cause and effect in statistical populations of events.

"One quantum does not cause another, any more than one letter in this sentence causes the next in sequence." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0740 "cause and effect are a function of statistical numbers of quantum events occurring in vast and fine-grained profusion." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0740

This does not by itself specify a probability distribution, independence condition or transition kernel. “Statistical” is not a quantitative law until those are supplied.

C06-H0005 — Instantaneous relations are nontransitive and do not form a regularly clocked universal sequence. A completed relation between two ends does not make every other relation simultaneous with it.

"the instantaneous to-and-fro linkages between phota into the sequences of which macrophenomenal time ultimately analyses, cannot be transitive." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0737 "the quantum stills do not follow one another in a regular sequence, one after another, like the photographic frames in a reel of film." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0475 "These quantum angular momentum systems are, by definition, connected only sporadically (intransitively)." — action-at-a-distance---oct-1996:P0027

C06-H0006 — A cycle's nonzero period is distinct from zero transition-time. The cinematic analogy has limits. It cannot justify either an elementary carrier or a universal projector.

"the period of each angular momentum cycle in itself was not instantaneous but irreducible" — the-odyssey:P0529 "Like the angular momentum period it, too, had a definite period, one which was, again, non-zero but irreducible." — the-odyssey:P0529 "the transfer-time of that same quantum from the atom in the light-source to the one in the spectrograph was zero" — the-odyssey:P0529 "The duration, or temporal ‘thickness’, of that present moment is the time of one quantum cycle, which is the inverse of its spectral frequency." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0477

The second quotation refers to the interval separating angular-momentum stills in the autobiographical explanation. It is not a claim that the transition itself has that duration. A full operational rule for the relation between cycle, interval, readiness and observed succession remains open.

C06-H0007 — Holistic balance is an explicit premise with both every-body and one-partner formulations. The all-body wording must remain in the Canon, together with its qualifications.

"POAMS begins with the premise that the observable universe is in an holistic balance, so that the natural or unrestricted motion of any body is (albeit in statistical numbers of sporadic quantum contacts) instantly correlated with that of each and every other body in the System." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P1046 "so, according to my thesis, every object is connected with every other at every distinguishable instant." — the-odyssey:P0588 "This is by hosts of different and overlapping angular momentum balances and transactions, with nothing ever falling out of account." — the-odyssey:P0588

In his reply to Clive Kilmister, Pope explicitly affirms both formulations:

"My answer is to affirm both, that every body is paired with every other and that one is paired with one other." — new-discussion-file-challenges-to-poams:P0067 "But nowhere in POAMS does it say – or even suggest – that those different transactions have to occur together, all at the same time." — new-discussion-file-challenges-to-poams:P0068 "when atom \(A\) swaps a quantum with atom \(B\), it cannot, in that same instance, swap that same quantum with atom \(C\)" — new-discussion-file-challenges-to-poams:P0069

The same quantum is not shared with a third partner in that transaction. This local explanation narrows the every/one conflict; it does not supply a complete many-body update law.

C06-H0008 — Orbital and spin angular momenta are distinct vector contributions. The source includes both in a total; naming one does not identify the other. The ensuing orbital-change claims and their assumptions belong to C07, not to a universal coupling law introduced here.

"Angular momentum is such a vector quantity. Orbital and spin angular momenta therefore add and subtract in the same way as velocities." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0787 "The total angular momentum of an orbiting particle is the sum of its orbital and spin angular momentum vectors." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0787

What the texts reject

They reject treating the statistical whole as one monolithic elementary system:

"A fallacy, then, is to imagine all these quantum stills (quantum angular momentum systems) forming one great, monolithic angular momentum system, or universe." — action-at-a-distance---oct-1996:P0027

They reject deterministic elementary selection as the required missing machinery. Pope's answer to Kilmister continues:

"there is no mechanism in POAMS to determine whether your A will transact with B or with C or, as you say, with any other particle in the universe." — new-discussion-file-challenges-to-poams:P0071

That is a rejection of micromechanical determinacy, not a quantitative derivation of the statistical weights. The apparatus-specific geometrical argument immediately following it belongs to C10.

They also reject conservation as sufficient by itself to produce dynamics:

"no conservation principle automatically provides an equation of motion." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0914

This does not reject conservation. It states the limit of what conservation alone establishes.

C07 — Orbits and gravity

Holdings

C07-H0001 — The account begins with observed orbital relations, not an unseen pull. Normal Realism supplies the philosophical starting point. Osborne presents naturally orbital free motion as a physical postulate, not as a definition forced by the philosophy alone.

"In particular, POAMS postulates that all ‘force-free’ motion (used here in the same sense as in GR) is naturally orbital, with no need to postulate any in vacuo ‘gravitational forces’ being responsible for these curvilinear motions." — immediate-distant-action:P1386

C07-H0002 — A natural orbit is the motion a body would have without imposed restrictions. The distinction is between a free orbit and a constrained one. Weight is treated through the latter, rather than as an invisible force already acting in the former. This is Osborne's account of the physical meaning of force.

"The natural orbit of any particle may therefore be thought of as the path described by the particle when all restrictions on its motion are removed, that is, when the particle moves freely under the influence of nothing but its own angular momentum." — immediate-distant-action:P1388

"In contrast, ‘real’ forces, i.e., forces which are actually experienced and measurable in some way, are very necessary in POAMS." — immediate-distant-action:P1387

C07-H0003 — Conservation alone does not select the equation of motion. Osborne explicitly retains mathematical elements of existing dynamics. An orbital calculation therefore needs its physical postulates, mathematical assumptions, initial conditions and empirical inputs stated separately. The joint Light-Speed book extends its time-dilation account to nonclosed, precessing orbits; its perihelion calculation expressly imports Møller’s standard derivation. The assumptions and reported comparison are retained in C07, Differences and open rules.

"It is quite clear that any conservation principle does not automatically provide an equation of motion." — immediate-distant-action:P1392

"Hence, for example, since observational and empirical evidence tells us that the planets follow nearly elliptical orbits, a (mathematically) Newtonian approach can be taken in POAMS when studying such orbits, so that the equation of motion is mathematically the same as the Newtonian equation of motion in this case." — immediate-distant-action:P1392

C07-H0004 — The worked two-body construction is an idealization. It is not the full many-body universe. The chapter starts with a much smaller orbiting mass and negligible spin; its time parameter is distinguished from discrete observational duration. In Einstein's Lost Legacy, the authors claim slower clocks nearer a central mass and an ideal remote zero of orbital time dilation called Deep Space Time (DST). Its satellite confirmation claim coexists with a numerical comparison reaching only approximately 60% of the cited difference. These are source reports under the stated idealization, not fresh measurements; C08 and C11 retain the DST reference-frame issues.

"Of course, we readily acknowledge that such a pure, isolated system is not observable in practice." — immediate-distant-action:P1394

"Here, t is used in its usual sense of the time parameter, which enters all the dynamical laws of physics, as opposed to time in the sense of observational time, or duration, which is discrete in POAMS" — immediate-distant-action:P1397

C07-H0005 — The later joint book proposes spin-dependent orbital changes. In its stated example, co-sense spin reduces the natural orbital radius and opposite spin increases it. These are source predictions. They are not measurements or a general transition law.

"In particular, if a satellite spins in the same direction as its orbital motion, then it will orbit closer to the Earth than if it were not spinning. If it spins in the opposite direction to its orbital motion, then it will orbit further out than if it were not spinning." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0641

"A gyroscope resting on the Earth’s surface at the equator should therefore weigh somewhat more when spinning in the same direction as that of the Earth, and less when spinning in the opposite direction." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0641

C07-H0006 — The authors distinguish their prediction from disputed experimental reports. The book questions both the magnitude and replication of the cited gyroscope results. Its hope for a decisive test remains prospective.

"It is to be hoped, however, that this problem may sometime be solved, creating a means of decisively testing the POAMS prediction of the ‘levitation’ and ‘depression’ of spinning bodies." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0641

What the texts reject

They reject the necessity of an invisible force maintaining a free orbit. Osborne's wording is narrower than a declaration that every possible vacuum force has been disproved:

"It should be noted here that POAMS does not imply that vacuum forces do not exist, only that they are superfluous" — immediate-distant-action:P1387

They do not reject measurable constraint forces. Nor does their synthesis reject every equation used in Newtonian dynamics or relativity: immediate-distant-action:P1381 explicitly retains selected mathematics while changing its interpretation. Substituting a familiar force name for an unexplained agency is rejected; explicitly labeled mathematical comparison is not.

Differences and open rules

Conservation versus dynamics. The qualification in H0003 must accompany claims of derivation by conservation alone. The accepted L02 review, Q01–Q05, identifies additional assumptions in Lectures 14–17: inward direction, rotational symmetry, closed-orbit selection and observational shape comparison. It also retains the rectilinear limiting case. The claim that acceleration is parallel to a radius does not alone choose its inward sign. These are review findings about the argument, not additional author holdings.

Nonclosed orbits and perihelion. The closed-orbit requirement in the initial construction is not a postulate that every natural orbit closes. The joint book claims that conservation with time-dilation effects yields approximately elliptical, precessing orbits (light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P1427–P1428):

"It is to be stressed that POAMS does not begin by postulating that all natural orbits are closed." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P1428 "In this case, some of the natural orbits are approximately ellipses with a small perihelion shift and so are no longer closed." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P1428

The construction assumes a static, spherically symmetric central body, identifies natural circular orbits with circular geodesics, and requires the noncircular equation to approximate the Newtonian elliptical-orbit equation (P1392, P1401–P1408, P1422). The source restricts the resulting description to radii greater than three times MG/c² (P1424). The perihelion approximation treats 2MG/c² as small relative to orbital radii and drops terms above first order (P1474–P1478). These are source assumptions and mathematical choices, not consequences of conservation alone. The calculation is explicitly imported:

"Since this result is fundamental, our derivation here expresses, in full, a standard derivation originally due to Møller [22]." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P1473

This is the book's claimed agreement, not a replay of observations or independent confirmation of its physical interpretation.

Spin and orbit are not interchangeable accounts. The accepted L02 review, Q06–Q09, keeps open the passage from conservation of total angular momentum to fixed orbital angular momentum, the proposed composition of energies, and the transition between circular solutions. Its inclination counterexamples prevent treating every orientation claim as settled. No new corrected spin law is supplied here.

C08 — Inertia, mass and Mach

Holdings

C08-H0001 — Mass is a measure within relations, not a self-sufficient substance. The philosophical order matters: the observed whole is analyzed into mass, length and time; isolated masses are not made the source of that whole.

"Ultimately, then, in Normal Realism there is no such thing as mass, existing in and of itself. Mass is just one of the triad of inseparable fundamental measures, mass, length and time, in all forms of angular momentum." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0443

Pope also states the relational restriction in terms of ordinary objects:

"There is no mass, motion, position, duration or whatever of any object relative to nothing but itself – that is, in complete isolation." — eye-of-beholder:P0334

C08-H0002 — Inertia is explained through the body's relation to other bodies. In the braking-bus example, the experienced force is the restraint changing the passenger's motion. The book denies a separate force that throws the passenger forward.

"There is no 'force' throwing us forward. The real force is what is holding us back." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0558

"This is where some surface or other stops our bodies from orbiting freely with respect to the Earth, the Sun, the stars and so on in the way they did before the thrust was applied." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0559

This is an author explanation of an ordinary reported experience, not an instrument record demonstrating the proposed distant connection.

C08-H0003 — The authors connect inertial orientation to a Machian reference frame. The joint book identifies the fixed-star frame with a rotational reference and a proposed reference-zero of time dilation.

"It is the reference-frame with respect to which, as Mach pointed out, all gyroscopes orientate themselves, and, of course, all free orbits and spins of bodies are ‘gyroscopes’ in their way." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0564

"That is to say, its intrinsic time-standard is what, in POAMS, we have called Deep-Space Time (DST)." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0564

The attribution to Mach is the book's historical attribution. No independent historical or experimental verification is claimed by quoting it.

C08-H0004 — Mass and moment of inertia are distinct quantities in the authors' calculations. Moment of inertia depends on geometry as well as mass. Calling both relational does not make them identical.

"Here, the quantity mr², which gives information about both the matter content and the shape of the orbit of P, is the moment of inertia, I, of P." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P1054

"The moment of inertia, \(I\), of \(P\) depends on the mass of \(P\) and its shape." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P1058

The surrounding construction, P1055–P1061, restricts its spin examples to a rigid, axially symmetric body about a fixed axis. These are defined measures in a mathematical representation, not a derivation of a body's mass from a cosmic census.

C08-H0005 — The later book describes the fundamental inertial connections as discrete. Its macroscopic discussion of an overall nexus is accompanied by quantum-sequence language.

"As it turns out, then, inertia, the action-at-a-distance connection between bodies envisaged by Mach, is basically quantised in the same way as light" — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0565

The direct-event and observer-time distinction belongs in C05; its application to joint statistics belongs in C10. Neither a human observer nor a continuously occupied intermediate medium is supplied as the cause of inertia.

What the texts reject

They reject mass possessed relative to nothing, and inertia as an additional force pushing the unconstrained body. They also reject the inference that removing a hidden-force interpretation removes the restraint experienced in a vehicle.

Pope's rejection of isolation is not a denial of objects when no person perceives them:

"Objects exist relatively to one another even when there is nothing around that would fit the description of a ‘percipient’." — eye-of-beholder:P0335

This is the relevant philosophical boundary. It does not establish the magnitude of an inertial response.

Differences and open rules

A relation is not yet a response law. H0001–H0003 identify the authors' interpretation and proposed reference frame. They do not provide, in these passages, an operational rule assigning weights to all remote bodies or deriving a local mass calibration from those weights. The limited finding is about the passages cited, not a declaration that every mathematical treatment in the corpus has been exhausted here.

Overall versus discrete connection. Einstein's Lost Legacy:P0560 says a jolt instantly affects the balance with other bodies; P0563 uses a hypothetical seismograph comparison, not an actual measurement receipt. P0565 then describes discrete connections. These passages must remain alongside the intransitivity restrictions in C06/C10. They do not license an editorially invented all-pairs update or universal clock.

Reference-time tension. Deep-Space Time in P0564 is affirmative source content. It cannot simply be deleted because P0476 denies a single overall now. The Odyssey:P0783/P0787 gives stronger universal-time wording. C11 is the primary home of that unresolved comparison.

Other contributors are not Pope or Osborne. B1A-R10 routes Graneau and Rowlands inertia constructions as alternatives. B2b P14-27/P14-29/P14-30/P14-32 retains Neal and Peter Graneau's model assumptions, simulation status, normalization issues and qualifications. Their equations do not become Pope–Osborne holdings because they appear in the same source collection. The accepted B3b index supplies destinations, not definitions.

B3a S03 authenticates the attribution, not a quantitative apparatus balance. This is dialogue testimony, not an independently measured mass-transfer law.

Later premises remain outside authorship. Later program rules about anisotropic response, custody or renewal are not used to fill the missing operational link. Their adoption belongs to later research, not this record of the authors' texts.

C09 — Atom, charge, electricity, magnetism, particle labels and nuclear status

Holdings

C09-H0001 — Electrical language describes phenomena before it describes analytical parts. Pope starts with such occurrences as rubbed amber, lightning and currents. Their conventional names do not arrive attached to independently known microscopic objects.

"We must not, however, lose sight of the fact that, in the last analysis, all this 'electrical' language is about phenomena and that those measures described in the above paragraph are measures derived from the analysis of phenomena on the ordinary macro-scale." — poams-and-electricity-synopsis:P0008

This philosophical priority governs the atomic comparison. It does not deny the occurrences being described.

C09-H0002 — The atomic representation is an indivisible angular-momentum organization, not a miniature planetary machine. The joint book expressly limits its familiar orbital picture.

"In POAMS, an ‘atom’ is therefore a configuration of pure indivisible angular momentum in which these component masses may be conceived, simplistically, as orbiting a common centre of balance (barycentre)." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0450

"It needs to be stressed here however, that there is no implication in POAMS that the component masses actually orbit each other as the planets orbit the Sun on the macro-scale." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0450

Conventional particle names in the authors' comparison identify measured quantities and roles in the model. They do not establish hidden, continuously orbiting constituents.

C09-H0003 — The authors replace microscopic charge-as-property with spin quantities, while retaining ordinary electrical measures. The replacement is an author proposal about interpretation, not the disappearance of voltage, current or stored charge.

"The traditional ‘electrostatic charge’ on those masses, in units of coulombs, is replaced by an equivalent spin kinetic energy in ordinary mechanical units of joules." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0450

"The meaning of that word 'charge' is retained, however, as appropriate to macro-level collections of either positive or negative ions in the usual electrician's sense, or the sense in which a cannon may be 'charged' with gunpowder." — poams-and-electricity-synopsis:P0006

The synopsis also says:

"In Normal Realism this language is virtually the same as in standard textbook physics." — poams-and-electricity-synopsis:P0007

C09-H0004 — The worked atomic example is a reconstruction with an empirical energy input. Osborne explicitly defines an energy through the conventional ionization potential and charge unit. The assignment of that energy to spin is a further interpretive step.

"Here, \(I_{0} = 13.6\) volts and \(e \approx 1.60217733 \times 10^{-19}\) coulomb." — immediate-distant-action:P1608

"And again it must be emphasized that what we are proposing here is not some new concept of the atom to compete with the current physical models that superseded Bohr's." — immediate-distant-action:P1619

"It is essentially a logical demonstration of how, ahistorically, our concepts of motion and distant interaction might have developed in direct observational terms, without postulating invisible intermediaries such as 'gravitational' and 'electrostatic' forces, linking one atom or part of an atom to another." — immediate-distant-action:P1619

Agreement with the supplied spectral or ionization scale is therefore not an independent prediction of that scale.

C09-H0005 — The magnetic account is proposed as a spin relation, not supplied as a complete material law. Osborne attributes this interpretation to himself and Pope. His own comparison with conventional electrodynamics qualifies a clean division between electrical and magnetic explanations.

"The implication here is that the POAMS model of the hydrogen atom contains an element of both electrostatics and magnetostatics." — immediate-distant-action:P1627

"Pope and I believe that whereas the POAMS pure-angular-momentum paraphrase of ‘electrostatic’ orbits is that of a spin-orbital relation between electron-masses and proton-masses, magnetic effects can be explained in terms of a spin angular momentum relation between electrons only." — immediate-distant-action:P1628

This quotation retains the source's conventional comparison labels. It does not supply an independently checked magnetic constitutive equation.

C09-H0006 — Nuclear unification is prospective in the cited paper. The degree of completion must not exceed the author's wording.

"Further studies (so far unpublished [19]) indicate that in vacuo nuclear forces may be dispensed with in the same way." — pirt-2004-proc-paper:P0076

"Indeed, it appears that the whole of physics could, in principle, be reconstructed on the basis of a systematic quantum-informational phenomenology" — pirt-2004-proc-paper:P0076

These are indications and a proposed reach, not a completed nuclear theory or a nuclear measurement receipt.

What the texts reject

They reject a continuously operating microscopic mechanism underneath phenomena and an unexplained intrinsic power of charge to pull or push across a void. They do not reject the ordinary laboratory or electrical vocabulary merely because its historical interpretation differs.

The atomic chapter explicitly says its model is not a literal picture:

"The fact that our model is not intended to provide a true physical description of the hydrogen atom does not prevent us from associating vectors with the quantities involved" — immediate-distant-action:P1595

Pope and Osborne retain real work required to separate macroscopic charged bodies in einsteins-lost-legacy:P0461. The denial concerns an invisible intermediary, not the work record.

C10 — Instantaneity, two slits, EPR, polarization and statistics

Holdings

C10-H0001 — The two-slit argument begins with the apparatus and its geometry. The observed pattern is not first explained by giving an unseen traveler knowledge of the screen. Pope asks what the experimental arrangement already determines.

"Let us, for the moment, forget about light and the technical questions as to whether it consists of waves and/or particles and look at the two-slit apparatus itself. Like any other object, this has built-in geometrical features." — a-commonsense-look-at-the-two-slit-experiment:P0020

The tape construction compares two source-to-screen routes. It is a geometrical model of alternatives, not a claim that tapes or an intervening substance are physically present in an exchange.

C10-H0002 — A single exchange and a statistical interference pattern are different subjects. Pope locates interference-type structure in the probability description of many events.

"There is, then, no interference, diffraction, diminution, entropy or whatever, in a single quantum jump." — action-at-a-distance---oct-1996:P0070

"All these 'wave'-type phenomena are features of the probabilistic wave-function which, in the two-slit apparatus, extends, in its statistical, fuzzy-geometrical way, through the two slits at once." — action-at-a-distance---oct-1996:P0070

This is source statistical language. It does not establish a material wave in empty space.

C10-H0003 — Instantaneous links are not transitive links. The later joint book explicitly limits the cinematic analogy. Connecting one pair and another pair does not establish one universal instantaneous event.

"Quantum interactions (quantum stills) are discrete and intransitive, which simply means that when atom \(A\) is interacting with atom \(B\), and atom \(B\) is interacting with atom \(C\), it doesn’t necessarily mean that atom \(A\) is interacting with atom \(C\)" — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0474

C05 owns the distinction between an exchange's own time and observer time. The present holding concerns the composition of exchanges.

C10-H0004 — Pope resolves the stated EPR paradox by changing the interpretation of c. The argument identifies the two ends of a direct exchange as one event; it retains the observer's time relation.

"In the Bondian interpretation of \(c\) as a plain constant, or distance-time conversion factor, the ‘paradox’ simply disappears." — eye-of-beholder:P0278

"As can be seen in that formula, when the intrinsic transition-time of the quantum is zero, the observer-time \(t_R\) is the delayed time \(s/c\)." — eye-of-beholder:P0278

This is the author's proposed resolution of a conceptual contradiction. It is not itself a derivation of a Bell correlation function. The historical experimental account in P0276 is a report about other experiments, not their raw measurement record.

C10-H0005 — Direction and polarization are described through atomic orientation and patterns of observations. The source gives a directional condition, not a complete analyzer-angle law.

"Every quantum of energy emitted by an atom is particular and directional, according to the vector orientation of the atom's internal angular momenta (orbit, spin, etc.)." — light-speed-gravitation-and-quantum-instantaneity:P0823

"Recall, also, that all physical phenomena such as polarisation, reflection, diffraction, the dispersion and refraction of light in some medium or other are, ultimately, optical projections from directly perceived cinematic sequences of informational patterns of quantum pixels (phota)." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0538

C10-H0006 — The probability mathematics is proposed for use, not wholly derived in the philosophical account. The Odyssey describes a possible accommodation of existing probability functions and expressly leaves detailed work to others.

"Now approaching his seventies, he decided to leave the detailed working-out of these suggestions to others, younger and more practically and mathematically disposed who, now that the implications for physics were plain, might wish to pursue them." — the-odyssey:P0630

The surrounding P0628–P0630 invokes de Broglie and Schrödinger mathematics. It is an adopted or proposed mathematical tool within Pope's interpretation, not a newly supplied frequency law.

What the texts reject

They reject the single quantum dividing into independent pieces or interfering with itself as a material wave. They reject the necessity of an unseen traveler selecting its course before the exchange can be complete. They also reject the inference from elementary instantaneity to a universal present.

The rejection of a traveler is not the rejection of the experimental pattern. H0001 and H0002 expressly retain the arrangement and statistical observations. Nor does changing the interpretation of c make an operator's elapsed time vanish.

"differ by some even number of units λ/2 the probability of a quantum appearing at that point on the screen approaches zero." — action-at-a-distance---oct-1996:P0070

"where the lengths either agree, or differ by some even number of units λ/2 the probability of a quantum of that wavelength λ appearing at that point is enhanced" — action-at-a-distance---oct-1996:P0070

Original-use authentication of these exact conflicting spans remains necessary before describing the defect as a verified first-print error.

"It either occurs or it doesn't. In probabilistic terms, this means that the probability of its occurring is between nought and one, with no possibilities in between." — a-commonsense-look-at-the-two-slit-experiment:P0025

Editorial analysis: an event's occurrence or nonoccurrence does not restrict its probability to those two endpoints. The quoted sentence does not supply an intermediate-frequency rule.

C11 — Cosmology, thermodynamics and chemistry

Holdings

Cosmology

C11-H0001 — The authors separate redshift observations from a recessional interpretation. Their alternative is a proposed cosmology, not a measurement established by the criticism of another interpretation.

"What is observed is the redshift." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0621

"For instance, one such alternative explanation of the Redshift is that it is a relativistic time dilational effect of increasing omnidirectional motion with distance, which has no bizarre ‘expanding universe’ and associated ‘Big Bang’ implications." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0621

The same passage assumes increasing random motion with distance and discusses a distributed barycenter. Neither the alternative's mathematical consistency nor its claimed plausibility establishes the distribution observationally.

C11-H0002 — The later joint book advocates a steady state of indefinite extent. This is affirmative author content, not merely an objection to expansion.

"It is the Normal Realist view then, as opposed to a current cosmological view of a closed universe, that the universe as such is in a constant, steady state of indefinite extent." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0622

Thermodynamics

C11-H0003 — Statistical laws govern wholes rather than arising from autonomous microscopic causes. This is the authors' interpretation of entropy and conservation. C06 owns the general account of statistical causality; here its subject is thermal regularity.

"Now the point to be stressed, here, is that all these laws, of entropy, energy conservation, the conservation of angular momentum and so on are, essentially, holistic laws. They all state, in effect, that the whole determines the parts and not the parts the whole." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0659

C11-H0004 — The book proposes a larger entropy–negentropy balance. It joins local decay and local organization within an overall steady-state picture.

"By default, then, the ‘universe’, however we may choose to conceive it, has to remain in an overall-conserved, non-local steady state, with entropy, or decay in one locality compensated by negentropy, or growth, in another" — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0680

This is an author inference. The passage does not provide a measured global entropy budget.

Chemistry

C11-H0005 — Chemical combination remains legitimate ordinary description. The atomic discussion retains conventional ionization and valency language as a comparison, rather than supplying a separate finished chemistry.

"Those separated masses, customarily called ‘ions’, are traditionally conceived as being captured by other ions of opposite sign to form other atoms in what is known as chemical valency – analogous to changing partners in Country-and-Western dancing." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0525

The analogy describes reorganization. It is not a derivation of a table of valencies, bonding energies or reaction rates. C09 owns the atomic and charge interpretation; C12 owns the account of life and purpose.

What the texts reject

The joint book rejects making a recessional interpretation logically obligatory merely because recession can produce redshift. It rejects the imagined outside standpoint from which the universe could be measured against an external ruler and clock, and opposes a global terminal decay picture.

These are the authors' criticisms, not independently verified summaries of every competing cosmology. Their thermodynamic argument likewise rejects microscopic autonomy without denying the regularity of pressure, heat or chemical work.

Differences and open rules

Universal time is a source disagreement. The Odyssey's third-person account of its investigator asserts:

"The proper-time of that statistical zero of motion and acceleration, he concluded, is the standard time —the universal 'GMT', as it were— of the whole relational system, or universe, the time relative to which all other times are either equivalent or dilated." — the-odyssey:P0783

"there is a universal now of physical existence in which it makes sense to talk about the present positions in relation to one another of distant bodies and a universal time in which all those universal nows, or present-moments evolve." — the-odyssey:P0787

The later joint book says:

"In Normal Realism, then, there is no need whatsoever to think of everything having to be connected in the single, overall now that classical physicists and astronomers talk about." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0476

These statements are not made equivalent by calling the first merely a reunion convention. B1b authenticated the Odyssey passages locally; its final ledger assigns this conflict here. Deep-Space Time in the later book, P0564, adds a further affirmative reference-frame claim. C05 and C08 should cross-reference this disagreement rather than assert unqualified corpus agreement.

Alternatives change. OD-C11-02 records the possibility of cumulative temporal-geodesic nonlinearity in the-odyssey:P0938–P0941. B2b P14-12 records a tentative bent-path account in the Heresy versions, distinct from the later random-motion account. These are not one established cosmological law. B2b also preserves differing claims about the role of background radiation and other evidence.

Dimensional and observational inputs remain distinct. The accepted source packages retain D2's observed/required/derived dimensionality issue and contributor-specific cosmologies. Graneau, Roscoe and Rowlands are not interchangeable with Pope and Osborne. No parameter fit or source-reported galaxy result is promoted to a measurement receipt here.

Entropy and purpose. The joint book extends statistical end-state language toward prospective activity in P0660–P0668. That extension belongs partly to C12, but it cannot be silently treated as a quantitatively demonstrated final-cause law. Local organization and an asserted global compensation are different claims.

Chemistry is sparse. The passages used here provide comparisons and interpretive vocabulary, not an independently specified chemical dynamics. This dossier remains short rather than importing a chemical theory to fill the gap.

C12 — Life and mind

Holdings

C12-H0001 — Mind and matter belong to one reality. Normal Realism's philosophical claim comes before its physical applications: removing the dualism does not remove mental life.

"Dispensing with dualism unifies philosophical realism and puts mind and matter into the one and only reality that it makes sense to speak of." — eye-of-beholder:P0337

Pope treats decisions as consequential within that reality, not as a second substance that must somehow reach into the first.

"That decision, either on my part or that of my commanders, is not part of the ‘material’ world, yet it has very definite material consequences, so that the classical separation between ‘mind’ and ‘matter’ no longer applies." — eye-of-beholder:P0339

The quoted distinction between a decision and the material world is descriptive within the argument against two separate realities; it must not be erased or converted into a renewed two-substance account.

C12-H0002 — Deliberation, volition and responsibility are taken as real. Pope does not offer them as illusions produced by a complete microscopic mechanism.

"Everyone knows that deliberation, volition and responsibility are realities, in no way reducible to the mechanical motions of atoms." — eye-of-beholder:P0340

This is a philosophical author assertion. It is not a new neurophysiological experiment or a proof of a particular mathematical account of free will.

C12-H0003 — Form and substance can be distinguished but not separated. Pope uses reproduction, planning and animal behavior to explain how an organization can persist or be represented without being identical to one parcel of material.

"Form and substance are correlative – no form without substance and no substance without form. But although form and substance cannot be separated, they can be distinguished." — eye-of-beholder:P0350

"Also, a form can be transferred from one substance to another, as in organic reproduction and replication." — eye-of-beholder:P0350

This is the source's account of form. The survival of a pattern does not by itself settle the survival or identity of a conscious person.

C12-H0004 — Life and intelligence are described as prospective and information-guided. The joint book groups them with the creation of order and illustrates prediction through ordinary animal behavior.

"Now information, intelligence and life are all in the same logical category. A defining attribute of that whole category is that of negentropy, or creation of order, as in writing a novel or in living growth and development." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0667

"The way this works is that nature supplies information, not only of the here-and-now but also of the probabilities or improbabilities of future events." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0667

The hound-and-hare example in the same block concerns the use of current sensory information to anticipate possible outcomes. It is not a measurement of information obtained from a future event.

C12-H0005 — The authors seek continuity between physical and life sciences. The proposal is broader than using a machine analogy for organisms.

"Removing that categorical division, as Normal Realism does, allows the same sorts of explanations to be applied to living as to non-living or purely mechanical processes, leaving these hitherto disparate disciplines seamlessly joined." — einsteins-lost-legacy:P0668

Pope also acknowledges ordinary detectable communication between organisms:

"On the other hand, it is plain that organisms communicate by physically detectable means." — eye-of-beholder:P0371

The fact that a nonmechanistic interpretation is proposed does not remove the need to identify an actual mode of communication.

What the texts reject

They reject both mind-only and matter-only reductions of the experienced world. They reject treating purpose and responsibility as inconsequential shadows of an exhaustive mechanical account. They do not reject ordinary causal explanations at the practical level simply because elementary causality is interpreted statistically.

Pope's creation-and-evolution comparison is expressly an argument against treating the alternatives as logically exclusive:

"But why, one may ask, is it so generally assumed that creation and evolution contradict each other when, logically, they don't?" — eye-of-beholder:P0343

The birthday-cake analogy following this sentence illustrates intended preparation followed by an unfolding process. It does not establish a biological origin mechanism.

Differences and open rules

Purpose is asserted more strongly than an illustrative analogy. The Eye of the Beholder:P0344/P0352 argues for a prior possibility of life and asserts a nil probability for its emergence from purely random happenings. The text supplies no sample space or disclosed calculation in those passages. The statement must remain an author argument, not a computed probability or experimental result.

Thermodynamic extension. Einstein's Lost Legacy:P0660–P0668 treats statistical future states and goal-directed behavior as connected. That is a substantive philosophical extension, not simply a neutral restatement of a heat law. C11 owns entropy and global compensation; this dossier owns the life/mind inference. No quantitative law of anticipation is derived here.

Consciousness and survival. The Odyssey:P0787 links consciousness to present interaction and rejects transported conscious immortality in that argument. Its later discussions of persistent form and hypothetical temporal dimensions do not establish personal identity. The accepted OD-C12-02 route explicitly retains that distinction. Theological Discussion:P1008–P1028 contains later personal reflection and uncertainty; C13 owns the theological implications.

Dialogue is not a joint declaration. A. M. and N. V. Pope's Wondering in the Wilderness gives different characters objections and proposals. In P2224–P2237, Noel and Carter challenge the idea of a harmonious perfected whole by asking about competing organisms and harmful forms. The accepted B3a Q28 retains that objection. The dialogue cannot be reduced to Turner's affirmative speeches.

Speculative reports stay speculative. B3a S08 records paranormal extensions in wondering-wilderness:P2631–P2636 as dialogue, not verified communication. B2a's Blue Anchor discussion reports several participants considering conscious systems and computers; it is neither a single-author definition of consciousness nor group consensus. There is no authority here for a new mechanism or universal classification of conscious entities.

The observer is a wider category. C03 owns the broad interagent sense of observer. That sense alone does not establish consciousness in every interagent. The mind-specific holdings above must not be enlarged by substituting one meaning of observer for another. There is also a stronger dialogue statement: Turner calls each informational focus a self, complex or rudimentary, in wondering-wilderness:P0983 (accepted B3a S04). That assertion must remain visible; it does not by itself specify the capacities or experience of each such self.

  • C12-T0002: Keep philosophical recognition of mind distinct from a biological or computational model and from measurement.
  • C12-T0003: Specify whether a claim concerns form, memory, individual identity, consciousness or moral agency; do not treat them as synonyms.
  • C12-T0004: Give the assumptions behind a probability claim. An analogy or an assertion of impossibility is not a calculation.
  • C12-T0005: Preserve character, participant and author distinctions, including objections. Do not turn speculative survival or paranormal reports into physical laws.

C13 — Ethics, society and religion

Holdings

C13-H0001 — Pope gives the philosophical reversal ethical and social consequences. Responsibility is not assigned to a hidden mechanism that absolves the person. This is a normative author position, not an empirical demonstration that one philosophy produces a particular society.

"Each of us would come to realise – or be taught by others – that the responsibility for moral, civilised behaviour, and concern for our own interests as social beings lies with ourselves as free-willed individuals." — the-constant-c-and-society:P0029

C12 owns the account of deliberation and mind. The present holding records the responsibility Pope draws from it.

C13-H0002 — Reuniting science and the humanities is an explicit social aim. It includes education, history, theology and the arts rather than confining the proposed change to physical equations.

"It would remove the traditional barrier between the sciences and the humanities, thereby reinstating a seamless, commonsense joining-up of the two." — the-constant-c-and-society:P0030

Pope predicts social and artistic consequences in the surrounding paper. Those predictions and his cultural judgments are part of the source, not independently verified sociology.

C13-H0003 — Moral and religious beliefs remain subject to rational examination. Pope does not infer the existence of an object merely from the fact that people believe in it.

"It cannot be that the mere fact that we can imagine something gives it objective existence. Beliefs, like all perspectives, are subject to rational analysis." — eye-of-beholder:P0399

Nor does he settle a universal moral code by declaration:

"The question of whether, or to what extent, there is some kind of objective common factor of morality, that is to say, some evaluation to be found in humanity generally as to what is morally acceptable or offensive, is a question which cannot be answered by fiat." — eye-of-beholder:P0403

C13-H0004 — Some passages make affirmative religious claims. A summary saying that the texts contain no theology would erase these passages.

"In the same way, we have to believe in a residual class of intelligence in nature which, regardless of what we might call it, is superior not only to our own intelligence but also to that of mankind as a whole." — eye-of-beholder:P0397

This is Pope's assertion, not a measured intelligence or a demonstrated theological conclusion. In his correspondence with Penny Gower he also states a personal commitment:

"Personally, if hard pressed, I would have to admit, as you have discerned, I am a believer, and a devout one at that. But as soon as I try to rationalise that belief, I am stuck." — theological-discussion:P0175

The speaker here is Viv Pope. The collection contains other correspondents; its title does not make every sentence his.

C13-H0005 — Pope preserves unresolved questions about providence and suffering. His personal interpretation of guidance does not supply an answer to the suffering of others.

"But then, as soon as I see all the horrors that some people, and other creatures in general, have to suffer, for no reason you can chuck a stick at, I am consumed with the feeling that if there is something looking after me, then why is it not looking after them?" — theological-discussion:P0641

This is a live objection within Pope's own letter, not an objection supplied only by a later critic.

C13-H0006 — His concluding personal account does not prescribe a named deity or establish an afterlife. It preserves secular ethical alignment and uncertainty.

"As for any notion of a deity, I wouldn't presume to provide it with a name, far less to live and act in its name, which would surely be disastrous." — theological-discussion:P1057

"Whether or to what extent the same moral code applies to us all, who can say." — theological-discussion:P1058

"Where, if anywhere, do we go? When it comes right down to it, no-one knows the answer to that, and no-one has come back to tell us." — theological-discussion:P1060

These are personal conclusions at this point in the compiled discussion, not a withdrawal of every earlier affirmative passage.

What the texts reject

They reject immunity from rational examination for religious belief, moral fiat as a substitute for judgment, and the assumption that materialist science has already disposed of every religious question.

Pope's correspondence rejects a fully solipsistic solution to suffering while exploring other possibilities:

"Besides, in the development of Normal Realism that kind of 'subjective idealism', or solipsism, has been firmly refuted, which makes that particular avenue of approach no more than a cul de sac." — theological-discussion:P0643

The next block, P0644, nevertheless considers the possibility that only some other persons are unreal. This troubling conjecture remains in the source. It is not an accepted ethical rule, a measurement, or an instruction for treating anyone as unreal.

Differences and open rules

Noncommitment and belief coexist in the record. The Odyssey:P0834 says its investigator had listed possibilities without committing to them; P0835 admits possible overstatement of that disinterested position; P0837 questions personifying logos. The Eye's superior-intelligence assertion and the correspondence's personal belief are stronger. These differences cannot be harmonized into either a prescribed creed or a uniform absence of religious conviction.

Turner's limiting sentence is:

"So there's no way of us ever solving the riddle of what "God" is by pure deduction" — wondering-wilderness:P2651

This is Turner's dialogue speech in A. M. and N. V. Pope's book. It is not an unqualified Pope–Osborne proposition. The passage contains affirmative and conditional language together.

This applies the discipline Pope describes in section 25 of A Synopsis of Normal Realism (Revised). A Normal Realist submits "to a discipline, which involves familiarising oneself with the full range of basic presuppositions and with their method of logical evolution." The system is to be preserved "from dilution in terms of foreign (incompatible) precepts and prejudices. Where these may be discovered in the course of argument they are signified by being declared unconstitutional."

Before construction

  1. The question. Star Lord's question, in his words. It heads every brief and every report.

  2. Source reading. Search the books, the papers and the record of earlier failures for the subject. Record the collections, versions, search terms and relevant works found. Read those works in full, in stages, and follow relevant references. A needed source that cannot be read blocks the approach decision. Write down what Pope and Osborne hold, what they left open, what tests they proposed and how those came out, with quotations and locations. Where two texts disagree, or a text looks wrong, say so. Do not blend them. Say what the search did not find.

  3. Grounding statement. Start from Normal Realism: words get meaning from experience; physical and psychological descriptions do not name separate worlds; dimensions are conventions, not a hidden stage. Cite the passages and show how they constrain this project. Name who observes, what they record and what they count. Then the physics: the two atoms in each exchange, the angular momentum conserved, the counts, and c as the conversion between units of distance and time. Mark what follows from the premises and what has to be added. Name the result sought, the record that would test it, and what it would be good for.

  4. Approach test. Compare two approaches, including the corpus's treatment when it has one. Say why one is chosen. For that approach:

    • Trace the proposed operation from preparation to final record: each exchange, its two ends, what each keeps and what every clock step counts.
    • Reject any unseen traveler, carrier, mediating substance, background stage or unexplained transit clock. Account for the apparatus and any intervening atoms as well. Renaming does not pass.
    • List borrowed mathematics apart from physical premises. Name each tool and its use. Reject a physical premise that conflicts with the grounding, wherever it came from.
    • An added premise is named as an addition, with the test that could defeat it.

  5. Independent review. A reviewer from a different vendor and model family gets the question, the Grounding Injection, the reading record, the source passages and both approaches. The review identifies the exact input versions and checks the proposed operation and every clock step, not just its summary. An unexplained essential operation or a contradiction means reject. Otherwise the reviewer gives a reasoned pass. A reject blocks the start until the reviewer clears the correction.

  6. Go from Star Lord. Only a passed approach reaches him. He sees the question, the chosen approach in plain words, the approach rejected, the review, the result sought and the budget. His go covers that paper work and nothing else.

During the work

  • Budget. Limits for the whole project in work packages, days and dollars, set before the start and counted across sessions and successor versions. Reading and review count. Stop at the first limit reached. Going on needs his approval of a named next result and a new limit.
  • Stop signals. Two reports in a row that cannot show the central object is the physical thing he asked about, or that still lean on a conventional model in place of the derivation: stop for a direction review. A new name or a new version does not reset the count. A contradiction with the grounding stops the affected work at once. After a stop or a change to an essential premise, restart requires a passed approach review and Star Lord's go.
  • Corrections. When a premise is corrected, withdraw every claim that depended on it. Do not put the correction on top of unchanged work.
  • Reports. First line: does the work still answer his question from the grounding, and why. Then the result and the next decision. A count of checks is not a result.
  • Briefs. Every brief to an AI worker, and every resumed session, carries his Grounding Injection word for word, the question, the grounding statement, the chosen approach and its test, the review decision, the active stop signals and the remaining budget. Check that the brief is not truncated and that the worker can search and open the full sources. Maintain one linked topic index of source passages, rejected approaches and prior failures.
  • Review. A different-vendor reviewer for the approach, for any change to a premise, and for the final claim. Arithmetic and software checks are local and are not called independent review.
  • Writing. American English outside quotations. Plain sentences. State assumptions, results and missing work once, directly. c is the measured conversion between distance and time, never a speed. Action is never a traveler or a carrier across empty space. Particle names only for comparison.

Before anything is published

  • The paper argument is complete and independently reviewed, as GROUNDING.md §0.2 and the Foundation Wall require. The page states what was derived, what was borrowed, what was measured and what it is good for. Publish only claims supported by that argument and evidence.
  • A different-vendor reader checks the page itself against the reviewed result, the sources and the wording rules. Preserve that version; substantive edits require another check.
  • Star Lord reads the page and approves it. A summary is not enough.

When an effort is stopped

Keep the original files and reviews in the private failure archive, with dates, hashes and the reason. Link them from the topic index. Take the claims out of public view. The next effort on that subject reads that record first.

Grounding: read this first

Normal Realism is the philosophy. POAMS, the Pope-Osborne Angular Momentum Synthesis, is the physics that follows from it. The philosophy comes first, in the work and on the page. This applies to every session and every sub-agent.

Before any work that touches Normal Realism or POAMS, use the reading order below. For that work, this order supersedes the older Every Session reading order.

The statements below are our reading of the texts, not substitutes for them. For source reading and review, the statements and tests are under examination, not acceptance criteria. What Normal Realism holds (canon page 01, draft 4):

  1. Normal Realism is objective commonsense empiricism.
  2. Phenomena are the physical reality.
  3. It removes two errors at once: the split between the world and our perception of it, and the retreat into a single mind.
  4. "Normal" names a standard to be met, not what is usual.
  5. Information comes first. Space, time, mass and motion are projected from it by the observer. The information is sensory and instrumental. Normal Realism does not construct the world from featureless numbers. Geometry is a feature of the phenomena being analyzed. It is not built out of relations between pure numbers.
  6. What is projected cannot also be placed underneath as the thing that produces the information.
  7. Nature is kinematic, and mechanical only in our representations of it.
  8. The elementary events are discrete. One does not cause the next. Cause and effect belong to large numbers of them.
  9. An observer is any interagent, conscious or not.
  10. Exchanges go on where no person observes them.
  11. Objectivity is the shared world of interacting observers.
  12. Matter, mind, space, time, mass, charge and force are words. They get their meaning from what they mark out in experience.
  13. Nature did not come labeled.
  14. A name is not a thing.
  15. A word has meaning as a description of phenomena, directly or at some remove.
  16. The language is never finished.
  17. Start from what is observed. Start with the observed whole. The parts reached by analysis have their properties only within that whole. They are not independent pieces from which the observed world is made.
  18. Observation always comes interpreted.
  19. Where conventions conflict, revise them.
  20. Use no more ideas than the phenomenon needs.
  21. Logic finds and removes inconsistency.
  22. Mathematics states relations and measures. A mathematical construction does not establish that anything physical answers to it. Continuous variables may be used to model phenomena on the large scale. Their use does not make the phenomena continuous. Keep the mathematical model apart from the phenomenon.
  23. This is empiricism, not a closed system of axioms.

The test for any new work (canon page 01):

  1. State the phenomenon and who or what observes it. Keep the record apart from its interpretation.
  2. For each term that matters, say what kind of term it is and how it connects to experience: a record, an inference, a definition or a proposal.
  3. Start from the exchanges of information. Do not put what is projected from them, the objects, distances and clocks, underneath as machinery that produces them. Calling that machinery a scaffold or a representation changes nothing.
  4. Do not turn discrete exchanges into a chain of elementary causes, or run them on a universal clock.
  5. List every premise taken on, physical and mathematical. Say what each explains and what stays assumed. A fit, or a consistent calculation, does not make its picture real. A conventional model may serve as a comparison. It is never the ground.
  6. Reject any premise that contradicts this page, and say what it contradicts. A premise that fits is not barred because this page lacks it.

Light (canon page 07, draft 7). Light is not something that travels in space. Space is in the light. A quantum of light is one event with two ends. c is the constant ratio between units of distance and units of time, for inertial observers using the same units. An elementary exchange has no carrier and no state in between. If the apparatus stores something, or a further exchange occurs, name it separately. c appears as the ratio of units and as the bound in the time relation. It is never a rate at which light goes anywhere. Read page 07 and its test before any work on light, optics, fiber or signaling.

Writing rules. Preserve quotations and source titles exactly. In our prose, use American English and plain declarative sentences. Omit performative qualifiers and repeated caveats. Do not add reminders that this is not the standard model. State assumptions, uncertainty, conditional results and open questions exactly. Do not call c a speed or describe light or action as traveling, moving, propagating, being carried or arriving, except in an explicit comparison or denial. Particle names appear only in quotations or explicit comparisons. Do not use "honest," "honestly," "honesty" or any derivative as authorial self-certification. Do not use "violent" or its variations. Give each consequential claim its source and status: native to the authors' texts (with the location), imported mathematics or physics, adopted premise, definition, measurement, fit, reconstruction, or conjecture. Preserve the governing measurement and paper-first requirements.
