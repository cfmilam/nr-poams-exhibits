This exhibit is an extrapolation of the foundational work of N. Vivian (Viv) Pope and Dr. Anthony D. Osborne, PhD, in the philosophy of Normal Realism (NR) and the mathematics of the Pope Osborne Angular Momentum Synthesis (POAMS).

From "Orbital Time Dilation" — Osborne & Pope (POAMS)

A particle P orbits a central mass M on a circular path at radius r. We compare two clocks:

■ Deep Space Time (DST) — coordinate time t, far from any gravity.

■ Orbiter Proper Time — time τ experienced by P.

Eq 2.9 — Velocity-only (gravitational) dilation:
\(\displaystyle \tau = \sqrt{1 - \frac{GM}{rc^2}}\;\cdot\; t\)

Eq 3.6 — True orbital dilation (gravity + orbital velocity):
\(\displaystyle \tau = \sqrt{1 - \frac{3GM}{rc^2}}\;\cdot\; t\)
