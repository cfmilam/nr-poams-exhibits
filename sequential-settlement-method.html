Pairwise Reality

The Sequential Settlement Method for reconstructing apparent many-centre behavior from two-ended completions, retained receipts and unresolved alternatives.

POAMS-NATIVE RESEARCH METHOD / CONDITIONAL MANY-CENTRE PROGRAM / NOT YET A GENERAL SOLUTION
Standing thesis. Reality settles through two-ended completions. Apparent many-body behavior is therefore to be reconstructed from an ordered ledger of admissible pair settlements, retained receipts and unresolved coherent alternatives—not presumed to be one simultaneous physical act among (N) independent bodies. This is a method declaration and research program. It does not prove that every effective many-body term reduces to a classical sequence or make aggregate calculation automatically easy.

1 · The ontological replacement

The conventional many-body question begins with a simultaneous state of many entities and asks how all of them influence one another. POAMS begins one level lower: which two boundary seats can complete a particular exchange, what receipt that completion leaves, and which completion is then admissible?

\[A+B+C\longrightarrow A'+B'+C'\]

is not taken as a primitive physical act. A candidate ledger history instead has the form

\[A\mathrel{\overset{R_1}{\longleftrightarrow}}B,\qquad B[R_1]\mathrel{\overset{R_2}{\longleftrightarrow}}C,\qquad A[R_1]\mathrel{\overset{R_3}{\longleftrightarrow}}C[R_2].\]

Each (R_i) is one co-signed completion receipt. Brackets denote retained state after earlier settlement. The apparent collective process is the coarse-grained reading of the ledger, not an additional substance or a force acting across a void.

2 · What the Alpha exhibit contributed

The Alpha exhibit supplies a bounded example of the method. It (1) names measured (alpha) as a calibration rather than a derivation, (2) applies the resulting coefficient (K_Q=\alpha\hbar c) to a declared pair, (3) keeps both endpoints in the reduced-mass account, (4) calculates a conditional receipt, and (5) refuses to promote the model-implied internal ratio (v/c=\alpha) into a direct measurement.

The reusable lesson is not that its circular model settles molecular or condensed-matter behavior. It is that every number travels with a typed source, pair of endpoints, posting rule and claim boundary. A many-centre calculation must preserve those receipts rather than average them away before the relevant pair is formed.

4 · Intransitivity is a completion rule

\[A\leftrightarrow B,\quad B\leftrightarrow C\quad\not\Rightarrow\quad A\leftrightarrow C.\]

Connection through a ledger does not automatically create a direct completion route. Each proposed handoff owes its own compatible boundary, phase relation, closure opportunity and two-ended co-signing. The intransitivity concerns admissibility of settlement; it is not a failure of conservation and does not forbid a later (A\leftrightarrow C) completion after the state has changed.

This distinction makes natural sparsity possible. The physically active graph is the graph of admissible completions at the present retained state, not the complete graph of all conceivable pairs.

5 · Order is physical

\[\mathcal T_{BC}\mathcal T_{AB}(S_0)\ne\mathcal T_{AB}\mathcal T_{BC}(S_0)\quad\text{in general}.\]

The first completion changes boundary availability, phase, custody or retained account before the next is attempted. Screening, saturation, finite coordination, hysteresis and path dependence may therefore be ledger-capacity effects rather than new primitive forces.

This does not abolish the traditional difficulty. It can relocate it from solving one simultaneous (N)-body influence law to selecting and composing admissible histories. There may still be combinatorially many histories, long-lived memory, congestion, closed transaction loops and effective higher-order terms after intermediate receipts are hidden.

6 · Completed settlements are not unresolved alternatives

A classical sequence applies only after a completion has left a discriminating record. Where several alternative pairings remain unresolved, POAMS uses the adopted Coherent Completion Kernel: alternatives compose as a phase-bearing state, admissible reversible controls act before terminal settlement, and the terminal record uses the declared CCK measure.

Pairwise endpoints therefore do not imply classical pairwise additivity. An effective three-body term may encode coherent alternative composition, order dependence or a hidden intermediate receipt without being a primitive three-ended act.

7 · What the method may explain

  • Screening: prior settlements change which seats remain exposed or compatible.
  • Saturation and finite coordination: one finite boundary cannot complete unlimited independent postings at once.
  • Exclusion-like occupancy: custody and seat availability restrict successor completions.
  • Hysteresis and memory: the retained ledger depends on settlement history.
  • Collective modes: repeated pair postings may coarse-grain into a stable far-field organization.
  • Effective higher-body terms: eliminating intermediate receipts or unresolved alternatives can produce non-pair-additive observables.

These are research prospects, not deductions from the pairwise thesis alone. Each application must state the ledger rule that produces the claimed behavior and the observation that could defeat it.

8 · What remains owed

  1. Retained-state sufficiency: what exact record predicts the declared future repertoire?
  2. Admissibility: which pair can complete next, established independently of the target result?
  3. Posting: how does one completion alter both endpoint accounts?
  4. Conflict resolution: what happens when candidate completions demand the same finite seat?
  5. Coherent composition: which alternatives remain unresolved, and what physical control supplies their relative phase?
  6. Coarse-graining: under what limit does the ledger recover the observed collective description?
  7. Empirical custody: what raw records distinguish the sequential ledger from a pair-additive or conventional many-body model?

Without these, “everything is pairwise” is an ontological direction, not a solved calculation.

9 · Application protocol

  1. Identify the actual completion endpoints and finite seats.
  2. Name the incoming retained receipts; do not substitute an untyped global state.
  3. Freeze the admissibility and conflict rules before comparison.
  4. Enumerate the settlement orders that remain physically possible.
  5. Keep unresolved alternatives coherent where a discriminating record does not exist.
  6. Post each completed receipt once to both endpoint accounts.
  7. Coarse-grain only after naming which ledger distinctions the observable discards.
  8. Derive the apparent many-centre observable and compare it with both naïve pair additivity and the relevant extant model.
  9. State the adverse raw result that would defeat the proposed ledger.

10 · First decisive test program

The first strong test should use a controlled three-centre system in which naïve pair additivity fails and settlement order or boundary occupancy can be changed without changing the nominal three constituents.

11 · Current verdict

POAMS replaces an assumed simultaneous many-body ontology with a pairwise settlement calculus. It recasts the hard problem as admissibility, ordering, retention, coherent alternative composition and coarse-graining. That is a substantive change in what must be calculated and controlled. It is not yet a general solution to many-body physics.

The method earns promotion one application at a time: construct the ledger without using the target, freeze the discriminator, preserve the raw record, and let nature decide whether the apparent collective behavior is the far-field reading of those settlements.
