2 · Two Regimes, Two Geometries — the Forced Pairing

The license, restated once. A quantum is a completed turn (\( \oint d\varphi = 2\pi n \)); a circulation that does not return on itself does not close and cannot persist. A census can only stack persistent closures. So the geometry of return is not a curiosity — it is the admission criterion, exactly the license Aufbau as Closure §4 states for the outer frontier. Here it is applied to the core.

The mathematics offers exactly two ledgers. Bertrand's theorem (1873): among all central ledgers, precisely two return every bound circulation to closure — the far-field profile (\( \propto 1/r \); one full turn between closest approaches, \( k = \omega_\varphi/\omega_r = 1 \); the eye at the focus) and the confined profile (\( \propto r^2 \); pericenter every half turn, \( k = \tfrac12 \); the eye at the center). These are the first two anchor panels of the Aufbau dictionary. There is no third.

And the vortex has exactly two radial regimes:

  • Outside the eyewall the net interior circulation reads from afar as a single center and the books thin as \(1/r\) — the far-field arm. Its ladder is the outer census: \(n\)-degeneracy, capacities \(2n^2\), the periodic table (already computed, including its ordering).
  • Inside the eyewall the census is saturated: the Mass Ledger proves (saturation-at-capacity, a theorem there — no hard core assumed) that the interior density is pinned flat at the capacity point, meeting \( \rho_0 \) at finite depth, with only the skin non-uniform. A quantum circulating within a flat, saturated accumulation keeps books whose restoring entry grows linearly with displacement — the interior-uniform result (extant anchor: Newton's interior theorem), read here as census subtraction exactly as "screening" is read in the outer channel. A flat interior is the confined arm.

So the pairing is forced from both sides. Two closure-compatible geometries exist; the vortex has two regimes; each regime's own profile is one of the two. The outer/pattern channel took the far-field ladder — that census is the periodic table. The inner/core channel takes the confined ladder. Translation, stated once: the "harmonic potential" of the extant shell model is the mechanistic representation of this kinematic fact — a flat saturated interior a circulation must balance its books against. No force crosses a gap anywhere on this page.

\[ N = 2n_r + l \]

(extant parenthetical: the oscillator's evenly spaced levels). Rung \(N\) holds the windings \( l = N, N-2, \ldots, 1 \text{ or } 0 \); each winding \(l\) supports \(2l+1\) orientations; each orientation holds the binary pair — the two signs of the quantum's own turn, the same doubling the outer census carries (the Mass Ledger's ↑/↓ column; extant: spin-½). \[ \mathrm{cap}(N) \;=\; 2 \sum_{l = N, N-2, \ldots} (2l+1) \;=\; (N+1)(N+2) \;=\; 2,\ 6,\ 12,\ 20,\ 30,\ 42,\ \ldots \]

4 · The Co-turn Split at the Eyewall

Every quantum on the ladder carries two circulations at once: its winding about the barycenter (orbit — writhe) and its own turn (spin — twist). The two never conflate, but they compose. Composing an orientation ladder of \(2l+1\) steps with the binary pair yields exactly two combined ladders — a counting identity:

\[ 2\,(2l+1) \;=\; \underbrace{(2l+2)}_{\text{co-turning}} \;+\; \underbrace{(2l)}_{\text{counter-turning}} \]

The co-turning multiplet — own turn composed with the winding — is always one pair longer. In the far field this split is the \( \alpha^2 \)-tiny fine structure (mechanism derived in The Elements; magnitude a declared flat direction).   • Direction (channel-motivated, named in Scope): a quantum whose own turn co-turns with its winding routes that twist through the coupling channel — co-sense contracts the natural radius, settling deeper in the ledger (the App-5 channel rule; micro and macro spin-orbit are one geometric effect — the corpus touchstone, §4).   • Growth (derived structure): each added winding step adds one full unit of composed circulation, so the co/counter rate split grows in proportion to \(2l+1\), while the confined ladder's rung spacing \(\Delta\nu\) (turns/s) is constant.
