Keren Li’s Angular-Momentum Calculator

This exhibit is an extrapolation of the foundational work of N. Vivian “Viv” Pope & Dr. Anthony D. Osborne, PhD, in the philosophy of Normal Realism (NR) and the mathematics of the Pope Osborne Angular Momentum Synthesis (POAMS).

A faithful reconstruction of the Java/Excel calculator, with the modern correction made explicit: the finite body is the calculation. A point-mass spin limit drives the required angular velocity to infinity.

Original archive: preserved intact Core result: mass cancels Correction: no point-body spin shortcut

Two layers, kept separate

Historical layer

Keren’s calculator asks how much rigid-body spin energy is required if a constrained orbit is compared with its force-free natural orbit. It implements a clean energy ledger:

v* = GME/(v rO)
KS = 1/2 m |v*² − v²|
ω  = sign √(2KS/I)

Modern correction

The calculation only works for a finite object with finite moment of inertia. If the small body is treated as a classical point, then I → 0, and the required ω diverges.

I = C m R²
ω² = |v*² − v²|/(C R²)
R → 0 ⇒ ω → ∞

Faithful calculator

Defaults and formulas follow the Java bytecode and workbook sheets. Use the presets to reproduce the preserved examples.

The sign branch is automatic: if the constrained orbital kinetic energy is larger than the natural-orbit kinetic energy, the calculator returns negative spin.

Why mass does not rescue it

Both the required spin energy and the rigid-body moment of inertia scale linearly with body mass. For any fixed geometry:

KS = 1/2 m |v*² − v²|
I  = C m R²
ω² = 2KS/I = |v*² − v²|/(C R²)

The mass disappears. This is the sharp result in Keren’s work: density changes do not fix the spin requirement when the size and shape stay fixed.

The preserved 2013 result

The handwritten pages use a 100 kg, 0.3 m-radius solid sphere with Earth-surface co-rotation values. They report:

v* ≈ 134,658 m/s
KO ≈ 1.08 × 10⁷ J
KS ≈ 9.07 × 10¹¹ J
I  = 3.6 kg m²
ω  ≈ 7.10 × 10⁵ rad/s ≈ 1.13 × 10⁵ rev/s

Historical wording corrected: the notes say “geosynchronous,” but the numbers are Earth radius and equatorial surface speed. That means co-rotating at Earth angular rate at the surface, not a satellite at true geosynchronous radius.

Ledger note (rate ontology): the joules above are the extant mechanical ledger, preserved as the 2013 record. In the ledger they are aggregate rate: E/h — KS ≈ 9.07×10¹¹ J ↔ ≈1.4×10⁴⁵ turns/s, summed across the sphere's ~10²⁷ quanta, each quantum's own rate shifting only microscopically. Bulk kinetic energy is distributed rate-increase across a census, not a new substance — kinematic, not mechanistic.

Normal Realism / POAMS reading

Read historically, this is an early AM-first feasibility calculation: if spin energy is asked to change the natural-orbit ledger, ordinary rigid rotation demands absurd angular velocity.

Read through the current touchstone, it also marks a trap. Orbit and spin are distinct channels. A finite rigid body has classical moment of inertia; a point body does not. So the modern exhibit preserves Keren’s computation while refusing the silent point-mass shortcut.

What this does not prove: no Hayasaka-style handedness/sign claim is present in the notes. Sign belongs to the modern channel question—twist through variable-G versus writhe through orbital AM—not to a retroactive claim about Keren’s calculation.
