Spectral lines. A re-closure from upper mode n to lower mode N is the difference of two ground-referenced terms:

$$\nu_{n\to N}=\nu_n-\nu_N =cR_H\left(\frac{1}{N^2}-\frac{1}{n^2}\right),\qquad n>N.$$

N = 1, 2, 3 gives the Lyman, Balmer, and Paschen series. The interactives below calculate vacuum gross-line values from this equation and the imported RH; they do not claim to derive the empirical scale, resolved components, or intensities.

7. The POAMS Mathematical Derivation

This is the complete derivation of the inverse-square shape used by the page. It begins from two stated POAMS premises and keeps the empirical normalization visible.

Step 1: Pythagorean time ratio

$$t_R^2=t^2+\left(\frac{s}{c}\right)^2,\qquad v=\frac{s}{t_R}$$ $$\frac{t_R^2}{t^2}=\frac{1}{1-v^2/c^2}.$$

Here t is the intrinsic leg and tR the reunited observational count.

Step 2: Whole-number closure

$$\frac{t_R}{t}=n,\qquad n=1,2,3,\ldots$$ $$\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{v_n^2}{c^2}=1-\frac{1}{n^2}.$$

The integer ratio is the POAMS discreteness premise. It is not inferred from the numerical spectrum.

Step 3: Mode-deficit bookkeeping

Pope's Appendix 3 defines the booked mode energy as en = mRvn². Therefore

$$e_n=m_Rc^2\left(1-\frac{1}{n^2}\right),\qquad \nu_n=\frac{e_n}{h}=\nu_R\left(1-\frac{1}{n^2}\right),$$ $$\nu_R\equiv\frac{m_Rc^2}{h}=cR_H.$$

The factor mR is Pope's spectral scale mass, not me. On this page νR is normalized to the imported hydrogen value.

Step 4: Subtract two terms

$$\nu_{n\to N}=\nu_n-\nu_N =cR_H\left(\frac{1}{N^2}-\frac{1}{n^2}\right),\qquad n>N.$$

The subtraction is exact. Thus POAMS derives the inverse-square ladder from its stated ratio and energy-booking premises; spectroscopy supplies the dimensional scale RH.
