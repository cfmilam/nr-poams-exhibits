2 · The reconstruction that closes

POAMS has adopted the Pope–Osborne orthogonal-turn premise. For a constant ring state:

a² + β² = 1,   a = dτ/dt,   therefore   dτ/dt = √(1 − β²) = 1/γ.

This is a constitutive premise, not a theorem of whole-turn closure. Given an independently measured ring state, laboratory interval t accumulates τ=t/γ of internal count. If a fixed organization has the same survival law when indexed by accumulated proper time:

Slab(t)=S0(t/γ),   therefore   τ̄lab=γτ̄0.

Using CERN's γ=29.33 and the current reported rest mean life 2.1969811 μs gives 64.4375 μs. The reported ring values are 64.419(58) μs and 64.368(29) μs. This is a successful reconstruction toward known results—not an independent prediction.

5 · The hazard chase

The proper-time relation changes the argument of a survival function; by itself it cannot choose the function. But the corpus already contains a disciplined candidate dynamics family: the Receipt-Local Dynamics Principle (RLDP). This experiment is its simplest case—one retained predictive state with one absorbing completion edge.

Conditional hazard-form theorem

Add one explicit clause: after each completed internal turn, every admitted future preparation and detector response depends only on the same retained state—not on its age or unrecorded history. With a stationary apparatus and fixed open channel, the per-turn survival factor q is then identical on every turn. Whole-turn discreteness gives exactly

S(n+m)=S(n)S(m),   S(n)=qn=(1−p)n.

This is a geometric law on the discrete internal-turn count n. Writing n=νintτ produces the far-field display

S0(τ)=exp[−λ0τ],   λ0=−νint ln(1−p),
Slab(t)=exp[−λ0∫(dτ/dt)dt].

The exponential is therefore conditionally forced by predictive-state sufficiency + stationarity + one absorbing edge; it is not a continuum underneath the turns. If age, preparation order, or hidden history changes the hazard, the state was incomplete and the theorem does not apply.

The clock no-go survives

Neither closure nor RLDP fixes p. Any 0<p<1 preserves the same state, edge, receipts, geometric survival form, and proper-time collapse while changing the lifetime. Multiplying the edge conductance rescales the clock without changing the ledger. The absolute rate is therefore proved non-identifiable from present premises.

Using the imported conventional mass calibration 105.6583755 MeV and E=hν gives νint≈2.5548×1022 turns/s. The reported rest mean life contains about 5.6129×1016 internal turns, so the calibrated small-p tariff is approximately p=1.7816×10−17 per internal turn. Those numbers measure the missing tariff; they do not derive it.

6 · Frozen discriminator and failure conditions

Proper-turn collapse test. Before examining a new multi-state dataset, transform every event time by τi=∫a(t)dt from independently logged ring inputs. Fit one λ0 globally. The proper-time hazard must be constant and shared across at least three γ states; two radii or focusing geometries; opposite circulation senses and conventional charge labels; and frozen early/late windows.

  • Kill: a reproducible residual following orbital acceleration, radius, circuit count, or circulation sense falsifies the minimal premise.
  • Kill: separate λ0 values required by different γ states after direct detector controls defeat the one-account proposal.
  • Artifact gate: residuals moving with acceptance, pile-up, gain, scraping, loss correction, timing cuts, or background remain instrumental.
  • Null meaning: successful collapse supports proper-time indexing but does not distinguish POAMS from an empirically equivalent extant account.
