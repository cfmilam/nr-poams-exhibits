The momentums formerly known as muons: circulating detector occurrences, accumulated proper time, and the exact point where POAMS runs out of earned law.

2 · The reconstruction that closes

POAMS has adopted the Pope–Osborne orthogonal-turn premise. For a constant ring state:

a² + β² = 1,   a = dτ/dt,   therefore   dτ/dt = √(1 − β²) = 1/γ.

This is a constitutive premise, not a theorem of whole-turn closure. Given an independently measured ring state, laboratory interval t accumulates τ=t/γ of internal count. If a fixed organization has the same survival law when indexed by accumulated proper time:

Slab(t)=S0(t/γ),   therefore   τ̄lab=γτ̄0.

Using CERN's γ=29.33 and the current reported rest mean life 2.1969811 μs gives 64.4375 μs. The reported ring values are 64.419(58) μs and 64.368(29) μs. This is a successful reconstruction toward known results—not an independent prediction.

3 · Native typing

  • Identity: the reproducible preparation-to-completion chain whose settings and outputs remain statistically stable—an operational criterion, not yet a microscopic construction.
  • Orbit: the approximately 7.1 m ring circulation is orbital writhe imposed and retained by the apparatus.
  • Internal count: a=dτ/dt is the remaining count projection of the same local turn budget, not a second time-substance.
  • Completion: a registered decay occurrence ends that retained preparation and posts a new source–apparatus–detector record.

5 · The exact missing law

The proper-time relation changes the argument of a survival function; it cannot choose the function. Closure, conservation, and an allowed channel do not force a constant hazard, an exponential distribution, or the measured scale.

Underdetermination: every survival curve S0(τ)—exponential, Weibull, delayed, or oscillatory—maps to laboratory time as Slab(t)=S0(t/γ). Ring dilation tests the rate map while leaving the completion law untouched.

Minimal candidate extension—not ratified

For a stationary retained organization with fixed open channels, propose constant completion probability per unit accumulated proper turn:

P(completion in dτ | survived)=λ0dτ,
S0(τ)=e−λ₀τ,   Slab(t)=exp[−λ0∫(dτ/dt)dt].

This is ontologically admissible, economical, and falsifiable. But λ0 remains measured, and “memoryless per proper turn” is an added premise—not a disguised derivation.

6 · Frozen discriminator and failure conditions

Proper-turn collapse test. Before examining a new multi-state dataset, transform every event time by τi=∫a(t)dt from independently logged ring inputs. Fit one λ0 globally. The proper-time hazard must be constant and shared across at least three γ states; two radii or focusing geometries; opposite circulation senses and conventional charge labels; and frozen early/late windows.

  • Kill: a reproducible residual following orbital acceleration, radius, circuit count, or circulation sense falsifies the minimal premise.
  • Kill: separate λ0 values required by different γ states after direct detector controls defeat the one-account proposal.
  • Artifact gate: residuals moving with acceptance, pile-up, gain, scraping, loss correction, timing cuts, or background remain instrumental.
  • Null meaning: successful collapse supports proper-time indexing but does not distinguish POAMS from an empirically equivalent extant account.
