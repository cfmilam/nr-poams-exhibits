2 · The reconstruction that closes

POAMS has adopted the Pope–Osborne orthogonal-turn premise. For a constant ring state:

a² + β² = 1,   a = dτ/dt,   therefore   dτ/dt = √(1 − β²) = 1/γ.

This is a constitutive premise, not a theorem of whole-turn closure. Given an independently measured ring state, laboratory interval t accumulates τ=t/γ of internal count. If a fixed organization has the same survival law when indexed by accumulated proper time:

Slab(t)=S0(t/γ),   therefore   τ̄lab=γτ̄0.

Using CERN's γ=29.33 and the current reported rest mean life 2.1969811 μs gives 64.4375 μs. The reported ring values are 64.419(58) μs and 64.368(29) μs. This is a successful reconstruction toward known results—not an independent prediction.

5 · The hazard chase

The proper-time relation changes the argument of a survival function; by itself it cannot choose the function. This experiment is the simplest Receipt-Local Dynamics case—one retained predictive state with one absorbing completion edge.

Conditional hazard-form theorem

After each completed internal turn, suppose every admitted future preparation and detector response depends only on the same retained state—not its age or unrecorded history. With a stationary apparatus and fixed open channel, the per-turn survival factor q is identical on every turn:

S(n+m)=S(n)S(m),   S(n)=qn=(1−p)n.

This is geometric survival on the discrete count n. Writing n=νintτ gives the far-field display

S0(τ)=exp[−λ0τ],   λ0=−νint ln(1−p),
Slab(t)=exp[−λ0∫(dτ/dt)dt].

The exponential is conditionally forced by predictive-state sufficiency, stationarity and one absorbing edge. It is not a continuum underneath the turns.

Finite Completion-Address Principle · ratified 30 September 2026

A stationary retained organization has a finite integer census NA of phase addresses relative to a declared preparation, boundary and completion channel. If exactly g addresses admit the named co-signed completion, then

p=g/NA,   S(n)=(1−g/NA)n.

This narrows the clock from an arbitrary real hazard to an integer closure construction. It does not authorize fitting NA or g from the known lifetime. They must be constructed independently from closure geometry, phase sense, twist/writhe, boundary readiness and channel conditions.

Remaining no-go: the present corpus does not yet construct those integers. Using the imported mass calibration gives νint≈2.5548×1022 turns/s; the reported rest lifetime therefore supplies the target NA/g≈5.6129×1016 and p≈1.7816×10−17 per turn. These measure the missing address ratio; they do not derive it.

6 · Constructing g and NA

Primitive eligible multiplicity: g=1

The named event is inclusive termination of one retained preparation, not a catalogue of downstream detector shapes. Receipt uniqueness posts that termination once. The primitive address pair must also be coprime; multiplying both g and NA merely duplicates labels without changing any physics. Under that declared granularity, the minimal inclusive channel has g=1.

Empirical census calibration

The independently reported mass-rate and rest-lifetime inputs give

νintτ̄0 = 56,128,652,261,607,648.695…

The nearest-integer representative is NAcal=56,128,652,261,607,649. This is calibrated, not geometrically derived. Propagating the quoted input uncertainties gives approximately

56,128,596,042,549,608 ≤ NA ≤ 56,128,708,480,665,689   (1σ),

containing 112,438,116,082 candidate integers. No existing POAMS invariant independently selects one member. Exact NA therefore remains open; printing the rounded central value does not close it.

Frozen discriminator and failure conditions

Proper-turn collapse test. Before examining a new multi-state dataset, transform every event time by τi=∫a(t)dt from independently logged ring inputs. Fit one address ratio globally. The proper-time hazard must be shared across at least three γ states; two geometries; opposite circulation senses and conventional charge labels; and frozen early/late windows.

  • Kill: reproducible residual dependence on age, acceleration, radius, circuit count, or circulation sense falsifies the simple address state.
  • Refine: separately resolved completion classes may require g>1 or multiple address subsets, but only when raw records establish distinct primitive postings.
  • Artifact gate: residuals moving with acceptance, pile-up, gain, scraping, timing cuts, or background remain instrumental.
  • Null meaning: successful collapse supports the address principle but is not POAMS-exclusive.

7 · Successor grammar: a useful failure

The deterministic-cycle construction is ruled out

Let one whole turn apply a deterministic successor T on NA finite addresses, with one completing address. For a fixed preparation, completion then occurs on a fixed turn. Even if the initial address is uniformly unknown, the waiting law is bounded:

S(n)=(NA−n)/NA   for 0≤n<NA,

not geometric. The conditional hazard rises as unused addresses are exhausted. Therefore NA cannot be constructed merely as ord(T).

The smallest viable grammar

Let K be the whole-turn transition kernel on surviving addresses and G the completing subset. The geometric law requires

Pr(An+1∈G | survived through n)=g/NA   on every turn.

Uniform re-equilibration over all addresses after every survived turn is sufficient. A correlated kernel is also possible, but only if it preserves the same conditional completing weight for every admitted surviving preparation. This makes the remaining debt precise: POAMS must independently construct both the finite address set and the physical mixing or re-equilibration operation.

No product-count shortcut. Closure phase, twist, writhe and boundary readiness cannot simply be multiplied. Lk=Tw+Wr constrains twist and writhe, so treating them as independent factors double-counts unless the constrained quotient is constructed. Boundary readiness is relational and could supply the needed re-equilibration, but no native or measured kernel presently establishes it.
