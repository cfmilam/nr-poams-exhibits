Reading conventions (the labels doctrine). 1 · The decade, honestly

Two computing programs pursued different operating regimes through the same decade. Gate-model hardware protects coherent phase over the finite depth required by an algorithm; conventional accelerators repeatedly commit irreversible operations. Their resource ledgers are not directly interchangeable, but the contrast motivates this exhibit’s proposal: use bounded phase evolution where useful, then schedule explicit completion instead of treating every completion as failure.

The record, stated at strength on both sides. Gate-model programs have produced genuine laboratory results: below-threshold error-corrected logical elements extant record: Google Willow, Dec 2024, low-overhead logical encoding in a declared trapped-ion regime company/preprint record: Quantinuum Helios, Nov 2025, and sharply reduced theoretical factoring-resource estimates extant estimates: Gidney 2019→2025. Superconducting platforms nevertheless require millikelvin operation and protected logical circuits can require large physical overheads whose exact value depends on hardware error, code, distance, algorithm, and target failure probability. Several advantage claims have also prompted stronger classical baselines. This exhibit treats those facts as a resource-accounting question, not as a universal verdict on every quantum platform or workload.

Meanwhile, large conventional accelerators scaled rapidly for machine-learning workloads sourced estimate: epoch-scaling analyses. The POAMS engineering question is narrower: can a processor combine bounded phase operations with scheduled, auditable completions at a useful error, energy, and throughput point?

2 · The ontological audit

Take the four load-bearing entity-labels one at a time and ask what the bench actually has.

“Superposition” → a controlled state with a phase coordinate. A bench implementation exposes operational coordinates such as relative phase and population/amplitude (or Bloch-sphere polar and azimuthal coordinates). This exhibit maps the phase coordinate to one POAMS turning. General preparation and single-seat control can change both coordinates; phase rotation is one operation class, not the whole control grammar. No foreign entity is adopted by making that operational distinction.

“Entanglement” → co-signing. A “maximally entangled pair” is one shared quantity of angular momentum booked across two seats — a co-signed exchange, in the corpus's standing grammar The Polyverse; POAMS & Memory. The closure is definite in magnitude — one whole ℏ, durable by whole-quantum bookkeeping — while its axis is not pre-set: the axis posts at readout, against whichever analyzer the seat chooses. That refinement is load-bearing — a pre-set axis read locally is precisely the model the Bell record falsifies (the derivation closes this section). The extant program’s entangled superpositions are open bookings that any stray completion re-writes; the co-signature is a completed one. The resource is real; the fragility is the grammar's, not the resource's.

“Measurement/collapse” → completion. Readout is a whole-quantum closure: an exchange completes, posts, and is final. The extant program treats this as a catastrophe to postpone; the mystery (“why does observation destroy the computation?”) is manufactured by the ontology. Natively there is nothing to explain — completion is the ledger's one primitive act, and a processor should use it.

“Decoherence” → a POAMS untracked-completion model. The simplest native model treats environmental disturbance as untracked completions that rewrite the phase ledger; an independent first-event process then gives a characteristic waiting time. That is a model to test against the measured decay law. Distributed rates, correlated closures, reversible dephasing, and control imperfections require richer ledgers. The exhibit does not identify every measured coherence time with one microscopic event by definition.

The headline: THE CLAMP. A gate-model computation must protect coherent phase over the finite space-time volume and depth of its circuit. The corpus has a standing POAMS premise The Polyverse §7: nature contains no perfect clamp; uncontrolled completion cannot be made identically impossible. Refrigeration, shielding, decoding, and redundancy are operational responses to that finite-depth requirement. Their overhead is compatible with the POAMS diagnosis, but it does not by itself prove the diagnosis or establish one universal overhead ratio.

The “Hilbert space” census

The $2^n$ joint-outcome census is not itself a count of hardware components. In this exhibit the physical inventory is $n$ local phase seats plus an explicitly engineered coupling graph and its controls; the relation count follows that hardware graph and need not be linear in $n$. The POAMS proposal must therefore be judged by measured circuit resources and validated operations, not by equating a descriptive state-space dimension with either literal parallel objects or free hardware.

Why small machines work anyway (honesty)

None of this says the laboratory record is fake. Interference algorithms are real phase arithmetic, and in the regime where the clamp approximately holds — small n, short depth, heroic isolation — the machines are phase engines, and they compute. The native claim is narrower and sharper: the resource was never the exponential state space; the ontology misdirects the hardware budget toward protecting a fiction; and the one application class that is native for free is completion-grammar emulation — what the extant program calls quantum simulation, which is its one commercially honest near-term lane extant record.

The Bell record: constraint, not derivation

Any reader of §2 should demand this: correlation experiments on separated pairs violate every local-deterministic bound — the loophole-free record is decisive extant record: loophole-free Bell tests, 2015→; Hensen et al., Nature 526, 682. If co-signing were a pre-set shared orientation read locally at each seat, Bell’s theorem caps its correlations at CHSH ≤ 2: the deterministic pre-set gives the sawtooth ($E=-(1-2|a{-}b|/\pi)$, CHSH $=2$ exactly), the stochastic pre-set undershoots ($E=-\tfrac13\cos(a{-}b)$, CHSH $\approx 0.94$). The record kills both. It constrains the ontology, but it does not by itself select the observed correlation law.

One seat: a whole-ℏ closure against an analyzer at angle θ posts ±1 — no fraction exists. With one named premise [P1] — the mean posted sense equals the geometric projection, $\langle s\rangle=\cos\theta$ (linear; nothing squared) — the outcome law is forced:

$$P(+\,|\,\theta)=\tfrac{1+\cos\theta}{2}=\cos^2(\theta/2)$$

The measured quantum correlation is $E(a,b)=-\cos(a-b)$ and gives $\mathrm{CHSH}=2\sqrt2$ for the standard settings extant record. This exhibit does not derive that cosine law from co-signing. The bound $|E|\le1$ alone does not imply the Tsirelson ceiling, and order-invariance plus no-signaling do not uniquely select the cosine. A POAMS account still owes a positive probability rule that produces the correlation without importing it.

Falsifiable status: co-signing is a two-ended POAMS transaction, not a local pre-set-axis model. The native prediction is therefore restored at prophetic grade: under the stated low-thermal-occupation and visibility envelope, four preregistered analyzer settings should give $E(a,b)=-\cos(a-b)$ and $|S_{\rm CHSH}|\rightarrow2\sqrt2$. The phase-sum variance and uniform single-seat distributions remain commissioning controls, not substitutes for the discriminator. Every trial—including loss and no-commit events—must remain in the raw table without fair-sampling deletion. Before unblinding, register numerical thresholds for pair-production fidelity, single-seat visibility, thermal occupation, setting independence, timing assignment, and detection/loss accounting. Only if every validity threshold passes does an upper 95% confidence bound $|S_{\rm CHSH}|\le2$ falsify this co-signing species. Reaching the target would support the architecture but would not turn the displayed cosine into a prior derivation.

4 · The constructive doctrine: compute with the grammar

Stop fighting completion. Schedule it.

evolve → close → post → repeat

A phase-completion processor alternates two kinds of stage. In an open-phase stage, continuous phase evolves and combines by interference — the analog compute fluid, fast and cheap and perishable. In a commit stage, accumulated phase is deliberately, irreversibly closed: read out, resolved to the nearest whole turn, and posted to a winding register. The commit is the machine's clock tick, its readout, and its error philosophy in one primitive.

Closure-as-commit versus error correction

The proposed program digitizes repeatedly: sub-turn drift during a bounded open stage is rounded at commit and the result is posted to an integer store. That can localize analog error, but it does not abolish it. With per-commit error $p$ over $M$ required commits, $P_{fail}=1-(1-p)^M\approx Mp$ for small $Mp$; fixed redundancy merely replaces $p$ with a smaller nonzero value. Commit cadence, guard bands, checkpointing, redundancy, and energy must be sized for a declared operation count and target failure probability.

Winding registers: discrete readout with finite error

A winding register can be wrong by an integer: a missed, duplicated, or misdirected wrap is a discrete error, but not automatically detectable. Modulus also requires machinery: integer winding supplies $\mathbb Z$, not $\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z$. A boundary detector and reset transition implement $N-1\rightarrow0$; carry or borrow is emitted only at that radix boundary in positional mode and suppressed in modular mode. 5 · The substrate: magnonic / YIG

The doctrine wants a medium with controllable phase, interference, nonlinear coupling, and room-temperature operation. Yttrium iron garnet supports spin-wave excitations across microwave bands and has among the lowest damping found in practical magnetic media; interferometer and logic functions are demonstrated in the extant record extant record: YIG spin-wave logic. Device-scale performance still depends on geometry, bias, mode, temperature, drive, and readout.

The room-temperature condensate: a driven coherent bus, not yet a register

Under parametric pumping, a YIG film can form a macroscopically phase-coherent magnon population at room temperature extant record: pumped room-T magnon condensation. That makes it a candidate phase/interference bus in this architecture. Pump-maintained coherence is not evidence of a durable completed register. A register claim requires a pump-off retained state with measured lifetime, write fidelity, read fidelity, erase/reversal operation, phase-slip statistics, and a closed energy/angular-momentum receipt. This is the same distinction ratified in the Brilliant Vortex programme: long-lived motion is not automatically held-in winding.

Collective scaling is open. If a fixed measured total disturbance rate $R_{env}$ produces independent phase kicks of size $1/N$, a toy model gives diffusion proportional to $R_{env}/N^2$. If disturbances instead arrive per constituent, total rate grows with $N$ and the scaling weakens before correlations, damping, and pump heating are included. No universal $N^2$ law is derived. Falsifiable: vary population while holding the independently measured total disturbance rate—not merely bath temperature or nominal spectrum—fixed; fit the exponent with uncertainty and report null or adverse scaling at equal strength.

Patent discipline: this page states architecture and reads the published field record; device geometries, drive parameters, and bench specifics are deliberately absent.

7 · The crux, stated falsifiably

The precision wall

Analog phase machines die, historically, on precision: continuous quantities must be resolved ever more finely as problems scale, and noise does not cooperate. Any proposal that keeps its arithmetic in continuous phase inherits the wall.

The ghost-factor wall (the named enemy)

The one serious extant attempt at interference factoring — Gauss-sum factoring, demonstrated in NMR, cold atoms, and shaped-pulse optics extant record, 2007–2012 — was killed by its own community's analysis: suppressing false (“ghost”) factor resonances requires the number of interfering terms to grow as a power of the target integer — exponential in the digit count extant record: Štefańák et al. 2007. Elegant interference, no scaling. That analysis is the standard this page's bet must beat, and we name it rather than hide it.

Fig. 3 — the winding ALU, interactive

Pedagogical modulus-N state machine. The display advances a residue; an explicit boundary rule implements $N-1\rightarrow0$. Positional mode emits carry at that boundary; modular mode discards overflow. The JavaScript computes the expected transition—it does not demonstrate a physical winding medium, commit fidelity, or native modular arithmetic.

The bounded claim: architecture, not a complexity theorem

The architecture keeps persistent values in explicit integer stores and uses continuous phase only inside bounded stages. That design establishes neither classical simulability nor quantum advantage. The disclosed period-finding embodiment steps sequentially through modular powers. Its work is $O(r\,\mathrm{poly}(d))$ for order $r$ and $d$ input digits; because $r$ can approach $N\sim10^d$, its worst case is exponential in $d$. This classifies this embodiment only—it is not an impossibility theorem for every completion architecture. Any value must be demonstrated as throughput, density, or energy per validated operation against a fair digital baseline.

Fig. 4 — the two walls (drawn honestly)

Scaling-class guides, not hardware forecasts. Gauss-sum suppression uses $M\sim N^{1/4}$ terms, hence $\log_{10}M\sim d/4$. The disclosed sequential order finder uses $O(r\,\mathrm{poly}(d))$ validated steps and can approach $\log_{10}W\sim d$ in the worst case. The gate-model curve is a polynomial asymptotic guide only, not a qubit-count estimate. No favorable polynomial winding curve is assumed or claimed.
