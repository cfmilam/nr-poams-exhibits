NASA MSFC POAMS Study

Space Act Agreement and experimental-apparatus record, 2015–2021

SOURCE / HISTORYEXPERIMENTAL PROGRAM RECORDONTOLOGICALLY NON-CANONICALPRELIMINARY / UNRESOLVED
Historical-source notice. This NASA Technical Memorandum documents the NASA MSFC–Quantum Machines POAMS program, its apparatus lineage, and preliminary experimental observations. Its “spin-coupled force” formulation preserves Newtonian gravitational force and adds a second corrective force. That reformulation is not adopted as current POAMS ontology or mathematics. The reported V3 observation remains historically significant but experimentally unresolved; the memorandum does not establish a validated POAMS effect.
Why this record is preserved. NASA/TM–20205010911 records work performed under Space Act Agreement SAA8-1519855, signed 1 July 2015. It credits Quantum Machines, LLC, and Christopher Milam for requesting and funding the effort, and records the V1–V5 experimental lineage. Preservation here establishes history and custody; it does not ratify the memorandum's interpretation.

The ontological break

In printed §§4–6, the memorandum rejects the source treatment of pair-dependent effective G, holds Newtonian gravity fixed, and defines a residual “spin-coupled force” by subtracting Newtonian force from a total POAMS-labelled force. It then assumes that construction applies to any body-centred force system and extends it from macroscopic rotation to intrinsic nuclear spin. This is a NASA reformulation, not a native POAMS derivation. Current POAMS does not recognize Newtonian attraction plus a compensating POAMS force as its ontology.

Measurement verdict. The memorandum provides no run-level time series, uncertainty budget, measured nuclear-alignment fraction, blinded or randomized ordering, dummy-rotor result, magnet-only result, stationary matched control, or configuration reversal that avoids disassembly. The V3 sign reversal therefore does not isolate the proposed cause. Its decay and disappearance, followed by recovery with replacement magnets, must remain part of the adverse record.
